# Video 5: fact lock

Topic: "Where do you put a machine this hot?" (picked by the owner 2026-10-29). AI chips make enormous heat, so data centres
end up in cold, strange places: a Cold War bunker under a Stockholm park, a working mine in the Dolomites, the sea floor off
Shanghai, a test in orbit (where it overheats). Payoff: Helsinki keeps the heat for homes. Locked 2026-10-29 (research only).

Verdicts: **VERIFIED** = primary source, or 2+ reputable outlets agree. **REPORTED** = one outlet, an aggregator, or a forecast
we can only quote as a forecast. **FALSE** = contradicted by the data, or wrong as currently worded. "est." = an estimate.
