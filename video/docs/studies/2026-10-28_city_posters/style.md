# Look lens 2: visual style (colour, light, shape, texture, line, sky, water, type)

*Study, not a rule change (README). Status: draft in progress; numbers from `video/scripts/look/posters/style.py`.*

**In short.** (1) The posters' base is calmer than v2's and their darks are many: median C* 5-8 against v2's 13-14, a
quarter to half of each poster near-neutral, and 8-24 % under L* 40 as small dark pieces (v2: 1-4 %). Their saturated
colour is confetti exactly as our DNA asks. (2) Their landmarks are modelled with one deep step (lit to shade dL* -21 to
-37, median -29; ours -7 to -12) and their planes are a hard-edged patchwork whose steps shrink with distance. (3) Four of
the seven sit in the owner's banned beige, olive and grey (25-46 % of the land) and every poster carries a paper grain and
mottling (DIRECTION rule 4 forbids it); both clash with owner rules and are left to the owner.
