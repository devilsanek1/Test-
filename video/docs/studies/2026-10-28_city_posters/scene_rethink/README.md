# Scene 1 rethought from the ground up (2026-10-29)

**The owner, on the look test v1** (`video/deliverables/tests/poster_look_test_v1.mp4`): "So what bugs me about it is yes
good, but we spent the entire day tweaking the style that is frankly barely noticeable like we focus all our attention
on details and missed the forest for the trees / So take a look again at our references I sent earlier / Rethink this
first scene with Google in the style that with our references rethink how you would construct the scene from ground up
/ Still image no animation just the first scene image".

**The job:** ONE still, 1080 x 1920: v2's first scene ("She quit Google.": she walks out of Google's office on the canal
at King's Cross, London) rebuilt from a blank frame the way the city posters (`video/docs/look/poster_refs/`) are built,
not v2's scene retouched. Order: the construction (the viewpoint and the designed view, the big shapes and their
values, the hero against the sky, the dark framing near ground, the far pale stage), judged as value thumbnails beside
the posters at phone size, then colour, then detail, texture last (playbook: "The forest before the trees").
