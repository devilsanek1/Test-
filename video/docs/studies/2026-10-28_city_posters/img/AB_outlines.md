- **A = our line today, v2 exactly:** tier A gets one closed 3.0 px outline in its own deep shade: her window frame `#003128` round `#0B8577`, and the same on her plum silhouette, where it vanishes. Pip gets its 1.8 px same-hue line `#616389`. The walker, the neighbours, the houses, the water, the boat and the tree get no line. v2's light: TINT_CANAL `#8C5E6B` multiplied at form 0.20 / cast 0.28 / contact 0.48, a shade face 5-9 L* down.
- **B = the posters' way:** no outline on anything. The same shapes and tones, but every volume has a lit face and a shade face from the same tint family at a poster-like step: form 0.56, so brick -18 L* and stucco or stone -26. Casts are flat colour, faces turned up are 6 L* lighter, and greens stay green. The railings, the Juliet rail and the glazing bars stay the thin lines they are in A.
- **What differs:** only the edges and the light and shade. The geometry, palette, sun direction and tiers come from one code path. The inset "B without the shade faces" (no lines and no shade) shows why B needs its light: shapes of one colour run together, so the bay goes flat and Pip's head sinks into its body.
