# City posters: what we learned (one screen)

**Do this (the biggest lessons):**
1. Make the near look near: a dark, detailed frame along the bottom and sides (leaves, a rail, roof edges), not pale
   open ground.
2. Put something WAY out: one big, pale, simple shape on the horizon (a ridge, a far skyline) for scale.
3. Add real darks in many small pieces (windows, doorways, the shade under eaves and trees): about 6-10 % of the
   picture, where the Google video has 2 %.
4. Calm the light colours and keep the colour in the middle tones, so the few bright accents ring. Keep the Google
   video's colours.
5. Let the sky be the only calm place: fill the ground with facades, boats and everyday objects, drawn quietly.
6. The subject crisp and big; everything else the same style in simpler flat facets ("low poly"), never blurred.
7. Shape with light, not lines: every volume gets a clear lit side and shade side. The posters have no outlines.
8. Nature: true leaf shapes at the edges, simple facets inside, all in one gentle wind.
