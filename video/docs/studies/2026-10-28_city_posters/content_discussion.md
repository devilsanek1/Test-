## How an idea is judged here
- **The look** (the seven posters, `video/docs/look/poster_refs/`): one real place, one hero landmark, layered depth
  with something WAY far out for scale, subtle faceted nature, vibrant coherent colour, a designed view.
- **The place DOES the story** (`content_fit.md` §1): v1's city was the mechanism (every "so what" happened to a
  building: 82 % place-led frames, skip rate 32 %); v2's canal was a stage while text boards carried the argument (the
  owner found that story weak).
- **No postcards** (`content_market.md` §2, §5): place stories that travel have a real named place, a twist or a
  conflict, and a reason people care. Shares come from identity ("that's my city, my festival") and arousal (awe,
  anger, worry). A positive story needs the stronger frame-0 picture.
- **Why now:** an evergreen story (history, a landmark) still needs a peg (an anniversary, a season, a festival, news
  about the place) to stay "the fuss" the channel is named for.
- **The promise, for places** (a suggestion, not a rule): "You've heard about it. Now actually understand it." becomes
  "You've seen it. Now actually understand it.": everyone has seen the landmark; almost nobody knows its story.
- **The owner's criteria and order** (`video/docs/TOPIC_CRITERIA.md`: relevant with buzz and surprise 6, true 5, visual
  4, mood 3, fresh 2, shareable 1), with its tech/AI clause lifted for this brainstorm. Examples are not fact-locked:
  a picked story gets one fact lock before any script.
