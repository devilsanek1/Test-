# Topic B: widening the consequence ("this hits you too")

Researched 2026-10-25 for video 3, topic B ("AI is outbidding you for the memory in your laptop"). Goal: give a viewer
who isn't buying a laptop a reason to care.

**Status key.**
- **VERIFIED**: the primary statement (company, analyst firm or official page) is quoted the same way by 2+ independent,
  reputable outlets.
- **REPORTED**: one reputable outlet, or secondary write-ups only.
- **UNCERTAIN**: a forecast, leak, sources that conflict, or low-quality or commercial sources only.

**Method caveat.** WebFetch failed (DNS) and curl was blocked by the egress proxy (403) for every domain tried, including
bls.gov, raspberrypi.com and techspot.com. So every item was checked against search-index excerpts from several outlets.
No primary page was opened directly. Before anything goes on screen as a quote, open the primary page from a machine that
can reach it.
