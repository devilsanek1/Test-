# Video 3, G1 script: "Bidding against a building"

Fact status: adversarial check 2026-10-25 (FL + W + fresh searches). Every spoken line holds, so no spoken word changed and `radio_input.json` is unchanged. Fixed on screen only: the L3 tag says "computers & peripherals" (the BLS category), and the L7 chip says shipments. Fresh checks: the BLS-based annual averages fell every year 2006-2012 too, so "twenty years… give or take a pandemic" holds past FL 1.6's 15 years; ~5x still holds year on year into Q3; DRAM contracts are still forecast up 10-15% in 4Q26 and DDR5 spot is still edging up in Oct (L7). Core line: on frame 0 under all three hooks, voiced verbatim in H1, paid off by L9. Still open: L8 "a year or two" is REPORTED (aggregated forecasts; Micron and Samsung say tight through 2028, Bloomberg Intelligence says it may have peaked); "Mostly" (L4) is the analysts' reading, since BLS gives no cause; tick the BLS series before G8.

- **Core line (locked, G0.5):** "AI is making your next laptop pricier."
- **Frame-0 on-screen text (all three hooks):** AI IS MAKING YOUR NEXT LAPTOP PRICIER
- **Word count (spoken):** 110 with H1 (hook 7 + body 103). With H2 it's 111, and with H3 it's 114.
- **Estimated length at 165 wpm:** 40.0 s of speech. Adding the beats (about 3.5 s) and the 0.5 s lead gives about 44 s.
  At the cued paces (158-185 wpm) it comes to about 43 s with H1, 43.5 s with H2 and 44.5 s with H3.
- **First-second phone sound:** `phone_cart_add` (our own, 0.41 s) starts at **0.07 s (frame 2 at 30 fps)**, exactly as the
  viewer's thumb lands on "Add to cart" under the new laptop on their phone. The bright tick inside the sound peaks at
  about 0.13 s, and on it the laptop's paper price tag ticks up a notch (an up arrow and blurred digits, never an invented
  price). The sound has finished by about 0.48 s, so the voice comes in clean at **0.50 s**, with the music almost silent.
  - Why it fits: it's the happiest sound in online shopping, and it's the viewer's exact moment, adding the next laptop
    to the cart. In this story that tap is also their bid in an auction they don't know they're in. The good sound plus
    the tag going up gives the plain core line its jolt before a word is said. The image is the same under all three
    hooks, and the last shot drifts back to the same button, so the loop closes on the same pop.
- **Second positive sound:** `phone_notify_ding` (0.90 s), at about 30.35 s with H1 (about 30.8 s with H2, 31.7 s with
  H3). It plays 200 ms into the beat after "building", and a "price updated ↑" card pops in place on the phone. By now the
  ear knows that happy sounds here mean "pricier", so it works as the re-hook past the 30 s joke. Its tail is ducked
  about 6 dB under L7.
- **Radio cut** (`radio_input.json`):
  - `lead` is 0.5 instead of 0.08, so the stand-in voice comes in after the cart pop as scripted.
  - The ding isn't in it, because the builder's sfx times are shared by all three hooks. Add it at G2, on real timings.

## Plain voice-over

H1 + body, exactly as spoken (for TTS; captions use digits: 10%, 20, 5x):

AI is making your next laptop pricier. You're up against AI data centers for memory chips… and losing. In the US, computers are up almost ten percent in a year, after twenty years of getting cheaper… give or take a pandemic. Mostly because memory chips cost about five times last year's price. AI data centers need mountains of them… and they pay more. It's less of a shortage, more of an auction… and you're bidding against a building. The bids are still climbing, so the cheap laptops drop out first. Experts give it a year or two. That's how a data center ends up on your next laptop's price tag.

H2: Buying a laptop soon? AI's making it pricier.

H3: Be nice to your laptop. AI's making the next one pricier.

## Why this version

1. **Base: the retention draft**, which had the top combined critic score (73.5, vs personal 72.5 and auction 64.5). From it I kept the frame-0 nook with the data center at the window, the scale pull-back, the polite-giant auction hall, the floor-a-shot easter egg, the hovering-thumb ending and the second "good news" ding as a re-hook past 30 s.
2. **The 2nd line (the jolt)**: retention's "You're up against AI data centers… and losing", with "memory chips" named (personal, via both critics). I applied the image fix so you still get your chip, it just costs more, and cut "inside it" so "it" can't be misheard.
3. **From auction**: "In the US, computers are up almost ten percent in a year", the SHORTAGE shelf that swings open into the auction, the cart tap as your bid, and H3 "Be nice to your laptop", improved to "the next one" (both critics' top hook).
4. **From personal**: `phone_cart_add` as the first-second sound (clean before the voice, and the loop lands on the same pop), "last year's price", and the callback with NEXT. H2 is the auction/personal question without "this Christmas".
5. **Critic fixes**:
   - "Less of a shortage, more of an auction" is the spoken version and keeps "shortage".
   - "The bids are still climbing… drop out first" keeps the auction alive after the joke.
   - "Experts give it a year or two" is shorter and still hedged.
   - Frame 0 is cut down to three readable things, with an AI DATA CENTER plate.
   - The scale shot is lighter (the dock/van gag is gone), and the chips are shorter.
   - "First dibs" was cut to stay at 115 words or under with every hook.
