## The story in 3 sentences

You're about to buy a laptop and still expect the old deal, where the better one shows up for the same money, but this
past year computers in the US went up almost 10% instead. That's mostly the memory chips: your laptop runs on them, AI
data centers run on mountains of them and pay more, so they get first dibs, which means what's left costs about five
times last year's price, and laptop makers pass that on. For now, the old move of waiting for the better one doesn't
save you, because the chip makers say next year's even tighter, and that's how an AI data center ends up on your next
laptop's price tag.

- **Throughline question:** "Laptops always got better for the money, so why is my next one getting pricier, and what
  do AI data centers have to do with it?" Lines 1-2 open it, lines 3-4 sharpen it, lines 5-7 answer it, line 8 shows why
  waiting doesn't help, and line 9 closes it.
- **The turn (line 5):** your laptop and an AI data center run on the same thing, memory chips. "What does AI have to do
  with my laptop?" answers itself.
- **The one joke (line 3):** buyer's remorse. The better one shows up right after you buy yours. Line 8 calls it back.
