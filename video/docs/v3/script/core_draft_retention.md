# Video 3 script: "Up against a building" (core line B, retention cut)

Draft, 2026-10-25. Not committed. Written by the retention editor around the locked core line (`../CORE_LINE.md`, pick B):
**"AI is making your next laptop pricier."** Test viewers found that line flat on its own, so this cut is about the jolt:
a wrong-way price alert on frame 0, a 2nd line that makes it a contest the viewer is losing, a re-hook every 3-5 s, three
open loops, and a callback that cuts straight back to frame 0. Grafted from `draft_retention.md` (the wrong-way alert),
`draft_personal.md` ("bidding against a building", the nook) and `draft_auction.md` ("give or take a pandemic").
Facts come only from `../research/factlock.md` (FL) and `../research/widen.md` (W). The voice is American English.

## At a glance

- **Frame-0 text (all 3 hooks):** **AI IS MAKING YOUR NEXT LAPTOP PRICIER**
- **First-second sound:** `phone_notify_ding` at 0.10 s, as a price alert pops up with its arrow pointing UP.
- **The jolt (line 2):** "You're up against AI data centers for the memory inside it… and losing." It names AI data
  centers by about 3.9 s, rules out the "AI features in my laptop" reading, and turns the headline into a contest the
  viewer is losing. The auction twist closes that loop at about 31 s.
- **Length:** 112 spoken words with H1, 113 with H2, 111 with H3. That's about 43 s at the cue paces (about 42 s if the take
  runs at 175 wpm).
- **Proof order:** (1) it's real (L3), (2) it's mostly the memory chips (L4), (3) data centers need mountains and pay
  more, so it's an auction (L5-L6), (4) why "next" (L7-L8), then the callback (L9).

## Frame 0 and the first second

- **Frame-0 image (same for all 3 hooks, mid-action, cozy enclosure):**
  - The desk nook at night: a bookshelf on the left, a tall frosty window on the right, a warm lamp pool, and string
    lights along the window frame.
  - Lower third, in the foreground: the viewer's hand (cream sweater cuff) holds the phone. On it is a shop page with a
    shiny new laptop and a paper price tag on a string.
  - The old laptop sits open on the desk behind it, with tape on the hinge and a small "since 2017" sticker.
  - Through the window, so close it fills the glass, an AI data center looms like a lit tower block. Its giant scoop arm
    is already mid-swing, lifting a glowing heap of memory chips off a conveyor, while a thin trickle carries on toward
    a small chute.
  - The text sits in bold ink in the upper-middle third, well clear of the top edge (no paper edge at the top).
- **Sound: `phone_notify_ding` (our own, 0.90 s) at 0.10 s (frame 3 at 30 fps).**
  - What happens on it: a price-alert card pops in place on the phone (scale 0.9 to 1.0, no slide-in from the top). It
    shows an up arrow and no number, and the paper tag ticks up a notch.
  - The voice comes in at 0.45 s, over the ding's tail (duck the tail about 6 dB under the voice). The music is almost
    silent.
  - **Why this one:**
    - A notification ding is the one sound that makes people look at their phone by reflex, so it buys the first second.
    - A price alert on something you're watching is usually good news (a price drop). This one points up, so the
      good-news sound brings the core line's bad news before a word is spoken. That mismatch is the first jolt.
    - It's "news about you", which is how H1 is read, and it fits all three hooks: H2's shopper, and H3's laptop in the alert.
    - The same ding comes back at about 32 s. By then the ear knows it means "pricier", so the second one is a quiet joke
      and a re-hook.

## Rules check

- **Frame 0:** it shows the core line as text, mid-action (the scoop is mid-swing and the alert is popping), with the
  data center next to the laptop. No greeting, no intro, no "I started reading".
- **Hooks:** H1 is the core line verbatim. H2 (a question) and H3 (a personal moment) are different openings, and each
  lands "AI's making it pricier" from about 1.3-1.7 s, under the same frame-0 text.
- **Sound:** a positive phone sound (`phone_notify_ding`) lands at 0.10 s. Three more positive sounds come later
  (sent swoosh, the 2nd ding, the like pop), each on a visual hit.
- **Structure:**
  - Hook (0-3 s), then stakes ("up against… and losing", 3-7.5 s).
  - Explanation in the locked proof order (7.5-26 s).
  - The twist ("less a shortage than an auction", 27-31 s).
  - The payoff for the viewer ("cheap laptops go first", "a year or two", 32-38 s).
  - The callback (39-43 s). It ends on the callback, with no "follow for more".
- **AI-tell pass:**
  - There's one comparison line ("less a shortage than an auction") and no "it's not X, it's Y".
  - No "here's the thing", "turns out", "delve", triplets, colon reveals, self-answered questions or em-dashes in the voiceover.
  - The connectives are because, so and and.
- **Facts:**
  - Every line maps to a VERIFIED FL/W row, or to REPORTED with soft wording (L8).
  - The only US figure (+9.7%) says "in the US" out loud, and every US chip says "US".
  - The 5x stays on the chip.
  - The voiceover never says "only", "all because of AI", "every", "double", "record", "ever", "AI is raising prices on
    everything" or "than your last one".
- **No villain:** the scoop is polite, the data center is a calm giant in a cardigan, and "they pay more" is a shrug.
