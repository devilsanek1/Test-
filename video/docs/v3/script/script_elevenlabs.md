# Video 3 script, ready for ElevenLabs (core line locked: "AI is making your next laptop pricier")

Each line has its delivery cue in brackets, then the spoken text. CAPS = lean on it, … = a short beat. About 110 words,
about 43 s. Hook H1 is the default. H2 and H3 at the bottom are alternate first lines, if you want to record them for a test.
Pictures, sounds and sources for each line: `script_g1.md`.

[plain, a touch amazed, like passing on news about THEIR stuff, lean on "NEXT"]
AI is making your NEXT laptop pricier.

[leaning in, dry, like "wait till you hear this", "AI data centers" crisp and clear, "and losing" flat with a tiny wince]
You're up against AI data centers for memory chips… and losing.

[straight and level, a flicker of "can you believe it", the number slow and clear, "give or take a pandemic" tossed off with a tiny smile]
In the US, computers are up almost TEN percent in a year… after twenty years of getting CHEAPER, give or take a pandemic.

[reset, plain, like explaining to a friend who doesn't do tech, the number clean, no gasp]
Mostly because memory chips cost about FIVE TIMES last year's price.

[quieter and a notch slower, a little awed, like looking up at something huge, lean on "MOUNTAINS", then a small shrug]
AI data centers need MOUNTAINS of them… and they pay more.

[a touch brighter, like it just clicked, lean on "AUCTION", then THE one joke, deadpan, half-smile, don't sell it]
It's less of a shortage, more of an AUCTION… and you're bidding against a building.

[warm and honest, a little closer, like talking to one person, lean on "STILL" and "CHEAP", no doom]
The bids are STILL climbing, so the CHEAP laptops drop out first.

[level, a plain shrug, easy, like passing on the best guess, not a warning]
Experts give it a year or two.

[relaxed and warm, wrapping up, light lean on "NEXT", let "price tag" fall softly, don't land it like a title card]
That's how a data center ends up on your NEXT laptop's price tag.
