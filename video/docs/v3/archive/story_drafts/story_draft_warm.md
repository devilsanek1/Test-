# Video 3, G1 script from scratch: "The laptop deal that flipped" (warm draft)

Draft 2026-10-26. Not committed. This replaces the rejected script, which was built as a list of facts to prove.

- **Core line (locked):** AI is making your next laptop pricier.
- **Frame-0 text:** AI IS MAKING YOUR NEXT LAPTOP PRICIER
- **Spoken words:** 113 (110 written; the two years read as two words each). About 42-44 s with the beats.
  If the take runs past 45 s, cut "just" (line 8) first, then the "So" that opens line 9.
- **Spoken numbers:** almost 10% (said as US), about 5 times, 2027 or 2028. Everything else is on screen.

## The story in one breath

**Throughline (one question):** laptops always got better for the money, so how did AI data centers flip that?

**Situation:** you're about to replace your laptop, and you're still counting on the deal you grew up with: wait a bit
and you get more laptop for the same money.

**Chain:** AI data centers are making laptops pricier, **but** that's backwards, because next year's laptop was usually
the better deal. **But** this year US computers went up almost 10%, **because** your laptop runs on memory chips and AI
data centers buy mountains of them. They pay more, **so** they get first dibs, **which means** memory chips cost about
five times last year's price. **So** normally you'd wait for next year's, **but** experts don't expect relief before
2027-28, **so** that's how an AI data center ends up on your next laptop's price tag.

**Turn (line 5):** your laptop and an AI data center run on the same kind of chips, and the data center buys them by
the mountain. That's the moment the question from the hook answers itself.

**Payoff (line 9):** a single closing line, with no stacked second ending. Line 8 has already called back the line-3
deal ("wait for next year's"), so line 9 only has to land the core line: AI data center, NEXT, pricier.

## Plain voice-over (captions and timing)

Buying a laptop soon? AI's making it pricier. Well… AI data centers are. Which is backwards, because the annoying thing
was, next year's laptop was usually better for the same money. But this year, computers in the US went up almost 10%.
That's because your laptop runs on memory chips… and AI data centers buy mountains of them. They pay more, so they get
first dibs. Which means memory chips cost about 5 times last year's price. Normally you'd just wait for next year's… but
experts don't expect it to ease before 2027 or 2028. So that's how an AI data center ends up on your next laptop's price tag.

## Why it now reads as one story

- **One picture carries it:** a single price line on the window falls (line 3), hooks up (4), gets its cause (5-7) and
  runs on, dotted (8). Each number is a point on that line, not an item on a list.
- **Every pair joins with a real link** (but, but, because, so, which means, so/but, so). No line could be moved
  without breaking the one after it.
- **Plant and payoff:** line 3 plants the old deal (next year's is better). Line 8 turns it into the viewer's own hope
  (just wait). Line 9 lands the core line.
- **The viewer stays in the scene** (the desk, the window, the price tag), so NEXT at the end isn't cold.

## Taken from the judges

  joke, "first dibs", and a word budget of about 110.
- Fixes:
  - One question and one closing line.
  - No "new customer showed up" (the data centers are already outside the window on frame 0, just a lot bigger now).
  - "Same few companies" moves on screen. It is never "the same three", and never "the exact same chip".
  - The 2021 bump is kept on the chart, and the chart is labelled US.
