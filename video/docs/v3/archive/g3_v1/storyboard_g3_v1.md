**Concept.** We watch everything from one lamp-lit desk nook at night, and its single frosty window is our screen onto the
world: first the data center far off, then fogged glass where a finger draws the price line, which hooks up and wipes the
fog right in front of that data center. At the turn, a memory chip held up to the glass looks as small as the far data
center, so we push through the window for the one scale moment, follow the chips to the factory dock and the laptop maker's
van, and come home to the same paper price tag, which turns over to show a data center on its back.

Cozy (the nook) runs about 33 s of 43.5 s; scale (the push-through and S9) about 4.7 s, once, at the turn.
