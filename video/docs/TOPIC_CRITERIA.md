# Topic criteria (G0): what a story must have to be picked

The one list the G0 editor scores every candidate on (1-10 each) and the owner sees with the shortlist. Sources: the
master brief's five tests (`CREATIVE_BRIEF.md` §4-8), the playbook's "Taste signals", and the owner's condition of
2026-10-28: "we should find a story that is going to be possible to make visually appealing". **A story that fails
criterion 1 or 2 is out, however strong the rest.**

1. **Visually appealing in our look (a must; owner, 2026-10-28)**, judged against `docs/look/DIRECTION.md`:
   a. a literal subject you can draw and read with the sound off (recognisable things, never a metaphor the facts
      don't support or a mechanism that needs diagrams and labels);
   b. one thread object that can be on screen through the whole story (CRAFT §a);
   c. one cozy home place (a room, a street, a shop: full, alive, enclosed) and room for one scale moment;
   d. a physical hook for frame 0: the expected thing, then reality overtaking it, drawn live in the clean pen;
   e. small simple people who act it with their pose (no faces), and Pip has a place in it;
   f. few words in the picture (numbers shown as things: 100 small people, a full shelf), gentle light that fits the
      mood, a warm palette;
   g. nothing we can't draw: no real logos or product screens, no likeness of a real person, nothing gory.
2. **True and solid**: the core fact checked against a primary source or 2+ reputable outlets; what it does and
   doesn't mean stated exactly (stage, peer review, how many people, when).
3. **Relevant**: a big question millions care about; tech, AI or AI-adjacent (science, health tech, space, robots,
   computers, energy); not a past video's topic; Anthropic not a key entity.
4. **Fresh**: genuinely new, ideally the last 72 h, up to ~2 weeks.
5. **The mood the slate needs**: a positive story after a negative one (hopeful, never hype), and vice versa.
6. **Surprise**: a "wait, seriously?" fact or a twist that changes the understanding.
7. **Consequence**: a concrete "so what" for an ordinary person, and when it reaches them.
8. **Buzz**: people are already talking about it (the "fuss").
9. **A real person and a plain core line**: one real person in their own words (or a direct question to the viewer);
   a line the video can pay off without walking it back.
10. **Shareable**: a reason to send it to someone.
