House palette family: same style, each one its own

One family, many tools. Every palette keeps our v1 DNA: a soft, light background (never saturated or dark) filling 50-80 % of the frame, soft mids from our clay family (cream, peach, sand, tan, sage), a dark warm ink for lines and captions (at least 7:1 on the background), and from the owner's references one saturated HERO on the subject (5-15 %), a deep tinted ANCHOR instead of black (~5 %) and at most one tiny hot POP for 'look here' (1-3 %). #1 is v1 as shipped, #2 the incremental polish, #3-8 sister palettes for different stories. Pick one per video (a sister palette may carry a deliberate shift, e.g. a cozy interior vs a dusk exterior); extend or add palettes when a topic needs it.
