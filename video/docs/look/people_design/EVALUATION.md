# Why the reference reads modern, natural and appealing, and ours does not (2026-10-30)

**The owner:** "Like this is one example just compare it overall and evaluate what makes the left to be modern and
natural and appealing and right us absolute garbage / I think we do need to combine it with the faces research". The

## The root cause
The reference is today's stylised 2D animation (feature-film appeal: a character with a face, built in 3D, lit). Ours is
the faceless flat "corporate" figure of ~2017-2020 illustration: cut-out shapes, no face, no light. That family now reads
cheap and dated, so no haircut inside it can look premium. Fixing the hair alone cannot close the gap; the head, the
face, the light and the values have to move with it.

## What we change (the brief for the people overhaul)
1. **Faces with features**, designed with appeal (the face research: her cute, him manly): eyes that look AWAY (never at
   the camera), brows, a small nose, a mouth as a tone, the ear in place; the closed-eye LASH stays for downcast,
   small and far figures. Shown both ways for the owner to choose.
2. **3D-aware heads and hair**: the head turned in space (3/4 built from one model, never a profile pasted on a front),
   the hair with thickness all round the skull.
3. **Light on people**: one key with soft gradients, a RIM light on the hair and shoulders separating the figure from the
   ground, a soft shadow under the jaw and the hair.
4. **A value plan per figure**: the darkest value (usually the hair) frames the face; the face a clear step from the
   ground (>= 25 L\*); no pale-on-pale.
5. **Hair as a real, living cut**: volume, an asymmetric parting, a broad S, ends of different sizes, 1-3 thin strands,
   a few flow lines inside, gloss along the flow.
6. **Edges**: thin dark accents only where forms overlap and inside the hair; light rims at the silhouette; never a
   uniform outline round everything.
7. **Proportion and gesture**: a slender tapered neck, sloping shoulders for women, a head that turns and tilts.
8. **Colour**: each figure's palette harmonious with the scene's light, with a real value range.

This is a step away from direction C's faceless flat figure; the overhaul builds it, and the owner sees it beside the
references (and the LASH version) before anything is decided.
