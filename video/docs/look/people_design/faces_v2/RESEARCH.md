# Faces and bodies with appeal: the research and the measurements (faces_v2, 2026-10-30)

**Status: an ITERATION (owner's verdict is the review; no critic, no panel).** Brief: `BRIEF.md`. The recipe these
findings feed: `RECIPE.md`. The sheets: `SHEETS.md`. It builds on `../design_research.md` §3 (proportions) and §11
(generic faces) and does not repeat them; where a number here differs, this file is newer and the reason is given.

Strength marks: **[S]** a peer-reviewed study; **[T]** a textbook, a surgeon's or a studio's own account;
**[P]** a practitioner's advice. Units: **h** = the skull, crown to chin (1.0); **H** = the figure, crown to sole
(1000 in the engines). A profile faces screen-left; "behind" = toward the back of the head.

## Part 1. Fourteen findings (only what decides the design)

**F1. Average and smooth is attractive; a pushed feature is not.** Faces averaged from many photos were rated more
attractive than nearly all their sources, more so the more faces went in; caricatured faces were rated less
attractive. *So:* every option is an average face with a designed dose; no feature is pushed to an extreme, and the
options differ by dose, never by a quirk.
[S] Langlois & Roggman 1990, https://digitalcommons.usu.edu/fchd_facpub/237 ; [S] Rhodes & Tremewan 1996,
Psychological Science 7:105 (averageness up, caricature down), https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/j.1467-9280.1996.tb00338.x/

**F2. Feminine reads beautiful in women; in men a moderate dose of masculinity wins.** Feminised female faces were
preferred in the UK and Japan; even male faces were preferred slightly feminised, and masculinising raised perceived
dominance, coldness and dishonesty (women vary a lot on men). *So:* the woman goes clearly feminine (small nose and
chin, fuller lips, a soft brow); the man gets clear masculine CUES (the jaw's corner, the chin, the brow, the neck) on
a clean, friendly face, never a brute.
[S] Perrett et al. 1998, Nature 394:884, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9732869/ ; [S] the variation:
https://facelab.humboldt.edu/students/masculinity

**F3. A beautiful woman's face pairs young features with mature ones.** Attractiveness rose with large eyes, a small
nose and a small chin (neonate), with prominent cheekbones and narrow cheeks (mature), and with high brows; forehead
height and the nose's length alone did not predict it. *So:* cute is not a baby face: a small nose and chin, AND a
defined cheekbone over a narrow, tapering lower face; with closed eyes, the lash's length and a low, wide-set eye
stand in for "large eyes".
[S] Cunningham 1986, J Pers Soc Psychol 50:925, https://genepi.qimr.edu.au/contents/p/staff/1986_Cunningham_FacialBeauty_Pers&SocialPsych925-935.pdf

**F4. "Cute" is the baby schema, used at a light dose.** Infant faces morphed to a round face, a high forehead, large
eyes and a small nose and mouth were rated cuter and moved adults to care. Illustrators get it by putting the features
LOW on the head (a big forehead), keeping the nose small ("a larger nose makes the face look more mature"), the cheeks
full, the chin round, and in profile letting the forehead bulge a little. *So:* our adults take the schema's direction
at a small dose: the features 0.03-0.05 h lower than real, a rounder forehead, a shorter nose; never chibi (the owner
rejected the 4.9-head her).
[S] Glocker et al. 2009, Ethology 115:257, https://repository.upenn.edu/cog_neuro_pubs/11 ;
[P] https://tips.clip-studio.com/zh-tw/articles/7135 ; [P] https://medibangpaint.com/en/use/2021/08/how-to-draw-a-girls-face-cutely

**F5. The nose in numbers.** The nasolabial angle (columella to upper lip) is 90-95 degrees in men and 95-110 in
women; the nasofrontal angle (forehead to dorsum at the bridge) 115-130 (wider, i.e. a shallower bridge, in women).
Raters asked for their ideal chose ~96 (men) and ~98-105 (women), and preference studies keep finding a lifted tip
pleasing in women. *So:* the woman's tip lifted to 105-112 (the cute end; 05 is ~120, a cartoon dose), a shallow
bridge 135-140 with a soft scoop; the man's 93-97, a straight dorsum, a deeper bridge 125-130.
[T] Pasinato et al. 2008 (citing the surgical norms), https://www.arquivosdeorl.org.br/conteudo/pdfForl/546_eng.pdf ;
[S] Armijo, Brown & Guyuron (ASAPS 2011 / PRS 2012), https://asaps.confex.com/asaps/2011/webprogram/Paper4804.html ;
[S] a general-population preference study, 98 raters (female 104.9 +- 4.0, male 97.0 +- 6.3; "The ideal nasolabial
angle in rhinoplasty", PRS 2014, record: https://sindex.sdl.edu.sa/esploro/outputs/journalArticle/The-ideal-nasolabial-angle-in-rhinoplasty/9924561808331 );
[S] Japanese raters, women 105 / men 95 (nasofacial 30 / 33), https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12268326/

**F6. The lips sit near the nose-to-chin line, and the taste has moved fuller.** The orthodontic norm puts the upper
lip ~4 mm and the lower ~2 mm behind the line from the nose's tip to the chin (~0.018 h and ~0.009 h); fashion models
have fuller lips than controls, and fashion-magazine profiles moved toward fuller, more forward lips through the
twentieth century. *So:* the lips are ONE soft bump in a tone (never a line), 0.005-0.012 h behind that line for her,
~0.015 h for him; ours sit 0.029 / 0.042 h behind (thin, sunken, older).
[T] https://speareducation.com/2013/08/evaluating-facial-esthetics-the-esthetic-plane ; [S] Auger & Turley 1999,
Int J Adult Orthod Orthognath Surg 14:7 (citation only; abstract not verified),
https://www.thieme-connect.de/products/ejournals/pdf/10.1055/s-2004-822956.pdf

**F7. The chin and the jaw.** Profile attractiveness falls as the chin moves away from the line under the lower lip in
EITHER direction; the best-rated profiles had the soft chin on the true vertical. A gonial angle near 125 degrees was
rated attractive in women (129 not, small sample), and younger-looking profiles have sharper jaw angles. *So:* the
chin never sits more than ~0.01 h behind the face front (ours 0.022 h reads weak); the jaw has a clear angle: hers a
smooth V that rises 24-30 degrees to an angle under the ear, his a lower, squarer jaw (12-16 degrees) with a corner.
[S] Naini, Garagiola & Wertheim 2019, https://eprints.kingston.ac.uk/43529/1/Wertheim-D-43529-AAM.pdf ;
[S] Mommaerts et al. (summary), https://www.drbicuspid.com/dental-specialties/oral-maxillofacial-surgery/article/15544729/survey-finds-which-angles-make-the-perfect-jaw ;
[S] https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9666021/

**F8. The forehead and brow carry sex at a glance.** Female foreheads are rounder and more upright and flow into the
nose without a break; the male forehead is two planes: a brow ridge that projects, then a frontal bone that slopes
back, over a deeper bridge. *So:* her forehead one round curve into the nose; his a brow shelf 0.015-0.02 h proud,
the forehead above it sloping back ~10 degrees, the bridge 0.03 h behind the brow.
[S] a forehead-feminisation review, https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/s00266-024-04143-6 ; [T] a surgeon's
account, https://deschamps-braly.com/?p=819 ; [T] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facial_feminization_surgery

**F9. Manly in a simple face = the jaw's angle, the chin's shape and width, the brow; shape language does the rest.**
Artists' teaching: the female head is the male's with the angular, broad features softened; the main differences are
the jaw's angles, the chin's shape and width, and the ears. Squares read stable and strong (Up's Carl was designed as
a cube "very stable"), circles friendly. *So:* the man is built of straights and corners (a square chin front, the
jaw's corner, the brow's shelf, a thick straight neck with an Adam's apple); the woman of curves (a round forehead, a
rounded tip, a round chin) with ONE sharp accent (the nose's point or the jaw's V).
[P/T] Proko on Loomis, https://proko.com/course-lesson/how-to-draw-the-head-front-view ; [T, secondary] Up's
co-director Bob Peterson, quoted in https://www.shmoop.com/study-guides/up/shapes-symbol.html

**F10. Appeal is for everyone, and twins kill it.** Disney's appeal principle: every character needs appeal (heroic,
comic or cute), meaning a pleasing, readable design; mirror-image "twins" look lifeless. Closed eyes are drawn as
ONE curve with a hint of lashes, the lid curving the opposite way to an open eye, and read relaxed, asleep or
absorbed. *So:* the man is designed for appeal as carefully as the woman; in 3/4 the two lashes differ (the near one
longer, the far one shorter and tucked toward the contour); the lash stays one tapered curve, bowed down, one flick.
[T] Thomas & Johnston, The Illusion of Life (1981), via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_basic_principles_of_animation ;
[P] https://www.animeoutline.com/how-to-draw-closed-squinted-anime-eyes/

**F11. The body's sex cue is the ratio, not the size.** Men preferred female figures with a waist-to-hip ratio ~0.7
(culture moves it: the Matsigenka preferred ~0.9); women rate men mostly by the waist-to-chest ratio (the V, the
inverted triangle), BMI second. *So:* her waist ~0.70 of her hips with the shoulders no wider than the hips + 0.015 H
(an hourglass read through clothes, never a bust or hip exaggeration); his shoulders ~1.6x his waist, the hips barely
wider than the waist.
[S] Singh 1993, J Pers Soc Psychol 65:293, https://labs.psych.ucsb.edu/roney/james/other%20pdf%20readings/reserve%20readings/singhwhr.pdf ;
[S] Maisey et al. 1999, Lancet 353:1500, and its replication, https://researchportal.northumbria.ac.uk/en/publications/male-physical-attractiveness-in-britain-and-greece-a-cross-cultur/

**F12. Legs ~5 % longer than average are the most attractive; much longer is not. Necks carry elegance.** 3,103
raters in 27 nations rated very short and very long legs less attractive; fashion illustration lengthens the legs
and the neck (9 heads). *So:* our legs stay where `../design_research.md` §3 put them (the crotch 0.485-0.49 H); the
woman's neck long and slim, the man's shorter and thick, flaring into the shoulders (the trapezius).
[S] Sorokowski et al. 2011, J Cross-Cult Psychol 42:131, https://gcris.ieu.edu.tr/entities/publication/806b5da6-c062-4787-bde7-c7cbd4c635cb ;
[T] Riegelman's 9 Heads, via `../design_research.md` §3 source [9]

**F13. What reads dated now: corporate Memphis (2017-2022) and its kin; what reads modern: designed features with
restraint.** The Alegria style (BUCK for Facebook, 2017) and its copies (tiny heads, rubbery oversized limbs, flat odd
skins, blank faces) are now mocked as generic. The current feature-animation look (KPop Demon Hunters, 2025, drew its
leads from real K-pop stars and actors) gives adults small, lifted noses, defined jaws, long necks and soft light.
*So:* no noodle limbs, no blank or dot faces, no big-eyed doll; our modern = a small lifted nose, full lips as a
tone, a clean jaw, a long neck, closed lashes, two flat tones.
[P] https://eyeondesign.aiga.org/the-internet-is-turning-on-big-techs-colorful-corporate-mascots/ ;
[P] https://creativebloq.com/news/corporate-memphis-style-is-dead ; [P] https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/1137949

**F14. Mixed realism is uncanny.** Faces whose parts sit at different levels of realism, or with one abnormal
feature, read eerie. *So:* every part at one level of simplification: the nose one shape and one shade, the lips a
tone, the ear one shape with one inner notch, no nostrils, no lip line; and no single feature changes alone.
[S] Seyama & Nagayama 2007, Presence 16:337, https://www.scinapse.io/papers/2101870300 ; see also
`../design_research.md` §11.1

## Part 2. Measured: the blue-cliff woman (05), the other references, and ours

**Method.** Ref 05 is 509 x 564 px; her skull is ~66 px (crown to chin), the face under the fringe ~32 px tall, so
every number is +-1 px (+-0.015 h; angles +-8 degrees). Her head is bowed ~30 degrees (the ear's lobe sits 13 px above
the nose's base, which a level head keeps level); the angles below are levelled by rotating the face up 30 degrees.
Ours: LASH's own points (`src/look/people_dirs/graphic_faces/head.ts`, `headProfile`, women `{jaw 0, nose 0, chin
0}`, men `{1, 1, 1}`), and the FcTest render (her and the man, profile and 3/4, magnified 3x).

**Why 05 reads cute and appealing (plainly):**
1. **A small face low on a big head.** The fringe covers the forehead, so the face is only the bottom ~40 % of the head
   with its hair; the features cluster low (the baby schema, F4) under a lot of hair.
2. **A tiny, lifted nose with a sharp point:** one clean triangle, the tip turned up hard (~120). The point is the one
   sharp accent in a round face (F9).
3. **A short lower face:** nose base to chin ~0.22 h; the mouth is almost nothing; the chin small and rounded but ON
   the face line, never receding (F3, F7).
4. **A short jaw that turns up under the ear:** the cheek-to-chin line is a small V, not a long shelf.
5. **The ear shows** where the hair is tucked behind it: a soft, round, young shape that also places the jaw.
6. **Body:** narrow, sloping shoulders; a long slim torso; long slim legs that taper hard (thigh to ankle ~4 : 1,
   ours 3.4 : 1) to thin ankles and small pointed feet; thin wrists; a curved, relaxed back.

**Where ours falls short (plainly):**
1. **The face is a realistic adult's, so it reads older and plain:** a tall forehead in view, the eye at 0.50 h, a
   long straight nose (0.22 h) projecting 0.08 h like a beak, a 0.26 h lower face.
2. **The mouth is sunken:** the lips sit 0.029 / 0.018 h behind the tip-to-chin line (1.6-2x the norm, F6), so the
   nose and the chin stick out past a thin mouth (an older, sour profile).
3. **The chin recedes and the jaw is a long low shelf** (0.56 h at 17 degrees): the heaviest part of the face is the
   lower half, the opposite of the V that reads young and feminine (F3, F7).
4. **The neck is wide and leans** (0.36 h; the hair round is already fixing the lean, `../hair_v2/BRIEF_v3.md`).
5. **The man has no sex cues:** no brow ridge (the forehead runs straight into the nose), a weak chin, a jaw with no
   corner; he reads as her with a longer nose (F8, F9).
6. **Bodies (S5, `../hair_v2/stills/05_heights_and_builds.png`):** boxy rectangular torsos; her shoulders (0.238-0.240
   H) wider than her hips (0.210 H), so through a coat she reads as a straight box (shoulder : hip 1.14); his V is
   mild (shoulder : waist 1.48) and his neck meets his shoulders with no trapezius slope; arms hang as even tubes;
   ankles and wrists thick for the style.
