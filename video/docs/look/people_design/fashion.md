# Fashion: what cool 20-30s in New York and Los Angeles wear, made drawable

**Status: a research proposal (2026-10-30), not yet tested in a film.** It is a look doc: it ranks below the
playbook, `DIRECTION.md` and `CRAFT.md` and never overrides them. It is the WARDROBE part of the people design
research: `design_research.md` (next to this file) is the craft (proportions, shape language, hair masses, the
clothing rules C1-C10, colour V1-V7, faces), `diagnose.md` is why our people read old. This file adds what young
people in the two cities actually wear and how they wear their hair in autumn 2026, and turns each look into a few
shapes at our sizes. The board `fashion_board.png` (next to this file; its generator `fashion_board.py`) shows every
look and hairstyle as shape thumbnails we drew ourselves: no photo was traced or copied.

**Units** (as in `design_research.md`): H = the figure's height, crown to sole; h = the skull's height, crown to
chin (0.128 H on our adult figure, so 7.8 heads). px = pixels in our 1080 x 1920 frame. Our people are mostly
130-220 px tall (the "you"), smaller in the mid and far ground, and seen from behind, in profile or in 3/4.

**How the sources were read.** Page fetches were blocked in this session (whowhatwear.com, fashionista.com and
marieclaire.com did not resolve), so the articles were read through search-engine summaries of them. A claim marked
[n] comes from source n (§10). Trend writing is opinion: a look enters the core (§2.8) only when two or more
outlets agree; a single-source item is at most an accent. Brands and garment types are named; no person is named as a
style target.

## 1. The answer on one page

1. **Draw the durable core, not the micro-trend.** A film lives on the feed for months, and at 130-220 px only
   silhouettes, lengths, values and one detail read. So we draw the shapes that have run for two or more years and
   still run in autumn 2026: the long open coat, the trench, the leather jacket, straight or wide full-length
   trousers, the midi skirt, slim low shoes, the slouchy shoulder bag, oversized sunglasses. The season's novelties
   (suede, fringe, butter yellow, burgundy, red accents, wired headphones) are at most ONE accent per figure. Never
   the one-season items (fawn print, pillbox hats, fur coats, cummerbund belts, bag charms). (§2.8)
2. **The 2026 line is long, clean and balanced.** After five years of oversized everything, autumn 2026 swings back:
   slim-straight jeans are "quietly taking the place of baggy jeans" [1]; fitted and cropped jackets, polished knits
   and streamlined denim replace the oversized blazer over baggy jeans [4]. Drawn: ONE roomy piece per figure (a long
   coat, wide trousers, a full skirt, a big shirt) against compact pieces, and an unbroken leg line down to a slim,
   low shoe. That is also what reads elegant (`design_research.md` §3).
3. **The feet are the tell.** Chunky sneakers and platforms read 2018-2022 [4]; 2026 feet are slim and low: ballet
   flats (in colour, in mesh), slim retro sneakers with a contrasting stripe, loafers (men with white socks), kitten
   heels [11]. Drawn: a long, low wedge, 0.13-0.15 H long and 0.025-0.035 H tall. Our people's round blob feet are
   one of their oldest tells.
4. **New York = structure and darks; Los Angeles = ease and light** [5][6]. New York: tailored, dark neutrals (black,
   navy, chocolate, burgundy), layered outerwear (the long coat, the trench, leather; a puffer in deep winter),
   polished low shoes, a tote or a shoulder bag, headphones. Los Angeles: relaxed and loose, light and varied colours
   (cloud white, butter, washed blue, baby pink), light layers (an open oversized shirt, a light blazer over a tank),
   flats, sneakers and sandals, a cap and big sunglasses, the athleisure set. In our films the two are grammars, not
   cities: **the autumn-city grammar** dresses any cool city (London, Paris, Tokyo in October), **the warm-coast
   grammar** any warm place (a beach, Lisbon, summer).
5. **The palette of the moment fits our house.** Cloud Dancer (a warm white, Pantone's 2026 colour) as the base,
   chocolate and cocoa, burgundy, butter yellow, dark indigo denim, navy as the neutral beside black, red accents
   [8]; and teal: WGSN and Coloro's colour of the year 2026 is "Transformative Teal" [7], so our "you"'s hero teal is
   the year's own colour. Fashion's olive, "sad beige" and grey are out of our house (`PALETTE.md`; "sad beige" is
   also dated [4]): sage, caramel, cream and cocoa stand in. (§2.5)
6. **The hair is the face.** With faces generic, hair carries identity and "now" [9][10]: the textured ("ripped")
   bob, long face-framing layers with curtain or "bombshell" bangs and bouncy ends, the claw-clip twist, the soft low
   bun with loose tendrils (not the severe slick bun), the sleek low ponytail, long boho knotless braids with curly
   ends, natural curls; for men the textured or French crop over a low taper, curls or twists on top of a taper, soft
   curtains with a longer nape, the overgrown buzz. Colours: glossy espresso brunette, muted copper, soft warm blonde,
   black. "Healthy-looking hair" is the season's word [9]: one sheen band makes it. (§5)
7. **Sunglasses solve the face and say 2026.** Oversized "bug-eye" frames and wraparound shields are THE street-style
   accessory of 2026 [1][12]; the tiny oval frames of 2018-2024 now read old. A lens shape over the eye is the most
   current generic face we have (`design_research.md` §11.6).
8. **Accessories that read at our size:** the slouchy shoulder bag tucked tight under the arm, the east-west bag,
   the big soft tote [13]; over-ear wired headphones (the band, the cup, a thin cable) [13]; a cap (warm coast); an
   iced coffee or a phone in the hand. Jewellery only at 300 px and up (one gold hoop's glint).
9. **Fourteen looks and ten hairstyles** (§4, §5), each as 3-6 shapes, a colour pair, the one detail that sells it
   and what is left of it at 50 px. The board draws them all at 520 px and again at film size.
10. **Never** (§8): skinny jeans with ankle boots; the belted A-line coat with tights, ankle boots and a blanket scarf
    (the 2014-2017 autumn uniform, and our refined her's current outfit); chunky sneakers; tiny oval sunglasses;
    micro bags; the oversized blazer over baggy jeans; head-to-toe beige; light-wash mom jeans; pompom beanies; the
    helmet bob and the ball bun; the high skin fade; corporate Memphis people.

## 2. What they wear (the research, autumn 2026)

### 2.1 The season's direction

- **Balance after oversize.** New York's February 2026 shows: slim-straight jeans replacing baggy jeans, the
  "non-basic" leather jacket as the key piece of 2026, oversize eyewear as the street-style accessory, printed coats,
  and "a sea of black trousers" [1]. For autumn 2026, editors list fitted jackets, slim cardigans, polished knits and
  more streamlined denim in place of the oversized blazer over baggy jeans and the chunky sweater over leggings [4].
  Barrel and horseshoe jeans are past their peak (still worn, softer); straight jeans "not too baggy and not too
  slim" and dark indigo washes are the safe pick [4].
- **The skirt is back.** At the September 2026 shows (spring 2027), street style "declared it statement-skirt
  season": patterned, textured and coloured skirts in every length, the midi reading "I mean business", the maxi a
  softer boho; one editor wore a deep plum T-shirt tucked into a tomato-red leather skirt [2].
- **Outerwear is reshaped, not replaced.** The trench gets funnel necks and shorter or oversized cuts; leather moves
  past the biker to cropped, clean zip-front and high-neck jackets; suede is the season's texture (short zip jackets,
  bombers, reworked trenches), brown most of all, also burgundy and navy [14]. Fringe stays, polished and one piece
  per outfit [14].
- **Colour accents:** red accessories (scarves, boots, cherry bags), orange, all-grey sets in Paris; in New York long
  fur coats and pillbox hats (micro-trends; we skip them) [3].

### 2.2 New York, the autumn city

**Women.** Structured and considered: the long, structured, often oversized wool coat in earthy shades; the trench;
the cropped or high-collared leather jacket; suede jackets; boxy sweater vests and sleeveless knits over shirts;
button-up shirts untucked or half-tucked with loafers; sheer and statement skirts; black wide trousers; navy beside
black as the neutral; red shoes and bags as the accent [1][2][6]. A March 2026 cool-girl look in Midtown: a sheer
lace midi skirt, a high-collared leather jacket and slingback pumps [5]. Jeans are worn with polished separates:
blazers and pumps rather than sneakers [5]. Shoes: loafers (suede), ballet flats, Mary Janes, kitten heels [11].

**Men.** Cleaner, less logo: relaxed but tailored; straight trousers replacing ultra-wide ones; refined, tapered
cargos; a prep revival (quarter-zips, rugby shirts, khakis); textured knits; chore coats in place of the wool topcoat;
suede jackets (a caramel or black oversized one, a brown Harrington); looser, higher-waisted pleated trousers;
penny and horsebit loafers; brown as "the new navy", with burgundy and navy [15]. Neutral or leather bombers [15].

### 2.3 Los Angeles, the warm coast

**Women.** Relaxed, light and varied colour, light layers [5][6]: an editor who spent summer 2026 in L.A. saw
oversized button-downs, relaxed, lived-in wide-leg jeans, knee-length skirts and non-black ballet flats "from baby
pink to butter yellow" everywhere [5]. Blue jeans are close to mandatory, worn with cozy sweaters and walkable
sandals; denim jackets and light blazers rather than heavy coats [5]. L.A.'s blazer is lighter, worn over airy tees or
ribbed tanks (New York's is charcoal or camel) [6]. Summer 2026 cool-girl pieces: capris, stretchy running shorts with
side stripes worn with kitten heels, silk trousers, sleep dresses [5]. Athleisure is its own uniform: matching black
leggings and tops with white piping, a sweater over a sports bra, the set worn under a trench with big sunglasses
and sneakers [5].

**Men.** Boxy, cropped short-sleeve camp-collar shirts (solid, retro stripes, knitted or open-weave), worn untucked;
longer, roomier shorts (knee-length jorts or chinos); suede or woven loafers with white crew socks; relaxed or barrel
trousers; oversized tees; a cap; a warm palette (burnt orange, ochre, crimson; forest green, camel, slate blue)
[6][15].

### 2.4 Hair

- **Women, autumn 2026:** the "ripped" bob (a layered bob with softly separated ends) is the standout cut; the long
  bob carries on from summer; curtain bangs stay, best "texturized"; "bombshell" bangs (long, opening around the eyes
  and cheekbones, blending into face-framing layers); long sweeping layers with fullness at the bottom; shags and
  natural texture at every length; the line is "trading precision for personality" [9].
- **Styling:** claw clips are bigger and everywhere; the sleek ponytail and the bouncy blowout are in; the severe
  slick bun softens into looser, sculptural buns [9].
- **Braids and textured hair:** knotless braids lead, in medium-to-small sizes; boho knotless braids with curly ends
  are the signature look; goddess braids, butterfly locs and stitch cornrows; honey and caramel blends at the ends
  (retailer sources, weaker) [9].
- **Men:** the textured crop and the French crop (a short forward fringe) over a low taper fade are the most
  requested cuts; the low taper has overtaken the high skin fade; a soft mullet with curtains; the overgrown buzz;
  curls on top of a taper, twists and sponge twists for tight curls, and more intricate braiding [10].
- **Colour:** glossy espresso brunette is the season's shade (dimension and shine over highlights); copper stays but
  muted (cinnamon, auburn, whiskey); blondes soften and warm, brightest around the face [9].

### 2.6 Shoes, bags and other accessories

- **Shoes:** slim, low-profile sneakers have replaced chunky basketball styles (Puma Speedcat, adidas Tokyo and
  Taekwondo, the ballet-sneaker hybrid); ballet flats keep growing (mesh, crochet, colours, slingbacks); Mary Janes
  with modern straps; suede loafers; kitten heels over stilettos [11].
- **Bags:** soft, slouchy shoulder bags that mould to the body; oversized lived-in leather totes; everyday east-west
  bags; a slouchy clutch replacing the micro bag; burgundy and suede [13].
- **Sunglasses:** oversized, bug-eyed frames and wraparound shields, frames that reach past the brow and cover the
  cheekbones; slim ovals remain for minimalists but now read like the last era [12].
- **Headphones:** wired over-ear headphones and earbuds are a fashion accessory again (US sales of wired headphones
  up about 20 % in early 2026), the cable looped through a belt loop or over a jacket [13].
- **Jewellery:** chunky gold, mixed metals, layered chains (retailer sources) [13]; at our sizes only a glint.

### 2.7 What reads dated (2026)

From the "dated" lists of autumn 2026 [4] and our own reading of what ages a drawing:
- skinny jeans with ankle boots; anything "painted on";
- the oversized blazer with baggy jeans, the chunky sweater with leggings (the 2021-2024 uniform);
- chunky sneakers and platforms; jelly sandals; boat shoes;
- "sad beige", quiet luxury head to toe, "Crayola" colours, print-maxxing, extra-wide belts;
- light-wash (normcore) mom jeans; cold-shoulder tops; peplums; tiered prairie minis; boxy longline vests;
- micro bags; bag charms (contested);
- tiny oval sunglasses and aviators as the default [12];
- heavy logos and hype streetwear [15];
- the severe slick bun; the high skin fade [9][10];
- and the drawing style itself: corporate Memphis people (huge bendy limbs, tiny heads, blue or purple skin, flat
  primaries; it ruled tech illustration about 2017-2022 and is now read as generic and dated [4];
  `design_research.md` §2).

## 3. From a garment to shapes (the translation rules)

### 3.1 What survives at our sizes

| figure height | what reads | shapes |
|---|---|---|
| 180-220 px (the "you") | the silhouette, three value groups, the hair's shape, the shoes' shape, the one detail | 8-12 |
| 100-130 px (near strangers) | the silhouette, the lengths (coat, skirt, trouser), the hair's shape, the shoes' value | 6-8 |
| 50-80 px (mid ground) | a top-bottom value split, the coat's length, the hair mass, two foot dots | 3-5 |
| 20-40 px (far crowd) | a column with a top-bottom split and a head | 2 |

A detail survives at phone size only if it is at least 0.02 H across (4 px on a 200 px figure) with a 15 L* step, or
a true line (a strap, a cable, piping) of 0.006 H (1 px) or more with a strong step. Logos, prints, buttons,
stitching and jewellery are never drawn under 300 px.

### 3.2 Lengths and widths (in H, measured up from the sole)

- **Hems:** cropped jacket 0.58-0.62; hip 0.48-0.50; oversized shirt or tee 0.44-0.47 (back); short coat or trench
  0.24-0.30 (just below the knee); long coat 0.17-0.20 (mid-calf); midi skirt 0.15-0.22; shorts 0.28-0.32 (at the
  knee); trousers 0.00-0.02 (touching the shoe, a slight break).
- **Distance of the outline from the body** (as `design_research.md` C3): compact 0.005-0.01; relaxed 0.015-0.025;
  roomy 0.03-0.06. A figure has ONE roomy piece.
- **Back-view widths at the hem:** long open coat 0.30-0.32; trench 0.28; A-line midi 0.30-0.34; bias slip skirt
  0.26-0.28; wide trouser leg 0.13-0.14 each; straight 0.10-0.11; slim-straight 0.085-0.09.
- **Shoulders:** dropped (the seam 0.02-0.035 H outside and below the shoulder point, one soft arc) for coats,
  shirts, tees and knits; natural for the leather jacket and the blazer.
- **Shoes** (profile): ballet flat 0.13 long x 0.025 tall, the vamp low (a skin sliver over the foot); slim sneaker
  0.15 x 0.035, a light sole band 0.008; loafer 0.14 x 0.035; kitten heel a 0.02 heel under a pointed toe.

### 3.3 Materials in two or three flat planes

No outlines and no texture; the material shows in the outline, the planes and one step (`people.md` §b,
`design_research.md` C8):
- **wool coat:** matte; a soft rim; straight outlines, a crisp hem;
- **leather:** dark; one crisp highlight streak along the shoulder and the sleeve (+20-25 L*, 0.005 H wide);
- **suede:** matte, no highlight; its rim a little wider and softer, a step lighter;
- **satin (the slip skirt):** one soft sheen band along the hip (+15 L*), clinging at the hip and swinging below;
- **denim:** indigo; a lighter fade on the front of the thigh (+8 L*, a multiplied overlay) and a lighter hem;
- **knit:** a ribbed band at the hem and the cuffs (a 0.012 H value step);
- **puffer:** 3-4 horizontal channels as value bands, each outline bump 0.005 H.

### 3.4 The one detail

Every look has ONE detail that sells it (the table in §4), chosen to read at 180 px: usually a silhouette event (an
open front, belt tails, a hood over a collar, a cap's brim, a half tuck) or a value event at the feet or neck (white
socks, the white tee's triangle, coloured flats). Never two details competing; the rest is quiet.

## 7. Dressing a film

- **Pick the grammar by place and weather,** not by the real city: autumn city (N1-N7) for a cool place, warm coast
  (L1-L7) for a warm one; a mixed crowd in a shoulder season (L7, N3, N4).
- **The "you":** one look from the library, the film's hero colour on its roomy piece (the coat, the trench, the
  skirt, the shirt), the rest tonal; the flowing element from the look itself (the trench's belt tails, the
  ponytail, the slip skirt's hem, the coat's open edge), never an added scarf.
- **A crowd of six:** at least four different looks and two men; hair from at least four styles; skins from the whole
  range; the accent on at most one person in four; the hero colour on nobody else (`design_research.md` §12).
- **Our proposal for the next cool-city "you":** N2, the trench in the hero colour with its belt tails as the
  flowing element, straight dark jeans, ballet flats, hair H1 or H3. It replaces the belted A-line coat, plum tights,
  ankle boots, wool tube scarf and bun of `people.md` §d, which read 2015.
