**The playbook rule now** (Visual style, "Hair is never static or one firm shape ... INSIDE A PROPER, STYLISH, CLEAN
HAIRCUT"): the cut comes first: a designed silhouette with one coherent flow (every lock follows the parting and the
crown), a few big shapes, a clean hem; the life is a touch (a soft wave, one or two deliberate loose locks or
flyaways), never bed-head; the head stacked over the shoulders on a long, upright neck, never pushed forward.
