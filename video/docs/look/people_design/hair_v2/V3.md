**Why ours reads "like a person woke up" and theirs clean and stylish** (plainly):
1. **There is no cut.** The references are first a recognisable haircut (a flipped jaw bob, a swept-back crop, a long
   straight fall with a V hem): a silhouette a stylist made. Ours is a smooth dome with 5-10 separate ribbons hung on
   it; the ribbons, not a cut, make the outline, so the outline is whatever the ribbons do.
2. **The flow is broken.** In the references every piece follows one flow from the parting or the crown (parallel
   lines down a long fall, one fan sweeping back on a crop, curls radial from one centre). Ours has locks that each wave
   on their own (1.4-1.9 waves per lock, phases staggered), outer locks that swing out, and a lock that crosses the
   others on purpose ("mess level 2"): neighbours are never parallel.
3. **The hem is frayed.** Their tips are few and alike (all the bob's flips turn out; the long fall's V has three tips
   all pointing down). Ours ends in 6-7 tips at random heights (spread up to 0.8 u) curling different ways, plus a gap
   and small locks poking out: the look of hair nobody has combed.
4. **Too many marks inside.** They use one shadow shape and one highlight (or a rim) per big shape; ours draws a shade
   edge and up to two sheen dashes on every lock, plus crown dashes: ~14 marks on one head, which read as tangles.
5. **The loose bits are many and random;** theirs are one or two single, thin, deliberate arcs (a hair over the crown,
   a face-framing strand), placed.
6. **The waves are crimps;** theirs are straight or one broad S across the whole head, in phase.
7. **The neck:** v2 pushed the head 0.03 H forward on a neck leaning 20 degrees (a craniovertebral angle ~58, from a
   study of relaxed students). On a slim illustrated figure that reads as a desk slump ("sitting at computer the whole
   day"). Every reference stacks the head over a long, upright neck. The study was right about real students and wrong
   for our people: elegance is the target, not the average.
8. **Short cuts:** our crop's tufts point every way off a dome (dark flames); the references' crop is all locks,
   one fan, sweeping back. Our twists are a row of blocks; our curls hook both ways at random.
