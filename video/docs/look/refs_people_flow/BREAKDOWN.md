# FLOW references: the art breakdown (2026-10-31)

For the art director rebuilding our people (owner's words: `README.md`; sheet: `breakdown_sheet.png`, 01-05 over
their silhouettes at one height, our v4 her last). H = hair top to sole, measured on the ~300 px screenshots; every
number is an observation, never a target. **06-12 arrived mid-study (seen, not measured):** they confirm the grammar
(a coat's back panel as one pointed flag, 06; a skirt streaming in pointed flame-panels, 09; faceted straight
planes, 07, 10; one tapered acting arm, 08; a full lit rim when backlit, 10) and add the calm one (11): nothing
blows, one shape in one colour still carries her.

## 1. Proportions
- About 5.5-6.5 heads, hair top to chin (01 5.8, 05 5.6; ~6 where a hat hides the chin); our her 7.5 by the same
  measure. A head about a quarter bigger, anatomy left undrawn (no bust, waist, hip) and stick-thin limbs make the
  "slightly cartoonish".
- The garment runs shoulder to shin (0.5-0.7 H); below it, two thin dark legs (0.05-0.3 H visible).
- 03 and 04 read as children: a neckless head dome, a knee-length bell on short stick legs. An adult 20-30 version
  keeps the bigger head, undrawn anatomy, slim dark legs, one garment; it swaps the dome and short legs for a slim
  neck, long legs, adult shoulders, a hint of waist (a belt, a wrap).

## 2. Overall shape
- One primitive plus a dark head cap: 01 a column (hem no wider than the shoulders), 02 a cone (hem ~2x the
  shoulders), 03 a bell (~1.7x), 04 a soft box, a lantern, 05 an A-line (~1.7x). Ours: a tube (hem = shoulders) on a
  second tube, the trousers.
- The garment is ~60-75% of the body's silhouette, in ONE saturated colour. Ours: four masses, none a colour: a
  near-black coat (~60%), a cream column (~24%, the strongest contrast), copper hair, skin.
- Negative space reads: sky between the stick legs, between the ribbons, under 03's arms. Hidden: arms, neck, feet,
  and the face in three of five.

## 3. Motion
- The body is calm and vertical (05 mid stride, ~0.3 H between the feet; 02 a heel kicked up; 03 arms out); the
  motion lives at the edges: hair, sash, hem points.
- One wind per picture; all that can move goes one way (01, 02, 03, 05 right; 04 left): 02's hair and the cape's
  point sweep right together; 01's sash rises at 30-45 degrees, the hair fanning near level; 05's hair streams back, the
  hem's back corner trailing.
- The hair draws the longest line, 0.2-0.65 H past the body (03: 1.5 H, curling up like smoke). Ours: nothing passes
  the outline; hair and coat hang.

## 4. Directional style of the clothing
- Long straight runs ending in sharp points: the cape's sides into one big point downwind, with a small step (02);
  the dress's corners kicked out by the stride (05); flag-like vertical tatters (01); a ragged hem, a sheer side panel
  (04).
- No flat hems (each a zig-zag of 2-4 points), no construction (seams, lapels, buttons, pockets, cuffs); at most one
  soft value shift where a fold would be.
- Curves belong to the hair, the head cap and the shoulder's roll; the cloth is straight-sided.

## 5. Hair
- The head is a smooth dark cap, the figure's darkest value, no strand lines; from behind it is the whole head.
- Ribbons: one (02); three plus wisps (01); four or five flat ribbons twisting to show a lighter side (03); one brushy
  mass breaking into points (04); one thick ribbon splitting into three or four (05). They leave the back of the head
  as one mass and split later.
- Each tapers its whole length to a needle tip: crisp at the root, the tips thinning or fading into the sky (05) or
  breaking into dry-brush points (04); a thin lit rim along one edge (05).

## 6. Face, hands and feet
- Face: a pale featureless sliver (01, an 18 px head); a profile edge (brow, nose, lips, chin) lit by a thin rim, no
  drawn eye (05, a 27 px head); from behind, nothing. Ours at film size: a skin shape, the profile line and, by the
  owner's rule, one small open eye looking away.
- Hands: hidden (01, 02, 04), a tiny pale tick (05), thin pale sticks (03). Feet: small dark boots (02's repeat the
  cape's pink once, below).

## 7. Finish and colour
- Flat shapes softly painted inside (one gradient or brush texture: 01's vertical rainbow, 04's sheer edge); no hard
  shade planes, no outlines; the outline crisp, only hair tips and 04's edges brushy.
- A thin crisp lit edge on the light side of garment and hair (02's left edge, 05's front, a halo round 04's head)
  lifts the figure off a sky of similar value.
- The garment is the frame's most saturated thing, on a soft sky of its own family (crimson on peach-mauve) or a muted
  complement (02's magenta on blue-grey); hair and legs the darkest values; skin barely shows.
- For our painted look: the garment as the person's one colour, the sky tuned to it for the "you"; the lit edge; soft
  gradients inside; the hair as the dark accent; fading tips (their dusk mood stays theirs).

## 8. Why they read more appealing and clean than our v4 (ranked)
1. **One shape, not a kit of parts.** Merge coat, sleeves and arms into one coat shape; close the open front (or give
   the inner layer the coat's colour); delete lapels, cuffs, pocket, finger separations.
2. **One wind, a calm body.** One wind per shot drives the hair chain and new hem and tail points on the spring rig.
3. **A dark cap and a few ribbons.** Delete the hair curtain (a flat rectangle from behind), strand lines, flyaways;
   author a smooth cap and two to four tapering ribbons that stream past the body.
4. **A flare with pointed hems, not a tube.** Narrow sloping shoulders, the coat widening toward the hem, its straight
   hem cut into two or three points sweeping downwind.
5. **One colour mass, not four.** One saturated garment colour per person; trousers, shoes and hair toward one dark
   value; the cream column goes.
6. **No detail on construction.** Delete fold slivers, the trouser stack, shoe profiles; legs become two slim strokes,
   feet small dark wedges.
7. **A slightly bigger head** on a slim visible neck, legs still long: a touch of storybook, never the references'
   child.
8. **A lit edge, soft paint.** A thin crisp rim along the light side; soft gradients, not hard-edged shade shapes.

## 9. The FLOW design grammar (a sketch, adult cast)
1. **One shape, one colour:** one garment shape in one saturated colour; hair and legs one dark accent. (Clean; read
   at a glance.)
2. **Narrow above, free below:** sloping shoulders, the cloth widening toward the hem; a slim column for stillness.
   (Grace, lightness.)
3. **A calm body, living edges:** torso and head upright and simple; the motion in hair, hems, tails, scarf ends.
   (Serene but alive.)
4. **One wind per shot,** its direction chosen with the composition; none for a still beat. (A world with air.)
5. **Straight runs into sharp points,** two to four per hem, all downwind; no flat hems, frills, ruffles. (Crisp
   energy, no noise.)
6. **Curves only where it flows:** ribbons, head cap, the shoulder's roll. (Elegance.)
7. **Hair is a dark cap and a few ribbons** tapering to needle tips; a short cut ends in two or three swept points.
   (Freedom.)
8. **Arms inside the shape;** one acting arm at a time, a slim stroke with a small tapered hand. (Calm.)
9. **Legs are two slim dark strokes,** feet small dark wedges; wide trousers one flared panel per leg. (Lightness.)
10. **A slightly bigger head, a near-blank face:** a slim neck; a skin shape, the profile line, one small open eye
    looking away; from behind, the dark cap. (Warmth, still adult.)
11. **Lit at the edge, soft inside:** a thin crisp rim on the light side, soft gradients, no outlines. (Glow: the
    person belongs to the light.)
12. **Today's clothes, translated:** for her, the long coat whose tails split into points, the belted trench, the
    long skirt as a bell, wide trousers under a cropped top; for him, the overcoat as a column flaring a little, the big
    open shirt fluttering at hem and sleeves, wide trousers, shoulders a touch squarer but sloping. (Current NY/LA,
    effortless.)
