# FLOW references: the art breakdown (2026-10-31)

For the art director rebuilding our people (owner's words: `README.md`; sheets: `breakdown_sheet.png`, 01-05 and our
v4 her; `breakdown_sheet_2.png`, 06-12). H = hair top to sole on ~300 px screenshots; numbers are observations, never
targets.

## 1. Proportions
- About 5.5-7.5 heads (hair top to chin), most near 6 (01 5.8, 06 ~6.8; ~ = an end hidden); our her 7.5. 08 (~7.3)
  and 09 (7.0) read as simply: the "slightly cartoonish" comes less from the head (a quarter bigger in most) than
  from undrawn anatomy and stick-thin limbs.
- The garment runs shoulder to shin (0.5-0.7 H; 08's to the ground); below it, two thin legs.
- 03, 04, 07, 10 read young (a neckless dome, a short bell or big hood, short legs); adult 20-30 takes a slim neck,
  long legs, adult shoulders, a hint of waist (a belt, a wrap, 08's bodice).

## 2. Overall shape
- One primitive plus a dark head cap: a column (01, 11), a hooded column (07, hem ~1.3x the shoulders), a cone (02,
  ~2x), a bell (03), a lantern (04), an A-line (05, 12; ~1.5-1.7x), a kite (10: widest at the elbows, one point
  down); or a calm hanging shape plus one flag downwind (06, 09, 08's train; 2.5-5x the shoulders). Ours: a tube
  on a second tube, the trousers.
- The garment is ~60-85% of the body's silhouette, in ONE saturated colour. Ours: four masses, none a colour: a
  near-black coat (~60%), a cream column (~24%, the strongest contrast), copper hair, skin.
- Sky shows between legs, ribbons, flag and hair; the neck and most arms stay hidden.

## 3. Motion
- The body is calm and vertical (05 mid stride; 02, 12 a heel lifted); the motion lives at the edges: hair, sash,
  hem points, flags.
- One wind per picture, hair and cloth going one way together: near level (06's flag beside the hair), a sash
  rising 30-45 degrees (01), a train and flames falling 10-50 degrees (08, 09). Or none (11): the shape alone
  carries her.
- Hair, flag or train draws the longest line: the hair 0.1-1 H past the body (07 ~1 H; 03 1.5 H, curling up like
  smoke), a flag or train ~0.6 H (06, 08). Ours: nothing passes the outline; hair and coat hang.

## 4. Directional style of the clothing
- Long straight runs ending in sharp points: the cape's sides into one big point downwind (02); corners kicked out by
  the stride (05); flag-like tatters (01); the back of a coat or skirt as ONE long pointed flag while the front hangs
  (06; 09 two flame points; 08 a long train).
- In wind no flat hem (2-4 points; 10 all downward triangles); still 11 keeps a plain one. No construction (seams,
  lapels, buttons, pockets, cuffs).
- Folds are long straight planes, never lines: lit against shadow, split by one straight edge along the fall (12), or
  a few long facets (07, 10). The cloth is straight-sided; curves belong to hair, cap and shoulder.

## 5. Hair
- The head is a smooth dark cap, the figure's darkest value, no strand lines; from behind, the whole head. Calm: a
  top bun (11), or one long dark mass ending in a hooked point (12).
- Ribbons: one to seven (03's twisting to show a lighter side), or one mass splitting into three or four (05, 06) or
  dry-brush points (04); they leave the head as one mass and split later.
- Each tapers to a needle tip: crisp at the root, fading into the sky (05, 07); a thin lit rim along one edge (05,
  07, 10).

## 6. Face, hands and feet
- Face: a pale featureless sliver (01, an 18 px head; 08's cheek); a profile edge lit by a thin rim, no drawn eye (05,
  27 px); from behind, nothing.
- Hands: mostly hidden or pale ticks (03, 05). 08's acting arm is the model: one tapered shape, an elbow-length
  sleeve in the dress colour, a bare forearm, a small closed fist, swung ~25 degrees out; no fingers.
- Feet: small dark boots. Legs read against their ground: dark on a light one, pale in the dress's family on a dark
  one (09).

## 7. Finish and colour
- Flat shapes, painted inside with one soft gradient (01's rainbow, 04's sheer edge) or a few long hard-edged planes
  (07, 10, 12); never fold lines, small shade shapes or outlines; edges crisp, hair tips brushy.
- A thin crisp lit edge on the light side (02, 05, 12) lifts the figure off a sky of similar value; backlit, the rim
  runs round the whole figure, hair and all, and glows (10).
- The garment is the frame's most saturated thing, on a soft sky of its own family (crimson on peach-mauve) or a muted
  complement (magenta on blue-grey); hair and most legs the darkest values; skin barely shows. Ours: the person's one
  colour, the sky tuned to it for the "you" (their dusk mood and rainbows stay theirs).

## 8. Why they read more appealing and clean than our v4 (ranked)
1. **One shape, not a kit of parts.** Merge coat, sleeves and arms into one coat shape; close the open front (or give
   the inner layer the coat's colour); delete lapels, cuffs, pocket, finger separations.
2. **One wind, a calm body.** One wind per shot (or none) drives the hair chain and new hem, tail and flag points on
   the spring rig.
3. **A dark cap and a few ribbons.** Delete the hair curtain (flat from behind), strand lines, flyaways; author a
   smooth cap and two to four tapering ribbons that stream past the body.
4. **A flare with pointed hems, not a tube.** Narrow sloping shoulders, the coat widening toward the hem, its straight
   hem cut into two or three points sweeping downwind, or its back streaming as one flag (06).
5. **One colour mass, not four.** One saturated garment colour per person; trousers and shoes toward the hair's dark
   value (light on a dark ground); the cream column goes.
6. **No detail on construction.** Delete fold slivers, the trouser stack, shoe profiles; folds become long planes,
   legs two slim strokes, feet small wedges.
7. **At most a slightly bigger head** on a slim neck, legs still long; never a child (08 and 09 stay clean at adult
   proportions).
8. **A lit edge, simple paint.** A thin crisp rim, all round when backlit; soft gradients or long planes inside.

## 9. The FLOW design grammar (a sketch, adult cast)
1. **One shape, one colour:** one garment shape in one saturated colour; hair and legs one accent. (Clean; read at a
   glance.)
2. **Narrow above, free below:** sloping shoulders, the cloth widening toward the hem; a slim column for stillness.
   (Grace, lightness.)
3. **A calm body, living edges:** torso and head upright and simple; the motion in hair, hems, tails, scarf ends.
   (Serene but alive.)
4. **One wind per shot,** its direction chosen with the composition; none for a still beat, the shape alone carrying
   her. (A world with air.)
5. **Straight runs into sharp points,** two to four per hem, all downwind; folds as long straight planes; no frills.
   (Crisp energy, no noise.)
6. **Curves only where it flows:** ribbons, head cap, the shoulder's roll. (Elegance.)
7. **Hair is a dark cap and a few ribbons** tapering to needle tips; a short cut ends in two or three swept points; a
   top bun when still. (Freedom.)
8. **Arms inside the shape;** one acting arm at a time, one tapered shape to a small closed fist. (Calm.)
9. **Legs are two slim strokes,** dark on a light ground, light on a dark one; feet small wedges; wide trousers one
   flared panel per leg. (Lightness.)
10. **A slightly bigger head, a near-blank face:** a slim neck; a skin shape, the profile line and, by the owner's
    rule, one small open eye looking away; from behind, the dark cap. (Warmth, still adult.)
11. **Lit at the edge, simple inside:** a thin crisp rim on the light side, all round when backlit; soft gradients or
    a few long planes; no outlines. (Glow: the person belongs to the light.)
12. **Today's clothes, translated:** for her, the long coat (tails split into points, or the back as one flag), the
    belted trench, the long skirt as a bell, wide trousers under a cropped top; for him, the overcoat as a column
    flaring a little, the big open shirt fluttering, wide trousers, shoulders a touch squarer. (Current NY/LA,
    effortless.)
