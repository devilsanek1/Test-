# Cine guidebook: light, colour, transitions and sequencing (Arcane S1, measured, 2026-10-26)

Guidebook section for ALL videos from G3 on. Source: the owner's recording of "Amazing Shots of ARCANE SEASON 1" (YouTube,
Amazing Shots, id HzJ7Nvwz1e8; 6:19, 24 fps); `#13 @14.6` = shot 13 at 14.6 s. Words only, no frames kept. Same cleaned set
as `cine_motion.md` (**136 shots, 376.9 s**). **[P]** = our pixel measure (CIE L* 0-100, chroma C*, hue; 320 x 136 frames,
captions masked, middle 70 % of each shot, 3 frames either side of a cut); **[L]** = labelled from our shot sheet. The editor
chose the reel's order, so transition rules come from the 36 cuts INSIDE scenes (#10-13, #77-81, #92-94, #110-113, #116-117,
#119-121, #123-124, #127-135, #140-143, #146-162) and from content matches between any two shots. Colour per `../PALETTE.md`.

## 0. The rules in brief
1. **One light, behind the subject, near the centre.** The brightest 2 % of the frame sits in the middle third in 72 % of
   shots, 82 % of silhouettes [P]; 44 % are backlit [L]. In 9:16: behind the head (x 380-700), its field rising into y 317-556.
2. **One hue per place.** 78 % of shots keep >= 85 % of their colour within +-30° of one hue (59 % >= 95 %) [P]. Gold =
   refuge and hope, crimson = danger and grief, blue-violet = power, magic, action.
3. **Warm against cold happens ACROSS cuts.** Inside a frame only 20 of 129 coloured shots carry an opposite-temperature
   accent, 0.1-3.7 % of the frame, median 1.0 % [P]: exactly our pop.
4. **Arcane is dark, we are light.** Median shot L* 19 (p5 5, p50 15, p95 49); only 20 shots have >= 5 % of the frame above
   L* 80 [P]. Its light frames (L* 45-69: #35, #47, #49, #61, #85) are resets after dark runs: our page lives there.
   Copy the value ORDER (light behind, dark around), never the darkness.
5. **Inside a scene the light stays put across the cut:** bright spot moves 0.13 H (64 % under 0.2 H), frame value +-4 L*,
   temperature flips 2 of 36. Between scenes: 0.45 H, +-12 L*, 32 % flip [P]. The eye lands where it already is.
6. **One change per cut** (scale OR value OR temperature). A flip = a new place, hidden in motion; a jump >= 25 L* = a new
   world or an impact (24 of 135 cuts) [P].
7. **Cut on a light event:** flash, eclipse by a dark foreground, bloom, dip. One real dip in 136 shots: once a video at most.
8. **A mini-sequence climbs in value to its payoff** (#126-132: L* 8 -> 9 -> 11 -> 21 -> 55) and keeps ONE source as the
   thread (#122-124: the lamp, then the face it lights, held within 0.05 H into the close-up) [P].

## 2. Value: Arcane's dark, our light page [P]
- **The structure is radial, not a ground-to-sky ramp:** light behind the subject, dark around it. Wides are darker at
  the bottom than the top only 49 % of the time (58 % for establishing and scale shots).
- **Ours, same order, flipped dose:** the light = the page (Sage L* 94, Dusk mist 88) or the cream core (90), 50-80 % of the
  frame; the dark = a near frame in mids (`#8A9AA8` 63, lip `#545F6D` 40) on 2-3 edges, 35-55 %; the anchor (14) ~5 %, on
  figures. Frame mean: ~84 in a Sage interior, ~70 in a Dusk scale wide (our estimate from the palette shares).
- **A "dark scene" for us** = more near frame and anchor, never a darker page: frame mean >= 55, page >= 50 %, captions on
  L* >= 70; a dark wiper may cross the full frame for <= 0.5 s.

## 4. Transitions
Inside scenes the 16 x 7 luminance layout correlates 0.75 across the cut (between scenes 0.25) [P]: a cut may change scale
or place and still read as one event. The big changes ride on an event: a flash, a wipe, a move.

## 5. Sequencing 3-5 shots
Half the in-scene runs build to their widest shot as the payoff (#81, #94, #113, #131-132, #143), half end close (#13,
#121, #124, #135, #156) [L]; a run that opens wide keeps the wide short (#126: 1.5 s). The thread is one light source.
- **Q1 Refuge -> world -> refuge** (#10 -> #11-12 -> #13): pair in a warm moon-gate 3.3 s (retreat pull), 0.9 s dip, the
  window tiny in a vast dark arch, back close; warm throughout [P]. Ours: nook 3 s, dark wiper, outside wide 1.5-2.5 s with
  our window the one lit point (pop), hands again within 5 s.
- **Q2 Light -> who -> feeling** (#122 -> #123 -> #124): one lamp in the dark ~4.5 s (hard cut at ~327.4 s [P]); straight
  down on the woman it lights 2.9 s; her face and reaching hand 2.8 s; L* 4 -> 14 -> 21, the lit face held within 0.05 H
  over the last cut [P]. Ours: the price tag or lamp alone (90 % page) -> the person under it -> hands and face in the pool.
- **Q3 Geography -> dive -> payoff** (#126 -> #127-128 -> #129 -> #130 -> #131-132): high wide 1.5 s, beat and dive 2.4 s,
  dutch two-shot 3.2 s, impact 0.2 s, tiny figure under a giant ring 1.6 s; cold throughout, L* climbing to 55. Ours: the
  page and hero share grow shot by shot; the payoff is the lightest frame.
- **Q4 Threshold** (#92 -> #93 -> #94): 0.8 / 0.8 / 4.9 s, gold throughout, the opening blooms (T7) = K9 -> K3.
- **Our default (establish, intimacy, scale; 8-12 s):** (1) 2-2.5 s: the place AND its light source in frame, temperature
  set; (2) 2.5-4 s: our person lit by that source (pool or rim, the warmest value on skin), 0.5-1 s still; (3) 1.5-3 s: the
  colossus against the same light, observer 0.04-0.08 H, bright shape within 0.2 H of shot 1's; (4) optional 1-2 s symbol:
  the self-lit story object; (5) a face or hands within 5 s. One temperature unless the sequence IS the turn (T6 or T9).

## 6. What the measurements do to COZY_AND_SCALE §4-5
- **§4 haze:** the per-plane step holds on vistas, but frames are radial (bright centre 72 %, darker bottom 49 %). Add: light
  behind the subject, near frame on 2-3 edges in mids; no forced dark ground band.
- **K1:** confirmed, sharpened: keep temperature and the bright area's position across the cut (T8).
- **K2:** add light: up = toward warm light (#58 @132.6 amber shafts up a tree; #69; #139 @350.5 stained glass; #94);
  down = away into dark (#50 @100.6 into the dim sill; #56-57 into green-black, L* 9).
- **K4:** confirmed: Arcane's one dip is our hidden cut; it uses true black, ours a wiper that never touches captions.
- **K7:** add: the light at the top behind the colossus (#3 red V of sky; #31 beams to a VP above; #62 @150.2 orb in the
  skylight; #159 bright opening); the colossus is a silhouette and the lit V holds the captions.
- **K8:** changed: shafts come diagonally from one top corner (6 of 7) and land as pools on seats or people (#38, #89
  @226.2, #99): tilt our core 15-30°. The lone warm light in a cold world is confirmed (#29; #39 amber case).
- **K9:** add T7, the opening brightening a step per cut. **K10:** confirmed cold and even (top light or shafts) with 1-2
  tiny warm points (#20 @19.3 two braziers; #89 amber arches); Arcane's crimson power hall (#21) stays cold in ours.
- **§3 "warm lamp one side, cool window the other":** confirmed as the accent rule (one opposite point, median 1 %).
  **"The threat is never warm":** a deliberate break from Arcane, which paints menace crimson (28 shots); keep ours.
- **§5:** safety confirmed and needed (#150-156). Every Arcane-derived frame must be re-valued (median L* 19 vs our caption
  field >= 70): the bright field behind the head becomes the caption field.

## 7. Caveats
A highlights reel over-samples dark, showy shots; "in-scene" is our reading (#92-93, #152-154 may be flare splits); hue
counts chroma above C* 12, so near-grey shots (#77-80) count by few pixels; source shares are keyword-counted labels, +-5 %.
