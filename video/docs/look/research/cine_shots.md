# Cine guidebook: shot vocabulary, angles and composition (Arcane S1, measured, 2026-10-26)

Guidebook section for ALL videos from G3 on (owner's request). Source: the owner's screen recording of "Amazing Shots of
ARCANE SEASON 1" (YouTube channel Amazing Shots, id HzJ7Nvwz1e8; 6:19, 24 fps, no narration); `#13 @14.6` = shot 13 at
14.6 s into it. Words only: never traced, committed or copied. **[M]** = measured by our flow fit (cuts, zoom, pan, easing,
parallax); **[L]** = labelled by eye from sampled frames (size, angle, lens, planes, figure % of frame height; +-1 class,
+-5 %). **Cleaning:** 165 detected cuts -> **137 editorial shots** (377 s): artefacts #1, #2, #144, #165 dropped; flicker or
flash splits #4-9, #14-19, #40-42, #44-46, #51-54, #56-57, #64-67, #131-132, #146-148 merged. **Caveat:** a "best shots"
compilation over-samples showy wides. Frame: captions y 317-556, read y 600-1250, UI below y 1250 and right of x 780 below
y 840 (COZY_AND_SCALE §5); colour per `../PALETTE.md`.

## 0. The rules in brief
1. **Length follows the job, not the size:** action 0.8 s median (IQR 0.5-2.0), transitions 1.5 s, scale and establishing
   2.9 s, intimate and symbolic 3.0 s, cozy 4.0 s (2.7-6.0) [M lengths, L jobs].
2. **Wides stand still, close shots breathe:** median |zoom| 1.3 % (wide), 1.6 % (extreme wide) vs 3.6 % (full), 4.5 %
   (medium), 5.6 % (close-up) [M, before cleaning]. A wide only travels far when it is THE big move.
3. **Eye level is home (48 %); low is the second angle (33 %) and means power:** 10 of 15 full shots are low. Backlit and
   faceless = menace, lit face = triumph. High + overhead (13 %) = order (institutions) or exposure. Dutch 2 %, fights only.
4. **Silhouette against ONE light is the house look (42 % of shots):** the light sits right behind the head.
5. **Frame within frame (23 %) and centred symmetry (24 %),** often a circle (moon-gate, round window, sun, orb) as a halo.
   Symmetry = order, ritual, standoff; rule of thirds = one person in a world.
6. **3-4 planes by default (83 %); 5 only on vistas,** mostly the big moving reveals; inserts and overheads use 2.
7. **Scale shots (32 %): a tiny observer at median 8 % of frame height (2-15 %) in 61 %, no person at all in 27 %,** a
   back-view figure at 22-38 % facing the world in 9 %. Across a cut, the figure drops 6-14x.
8. **Lens feel:** wide (29 %) on colossi, worm's-eyes and action (near:far speed 1.5-2.5 [M]); telephoto (20 %) on faces,
   inserts and a huge disc behind small people; normal (50 %) everywhere else.

## 3. Lens feel, and how to fake it in flat 2D
- **Normal (50 %):** VP inside the frame, neighbouring planes at our usual f steps (COZY_AND_SCALE §4).
- **Wide (29 %; 52 % of extreme wides, every worm's-eye, 44 % of action):** deep and distorted. Measured near:far speed
  ratio 1.5-2.5 on wide moves (#104 1.88, #126 1.86, #135 1.9, #64-67 2.55 [M]). 2D: foreground at f 1.5-1.9, the VP near
  or past a frame edge, near objects drawn 1.2-1.5x bigger with thicker ink (#124 @330.3 a hand reaching at the lens).
- **Telephoto (20 %; faces #43-46, #77-80, inserts #14-19, compressed vistas #34, #60, #125):** flat, stacked planes.
  2D: parallax spread <= 1.3 (f 0.8-1.25), a huge background shape behind small people (#125 @333.1 two 22 % H figures
  inside a red sun disc ~75 % H: sun 780 px at y 1000, figures 420 px), defocus = fill-only, no ink, one value lighter.

## 4. Composition patterns
**P1 Silhouette against one light (42 %; 61 % of them wide or extreme wide).** Rule: one source behind the subject, the
subject a flat dark shape, the brightest field behind the head. Refs: #10 @10.4 two figures in a round window at sunset;
#23 @27.8 a back-view figure before towering white windows; #59 @141.4 a lone chair-back haloed by a round window.
2D: our page stays light, so a silhouette = >= 25 L* darker than its field, no outline (PALETTE). Anchor `#1F2433` only on
figures <= 0.35 H (~5 % of the frame); a bigger one (medium back view) in `#8A9AA8` with a `#545F6D` lip on a field at
L* >= 88; a 2-3 px rim one step lighter on the lit edge. The bright field behind the head is the caption field.

**P2 Frame within frame (23 %), often round.** About a dozen circles behind or around the subject (moon-gate #10, #13;
round windows #21, #25, #27, #59; sun and orbs #60, #116, #125, #162). Two doses [L]: figures IN the portal, opening 30-40 %
of the frame (#13 @14.6 circle ~95 % H; #26 @36.7 a blazing doorway ~30 %); a refuge looking OUT, opening ~24 % (#81 @193.9
one arched opening in a black mass), which confirms our 15-30 %. 2D: circle 760-1000 px at x 540, centre y 800-1050, sill at
y 1230-1250; either its top stays below y 560 or its flat sky holds the captions; evenodd hole in a wall plate (`Portal`).

**P3 Centred symmetry (24 %) vs thirds (11 %).** Symmetry for power and institutions (#21 @22.1 crimson round window over a
long table; #38; #115 @305.8 a throne fanning out like wings), ritual and standoffs (#30, #136, #165), the stare (#43). Thirds
for one person in a world (#37 @68.6 back-view figure on a boat deck, fortress across the water; #61). 9:16 suits a vertical
axis: symmetric frames centre on x 540; thirds use the vertical stack (captions top, subject y 600-1250, mass at the bottom).

**P4 Negative space.** 60-90 % of the frame empty, subject <= 12 % of the area: #4-9 @4.7 one speck in a blue void; #87 60 %
empty dark beside the pair; #116 @309.2 a hand reaching into 60 % empty night toward a small orb; #122 @322.9 one caged lamp
in ~90 % black. 2D: the empty field is the caption field (y 290-600); keep it one flat value, the subject in the stage.

**P5 Leading lines and diagonals.** Converging lines to one point (#3, #20 tile lines to a lectern, #31 beams to a VP above
the frame, #57, #89 shafts onto a table); a diagonal of reach or action (#14-19 hand rising to the butterfly; #164 @376.0 an
arm and pistol pointing across the gap). 2D: one VP per shot, lines kept out of y 317-556 or fading there.

**P6 Foreground framing (~30 %).** A near mass cut by >= 2 edges: #3 cog wheel; #74 @182.3 a blurred mass over 35 % W;
#88 iron girder; #113 railing; #135 gauntlet. Often doubles as a wiper on the cut (#13, #22, #78). 2D: f 1.3-1.6, fill-only
(no inner ink), 15-40 % W, value one step off the subject; legal under the UI zone.

## 5. Depth layering
Planes [L]: 3 = 49 %, 4 = 34 %, 2 = 10 %, 5 = 6 %, 1 = 1 %. Five only on vistas: the big moving reveals (#55, #56-57, #72,
#94, #113: the planes give the move parallax) and three held vistas (#24, #35, #37). Two: inserts, overheads (#20, #83,
#84), flash frames (#133, #146-148). **Recipe:** 3 by default (flat back, subject, a foreground frame or haze), 4 for
establishing wides, 5 only on our ONE big move, 2 for top-down and inserts.

## 6. Subject scale in scale shots
44 scale shots [L]. **Tiny observer (27, 61 %):** 2-15 % H, median 8 % (#97 @246.5 two walkers 6 % under a storm sky;
#103 @271.4 a 5 % figure by a cyan glow under a giant ruin; #143 @355.6 a 4 % figure on a 50 % H painted face). **No person
(12, 27 %):** #3, #24, #31, #58, #75, #85, #86, #89, #94, #163. **Back view 22-38 % facing the world (4):** #37, #64-67, #72,
#125. **Big silhouette before monumental light (1):** #23, 70 %. On screen the colossus is only 6-12x the figure (#74 1:6,
#21 1:11, #143 1:12): it is always cut by the frame. **Across a cut:** #90 -> #91 (55 % -> 4 %), #130 -> #131 (full frame
-> 10 %), #158 -> #159 (80 % -> 12 %), #110-112 -> #113 (55 % -> 9 %), #134 (5 % -> a 70 % gauntlet). Scale-shot angles:
eye 43 %, low or worm's-eye 36 %, high or overhead 20 %; 48 % wide lens. **9:16:** keep the fraction of height: observer
0.04-0.08 H (77-154 px; our 60-150 px holds), back view 0.28-0.38 H (540-730 px).
