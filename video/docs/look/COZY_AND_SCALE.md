# COZY_AND_SCALE: the rulebook for coziness (enclosure) and scale (Arcane-style), G3-G5

The owner's two visual twists (playbook, 2026-10-25) for G3 (sketch animatic), G4 (look) and G5 (blocking), for v3 (laptop
prices) and v2 (DeepMind resignations). Evidence: `research/` enclosure [EN], coziness [CO], arcane [AR], vertical [VE], audit
[AU]. Colour obeys `PALETTE.md`. Tags VERIFIED / REPORTED / UNCERTAIN; untagged numbers are proposals, to test at G4. 1080 x
1920 (G3 at 30 fps, production at 60), captions 98 px at y 387 / 510 (block y 317-556, x 95-945). Arcane in words only: never
traced, recorded or copied. **Coziness evolves one measured reference at a time** (playbook): the owner's cozy references
C1-C3 and what each taught are logged in words at the end of section 3b; section 3b (Coziness v2) wins where 2-3 differ.

## 1. Principles
1. **Cozy = held, not shut:** walls on 2-3 sides, ONE opening the subject faces, the threat visible but outside it
   (prospect-refuge; safety + abundance + softness, Project Horseshoe [CO §1, VERIFIED]). Shut rooms read as a trap [EN §9].
2. **Scale = a colossus too big to take in whole** (top >= 45° up, cut by the frame; Maertens [EN §4, REPORTED]) against a
   tiny, readable person on our side, ratio >= 10:1, **or a world that keeps going** in a long wide, its landmark only 1.1-1.4x
   the figures yet huge and far (#55 measured: `CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md` "Scale cues"; "contrast across a cut" was half true).
3. **Cozy holds the viewer; scale, only occasionally (playbook), makes them feel the stakes.** Cozy >= 50 % of the runtime
   outside full-frame chart inserts (frame 0's world, the ending; an insert is a neutral breath, neither cozy nor scale:
   v3 G3 is 38 % of all 41 s, 55 % of the non-chart time); 1-2 scale moments, never 3 (v2: 1), 1.5-4 s each, <= 20 % of
   the runtime (VE: 25 %).
4. **ONE big move per video** (2.5-4 s + 0.8-1.5 s hold), <= 1 mid-size move, other story moves push 3-8 %; cozy holds are
   locked or float, never an invisible push (3b.5). Never two scale wides in a row; within ~5 s of each small-making beat, a
   beat of agency (a face, a voice, what is being done or can be done)
   [VE §7: our proposal, built on awe (Piff 2015) and threat-needs-efficacy (EPPM), both VERIFIED].
5. **Coziness belongs to the people affected, never to the threat.** Break it once, at the turn; never twice (the "cosy
   catastrophe" trap [CO §8, VERIFIED term]). Scale shown as fact must be true or signposted as a metaphor (SPJ [VE §7]).

## 3. Cozy rules
- **Light:** one low lamp + <= 1 secondary (a screen), no ceiling light; warm lamp one side, cool window the other (warm, dim
  light raised perceived cosiness [CO §3, REPORTED]). Pools: flat, opaque, a cone widening ~3x, an ellipse 0.5-0.7 W, <= 2
  rings LIGHTER toward the centre (ref 2: peach outer, cream core [AU §1]), darker gaps between pools, no gradient or blur.
  Sage: ring `#F2D49B` (L* 86, C* 32, <= 5 % of the frame), core `#F0E2BF` (L* 90), on a surface at L* <= 80 (desk tan
  `#D9BBA1`; on the L* 94 page a pool reads as a stain); corners `#D7E0C8`; it lights the hands and face.
- **Temperature, not darkness:** cold only in the window (Dusk `#BAC3CE` / `#8A9AA8`, frost `#D9DEE5`), glass <= 20 % of the
  frame; spines, mullions, hair in the anchor `#22332C` (~5 %); page L* >= 75 at night too. Hero (5-15 %) on the story's object,
  one hue all video, across the Sage -> Dusk shift too; ONE pop (amber `#F2A541` or coral `#FF7A4D`), 1-3 %, look-here only.
- **Density:** abundance at the edges and the far side, a calm (never empty) centre: props cluster on the shelf, sill and
  desk edge, which hold 3-6x the linework of the centre (3b.3), each >= 60 px, in many soft flat fills (line drawings only far
  back); the paused frame must read full (`LIFE_AND_DENSITY.md` §0); >= 1 sign of care (steaming mug, blanket, plant)
  [CO §4]. Seen from outside, light 1-3 windows (a few lit windows read as human scale [EN §5]), never most (Kinkade
  [CO §5]); only ours in the pop.
- **Shape:** inside rounded (lamp, mug, cushions, window corners), outside rectilinear and hard (curves are preferred: Bar &
  Neta [CO §4, VERIFIED effect]). The threat is never cute, rounded or warm, and stays visible in the cozy frame. On the
  heaviest line, strip the comfort: stop the loops, drop the bed, no cute props near real harm.
- **Motion:** small slow loops inside (steam, breath, a page), steady weather outside, life passing through outside (3b.4);
  the camera locked or a slow 2D float (3b.5), never a zoom in a hold (the old "push 3-5 %, handheld <= 1 px" is retired for
  holds: too slow to see, so it read as a frozen frame); no flicker inside until the breach; one 0.5-1.0 s hold after the
  heaviest line ("ma" [CO §6, VERIFIED concept]), still with the camera's float or one mover, never a frozen frame.
- **Sound:** room tone + weather behind glass (low-passed rain, or wind for snow) 18-24 dB under the voice, ducked; soft close
  foley (mug down, page, lamp click); the breach opens the filter, then near-silence. Mix per playbook (-14 LUFS).

## 3b. Coziness v2: two measured references (2026-10-26)
A recipe for a FEELING: safe, held, unhurried, among people. Numbers are guides measured on the owner's references (C2 the
street from a balcony, C3 the riverside: "References" below), never a quota; judge at phone size, paused first, then playing,
sound off. [C2] / [C3] = our measurement [VERIFIED]; the 9:16 translations are proposals to test at G4. Wins over 2-3.

### 3b.1 Enclosure at any angle: enclosing mass : open field
- **E = enclosing mass : open field, in screen area.** Mass = what holds the place: walls, facades, a bank or kerb face,
  canopies, hedges, shelves, near frames, a lid overhead. Open field = the rest, in two kinds: **VOID** (sky, a horizon, any
  view to infinity) and **FLOOR** (ground, water, a table top seen in front of or below the viewer).
- **Cozy band: E 1:1.5-2.5:1 (mass 40-70 % of the frame), with the void inside its dose.** C1 the lane ~1:1.5 (walls ~40 % vs
  sky 27 % + cobbles ~33 %), C2 1:1.4 (masses 41 % vs floor 59 %), C3 ~1.8:1 (facades and the stone bank ~64 % vs sky 16 %,
  path 3 % and water 17 %). Exposed: E under ~1:2 or void >= 36 % (v3 G5 S10: a 0.04 H row of houses on a 0.6 H snow field,
  ~1:5). Shut: E over ~3:1 with the opening under ~10 % and no floor to look out over (a canyon, a closed room): a trap
  (principle 1). A nook sits at the top of the band (window 20-25 % + desk top), a balcony at the bottom.
- **H:W is the eye-level special case:** along a street at eye level, H:W sets both E and the void (`research/enclosure.md`
  §3 gives the sky share per H:W). Build with H:W at eye level; check E + void at any angle (high, level-across, interior).
- **VOID leaks enclosure; FLOOR is the prospect.** Void: 15-30 % at eye level (§2), 10-18 % as a jagged sky over a backdrop
  (C3), 0 % from above (C2). A floor may fill 50-60 % (C2 54-59 %) when it is bounded on 3 sides (masses, or the world running
  past the side edges), its far end hidden (dives under a mass, or a facing wall closes it), lighter than the masses (C2 +15-18
  L*) and it carries the life. A floor whose far end shows (a road to the horizon, open sea) counts as void.
- **Closed where it matters:** >= 40 % of the frame's perimeter is mass (C2 49 %, C3 ~40 %); the open part faces the viewer
  (bottom, lower sides) or is the sky; the far end is closed (a wall, a bend, a mouth under leaves): no vanishing point at all
  is the coziest vista.
- **The viewer is the refuge** when no protagonist is in frame: up behind leaves (BALCONY) or safe across a moat (RIVERSIDE),
  unseen; the life is the prospect, small and unthreatening; nobody addresses us.

**BALCONY [C2]: a high angle from a window over a street, square or courtyard.**
- From a 3rd-5th floor window or balcony, 35-55° down; no sky, no horizon; figures grow 1.4-2.5x from the far kerb to the
  near floor.
- **LID (y 0-600):** >= 90 % mass (canopy, facades, awnings) cut by the top edge, so it does the holding a paper edge would
  (never an edge at the top). One corner mass cut by two edges (12-18 % of the frame, 45-60 % across, 35-45 % down), 1-2
  smaller side masses at staggered heights (4-10 % each, <= 35 % in): a C-shaped cradle. One near repoussoir (a bush, a
  rail, a pot) overlaps the floor on one side: we look out from among leaves. 3-6 sagging wires stitch the sides, in y 0-300
  and 560-640, never across the caption lines. Behind the caption block the lid is ONE calm plane (a facade in one light
  fill with unlined windows, or the canopy in one flat leaf tone); its busy detail sits above y 290, below y 556 and in the
  gutters (x < 95, x > 945).
- **C-curve:** one leading curve (kerb, bank, shelf edge) enters one side at y 450-650 and leaves the other at y 860-1060,
  concave to the viewer; its far end dives under a mass through a mouth <= 8 % of that edge (<= 150 px), the only far exit.
  The traffic follows it.
- **LIFE (y 600-1250, the stage):** the far kerb with shopfronts, people, vehicles and the story object; with the lid it holds
  most of the detail.
- **FLOOR (y 1250-1920, under the platform UI):** >= 90 % one calm field in <= 2 tones, <= 6 guide marks echoing the curve,
  15-18 L* lighter than the masses; a passing near vehicle may cross it (dressing), key reads never.
- **People and vehicles:** 5-8 people 70-190 px (3.5-10 % H) in the life band, about half standing or sitting still;
  vehicles rounded and toy-like, the largest <= 2/3 W and <= 10 % of the frame (the one "event"); movers enter and leave only
  through the open sides or the mouth, never across the lid.

**RIVERSIDE [C3]: eye level across water (a canal, a river, a pond, a square seen across a fountain).**
- Level, long lens: the eye line on the far path's standing heads; verticals vertical; every plane shows a little of its top
  (roofs, stair treads); depth by overlap and stacking upward, no vanishing lines.
- **BACKDROP (the facade wall):** full width, 5-7 overlapping planes, 7-10 buildings across (a new one every 0.10-0.15 W), no
  base visible, each farther row higher and ~10-15 L* lighter; a jagged skyline (amplitude >= 0.12 H, never one straight
  roofline) with sky 10-18 %, the lightest and flattest field (L* ~90). The light, low-detail back row and the sky notches are
  the caption field (y 317-556); the busy facade belt sits in y 560-1000.
- **Value sandwich:** the darkest mass (a hedge, trees, a bush, L* 20-38) right behind the seam, so light figures pop; the
  path where people sit is the most neutral ground (C <= 15); the bank and the water below are light again.
- **The moat:** the lane (seam) at y 1115-1230, above the UI; a stone bank under it (y 1230-1420, dabs, part of the mass), then
  the water (y 1420-1920, ~26 %): calm, patterned, still. We watch from across it, safe and unseen.
- **Sides and stitches:** every band runs off both side edges (>= 5 cut elements per side); asymmetric wings (a soft dark
  tree mass ~0.3 W on one side, a hard building column on the other); the top edge sky and wires only, the bottom edge water
  only; >= 3 stitches cross the bands (a tall tree, poles, a stair from the seam into the wall, sagging wires).
- **Life in ONE lane:** 5-8 people on the seam, about half sitting still on its edge (a cluster of 3 at 1.2-1.5 body heights
  apart + a loner), backs to the wall, facing the water in side or 3/4 view, featureless at this size (a head in the hair
  colour: LIFE_AND_DENSITY §4's no-faces rule holds); 1-4 movers cross parallel to the bands, both ways; one far, slow,
  small mover higher up (a stair, an upper walk, ~60 px). Seated ~85 px (0.045 H), standing ~140 px (0.074 H); wall :
  standing figure 6-9:1 (>= 10:1 is scale); a storey ~1 standing figure (compressed depth).

### 3b.3 The detail gradient: busy edges and far side, calm centre or floor
- The far side (lid, far kerb, facade belt) and the frame edges hold 3-6x the linework of the calm field: C2's top 35 % holds
  56 % of all linework (lid cells 3-6x the floor's); C3's facade belt has 66 % detail tiles while sky and water stay low in
  contrast (local colour std 10-22 vs 39-46 in the busy belts).
- Calm sits only where the eye can rest: one band touching the top or bottom edge (sky, water, floor), the subject's ring,
  the caption plane. Never a slab mid-frame (C3's middle 60 %: 2-3 % flat tiles).
- Calm is never empty: patterned (<= 6 lane marks, ripple dashes, two long soft tones), and no hole bigger than the caption
  band anywhere else (`LIFE_AND_DENSITY.md` §0).
- Rhythm in tiny things: kerb stripes, rails, wires, windows, blossoms, each 1-6 % W; the frame's strongest contrast goes to
  one tiny rhythmic element (C2's kerb stripes, 3 % W period), never to a big shape.
- Lived-in infrastructure is the cheapest fullness: thin lines on 3-4 % of the frame (~3 px: 5-9 sagging wires, poles with
  crossarms, antennas, railings, mullions); per exterior frame >= 3 rooftop items, >= 2 street items (pole, lamp, meter box),
  balconies or railings on every other facade, 1-2 signs, a reflection in one glass face [C3].

### 3b.4 Life in a cozy beat: it passes through
- **Sideways, along the place:** movers cross parallel to the picture plane or along the C-curve; they enter from an edge or
  the mouth already at cruising speed, keep it, and leave by an edge or behind a mass; no stops, starts or easing on screen.
  Nothing comes AT the viewer (no mover grows toward the lens or heads for the frame centre); from a high angle a mover may
  pass below us out of the bottom edge (C2's car).
- **Real-world speeds, in body heights per second** (lengths for vehicles), the same at every depth, so far figures are slower
  in pixels (C3's far walker 1.1 bh/s like the near one): walk 0.7-1.1 bh/s (C3 0.95; C2 0.64 in high-angle foreshortening),
  strolling bike 1.0-1.5, bike 2.5-3, town car or rickshaw 1.2-1.8 lengths/s, bus ~0.4 lengths/s. Screen ceiling ~30-37 %
  W/s (~400 px/s); each crossing takes 2.5-7 s, none under ~2 s.
- **1-5 movers at once, never zero** (C2 mean 3.2, min 1, max 5; C3 2-5, no frame without one): a small far mover is the
  heartbeat; the next enters 0.3-0.5 s before the last leaves; arrivals 0.4-1.3 s apart; a crossing or an occlusion every 2-3 s.
- **Steady change:** ~1 % of the pixels change per frame (C3 0.85 %, CV 0.14) and movers cover 1-15 % of the frame (median
  ~3 %), either constant (C3) or one slow swell per 6-7 s (C2: one tiny mover, then a peak of 4-5 with the one big vehicle,
  then release). No bursts: bursts belong to the hero beats.
- **Rest next to motion:** about half the people are at rest (sitting on an edge, leaning, chatting, waiting at a kerb)
  right beside the moving ones; stillness among motion reads as leisure. They hold still (at most a 1-2 px breath).
- **Motion lives in one band:** the lane, kerb or road (C3 ~10 % of the frame; C2 the road, 27 % of pixels ever move); the
  lid, the caption plane, the backdrop and the water have no motion of their own; a near lane plus one far slow figure give
  depth without parallax.
- **Ambient stays under the movers:** neither reference sways, shimmers or boils. Allowed: one very slow light shift (C2: sun
  warming a canopy over ~2 s), a window lighting somewhere, steady slow weather; always lower in contrast and speed than the
  movers, never in the caption plane.
- **Cut or loop when the lane is nearly empty** (a mover leaving by an edge), so the jump reads soft; a loop brings every mover
  off-screen or back to its start state.

### 3b.5 The camera for cozy holds: locked, or a slow 2D float; never a zoom
- **Locked** (C2: <= 0.1 px over 7.8 s) when the life crosses a floor: the still observer at a window.
- **2D float** (C3) when the life is one lane: translation only, 8-15 px/s at 1080 (0.7-1.4 % W/s), peak <= 25 px/s, in
  straight legs with a steady heading, turning (eased) every 0.6-1.5 s; 15-25 px of travel per 4 s; the whole set moves as one
  card (no parallax needed, no rotation, no zoom); >= 40 px of overscan on each side; sub-pixel transforms (at 10 px/s a
  rounded one steps 1 px every 6 frames at 60 fps). Captions, the paper edge and the UI stay fixed in screen space: they are
  the fixed mat the picture slides under. `CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md` rule 3's "breathes" means this float on a cozy wide.
- **Never a zoom in a cozy hold.** v3 G5's centred 1.4 % push-in (10 of 14 shots) moved ~2 px/s at the frame edge, under the
  change threshold: it read as frozen (static runs up to 0.8 s), and a push leans in at the viewer. The float is ~5x faster,
  the same speed everywhere, visible but calm. A zoom stays a story move: visible (>= 3 % in <= 2 s), on a line (K5, K9, the
  one big move, the frame-0 push on the hook word). No handheld jitter in a cozy hold (C2 0.07 px): the float is the breath.

### 3b.6 Colour and light (the references' structure at PALETTE's levels)
- **One calm field:** the largest field is one hue in <= 2 tones (dL* <= 6) laid as long soft shapes along the perspective
  (C2's road: 56 % of the frame in two tones dL* 6), lighter than the masses around it; at our levels: L* >= 75, C <= 20 for a
  floor or sky, C 20-35 for a cool chromatic field (water, a dusk sky). The references' saturation (C* median 33) is louder
  than our DNA: we copy the structure, never the level.
- **A warm accent where the eye rests, as the field's near-complement** (135-175° apart): C2 17-25 % warm on the lid and edges
  against a cool road; C3 a warm facade block between a cool sky and a cool river. Our warm palettes keep the structure: a
  warm made world held by one cool chromatic field (sky, water, the dusk window, C 20-35, never grey); >= 10 % of the opposite
  temperature in every frame (v3 G5 had 0 % cool at 17.4 s); a yellow-green bridge (~15 %, toward yellow, never blue-green).
- **Saturation as confetti, not fields:** 20-50 small patches each < 1 % of the frame (awnings, balcony panels, signs,
  clothes, a reflection) in 8+ hue families (v3 G5: 2-4), at C 25-45 in hues other than the hero's and the pop's, each far
  smaller than the hero, so the hero and the one pop (C >= 50) stay the loudest (colour jobs, PALETTE).
  PALETTE's "background 50-80 %" is met by many light soft fills together (each facade, the sky, the floor its own tint),
  never by one empty slab: the biggest single flat region <= 20 % of the frame except the one calm field (C3 15 %, v3 G5 23-45 %).
- **Good-weather light, one sun:** lit tones lean yellow-gold; lit : shade on buildings ~3:1 (C3); shadows short (<= 3-4 % W
  past the object, C2) and soft in value and edge: flat, or a 2-step band with a lighter 15-25 px penumbra (C2's soft edge,
  25 px at 1080), never a blur or a gradient.
- **Shadows are colour, never grey:** dL* -25 to -40, chroma x0.6-0.7, hue turned away from yellow: 10-15° toward blue on a
  cool surface (C2), toward plum on warm walls (C3: orange to plum), a deep red-brown under eaves (L* ~29); cast shadows on
  the ground take the darker tone of the cool field. On a warm palette: red-brown and plum, never grey or olive.
- **Tinted extremes, both rare:** whites carry the scene's tint (C 7-15: a cream on a warm set, pale cyan on water); pure
  white only as glints (<= 5 % of one object); darks warm and rare (L* < 25 on <= 6 % of the frame) in the palette's anchor
  (red-brown, aubergine). The references' olive darks and lines are NOT copied (playbook: "Warm, never sickly").
- **Value framing by content:** the perimeter 5-8 L* darker than the centre through the masses (C2), or the light-dark-light
  sandwich (C3); never a vignette gradient, never a dark band in front of a cozy backdrop.

### 3b.7 Texture and edges (VERY subtle, playbook)
- **Texture goes inverse to area.** Large fields stay flat in fill (C2's road: high-pass 1.5-2 L*); their "paint" is a few
  long tone-on-tone drawn shapes (dL 3-8): lane marks; ripple dashes on water 15-55 px long, 6-8 px thick, ~10-15 per
  100 x 100 px (C3); a few dry vertical streaks on the biggest wall; stone dabs 25-80 px a value step darker. Small organic
  things carry the tactile texture at 3-4x the field's (foliage hatching; blossom daubs 23-45 px with a slightly darker rim,
  no outline; a rug; wood); the optional halftone (<= 6 %, PALETTE) goes only there. Nothing on people, props or captions.
- **Locked to the world, never boiling** (both references: no boil, no crawl you can see).
- **Must read full with texture OFF:** C3 flattened to 24 flat colours still reads full and cozy (flat 18 %, detail 37 %);
  texture is worth the last ~5-15 points of flat share, never the fix for an empty set.
- **Lines:** no outlines; thin warm-dark lines (~2.5-3 px at 1080) only in key places (poles, wires, tyres, rails,
  mullions), 1-4 % of the pixels; light lines too (railings, lane marks).
- **Paper edges:** both references edge all four sides; we never put one at the top or beside the caption band. If used:
  the bottom and the sides from y >= 640 (fading in), inset 12-40 px, ragged (peak-to-peak 20-45 px), a dry-brush zone of
  10-40 px, the surround a dark warm tint (anchor family, L* 15-35), never grey, white paper or a photo. At the top, the lid
  or a wall cut by the top edge does the holding job.

### 3b.8 Check a cozy shot (phone size, sound off)
1. Paused: is it full? No hole bigger than the caption band, many small things, one calm field at an edge (LIFE §0).
2. E in 1:1.5-2.5:1 with the void in its dose; a floor bounded, its far end hidden; >= 40 % of the perimeter closed, open toward us.
3. Detail gradient: busy far side and edges, calm floor or centre; the captions on one calm plane.
4. Playing: >= 1 moving figure in every frame (aim 2-4), steady change, half the people at rest, nothing coming at us, the
   camera locked or floating (the cozy check in `LIFE_AND_DENSITY.md` §9).
5. Colour: one calm field, warm world and cool frame, confetti not fields, short coloured shadows, tinted whites, rare warm darks.

## 4. Scale shot grammar (Arcane-inspired; framing [UNCERTAIN] until we measure the scenes in section 8)
**Scale must feel GREAT (owner, 2026-10-26).** A scale wide (any K wide, the S1/S5 landings) is judged by whether it feels
vast and alive, never by an object count: run the 11 cues and the checklist in `CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md` "Scale cues: why a wide
feels vast" (a world that keeps going past every edge, a size gradient, a huge-yet-far landmark, tiny anchors, the drop, a
big sky with huge slow clouds, life in the hold). Where the planes, haze or life numbers below differ, that section wins.

**Shared rules.** *Haze:* 4-6 flat planes (5-7 on a city vista), a value step darker per plane toward the viewer (one still,
A7: 12 / 25 / 32 / 36 on a 0-255 scale, ~5 / 10 / 13 / 14 L* [AR §3, our measure]; on a vista the mix toward the sky doubles
per plane going AWAY, 0 / 12 / 22 / 40 / 65 %, #55 measured: Scale cues 5); far planes fill-only, then key-place ink
1.5-2 -> 2-3 -> 3-4 px, tapered, no outlines (PALETTE). Dusk ladder: sky `#D9DEE5` (L* 88) -> `#BAC3CE` (78) -> `#8A9AA8` (63)
-> `#545F6D` (40) -> anchor `#1F2433` (14) on the observer; L* <= 40 is anchor-dark, so those two stay ~5 % of a held frame
and big foregrounds are `#8A9AA8` with a `#545F6D` lip (PALETTE). *Parallax* f = 1 - depth: sky 0, far 0.1, city 0.3, near
0.55, subject 1, foreground frame 1.3-1.5; neighbours >= 1.8x apart (the frame separates by value). *Speed:* peak <= 15
px/frame (cap 18; `scripts/camspeed.py`), so with `CAM.reveal` >= 1 s per 315 px of the fastest layer's travel: tilt / crane
<= 940 px in 3 s, dolly-out <= 2.3x in 3 s centred, <= 1.7x anchored low (at the cap 1,130 px, 2.8x, 1.9x) [AU §4, VERIFIED
arithmetic]. *Physical camera:* crane, dolly, truck, tilt, no spins; ease in, land over the last 35 %, drift 1-1.5 px, cut
while it drifts (Fortiche treated the virtual camera as a physical one [REPORTED]).

## 5. Vertical short-form rules
- **Stage:** key reads in x 120-880, y 290-1250, minus the right rail (x > 780 from y 840); the bottom 35 % and the rail are
  platform UI (ad templates [VE §2, REPORTED]). Masses may run under the UI; figures, labels, the pop and contact points never.
- **Stack the depth vertically:** captions on a flat field at L* >= 70 (7:1 with the ink; `#8A9AA8` is 5.9:1) in y 290-600,
  the read in y 600-1250, a foreground mass cut by the bottom; <= 2 points of interest per scale shot, x 380-700 [VE §3].
- **Observer** 60-150 px (floor 40), fill-only below 80 px, the stage's only high-contrast mark. **Horizon** in eye-height
  scale shots 0.52-0.59 H (top edge >= 45° up, feet above y 1250); a 0.3 m camera allows 0.64 H [VE §1]; a city vista from
  a roof (S1/S5 landings) 0.43-0.46 H, feet 2-3 figure heights below it (`CINEMATIC_GUIDE.md` Scale cues 6).
- **Time and captions:** payoff words on frame 0, never a silent scale opener; nothing scale before 3 s unless the move IS the
  claim; captions pop in place in screen space (never on a camera layer or clipped by a wiper), a change at least every 1.5 s,
  also through moves; still peak frames 1.5-3 s; target 30-45 s (playbook).
- **Safety:** never > 3 flashes in any 1 s, at any size (Ofcom's 25 %-of-screen allowance assumes a TV at >= 2 m; WCAG's
  0.006 sr is ~9 % of our frame at 34 cm [W3C VERIFIED; Ofcom REPORTED, via ASA]); no rapid zoom in-out; captions never shrink.
- **Respect:** camera at or below the person's eye level (K10 is a crowd, never one person); no glow, rim or hero colour on
  the thing under critique (low angles flatter [VE §7, REPORTED]); anonymous back-view figures, never real staff's likeness.
