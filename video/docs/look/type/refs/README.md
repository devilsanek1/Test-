# The owner's type references (2026-10-30), measured

**The owner:** "One aspect I want you to evaluate and research is the fonts and the text caption positioning I'll give
you a few references / We previously would have the text to be pretty static but there is so much more we can do to
make it look premium, more interesting, dynamic and more engaging / Research —-> distill it in the guidebook and
recipes to use —-> give me still examples". ("A few references": more may follow; add each here, measured.) The
creators aren't named; we learn the principles, never copy their art or lettering.

## Ref 1: a text-only reel over a drawn cat (screen recording, 9.0 s, 60 fps; Instagram's UI burned in)
Files: `ref1_cat_reel_groups.jpg` (one frame per word group, the app's bars cropped), `ref1_lettering_*.png` (full-res
crops of the lettering, for matching fonts). Music only, no voice (Whisper hears no speech): the words ARE the line.

| | measured |
|---|---|
| The picture | a grainy, fluffy black cat (textured, hand-made) low in the frame against ONE calm wall: flat muted blue-grey (rgb ~105,128,157) with a fine painted mottle; a pale far roof shape; the cat blinks. The open wall above the subject is the stage for the words |
| The line | "Sometimes / we just / have to / accept / whatever / is going / on / in / our day": 9 groups in ~8 s, **about 0.9 s each** (0.5-1.5 s), **one or two words per group**, lowercase except the first word |
| The lettering | **thin, monoline, rounded hand-print** (a felt-tip with round ends; print, not cursive), off-white (rgb ~224,226,233); stroke ~7-9 px in our 1080-px frame = **~6-8 % of the cap height** (light; our Patrick Hand at 98 px is ~14 %); **wide letter spacing** (open, airy); contrast only ~3.1:1 on the wall, carried by the size |
| Size | "Sometimes": ascender-to-baseline ~118 px in a 1080-px-wide frame (~2x our caption's cap height), 54 % of the frame width; a one-word group fills 25-55 % of the width |
| Position | **in the open wall above the subject's head: 28-41 % of the screen height**, never at the bottom; the group's centre moves per group (31-54 % of the width), always clear of the right-rail buttons |
| Layout | a two-word group is **staggered on two baselines like hand lettering**: "we" small and up-left, "just" lower-right; "have" then "to" lower-right; "our" then "**day**" lower-right and **bigger** (the landing word of the line); one-word groups centred |
| Motion | each group **cuts in whole, in place** (no fade, no slide, no scale), or **word by word** ("is" -> "is going"; "our" -> "our day"); the first word of a group can **leave first** ("we just" -> "just" -> "have to"); sometimes one empty frame between groups; nothing moves while it's on screen |
| Why it feels premium | the words look written by the same hand that drew the picture; few words, big, light and airy; the open space is part of the composition; calm timing |

## Ref 2: a "sketched animated stories" reel's title card (screenshot)
Files: `ref2_scratchy_title.jpg`, `ref2_scratchy_lettering.png`. "Scratchy" in a white **dry-brush marker script**
(tall, narrow, connected, rough brush edges, a long "y" tail), the line under it, "Sketched animated stories", in a

photo, white on the darker tree mass, no box, no visible shadow. A series/title lockup: script + print.

## What the two refs share with the posters (the first read)
Light strokes, generous letter spacing, few words, big; set in open space (wall, sky) as part of the composition;
no boxes, outlines or heavy shadows; one hand for picture and words.
