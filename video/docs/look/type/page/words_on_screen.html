The six picks, made

You asked me to choose for engagement, pleasantness and attention retention. The recommendation under each question is now the rule in our guide and layout docs.

1Which hand writes every spoken word?

A. Gaegu Light (top): warm and hand-made, closest to your cat reel.B. Mali Light (middle): cleaner and rounder.Zen Kurenaido (bottom) is out: its "AI" reads as "Al".

Recommended: A.

2How much do we lift the word the voice leans on?

Recommended: both. A for the few landing words and numbers, B for everyday stress.

3On light skies, a soft warm halo behind the stressed word, or none?

C. The halo: the word glows softly, as if the dusk light caught it.B. No halo: the same bold plum word, plain.On night skies the word is always lit (honey with a glow), like the windows.

Recommended: C, unless it reads as a smudge to you.

4Where do the words sit?

A. Follow the open space by the subject: above it by default, beside it in close shots, moving a little between groups (four moments of one shot above).B. One fixed band, only the line stagger varies.

Recommended: A.

5Which cover title?

Recommended: A, the same hand as the film so the grid reads as one series.

6Is the size contrast enough?

Steps: everyday 116 px, hook and payoff 136 px, a stressed word about 160 px, a big number 250 px.A. Keep it. B. Bigger everyday words (fewer words per line, more two-line groups).

Recommended: A. It's your call by eye.
