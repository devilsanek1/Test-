"Even the FBI said if you can't update yet, just turn it off, which is basically tech support's answer to everything."
(w144-w164). Film 45.618-52.163 s (6.55 s, 197 frames): cut(anymore) -> cut(everything). Day, baseline scale tier, hard

## Render cost

Full 1080 x 1920, 24 frames spread over the scene: **167 ms / frame** incl. page setup at concurrency 2 (machine load
~4.5); the 0.5x QA render: **49-55 ms / frame** at concurrency 2 (197 frames in 9.6-10.9 s). The two onlookers and the
lamp add ~5 %; the lights-out cascade doubles the set on 2 frames only. Savings kept: the set's citizens and edge
onlookers off (under the UI / off-frame at zoom 1.6).
