## Shims / kit status (`scene11/shims.tsx`)

- **S11Padlock + S11Tag = stand-ins** for the set's govB rigs. GovernmentPlaza's GovB draws the sill `<Ink>` AFTER
  `<WindowRig id="govB">` (which carries TagRig + PadlockRig), so the two sill strokes ran through the padlock and
  struck through 'back' on the tag. S11 now passes no padlockAt / tagAt and draws both after the set: the padlock with an
  OCHRE body and a 3 px misregistered fill (the sand body read hollow on the darkened facade), a cream keyhole plate,
  size 34 (~57 px); the tag in CREAM (label style), size 48 (lettering ~27 px), hung from a nail 20 world above the rail
  so its lower edge stays at screen y <= 1500 at the 1.71 lean. Swap back to `gov.govB.padlockAt / tagAt / tagProps`
  once the sets lane moves the sill ink under the rigs AND the props lane offers the cream fill / ochre body (or S01 /
  S12 adopt the same look).
- **NoteBreeze** (flutter on the adhesive edge + the pen jolt) and **NoteFlight** (the ride in across the right edge)
  wrap the shared StickyNote, which is used with `flutter={false}`: its own flutter pops the note in full size mid-air
  and peaks at ~37 px/frame at this zoom. Drop them if StickyNote gains `flutterAt` / `joltAt` / an edge-entry flight.
- **Sparkle**: a cream 4-point sparkle for the padlock glint (ShutterPadlock's glintAt only lengthens a 3 px stroke).
- `actors.tsx` `crtContent` mirrors the set's (unexported) crtContent: S11's `windowSlot` overrides every window, so the
  set's CRT power-off for govA / govC has to be drawn here from the exported `crtPhase`.
