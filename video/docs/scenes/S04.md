## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Passers-by: complete line-art people on the road at the left row's base**, not "ink-only ghosts on the pavement at
  screen x 600-900": in this framing that spot is the hero facade; the only ground right of the stoop is the road edge.
- **Pip's yawn on w48 'was'**, not w46+0.12: on 'bug' it was the brightest, biggest event right beside the lock.
- **The 'bug' spot** (peach disc + ink ring) is S04's own mark for the most stressed word (the storyboard has only Bit's
  hop, which is 24 px on a phone).
- **Sun through the dive:** it stays in the sky slot (the storyboard's "it leaves the frame with the dive" by occlusion:
  the receding row slides up over it); S05's notch sun appears on the cut.
- **The dive's caption band:** one flatten at the dive's first frame (all of the set above world 990), the browser bar
  repainted teal (not tealDark) so the captions stay >= 3:1, its page one cream field and the blinds sand (inside the band
  peach is only the caption highlight); the pigeons take off and the wire snaps on that frame (motivated, not a vanish).
- **Sleep on w51-0.05** (storyboard w51+0.10: it trailed the word; rule-5 lead); drowsy 0.24 s after the yawn.
- **Leaf:** tan, starts at tear 7 + 0.035 = w53+0.12 (storyboard w53+0.20) from the door's left-frame ivy, one frame after
  the vine turns tan; the vine goes bare while it falls (season order tan, bare, green, tan, bare); it lands 0.3 s
  before the dive.
- **Calendar:** S04's own `YearCalendar` (95 x 105 world; a year page = a tan title band with an illegible scribbled
  'year' (no numerals: a ticking 2012 -> 2026 needs the storyboard owner's OK) over twelve month blocks, a dog-eared
  bottom-right corner, the next pages peeking out under it, a torn-stub fringe at the binding once pages are torn), not
  the kit `Calendar` (its year page is one grid, 70 x 80).
- **Near tree** shifted 70 world right (only in S04).
- **Cobweb:** props `Cobweb` r20 world on the box's top-left corner; **Ivy:** S04's own `SeasonIvy`; **Lock4** drawn by
  the scene (`heroLock={false}`) so it can rattle.
