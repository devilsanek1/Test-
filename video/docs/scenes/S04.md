## Camera

`screen = (world - (x, y)) x zoom + (540, 960)`. Creep 0 -> 2.055: zoom 1.50 -> 1.55 (in-out sine, log zoom) ABOUT THE
HERO CORNICE (camera y solved per frame so world 640 holds at screen 570); lock4 drifts 1305 -> 1330. Dive 2.055 -> 2.42
(`DIVE` 0.365 s, from w54 'years' to the cut):
- log zoom 1.55 -> 1.90 on a cubic Hermite with zero start slope and end slope 1.5 (~0.84 /s at the cut);
- lock4's SCREEN position travels (540, 1330) -> (483, 960) on a TRAPEZOID velocity profile over the first 85 % of the
  dive (`TRAVEL`), accelerating over its first 42 % and decelerating over its last 42 % (`ACC`; A1 fix round: was 30 %,
  the whole acceleration in ~3 frames), so the last 15 % is a pure zoom about the lock.

Measured (`scripts/camspeed.py` on `S04seam` f232-f255, A1 fix round), camera px / frame:
0.03 | 13 -> 37 -> 60 -> 81 -> 91 -> 89 -> 73 -> 56 -> 39 -> 29 | cut | S05 28 -> 19 -> 13 -> 9 -> 7. The onset ramps over
five frames (dv 13, 24, 23, 21, 10; was 1.7 -> 23 -> 50 -> 72, dv up to 27), peak 91 (was 81), 29 into the cut and 28
out of it (velocity-continuous). With the dive allow-listed (`--allow=7.80-8.40`): OK, no strobing and no velocity step
outside the window.

The SUN is screen space for the whole scene: `hops` (S03's glide-hop), then `moves` (a 7 px sink after the touch-down,
then the dive slide (930, 697) -> (930, 725)). Z-order: in front of the set until the sweep (0.18-0.28 s), then in the
set's sky slot (behind the towers, the rows' rooflines and the wire), clipped to the sky above the left row's roofline

vertical edge at screen x 826 -> 1048, so on f6 the disc was a D with a straight vertical side). In the dive the

## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Passers-by: complete line-art people on the road at the left row's base**, not "ink-only ghosts on the pavement at
  screen x 600-900": in this framing that spot is the hero facade; the only ground right of the stoop is the road edge.
- **Pip's yawn on w48 'was'**, not w46+0.12: on 'bug' it was the brightest, biggest event right beside the lock.
- **The 'bug' spot** (peach disc + ink ring) is S04's own mark for the most stressed word.
- **Sun through the dive:** it stays in the sky slot (the receding row slides up over it); S05's notch sun appears on
  the cut.
- **The dive's caption band:** treated row by row (tan as each row reaches the band, fill-only inside it), the browser
  bar repainted teal (not tealDark: >= 3:1 under ink lettering) and flat in the band; the pigeons take off and the wire
  snaps on the dive's first frame.
- **Sleep on w51-0.05** (storyboard w51+0.10); drowsy 0.24 s after the yawn.
- **Leaf:** tan, starts at tear 7 + 0.035 = w53+0.12 (storyboard w53+0.20), one frame after the vine turns tan; the
  vine goes bare while it falls (with four more leaves dropping); it lands 0.3 s before the dive.
- **Calendar:** S04's own `YearCalendar` (95 x 105 world; tan title band with an illegible scribbled 'year', no
  numerals; twelve month blocks; dog-ear; page stack; torn-stub fringe), not the kit `Calendar`.
- **Website ages** (A1 fix round, not in the storyboard): the hero site is redesigned seven times through the
  time-lapse (fill-only, never peach) while the lock never changes; it carries "fourteen years" in the biggest object in
  the shot.
- **Near tree** shifted 70 world right, **planter** 42 world right (only in S04).
- **Cobweb:** S04's own `HairWeb` (four radials, two arcs, outside the box corner), not the props `Cobweb` (its fan into
  the box corner covered the keyhole at any readable size). **Ivy:** S04's own `SeasonIvy`. **Lock4** drawn by the scene
  (`heroLock={false}`) so it can rattle.
