## Camera

`screen = (world - (x, y)) x zoom + (540, 960)`. Creep (330, 900, 1.50) -> (330, 898, 1.55), in-out sine, log zoom,
0 -> 2.09 (cornice >= ~560). Dive 2.09 -> 2.42 (fixed 0.33 s ending on the cut): log zoom 1.55 -> 1.90 in(quad) (rate
1.23 /s at the cut, S05 lands at ~1.05 /s), and lock4's SCREEN position travels (540, 1320) -> (483, 960) with an
in-out sine, so the lock arrives at its S05 spot at rest while the zoom is still accelerating (S05's landing keeps
zooming about the screen centre with the lock nearly still). The creep ends and the dive starts at zero velocity.
`scripts/camspeed.py S04 --allow=2.08-2.42`: no strobing and no velocity step outside the dive.

The SUN is screen space (pinned, no idle drift: it is the match object) until the dive starts; from then on it stays
with the world and leaves with the dive (screen ~(960, 190) r~135 on S04's last frame); S05's sun is already in its notch.

## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Ghost passers-by are LINE-ART people (white paper + ink, kit `Person style="line"`), not ink-only transparent
  outlines.** The props `Ghost` (no fills) reads as scribbled arches at film size over the facade; white line-art
  figures are reference 1's own way to draw people as "ink only" (f_9.0's readers) and read instantly as transient
  passers-by in a coloured world. Backs to us, walking away up the street; eye-level heights from the street's camera.
  The flicker ones keep right of the L5 lettering once it is up (feet x 860-960 on screen).
- **The dive's caption band:** the storyboard flattens the hero browser chrome to one tealDark field. Ink captions on
  tealDark are ~2.2:1 (capcheck fails), so instead, during the dive and ONLY inside the caption band (a world clip at
  the camera's screen y 588), the upper hero facade is redrawn flat (no ink: sand wall, tan cornice and shade, flat
  windows, the browser as one cream field): a smear-frame simplification for the last ~6 frames. framecheck: no caption
  error (2 / 5 % warnings on f66 / f68).
- **Cobweb:** the Lock's own `cobwebAt` web is ~15 world px at lock4's size (unreadable at 1.5x); S04 draws the props
  `Cobweb` glyph r20 world (30 px) on the lock box's top-left corner.
- **Ivy:** the set's ivy is sage only and has no seasons; S04 draws its own `SeasonIvy` (vine + two two-tone leaves per
  node, season palettes, bare = vine and twig ticks) on the same spot (x ~215-235 from step 2's tread up the frame, then
  over the lintel). It starts 8 nodes tall so it peeks above Pip at the cut.
- **Extra time-lapse life (not in the storyboard, subtle):** the hero house's windows change on every other tear
  (`WindowLife`), and the near tree takes the ivy's season keys (a green tree beside a cycling ivy would contradict it).
  The lock box glints once on 'bug' (focuses the eye on the lock while Bit hops; also lifts the 'This'-'was' gap).
- **Calendar:** the props Calendar shows only one flying page at a time with overlapping tears, so it swaps pages
  instantly (`tearDur` 0.001) and S04's `YearFlyers` flies each torn page off (0.36 s life, several in flight).

## Shims used

`wrap` for the people layer), props `Calendar` + `yearPages`, `LabelTag kind="tag"` (box + hang), `Cobweb`, `Bit`
