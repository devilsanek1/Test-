## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Passers-by: complete line-art people on the road at the left row's base**, not "ink-only ghosts on the pavement at
  screen x 600-900, y 1300-1470": in this framing that spot is the hero facade / plinth and the left row's wall; the
  only ground right of the stoop is the road's edge (x ~825-990). Lens ratio 1.45 keeps them 250-280 px and their
  heads under the near tree's crown. The time-lapse flickers are far up the road, half size (in the right rail: they
  are background).
- **Sun through the dive:** the storyboard says "it leaves the frame with the dive; S05's sun is already in its notch".
  Following review it is screen-anchored and slides into S05's notch instead (a second match anchor; it no longer
  crosses the caption band). It is drawn in front of the facade for those 10 frames.
- **The dive's caption band:** the storyboard flattens the browser chrome to one tealDark field (ink captions on
  tealDark are ~2.2:1); S04 pops every band object to its flat no-ink version and the browser to the wall colour.
- **Cobweb:** props `Cobweb` r20 world on the box's top-left corner (the Lock's own `cobwebAt` web is ~15 world px,
  unreadable), at w53 (the storyboard's w53+0.10 with the rule-5 lead), with the 'fourteen years' highlight.
- **Ivy:** S04's own `SeasonIvy` (the set's ivy is sage only, no seasons), node by node (fills then that node's ink).
  The near tree no longer cycles seasons (a 7.5 Hz full-area flicker in the rail): the ivy alone is the season cue.
- **Lock4** is drawn by the scene (`heroLock={false}` + the same Lock props/seed in `heroSlot`) so it can rattle.
- **Leaf:** orange (sun accent), ~30 px, from the lintel ivy across the door glass, lands 0.14 s before the dive.
- **Ground:** no 'kerb' foreground (its cropped bollards / bike wheel read as fragments at this zoom); kerb-stone joints
  along the near kerb and a drain grate instead.
- **Calendar:** props Calendar swaps pages instantly (`tearDur` 0.001) and `YearFlyers` flies each torn page off.
