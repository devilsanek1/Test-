## Deviations from the storyboard (and why)

- **Multiplane factors** (`layout.ts F`): rows 0-1 + dome 0.02, row 2 0.25, row 3 0.62 (storyboard 0.15 / 0.30 / 0.55 /
  0.80). With the storyboard's factors rows 1-3 sit over the sun on the crane's first frame (camera (540,1260) zoom 1.3), so
  the S07 match ("half behind the skyline") is lost. With these, the far rooftops line up on the skyline at the cut, row 3
  is still behind our parapet and the crane opens the city row by row. Rows 0-1, the dome and its glass share one depth so
  the set's single glass clip stays registered on the dome through every move.
- **Board** 430-890 x **1046**-1436 (storyboard 1080-1440): the storyboard's label sizes (38 / 30 / 28 / 34 px on screen)
  do not fit its own zones; with 1046 they do (header 36, note 27, stamps 31, footer 26 world px; x1.15 during the push).
  Clipped above our coping (it stands behind it), so the set's chimney stays behind the board.
- **Pip** on the coping at (915, coping), not DOME.pip (500, 1448): at x 500 he would sit in front of the board's footer
  (L9b) and be hidden by the board's rise.
- **Stamp ring pops off** ('minutes' +0.25): the shared LabelTag stamp swaps its slam ring for the print; S08 adds a short
  impact-tick ring at the landing instead.
- **Horizon band**: the set's peach band does not relight; it stays peach (the last light) through the dusk steps and snaps
  to a light sage on 'day' (an overlay in the sky layer + a glassed copy inside the dome).
