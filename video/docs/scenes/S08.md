## Deviations from the storyboard (and why)

- **Dome + watchtower moved** (fix round 1): the storyboard's dome (180-600) and tower (x 600-650) ended exactly on the
  board's top edge (tangent at rest and during the push) and the board hid the dome's right half. The dome is now
  `DOME8` (75-415, apex y 835: ~1/5 of the city, whole arc visible, >= 20 px of air left of the board at rest and at the
  1.15 push); the tower stands INSIDE the glass by the right flank (outside it would stand in the board), so it is no
  longer silhouetted against the sun, and the sun no longer tucks behind the dome's flank. The beam sweeps across the
  inside of the glass and stops at the far side; no clamp is needed (it never leaves the dome, screen y >= 835).
- **Push target x 530** (storyboard 600): keeps the whole board left of the right rail at 1.15 (screen 437-943) with the
  dome clear on its left.
- **Board 440-880** (storyboard 430-890 x 1080-1440) x 1046-1436: sizes header 38, L9a 28, stamps 34, footer 28 world px
  (= 44 / 32 / 39 / 32 on screen at the push); footer 10 px under its divider, 8 px above the frame (`footerH` 70).
- **Pip** at (390, coping), LEFT of the board (it was 915: on the board's corner and in the right rail); he hops in from
  the left. He covers set door x 370: its rattler hops to a new door at x 330 on 'attempts'.
- **The flood stays OUTSIDE the dome** (storyboard: inside and outside): the dome district reads as the one protected
  island in a flooded city, and the counted attempters stay "seen, never getting in". Ink-dark, not tealDark: the
  storyboard's tealDark specks had the value of the facades.
- **Stamps land ON their words** (storyboard: ink ring at w-0.08, print landing ~0.3 s later): the print appears at
  w-0.20 and lands at w+0.00 ('minutes' 32.095, 'millions' 32.935); impact ticks on the landing. Flag for the
  storyboard owner: the stamp anchors are now `w106 / w108` landings.
- **Sunset steps lead their words by 0.13 s** (inCubic 0.16 s completes on the word, ENGINE rule 5); the rings are off
  before each sun move instead of on 'within'.
- **Multiplane factors** (`layout.ts F`) as before: rows 0-1 + dome 0.02, row 2 0.25, row 3 0.62 (the S07 match).
- **Horizon band**: the set's peach band holds through the first dusk steps and snaps to a light sage on 'day'.
- **Dusk relight of rows 0-1**: a flat colour override of the set's fills on 'day' (a CSS rule on the layer group,
  lit peach windows excluded; no filter, no fade) until the set has a far-row dusk relight.
