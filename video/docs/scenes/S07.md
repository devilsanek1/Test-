## Wire copies (velocity plan)

Each copy: peg at (x, pegAt), cruise `vc` to the tilt, then a quadratic speed ramp (no acceleration jump) over the
0.40 s tilt to `vEnd`; `cruiseFor()` solves `vc` so it ends exactly at S08's first-frame peg. `S08_COPIES` mirrors
Scene08.tsx COPIES / BIT_COPY (x0, v0, vr, seed). Hand-over (`cutV`): S08's ON-SCREEN speed on its first frame =
copyX'(0) = (v0 + vr) at zoom 1.3, minus the crane zoom's pull ((x0 - 540) x dz/dt). S08's crane zoom now runs on its
own track (`craneZoom`, inOutSine in log space: ZERO rate on the cut), so `S08_DZ = 0`; the review's "copies brake on
the cut" (the old out(quart) zoom, dz/dt = -0.6 / s) no longer applies, and the fix round adds the drift vr that was
missing. Over the ramp each copy's lean blends (smoothstep) into S08's `copyLean` for that copy (same angle and
angular speed on the cut).
