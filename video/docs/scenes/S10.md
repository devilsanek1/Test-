## How it uses the kit

- **Set `OldQuarter`** (sets lane, v3 API): `noticeSign` slot (L13 via the shared `LabelTag kind="sign"`), `cart` slot
  (our cart), `furniture="bike"`, `people={false}`, the railing / tree / bollard / ground as shipped. Through its `wrap`:
  - `far` -> null (the peach band + far city sit behind the far row and float in the empty street at the reveal),
  - `second` -> local `SecondRow` (see below), `sites` -> local `Sites` + the gap walker, `sign` -> + the flying tags,
    `near` -> + Pip.
- **Props (shared)**: `SunPie`, `SupportTag` (landed + flying), `LabelTag`, `MakersCart` (pusher), `ElephantEmblem`,
  `ElephantPlaque` (far plates), `Bit`, `Cobweb`, `BrowserChrome` (ring + `content`), `Citizen`, `SiteFacade`, `PopUp`.
- **Mascot**: `Elephant` / `ElephantTimeline` / `hop` (pose pops, no fades).
- **S09 (read-only imports)**: `TramWiper` (standalone preview of the wipe + its `half`), `tramPipAt` (Pip hand-off).

## QA (official `scripts/qa/run_all.sh S10`, final run)

- **Gate PASS**. Life **9.5 / 10**, 4.50 events/s (tool), longest gap between onsets 0.77 s, 16 dead frames, longest
  dead run 0.10 s, no flagged run. framecheck: 0 errors; 1 warning: ui-zone (right rail) f162-f174 = the cart rolling out
  at the right edge (intended, not important content). capcheck: 0 fail.
- The tool's 4.5 events/s is below the 5/s target although the scene has ~30 discrete beats in 6 s: the tracker merges
  overlapping changes into one chain, so the cart (one chain from 3.7 s to the end, absorbing both tosses, landings and
  ticks) and the old plaque (flip -> Bit -> cobweb -> dust -> cane every ~0.3 s) count as a few long events. Tried and
  kept: Bit frozen between struggle bursts, no pendulum jolts on the old plaque, the walker stops; reverted: extra Pip
  glances (they merged Pip's 'hey!' into a chain).

## Known gaps / notes

- "The old emblem's ear droops" is drawn as the emblem sagging further on its cane (+6 deg, outBack) with a falling dust
  speck: the emblem's ear is not a separate part of the props geometry.
- During the first toss the cart itself is behind the A-frame sign (storyboard: the cart line is behind the sign); the
  maker's head and shoulders, the satchel tag and S09's sign card show above it.
- The opening (0-0.46 s) is the storyboard's "empty street": sky, sun hop, Pip's drop, the bollard, bike, tree, railing
  and the lone walker at the end of the gap, until the sites pop on 'something'.
- In the standalone composition (no Film transition) S10 draws S09's Wiper itself for t < 0.25 (time-shifted to S09's
  clock); in the film Film draws it. If S09 renames `TramWiper` / `tramPipAt` / `TRAM`, update the import in
  `Scene10.tsx`.
- If the sets lane renames OldQuarter's layers (`far`, `second`, `sites`, `sign`, `near`) the wrap overrides here
  silently stop applying: re-check after set changes.
