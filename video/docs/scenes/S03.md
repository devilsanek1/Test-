## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **The pen-nib sign is re-hung.** BLUEPRINT.sign (795, 930) sits entirely behind R4 in this framing (R4 covers x 760-840
  from y 800), so Bit would be invisible. S03 hangs the same sign (cream disc, ink nib) from a small iron pole on the
  press house's left roof slope, against the sky (`plan.tsx` `RoofSign`, disc (392, 762) r32), and clips the set's
  own sign out while R4 pops. Request (sets): a `signAt` slot or a visible BLUEPRINT.sign.
- **Near walkers = two `EdgeOnlooker`s at the bottom corners**, not BLUEPRINT.walkers: in this framing those anchors
  (866, 1290) / (214, 1318) cover the R3 / L3-L4 plaques and the title card during the count. The edge figures (heads
  at (64, 1492) / (1036, 1432)) frame the street without covering any counted plaque (L1's plaque stays just above the
  left head; R1 is under the right UI rail anyway).
- **The "six tiny walkers" are pop-up figures on the plan** (70-104 px, heads below the press house's base line) instead
  of eye-level people: on this street an eye-level person is 210-330 px tall and would cover the press-house door,
  sticker and plaques. They pop up out of the plan like the buildings (scaleY about the feet), which also avoids anyone
  sprouting out of bare pavement.
- **Screens before their beat are unlit sage glass**, not tealDark: tealDark is S01's "switched off". The three
  non-PHP screens light with their blank plates on 'ten' (so the end state is every window lit, as the light note asks),
  the seven PHP ones in the 'websites' wave.
- **Grid direction**: the set draws its grid near -> far (the storyboard says from the press house toward the viewer);
  the scene's own horizon construction rays draw out of the vanishing point at the same time. Request (sets): a
  `gridDir: 'far-near'` option.
- **Vent puffs x3** (end(PHP), end(loves), 'run'): the set's `ventAt` takes one time, so `plan.tsx` `VentPuffs` draws them
  (2 sheets each, kept under screen y ~620). Request (sets): `ventAt: number[]`.
- **Screen states** need per-building `screenOnAt` on BlueprintStreet; `plan.tsx` rebuilds the set's side-row layout with
  the same inputs (LX / RX / lTop / lBase / rTop / rBase copied, `quadRowLayout` is pure) to overlay unlit glass and the
  glow pop. If the set's geometry changes, update those constants (or land the request and delete the overlay).
