## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **The count is on the SCREENS, not facade plaques (polish round 1: ONE street encoding).** The fix-round-2 blade
  signs on iron brackets (six elephant plates + three blank plates in a staircase at y 1040-1270, touching each other and
  walkers' heads, plus seven elephant pages on 'websites': ~16 elephant glyphs) are gone, brackets included. The side
  screens sit in a clean band (screen y ~870-980) that reads at phone size: on 'seven' the press house counts first
  (its plaque, the only plaque kept, pings orange and its lit screen re-pulses), then the six side PHP screens light with
  the elephant page and an orange ping; on 'ten' blank cream pages pop on the three non-PHP screens; on 'websites' the
  PHP pages re-pulse ('cream glow pop'). The storyboard's blanks L3, L5, R4 become **L1, R1, L5** (`S03_BLANK`): L1's
  screen is cut by the left frame edge (x -7..57) and R1's sits under the right UI rail (x 1012-1076), so the two nearest
  sites are the non-PHP ones and every PHP screen is inside x 89-980 (count order P, L2, R2, L3, R3, L4, R4). Elephant
  glyphs in frame at 'websites': 9 (stamp, Pip, press plaque, six pages).
- **The non-PHP screens get a blank page, not an elephant page**: 7 elephant pages + 3 blank pages = the 7/10 pie on
  the street.
- **Grid order:** far + mid rows are inked from the cut (the hard-cut frame reads as a plan); only the near rows draw on,
  on the 'It's' lead (8.445, 0.3 s), so the plan still completes far -> near.
- **Stamp:** ring 0.06 s + drop 0.10 s instead of the label spec's 'ring 0.13 s, print 2.6x -> 1.0x in 0.27 s': the
  print lands ON 'PHP' (+0.03) as a slam (ease-in), not 0.27 s later with a settle (ease-out). The ring start stays on
  the storyboard anchor w31-0.13.
- **Pip on the stamp's rump** (world (420, 1405)), not BLUEPRINT.pip (350, 1420): at the centre he hid ~80 % of the print.
- **Pip's pride on 'loves' is sit-front (happy: closed eye-smiles + closed smile) + a -7 deg lean back (chin up) + a
  1.10 -> 1.0 chest stretch**, held to end(loves)+0.10 (the storyboard's sit-front + -0.06 pulse alone did not read at
  phone size; polish round 1 dropped fix round 2's trunk-up, whose open mouth and trumpet marks read as a cheer).
- **The pen-nib sign is re-hung** on the press house's left roof slope (`RoofSign`, disc (392, 762) r32): BLUEPRINT.sign
  (795, 930) is behind R4 in this framing. The set's own sign is off (`sign={false}`).
- **The roof vent moved** to the left slope (world (530, 786)): the set's vent cap poked into the sun disc's lower-left
  edge. S03 clips it out of the press layer (`VENT_HIDE`, scaled with the house's pop-up) and redraws the roof band, its
  ink stroke (the set's own `bps/g2` path) and an 8 px strip of gable there (`VentPatch`).
- **The pie snaps back to solid at 13.81** (w45-0.045: first solid frame f163 = 13.826, 0.03 s before 'it.') in one
  frame, instead of a shrink on 'it.'.
- **The bare plan is all paper** (polish round 1): S03's `FarPlain` (cream to the horizon, the grid running on to it,
  no far skyline) replaces the set's sage far peeks for the whole scene, and the sand street only exists from the
  pop-up (`GroundS03 streetAt`).
- **Side rows re-coloured and re-propped** (S03 renders the set's rows itself): L2 / R2 mintPale, L4 peach, L3 tan, R3
  cream; no tiny side plaques; no PHP lock on the three non-PHP doors; peach awnings on L2 / R2; window boxes on L2 / R3;
  a sage street tree in front of L1, cropped by the left edge.
- **Near walkers = two `EdgeOnlooker`s at the bottom corners** (polish round 1: raised 112 / 84 world px, heads at
