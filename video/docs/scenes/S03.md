## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **The count is on blade SIGNS, not facade plaques; R1 and R4 swap roles.** Facade plaques at 2x on the side walls
  would sit at the frame edges (L1 at x ~0-70, R1 entirely under the right UI rail). A sign projecting from a side
  facade is parallel to the picture plane in one-point perspective, so it reads undistorted and sits INWARD of its
  facade. R1 (screen x ~1000-1100, all under the rail) is now one of the three non-PHP sites and R4 a PHP site
  (`S03_BLANK` = L3, L5, R1; count order P, L1, R2, L2, R3, L4, R4). The storyboard's '7 plaques tick, 3 blank plates on
  L3, L5, R4' becomes '7 signs tick, 3 blank plates on L3, L5, R1'. The count signs use a SOLID ink elephant, not the
  plaque prop's line-art emblem (only a silhouette reads at ~23 px on a phone).
- **The non-PHP screens never light** (the storyboard has every screen light): 7 lit pages with an elephant and 3 dark
  screens repeat the 7/10 pie on the street.
- **Grid order:** far + mid rows are inked from the cut (the hard-cut frame reads as a plan); only the near rows draw on,
  on the 'It's' lead (8.445, 0.3 s), so the plan still completes far -> near.
- **Stamp:** ring 0.06 s + drop 0.10 s instead of the label spec's 'ring 0.13 s, print 2.6x -> 1.0x in 0.27 s': the
  print lands ON 'PHP' (+0.03) as a slam (ease-in), not 0.27 s later with a settle (ease-out). The ring start stays on
  the storyboard anchor w31-0.13.
- **Pip on the stamp's rump** (world (420, 1405)), not BLUEPRINT.pip (350, 1420): at the centre he hid ~80 % of the print.
- **Pip's pride on 'loves' is `trunk-up` (happy) + lean back + chest stretch**, then sit-front at end(loves)+0.10
  (the storyboard says sit-front + a -0.06 chest pulse, which did not read at phone size).
- **The pen-nib sign is re-hung** on the press house's left roof slope (`RoofSign`, disc (392, 762) r32): BLUEPRINT.sign
  (795, 930) is behind R4 in this framing. The set's own sign is off (`sign={false}`).
- **The roof vent moved** to the left slope (world (530, 786)): the set's vent cap poked into the sun disc's lower-left
  edge. S03 clips it out of the press layer (`VENT_HIDE`, scaled with the house's pop-up) and redraws the roof band, its
  ink stroke (the set's own `bps/g2` path) and an 8 px strip of gable there (`VentPatch`).
- **The pie snaps back to solid at 13.78** (w45-0.075) in one frame, instead of a shrink on 'it.'.
- **Side rows re-coloured and re-propped** (S03 renders the set's rows itself): L2 / R2 mintPale, L4 peach, L3 tan, R3
  cream; no tiny side plaques; no PHP lock on the three non-PHP doors; peach awnings on L2 / R2; window boxes on L2 / R3;
  a sage street tree in front of L1, cropped by the left edge.
- **Near walkers = two `EdgeOnlooker`s at the bottom corners**, 40 px lower than fix round 1, with the near arm, a hood +
  bag strap (left) and a bun + collar (right), rim strips on their sun sides (not BLUEPRINT.walkers: those anchors cover
  the signs and the card).
- **Walkers are x1.4 (98-146 px) but still not eye-level**: an eye-level person on this street is 210-330 px tall and
  would cover the press-house door, sticker, signs and the card. Accepted: the street reads as the pop-up plan's model.
- **Vent puffs x2** (end(PHP), end(loves)), tumbling up-left over the sky left of the press house.
- **No bike** (asked as optional detail): L1's door and pavement are at the frame edge under the near walker and tree.
