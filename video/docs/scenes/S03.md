## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Grid lead:** the grid starts at w29-0.16 (8.445) instead of on 'It's' (a big build leads its word, ENGINE rule 5;
  it also makes the hard-cut frame alive at once). Sound cue pencil_scratch stays on 'It's'.
- **Stamp slam timing kept** at the storyboard anchor w31-0.13 (impact at 9.23, mid 'PHP'). Reviewer option (stamp at
  w31-0.40 so the print lands on 'PHP') needs a storyboard OK; it would also put the slam before Pip lands on 'in'.
- **Pip on the stamp's rump** (world (420, 1405)), not BLUEPRINT.pip (350, 1420): at the centre he hid ~80 % of the print.
- **The pen-nib sign is re-hung** on the press house's left roof slope (`RoofSign`, disc (392, 762) r32): BLUEPRINT.sign
  (795, 930) is behind R4 in this framing. The set's own sign is off (`sign={false}`).
- **Side rows re-coloured and re-propped** (S03 renders the set's rows itself): L2 / R2 mintPale, L4 peach, L3 tan, R3
  cream (was ~70 % one brown family); no tiny 9-14 px side plaques (the count badges carry the count); no PHP lock on the
  three non-PHP doors; peach awnings on L2 / R2; a sage street tree in front of L1, cropped by the left edge.
- **Near walkers = two `EdgeOnlooker`s at the bottom corners** (arms off: no hand circles), not BLUEPRINT.walkers (those
  anchors cover the R3 / L3-L4 badges and the card during the count).
- **Walkers are x1.4 (98-146 px) but still not eye-level**: an eye-level person on this street is 210-330 px tall and
  would cover the press-house door, sticker, badges and the card. Accepted: the street reads as the pop-up plan's model.
- **Screens before their beat are unlit sage glass** (tealDark is S01's "switched off"); the glow pop is peach, not white.
- **Vent puffs x2** (end(PHP), end(loves)), drawn BEHIND the sun and tumbling up-left (end at screen x <= ~540): the
  'run' puff is dropped (it flew through the pie and the take-off).
