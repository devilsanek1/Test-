## Deviations from the storyboard (deliberate)

- **The detail loupe** (phone legibility): the storyboard keeps the lock at 78 x 98 px; at that size the bolt / gap /
  Bit / thumb-turn are ~10 px on a phone. The match frame is untouched (lock 69 x 86 px at (560, 1150), ring included);
  from the cut the blueprint's own callout circle swells into a magnified detail (x2.8, r 140 world = ~375 px), the
  classic drawing convention, and it carries the dive's zoom rate on the match object.
- **Camera landing out(quad) 0.40 s instead of out(expo) 0.30 s** (gate 1): out(expo) halved the frame's speed every
  frame right after the cut (camspeed FAIL: 16.7 -> 7.7 -> 3.6 px/f). The dive's rate now lives on the lock (loupe), so
  the camera lands with a constant deceleration (~2.2 px/f per frame, limit 2.5). Keys, end state and framing unchanged.
- **The lock's section is coloured** (mint cavity, cream bolt, white guide / strike, tealDark socket with a cream rim,
  a peach gap once marked); the closed state is ink on white, so the match frame reads as the S01 lock box.
- **Positions:** sun (650, 705) instead of (870, 740) (it sat behind Pip and leaked into the facade / wing notch); the
  site icon 30 px up (world 640-760 x 1060-1180, clear of the elephant stamp); the key at world (292, 826) on an erased
  patch (the storyboard spot is inside the loupe); L2 at world (214-596, 1114-1202): 14 under the loupe, 12 above the
  door-width dimension line; L1 sized by its 40 px text (world ~244-576 x 700-748, the sheet's clean top margin).
- **Added:** the plan grid, three back-view onlookers (`Onlookers.tsx`, EdgeOnlooker, white coats, mid ink; the set's
  own `foreground` heads sit below this framing, so `foreground={false}` and S02 places its own), the eraser rub.
- **Extra life beats** (quiet, none on a stressed word's focal event): the hand's grab, release and hover, the icon's
  squash-pop, ring and jolt, Pip's glances, the onlookers' head turns and phone.
