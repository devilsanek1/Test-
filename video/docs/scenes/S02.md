## Deviations from the storyboard (deliberate, for phone legibility)

- **The detail loupe.** The storyboard keeps the lock at 78 x 98 px for the whole shot; at that size the bolt / gap / Bit
  / thumb-turn are ~10 px on a phone and the scene's whole point (a lock opened with no key) did not read. The match
  frame is untouched (lock 69 x 86 px at (560, 1150), ring included); from 0.10 s the blueprint's own callout circle
  swells into a magnified detail (x2.4, r 145 world = 390 px on screen), the classic drawing convention. The camera keys,
  the sky ceiling and every placement around it are unchanged.
- **The lock's section is coloured** (mint cavity, cream bolt, white guide / strike, tealDark socket with a cream rim):
  all-ink-on-white did not separate the bolt from the cavity. The closed state (white box, white cover, ink keyhole) is
  still ink on white, so the match frame reads as the S01 lock box.
- **Positions moved:** the site icon 30 px up (world 640-760 x 1060-1180) so it clears the sheet's elephant stamp; the
  key cross up-left of the loupe at world (292, 826) on a cleared paper patch (the storyboard's (560-620, 1000-1040) is
  inside the loupe now); L2 at world (214-618, 1110-1198) (12 px lower: clear of the loupe's bottom), L1 sized by its
  40 px text (world ~244-576 x 700-748, inside the sheet's clean top margin); the connector leaves from the loupe's edge.
- **Extra life beats** (all quiet, none on a stressed word's focal event): the hand's grab, release and hover, the
  icon's squash-pop and jolt, Pip's glances at the hand / icon / key. Without them 2.2-3.0 s was a 0.77 s dead run.
- The diagram hand has a white paper fill (`handFill`) over the drawing; dashed ink, teal sleeve, tealDark cuff.
