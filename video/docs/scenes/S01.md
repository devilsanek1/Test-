## The crowd (fix round 1)

Three rows of back-view people facing the plaza (the references' signature, playbook peak dose), kept clear of govB's
door and lock box (world x 470-620, y 1230-1400: the dive target), of Pip (x 760-870) and of the onlooker's phone:

- FRONT row, 134-192 px on the plaza: fA (300, 1540, 190; phone), fB (415, 1514, 162; walker), fC (712, 1532, 180;
  walker), fD (905, 1494, 134; the pointer, straight up at govC's clock tower, x < 950 so out of the right rail).
- SECOND row, ~110 px (feet ~1472): sA (255), sB (645).
- FAR row at the steps, 52-66 px (the set's citizen seeds `plaza/cit0/4/6/7`), one silhouette on the sun path.

The head turn is a back-view -> 3/4 head swap (cheek and jaw edge + ear appear on the turning side, grows 20 -> 100 %
in 0.1 s; `HeadTurn` in `cast.tsx`), not a lean. Phones are drawn by the scene (`ElbowArm` 2-link IK, the elbow
prefers DOWN then outward, so the arm folds up and never sweeps flat across the plaza) with a lit screen showing a mini
govB (dome stroke, tealDark block, cream slats): everyone is filming the shutdown.

## Camera (`scene01/camera.ts`)

Storyboard keys on the buildings plane: push (540,960,1.00) -> (552,972,1.04) inOut(sine) 0-1.98 from rest, as a zoom
about the key pair's fixed screen point on log zoom; hold to 4.385; dive to (536.7,1258.4,3.19) in 0.33 s.
Fix round 1, the dive is ANCHORED ON THE LOCK: the lock box's screen point p(u) = lerp((530.6,1319.8), (560,1150),
inOutSine(u)) while the zoom z(u) = 1.04 x (3.19/1.04)^inQuad(u) grows about it (u = (t - 4.385) / 0.33). The end
camera is unchanged (lock box 70 x 86 px at screen (560,1150), checked against S02's frame 0: same position and ring),
and the lock's screen velocity is ZERO at the cut, matching S02's still lock (gate 1: no translation stop at the
match). Peak translation ~27 px/frame mid-dive, inside the allow window; both curves start with zero slope (no velocity
step at 4.385). Light multiplane parallax through the set's `wrap` (far + sun 0.43, buildings / ground / light 1, near
1.43). During the dive the SUN's exponent ramps 0.43 -> 1.5 on the dive's inOut(sine) curve so the sun leaves the
frame with the dive (f141: disc bottom at y -168); the far layer keeps 0.43 (ramping it made the civic silhouettes
loom up in the gap).
