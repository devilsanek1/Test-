## Camera (`scene01/camera.ts`)

Storyboard keys on the buildings plane, as zooms about each key pair's FIXED screen point on log zoom (so the plane hits
the keys exactly): push (540,960,1.00) -> (552,972,1.04) inOut(sine) 0-1.98 from rest; hold to 4.385; dive to
(536.7,1258.4,3.19) in(quad) in 0.33 s: govB's lock box lands at screen (560,1150), 70 x 86 px, exactly where S02's
blueprint lock lands (checked against S02's frame 0: (562,1157), same ring). Light multiplane parallax through the set's
`wrap` (storyboard far x0.3 / buildings x0.7 / near x1.0 -> zoom exponents far + sun 0.43, buildings / ground / light 1,
near 1.43). `scripts/camspeed.py` on the render: no strobe and no velocity step outside the dive (allow 4.385-4.715).

## Shims

None left. The first pass used local stand-ins (CRT screens, far civic skyline, half shutter, lettered tag); they were
removed when the kit landed them (GovernmentPlaza v3 `state="frame0"`, `screenOffAt`, `shutterStop`, `farCivic`,
`civicOffAt`, `civicLanternsOffAt`, per-building `lanternOffAt`, `tagProps` / `padlockProps`; props `BackSoonTag lettered`,
`SunPress mode="release"`). Scene-local only: the cast acting (`cast.tsx`: walking / stopping / leaning citizens on the
SET's citizen seeds, a pointing arm, flying pigeons, Pip's flinch / startle hop / lens leap) and the parallax camera.
