## Staging decisions (deviations from the storyboard's numbers, and why)

- Pip is scale 1.1 in Part A (storyboard 1.2), on the facade LEDGE left of step 2 (125, 1230) instead of step 2's tread
  (300, 1230): at 1.2 / x 300 his head covers lock4. The crate (LockCrate 85) is at step 2's left end (232, 1226).
- Pip leaps in from off-frame left (not a walk: 'But' is 0.23 s after the cut); the cut frame has no Pip, so S08's
  right-side Pip does not visibly jump.
- The old lock lets go one frame into A2 and lands at film 36.30 (it was in the air on the 36.23 clunk).
- The keeper stands on step 1's right end (420, 1300), h 186; his raised hand is a cuff + mitten (`holding` slot).
- The cart is parked right of the stoop (760, 1432); its J5 sign is the shared FixSign in the MakersCart sign slot
  (post 132, card 224 x fs 36). The strike leads to 'fix' and 'fixed!' to out-0.10 so the payoff holds before the cut.
- The sunrise column is kept clear: the near tree is clipped out of A1/A2 (`TREE_HOLE`), the far towers' dawn rims
  (STOOP.dawnRims[1..4]) are clipped out of the hero layer (`RIMS_HOLE`; the hero's own rim [0] stays), the walker is a
  small figure on the right pavement (842, 1192), only tower [3] is in front of the sun. The sun glides to world y 885
  (storyboard sunKey screen (700, 890) = world 952 would leave ~60 % of the disc behind the rooflines).
- The near layer (post, kerb, Pip, snail, Bit) is screen-space, lowered into the plaza (post base y 1600, cap 1502),
  sand/tan with a tealDark cap (not a door shape or colour), the kerb from x 800 on a slight slope off the right edge.
- TerraceHill people `riseAt: 3.55`: the right stander (2-3x the post's size during the cruise) would otherwise rise
  through the near layer.
- No label (L12 declined in the storyboard).
