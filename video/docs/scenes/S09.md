## Staging decisions (deviations from the storyboard's numbers, and why)

- Pip scale 1.1 in Part A (storyboard 1.2), on the facade LEDGE (125, 1230): at 1.2 / x 300 his head covers lock4. He
  starts in frame on the pavement at the left edge (62, 1372) (the storyboard's "walking in since the cut"; a leap,
  since 'But' is 0.23 s after the cut).
- The crate (LockCrate 85) on step 2's left end (232, 1226); the cart right of the stoop (760, 1432).
- The sun glides to world y 822 (storyboard sunKey (700, 890) would leave the disc wedged in the rooflines' V).
- The ground relight stops half a step under day in Part A (storyboard: "the day relight arrives with S10's tram").
- Bit body length 16 (A1 16 px, A2 24 px; storyboard 18 px) and one continuous Bit: lock -> jamb -> leap onto Pip ->
  rides Pip's install hop -> hops onto the snail.
- The pull-back's first 0.43 s show more of Stoop Street (the ghost) before the hill pops in at the cruise speed: a
  full-frame swap in motion (the door, the actors and the doorstep hold), instead of a seam at the frame sides at rest.
- The set's own check-box tags are OFF (`checkBoxWaveAt` not passed): terrace.tsx draws the wave (white cards: cream
  cards read as plaques / frames on cream facades; sage ones merged with the teal doors).
- The set's cream light path is covered (it read as a necktie); a stepped peach pool sits on the kerb instead.
- `extendAt` moved from 'every' to server-0.05: 'every' draws the walls + stairs, 'server' extends the sides.
- TerraceHill people `riseAt: 3.55` (the right stander would rise through the near layer). No label (L12 declined).
