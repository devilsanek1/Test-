## The hidden cut (how it holds)

`camera.ts` exports `a2ToBMat`: B-world = (457.5, 1182) + 0.25 x A2-world (lock4 (330, 1130) 42 px -> house #4's lock
(540, 1464.5) 10.5 px). Part B draws the SAME `<A2Actors/>` component (crate, fallen old lock, Bit's crawl, the keeper
and his walk to door #5) through that map, so at the zoom-6 match they land on their A2 pixels (same ink, same LOD), then
shrink with the pull-back like world objects. Pip is mapped the same way until 'install', then hops into screen space.
What does change at the cut is the world around the door (A2's hero facade, stoop and street vs house #4's neighbours
and the near street): read as reference 1's match-cut grammar (the door and its people stay, the world changes) under
a camera that starts moving at once. A cream left neighbour (PartA `Neighbour`) frames A2's left edge like house #3
frames B's. The window bar is 45 px tall in A2 vs 57 px in B (BrowserChrome's bar rule); the elephant plaque moves
~30 px (the sets' plaque slots differ).

## Staging decisions (deviations from the storyboard's numbers, and why)

- Pip is scale 1.1 in Part A (storyboard 1.2), and sits on the facade LEDGE left of step 2 (125, 1230) instead of on
  step 2's tread (300, 1230): at 1.2 / x 300 his head covers lock4 (the focal object of the drop, the toss and the
  pop-on). The crate moved to step 2's LEFT end (215, 1226) to stay beside him; the lock axis (x 313-347) is clear.
- The keeper stands on step 1's right end (420, 1300), h 186 (a person-height for a 200 px door), reaching left to
  the lock; his mapped Part-B self is the 46 px speck the storyboard asks for (>= 40 px at rest).
- 'Pip hands the lock to the keeper' is a short TOSS (arc 0.16 s, peak on 'STILL'): Pip can't reach the door from a
  clear spot; the fling is the gag.
- The cart is parked right of the stoop (760, 1432) instead of (520, 1400): the maker pushing it would otherwise
  stand in front of the stoop and the crate. Its sign card is 224 px wide, CaptionHand 36 px (on screen in A1).
- The rising sun sits BETWEEN the far tower pairs (the nearer pair is re-drawn in front of it): behind all four
  towers an r110 sun showed only as slivers. A1's people layer keeps only the far walker (the stander and the
  backpacker would sit behind the cart sign). The pigeons on the wire stay perched (a fly-off crossed the captions).
- The near layer (bollard, kerb, Pip, snail, Bit) is screen-space but inside the World (it rides any Film transition
  camera with the scene; v3 wipes have none). The bollard is teal so it reads against the cream / sand facades.
- No label (L12 declined in the storyboard).
