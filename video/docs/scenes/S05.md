- **Teams pop up instead of rising** (open steps: a clipped rise would sprout them from the pavement, ENGINE 10).
- **Label boxes.** L5 uses the S04-continuity box, L6 is 24 px taller (205-650, 688-784): two 38 px lines fit.
- **Scan beams** are OPAQUE white light wedges under the plan's cream and ink (the storyboard's mintPale at 0.5 would
  vanish on the mintPale paper and stack translucency).
- **'bad'** adds the door swinging ajar and the bolt's full throw to the hand's turn (the lock alone was a 65 x 80 px
  event); the '!' is a white balloon (r30 world) with a 38 world '!'.
- **The camera's y** rises 960 -> 940 during the landing (the storyboard keeps 960): the cornice must stay out of the
  caption band (ENGINE 10).
- **The messenger** raises the envelope to his RIGHT shoulder (S06's carry), not 'from his coat to the left shoulder'
  (round 1), and runs (3/4-back) rather than walks: a cut on action needs a stride.
- **Pip's perch** is the set's `perchBottom` moved 10 world left; his leap is HIGH (apex ~world 1100) so he clears the
  elephant stamp, and he is mid-air on the cut.
