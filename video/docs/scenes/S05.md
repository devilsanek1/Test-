leap -> Bit's leap. Foreground layer (the set's `foreground` slot): `Audience`. The sun is screen space.

## Camera

hold, then x 540 -> 690 in(sine) over the last 0.30 s. **A1 fix round** (review: the A1 landing, 1.055 over 0.30 s on

Hermite dive ends at 0.84 /s, scene04/camera.ts): one decelerating push across the matchcut. y has its OWN zero-velocity
ease: 960 until 0.05 s, then in-out(sine) to 940 by 0.65 s, so the lock holds its screen spot through the match; the 20
world px rise lands the cornice ink at screen ~588, under the caption band.
**Multiplane**: each set layer sees the camera x `540 + f (cam.x - 540)`, zoom and y shared: steps (the match plane:
teams, messenger, Pip in the air, Bit's leap) f 1.0, facade and banner 0.67, wing 0.4, foreground audience 1.0 (final
polish: was 1.12, its big flat heads strobed at 32.7 px/frame on the last 3 frames; now ~29.6 = the steps plane, the
cut-on-action match unchanged), sun 0.05 (screen space).
**The y rise stays 20 world (final polish, tried and reverted):** a review asked to take back 10-15 px so the teams' feet
leave the bottom UI band "if the cornice allows". At y 950 the cornice ink came to screen ~576 and the caption check
failed (f63, the 'how bad it was.' roll: 8 % of its box under 3:1; f24 warned), so the camera keeps 960 -> 940 (feet
~1559, foreground framing; the key reads sit above y 1480).
**No push in the hold (review suggestion declined):** the storyboard locks the camera while the teams, tablets, beams and
ring build (no sign-off for a move there), the landing already carries a 9 % zoom (1.033 -> 1.13), and a 3-4 % push would

cornice to ~575. 'bad' is carried by the hand, the '!', the bolt, Bit's hop and the door leaf (now foreshortened, with its
thickness strip) instead.

  starts at 1.033 (not 1.08) and lasts 0.40 s (the rate handoff with S04's Hermite dive).

- **Pip** sleeps facing LEFT (S04's facing), his perch is the set's `perchBottom` moved 10 world left, and he leaps just
  BEFORE the truck (2.853) and exits frame right on the cut (A1 fix round).
- **The sun** sits at (885, 790) with an up-right drift instead of the storyboard's notch (930, 725) (tangents, see Look).
- **Colour beat**: the callout disc is peach until 'out' (S04's lock spot carried across the cut), white from 'out'.
- **L6** pops at 18.07, one frame before the storyboard's w58+0.40 (the shared slip's spring needs it to be legible by
  ~18.19).
- **The flag** goes slack on 'how' (not on 'bad': nothing moves outside the lock on 'bad' but its gentle wave).
- **The door's void** is a light hatched strip (the plan's own convention), not a dark gap.
