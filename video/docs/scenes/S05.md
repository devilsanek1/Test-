## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Teams pop up instead of rising.** They stand on open steps in front of a bare forecourt; a clipped rise would sprout
  them out of the pavement (ENGINE 10: a person rises only where the entry is hidden). They pop up about their feet
  (scaleY 0 -> 1.07 -> 1 in 0.26 s, the sets' `PopUp`, like S03's plan figures), same times and stagger.
- **Label boxes.** The storyboard boxes cannot hold two lines at 38-40 px (L5 (420-680, 1010-1080) would shrink the
  text to ~20 px). L5 uses the S04-continuity box above (300 x 108 world); L6 is 24 px taller (205-650, 688-784): it
  overlaps the door elevation's top edge a little.
- **Scan beams** are sage at 0.3 (static translucency, light only) instead of mintPale at 0.5: mintPale vanishes on the
  white sheet. Dashed ink edges as specified. They retract on 'how' so nothing else moves on 'bad'.
- **The '!'** is a white balloon (r25 world) with a 32 px ink '!' and a tail to Bit, up-left of the lock outside both
  rings: Bit's own `alertAt` '!' is 11 px at his size and sits on the lock's linework (unreadable on a phone).
- **The hand's knob.** The shared blueprint Lock has no thumb-turn, so `LockTurn` (cast.tsx) draws one on the box's top
  edge with the hand (white disc + ink ring and bar, turns 90 deg) and retracts the short bolt (a white patch over the
  bar + the bar redrawn at its new tip): S02's "turns it with no key" read, at the S05 size.
- **The messenger dashes.** To leave frame right while the camera trucks right with him he has to outrun the truck: he
  accelerates (in(quad), 285 world px over the last 0.31 s) with a 7 deg lean and three ink speed lines. It reads as a
  hurried cartoon exit; S06 decelerates him.
- **Pip's leap** lands far right (world 1180) after the cut so his on-screen motion is to the right, faster than the
  truck (otherwise the truck makes him drift left on screen).
