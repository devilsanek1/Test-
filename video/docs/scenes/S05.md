"before researchers using AI figured out how bad it was." (w55-w64). Film 16.59-19.773 s (3.18 s): cut(years) ->

## Camera

`screen = (world - (x, y)) x zoom + (540, 960)`: (540, 960, 1.08) -> (540, 960, 1.13) out(expo) over 0.30 s (log zoom),
hold, then (540 -> 690, 960, 1.13) in(sine) over the last 0.30 s. **Multiplane** (fix round 1): each set layer sees the
camera x `540 + f (cam.x - 540)`, zoom and y shared (so the t = 0 match and the hold are exactly as before): steps (the
match plane: teams, messenger, Pip in the air) f 1.0, facade and banner 0.67, wing 0.4, foreground heads 1.3, sun 0.05
(screen space). Pip rides the banner layer (and its sway) until take-off, then the steps plane, starting from the point
the banner layer showed (no jump). Measured (`camspeed`, last frame pair): dominant plane (facade / banner) **19.5
px/frame**, steps plane **29.1**, foreground heads 37.9 (under the bottom UI); S06's first pair measures 17.1 (its houses
at 0.6), so the perceived speed drops ~12 % across the cut instead of 41 %.

The landing keeps the storyboard's out(expo) (log-zoom rate ~1.05 /s on the cut vs S04's dive at ~1.23 /s): because the
cornice caps the zoom at 1.13 the push can only travel ln(1.13 / 1.08), so it settles in ~4 frames (24.4, 11.2, 5.3,
2.4, 1.0 px/frame). **Accepted as a deliberate landing thud** (the dive's arrival lands on the drawing); it would only
become a glide if S04's dive arrived slower (e.g. ~0.45 /s, then S05 out(cubic) over 0.35 s): S04 lane request below.

Exact check command (the allow windows are the storyboard's `cameraSpeedAllow`, which `src/data/scenes.json` does not
carry):

    python3 scripts/camspeed.py S05 --allow=0-0.30,2.883-3.19

Result: OK (no strobing speed, no velocity step outside the windows). One WARN at 1.50-1.57 s = the L6 slip's 7 % pop.
Cornice at screen 564.5 on the lock (582 at the landing): only flat sky in the caption band.

## Deviations from the storyboard text (and why)

- **Teams pop up instead of rising** (open steps, a clipped rise would sprout them from the pavement, ENGINE 10).
- **Label boxes.** The storyboard boxes cannot hold two lines at 38-40 px; L5 uses the S04-continuity box above, L6 is
  24 px taller (205-650, 688-784).
- **Scan beams** are sage at 0.3 (static translucency, light only): mintPale vanishes on the white sheet. They retract on
  'out' (gone before the hand starts) so nothing else moves on 'bad'.
- **The '!'** is a white balloon (r25 world) with a 32 px ink '!' and a tail to Bit: Bit's own '!' is 11 px.
- **The hand's knob and turn.** The shared blueprint Lock has no thumb-turn, so `LockTurn` (plan.tsx) draws one on the
  box's top edge (turns 90 deg) and retracts the short bolt. The hand's own `turnAt` (fixed 0.2 s) is not used: the scene
  rotates it 12 deg about the pinch point over 0.28 s in-out sine (sleeve end <= ~14 px/frame, was 34).
- **The messenger's dash** is defined in screen px relative to the trucking camera (he outruns it at a near-constant
  27 px/frame after a 0.1 s ramp); ~1500 world px/s at the cut (was 1850).
- **Pip's perch** is the set's `perchBottom` moved 10 world left so his trunk-up wake clears the stamp; his hop is low
  (45 world) and lands on the steps (1440) after the cut.

## Shims used (swap when the props lane adds them)

`scene05/shims.tsx` exports `Investigator` and `Messenger` with the shared props plus:
- **Investigator**: `tabletSize` (world px, default 60), `tabletLift: 'overhead' | 'head'`, `pose` ({kind: 'point', at,
  target} | 'shade' | 'hips' | 'clipboard'), and a `tabletCentre()` helper. Geometry, coat, badge, sleeves and tablet
  mirror props `Figures.tsx`, so the swap is invisible.
- **Messenger**: `side: 'left' | 'right'` (the hand that pulls the envelope), `lift: 'shoulder' | 'high'`, and a
  `messengerEnvelope()` helper.
Every other piece is the shared kit (BlueprintWall, Lock, ScanBeam, AlertMark, DiagramHand, Calendar, LabelTag, Bit,
SparkleTablet, Envelope). The old MarkRing (r55, ping) is replaced by the local `FoundRing` (one open stroke + a 0.15 s
pulse): a props `MarkRing` with `open` / `overshoot` / `pingDur` would replace it.
