## What is in it

low sun (r120, screen (420, 985)) behind a varied far skyline (roof boxes + chimneys, a water tower over the sun, a
clock spire, a tree crown), four telephone poles along the left kerb with two wires converging on the vanishing point
(two pigeons on the outer wire), the makers' workshop on the right (browser window upstairs, round elephant sign, shop
window with the blind half down and the J3 card 'still fixing...', the door with the mail slot, now on a cream stoop
step, a potted plant and a crate beside it), two passers and a queue of four tiny ones walking up the lane into the sun,
a bike and a planter by the left row, the makers' cart cropped in the bottom-left corner, a shrub planter + bollard
cropped at the bottom-right edge. Pip (1.2), the messenger (S05's, seed `s5msg`, 260 px, dark hair), Bit (20 px).
Short flat clay shadows run from the messenger's and Pip's feet toward the camera (the sun is behind them).

- `parts.tsx HairCollar`: props `Messenger` hard-codes `hair="short"` (seed 's5msg' draws a skin-coloured sand disc) and

  used by S05 and S06 for seed 's5msg'.

## Deviations from the storyboard (deliberate)

- Pip lands at cut+0.15 (not +0.10): at the cut he is at the apex of the hop he started in S05.
- The page's zoom starts at 21.732 (S07's lens start); the sheet's blow-in leads its anchor by 0.035 s (21.500) so it
  reads ~0.2 s at the left edge before the lens; the hold is centred at y 935 (not 960) so the heading bar sits above
  the caption band.
- 'shh' gag: the mascot has no shush pose; the trunk-up rig with its trumpet marks clipped away and the open mouth
  closed (a body-colour patch + a small pursed 'o'), eyes open glancing right, + the lettered 'shh'.
- "The blind jolts once": the set has no blind-jolt API; the J3 card swings and two ink jolt ticks flick at the blind.
- "A neighbour's curtain": the set's `curtainsAt` pops the whole row's panes peach at once.
- Passers: re-sized on the messenger's camera (114 / 141 px) + four tiny ones (40-74 px) toward the sun; the set's
  stander is dropped (Pip's lane); the cart moved to the bottom-left corner (cropped framing).
- Camera factors blend during the truck (above): the storyboard's 0.6 / 0.8 hold from 20.403 on.
- Not done (optional in the review): peach sun slits across the teal shade (with the sun at the vanishing point the
  gaps between houses would not cast slits), the browser bar's dots blinking on the pop, the sign's longer pendulum.
