## Deviations from the storyboard (deliberate)

- Pip lands at cut+0.30 (not +0.10): he must trail the messenger across the cut (S05 has him behind), so he is off frame
  left on the cut and leaps in after him; landing at +0.10 / +0.15 would put him under the messenger's legs or leave
  his shh at the frame's left edge.
- The page's zoom starts at 21.732 (S07's lens start); the sheet's blow-in leads its anchor by 0.035 s (21.500); the
  hold is x8.5 at (230, 830), +1 deg (not x8 centred) so the ringed Bit doodle is whole.
- 'shh' gag: the mascot has no shush pose; S06 draws it on the side rig: the rig's trunk is clipped below the head and
  a J-curled trunk (the mascot's own tube / brush drawing, its own root) hooks back up onto the lips; expression
  'closed' (knowing), + the lettered 'shh' with a tail.
- The makers' heads lead 'heads-up' by 0.07 s (their pop completes on the word); the ticks + SFX stay on 21.285.
- "The blind jolts once": the set has no blind-jolt API; the FixSign swings and two ink jolt ticks flick at the blind.
- "A neighbour's curtain": the set's `curtainsAt` pops the whole row's panes peach at once.
- Passers: re-sized on the messenger's camera (114 / 141 px) + four tiny ones (40-74 px) toward the sun; the set's
  stander is dropped (Pip's lane); the cart moved to the bottom-left corner (cropped framing).
- Shadows: one stylised length rule (2.13 h (d / 260)^1.2 along the ray from the sun's ground point, d = feet depth
  below the horizon), not the strict horizon-sun projection (that would run every shadow off the frame).
- Camera factors blend during the truck: the storyboard's 0.6 / 0.8 hold from 20.403 on.
