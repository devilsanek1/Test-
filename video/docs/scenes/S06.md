## Camera (scene06/camera.ts)

`screen = (world - (cx_f, 970)) * z + (540, 960)`, a layer with parallax factor f sees `cx_f = 560 + f (cx - 560)`;
at rest every layer lines up (world = storyboard numbers). Truck 380 -> 560 at zoom 1.04, out(cubic), a fixed 0.63 s
from the cut (entry 3 x 180 x 1.04 / 0.63 = 891 px/s); breath 1.04 -> 1.06 from 20.403 to 21.775; hold. Factors
(storyboard layers): band / sun / far roofs 0.1 (the sun is screen space: it does not zoom), left houses + lane floor
(+ the passers, bike and cart standing on it) 0.6, workshop 0.8, messenger / Pip / Bit 1.0.
`scripts/camspeed.py S06 --allow=0-0.15` (the storyboard's cameraSpeedAllow 19.773-19.923): camera peak 17.1 px /
frame, no velocity step; fast-local WARNs are the seal's drop, the heads' pop and the page.

## Continuity

- **In (cut on action from S05, 19.773).** The messenger is S05's (`MESSENGER.seed` 's5msg'): he leaves S05 in a dash
  (S05 exit ~1200 px/s on screen) and enters S06 at the left edge at ~780 px/s on screen (world 1600 px/s against the
  camera's 857), braking evenly to the door on 20.665; his walk clock runs at his speed and its rate is solved so his
  last step lands with both feet down (no leg snap). Pip is mid-hop on both sides of the cut (S05 `pipHop` is
  mid-air on the cut). S05 is still WIP: on its current last frames its own truck / dash do not show yet, so the speed
  match is by the numbers, not yet by a render.
- **Out (foreground-wipe into S07, window 21.775-22.195, element 'scene' = this module's `Wiper`).** One paper with ONE
  screen trajectory `pageAt(T)` (film time), drawn identically by S06 (also in the S06-only comp) and by the Wiper:
  flutter in from the left (21.535), T-MATCH zoom at S07's own lens start (21.732) to S07's hold (x8 at (540, 960),
  rot -2), then EXACTLY S07's sheet (`LeakSheet` 210 x 270, seed 's7notice', `lensWipeAt` with S07's parameters) as it
  shrinks into the notice; the Wiper stops drawing at S07's pin (22.165). `Wiper.half = 6000` makes the wipe edge jump
  the whole frame between film frames 659 (21.967, edge x -360: S06 under the full page) and 660 (22.000, edge x 1277:
  S07 with its shrinking sheet), so no 30 fps frame ever shows the clip edge, and the two copies coincide pixel for
  pixel. Checked on `S06Film` every frame 21.767-22.200: 4 frames of full page (21.867-21.967), then S07's shrink; no
  seam, no pop. Captions stay ink over the page (`captionRegions` dark).
- `page.tsx` MIRRORS S07 constants (`S07_SHEET` = Scene07 NOTICE, `S07_PIN` = 'word'-0.05+0.06, the lens from / fromS /
  peak / focus). If S07 retimes or moves its sheet, update them (or S07 can import `S07_LENS` / `pageAt` from here).

## Deviations from the storyboard (deliberate)

- Pip lands at cut+0.15 (not +0.10): at the cut he is at the apex of the hop he started in S05, so the leap reads
  across the cut without a 100 px / frame streak.
- The page's zoom starts at 21.732 (S07's lens start, 0.04 s before cut(heads-up)-0.21) so the spec's 4-frame
  accelerating zoom fits before S07's hold.
- 'shh' gag: the mascot has no shush pose; 'hold' (trunk curled up to the mouth, eyes closed) + a lettered 'shh' with
  two hush ticks (pops in, shrinks away).
- "The blind jolts once": the set has no blind-jolt API; the J3 card swings and two ink jolt ticks flick at the blind.
- "A neighbour's curtain": the set's `curtainsAt` pops the whole row's panes peach at once (a bigger, still 1-frame
  event).
- Passers: the set's walker anchors are eye-level-sized (272 px at feet 1340) and read as giants behind a 260 px
  messenger, so they are re-sized on the messenger's camera (114 / 141 px) and the second one moved to (455, 1300);
  the stander is dropped (Pip's lane); the cart moved to the bottom-left corner (cropped framing).
- Gust lines were tried and removed: thin wind strokes over the dark facades read as glitches.
