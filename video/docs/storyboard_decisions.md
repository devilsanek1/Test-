# Storyboard decisions: what came from where, and why

**Backbone:** metaphor won two of the three judges (art director, clarity). It gives the causal spine: one elephant-stamped
blueprint, and every building made from it shares the same faulty lock. The retention draft (the virality judge's winner)
supplies the hook, the rewatch layer and the pattern-interrupt map. The editorial draft supplies the accuracy guardrails
and the best physical gags. Every scene was re-staged for full-bleed: eye-level, deep, the viewer slightly small.
- **From retention:** the big browser windows revealed on "websites"; the four-shutter rhythm with the highlight on
  "themselves"; Bit on the cornice from frame 0 to the last frame; the sun stamping the wax seal; copies riding the wires;
  the flashlight "?" sweep, then sunrise; the crawling progress bar and the peephole; the 7/10 and 1/3 pies; emblems on
  canes; the two-option board finale; the caption chunks for S05 and S12.
- **From metaphor:** PHP as one blueprint and the pop-up-book build; the master blueprint on a wall with two teams and Pip
  asleep on the frame top; the board whose top rail is an address bar, with 2-4-8 clones; the dome over a big chunk with
  tallies climbing the glass; step 2 stretching into a ledge to the horizon; the "back soon" tags planted at 3.40 and paid
  off on "until it can"; the frame-0 caption; the sun screen-fixed in the big pull-back; the caretaker with keys.
- **From editorial:** the S02 lock close-up with Bit and the double-take; the "hard part" step and the look to camera on
  "right?"; trunk "shh"; the flyer on Pip's face; dashed ghost plates; a count that never settles; Pip on a constant-size
  foreground perch; the "tick, skip, tick" cart; the exact un-build recipe; the snail running gag.

**Must-fix items applied:** one metaphor spine (no foundation cross-section, no blueprint inversion, and the S07 board
returns in S13 instead of a second board system). S02 opens on one lock and adds at most 3 new ideas per 1.5 s. The ghosts
for "everywhere" are dashed outlines, and nothing is fixed before S10. Attempts are seen, never blocked and never
successful, and hoods appear only in S08. The leak sheet comes from off-frame, and the workshop blind stays down until
"came out". The makers ship the fix and a generic keeper installs it. Only the PHP plaque ages, never the site.
"Themselves" reads as deliberate because the sun is pressed first. The 14 years are shown with a calendar and seasons, not
a clock, and the hour hand sweeps less than one turn. There is one sunset ("by evening") and one sunrise ("step two"). The
hosting visual holds 0.77 s before the un-build starts on "sorts", and "out." stays on screen until it ends. "7" is re-timed
to 9.38 by an energy check of the VO.

**Art-direction rules:** periwinkle belongs to Pip alone (PHP is sand with an ink elephant). Orange is the sun plus one seal.
Only the 13 palette colours are used, and "lights out" is a pop to the next palette step. There are no blurs, fades, whips,
irises, text or numerals in the art. Pip never goes below scale 0.75. Each scene has one quiet field and at most two
framings of one set.

**Balance note (user):** true Arcane scale appears only in S01, S08 and S10. There are only two big moves, the S08 crane and
the S10 pull-back. Everything else is a 5-15% push or a medium move of 2.2x or less lasting about 1 s. The light is mostly
day and golden, with 1.75 s of night. Captions always sit on a clean sky. The tone is a curious explainer, not a trailer.
**Dropped:** the underground world and code glyphs, patch stickers, bouncing darts, doors that hold, footprints, the server
rack, the periwinkle speck gag (it breaks the mascot-only colour), odometer digits, the sneeze (it would crowd the cane gag),
the eye glint (it can't read in flat colour), and the retired 3D flip (a foreground wipe on "Except," replaces it).
