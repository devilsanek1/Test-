# IDEAS: three whole-frame ideas for the moment (process test 01, Amsterdam)

**The moment** (`CORE.md`): on the little bridge by her house at dusk she has just asked her phone a question; below
her the canal's reflection has become the street's paper twin, and right under the bridge its old handwriting is
lighting up, warm, rising toward her. **Felt:** a hushed "whoa": the city remembers, and it is talking to her; then the
pull to stay (what is it saying?). Warm and full of wonder, never ghostly. **Read in a second, sound off:** the water
under this Amsterdam street holds its past in old writing, and it is answering her. **The words:** the caption "now
answer your questions" ("answer" lit); the picture alone must say "old papers". The world and the look: `VISION.md`.
**The thumbnails:** `ideas_sheet.png` (grey values, one accent: the gold of the waking pages; drawn by
`ideas_sheet.py` from one small 3D sketch of the same canal, so the three are views of one world).

## 1. THE PAPER HALF (picked)
- **The picture.** From a small boat low on her canal we face her little humped brick bridge. Its arch and the arch's
  "reflection" close into one ring: the top half brick, the lower half the paper twin, bound ledgers standing spine-out
  where the stones would be and long old sheets laid in courses for the bridge's face. Through the ring the canal runs
  on, the houses above the waterline and their paper twins below it, into the dusk and out of sight. On the crown she
  leans on the near railing, forearms on the rail, phone held out over the water, her bike beside her. Right under
  her, inside the ring, the pages have woken: they glow like paper lanterns with their writing showing through, a few
  sheets peel up off the water, and a winding stream of light, loops of script coming loose around it, climbs the
  bridge's face to her phone. The rest of the twin stays dark and unreadable.
- **The world and the light.** Dusk; beyond the bridge the street goes on (gables shoulder to shoulder, elms, a
  houseboat with lit windows, a church tower faint in the haze down the canal); one calm sky above holds the words.
  One story light, the waking pages: it fills the ring from inside, lights the vault's underside like a lantern in a
  cave, and catches her face, hands and coat from below. Everything else is cool and quiet; windows and the bridge lamp
  are a lower, softer warm.
- **The people and the words.** Her alone, small and simple, head bowed to the light (open eyes, looking down at it),
  the scarf's end the one thing moving. The caption in the sky's band, "answer" lit in the same gold as the pages and
  her phone. No words in the picture: the handwriting is a texture.
- **The feeling it gives** (named looking at the thumbnail at phone size, before re-reading the brief): a hushed,
  glowing doorway. A held breath; warm at the heart of a cool evening; a pull into the dark beyond the ring.
- **Why it could stop a thumb:** the most familiar Amsterdam picture (a bridge and its reflection making a circle) with
  one thing wrong: half the circle is old paper, lit from inside. A double take in a second, then the eye climbs the
  light to her.

## 2. HER QUESTION LANDS
- **The picture.** We stand on the bridge just behind her right shoulder: her hair, coat collar and scarf and her arm
  reaching out over the iron railing fill the lower left, her phone glowing at the end of it. Below and beyond, her
  canal runs away between its two rows of houses to a far tower; in the water the paper twin hangs upside down along
  both quays, dark. Where her question fell, ripples spread across the twin as broken rings of gold, the pages inside
  them glowing, and the writing climbs back to her hand.
- **The feeling it gives:** quiet, personal, a little dim: "it is answering ME".
- **Why it could stop a thumb:** a point of view; the viewer stands where she stands.
- **Why it lost:** the whoa is small. The magic is a small patch, and her dark back is the biggest shape in the frame.
  At a squint her silhouette and the canal's vanishing point are two places to look. The twin, seen down the canal,
  reads as an ordinary reflection, the rings edge toward a sonar graphic at phone size, and "hushed" turns into "dim".
  Its strength (the viewer in her place) suits a later beat, her own house answering her, more than the hook.

## 3. COME TO LIGHT
- **The picture.** Low on a houseboat deck beside the bridge, looking up at her against the dusk: the twin's pages
  lift off the water, glowing like paper lanterns with their writing showing through, and rise in a loose column to
  her reaching hand; the deck's potted plants cut by the bottom corner.
- **The feeling it gives:** a joyful lift, magic: the archivist's "finally come to light" made literal.
- **Why it could stop a thumb:** the clearest "old papers" of the three: glowing pages flying out of a canal.
- **Why it lost:** it is an event, loud rather than hushed, and its strongest image is a familiar one (letters flying
  through the air), so it is the least invented for this story. The street's paper twin, the film's concept, barely
  plays, and the pages' bright diagonal against the bridge's big curve makes the frame busy. Its lesson goes into the
  pick: the paper must read as paper where the light leaves the water.

## The pick: 1, THE PAPER HALF, by the feeling it gives
At phone size the three gave: 1 "a hushed, glowing doorway"; 2 "quiet, personal, dim"; 3 "a joyful lift, flying
letters". The moment asks for both halves of a hushed whoa, and only 1 has both. The hush comes from stillness, a
near-symmetric frame, a big quiet dark below, a calm sky above and one place to look at a squint. The whoa comes from
half the bridge being paper, lit from inside. "Talking to her" is the climbing light that ends at her phone; the pull
to stay is the dark twin running on through the ring. It is also the picture that only this film could have: the ring
exists because of the twin. And it opens as a bold event: frame 0 an ordinary bridge and its reflection, the question
drops, the reflection's lower half turns to paper from the centre out and lights, the light climbs.
**What it carries from the others** (inside this frame, never a collage): from 3, the paper must read at its source
(sheets peeling up and glowing where the light leaves the water, big enough for a phone); from 2, her stake (the light
ends AT her phone and lights her face).

## For the builder (the frame card comes next; build it whole)
- The ring is the frame's one big shape. The light's source sits inside it at the waterline, under her. The stream ends
  at her phone. The dark twin runs on through the ring. A calm dusk sky holds the caption band. The bottom is one big
  quiet dark of water, alive with a few faint pages and ripples.
- The paper must read at phone size in the build: cream and sepia against the dusk blue, the ledgers' spines with their
  bands, sheets askew with curled corners, writing as a soft texture showing through the lit paper (never ink lines,
  never letters).
- Her: small and simple, but her bowed head, hands and phone must read; her face and forearms lit gold from below.
- Pip: hidden on the houseboat's roof among its pots or in a lit window, off the eye's path.
- **The evaluator's test:** does it give "a hushed, glowing doorway into the city's past, and it is answering her"?
