# Process test 01: the lessons (2026-10-31)

**The owner:** "So the final image is actually okay / So what were the lessons?" (on `hero_r1.png`, the evaluator's
ALMOST; the adjust round was stopped for cost: "almost 2 hours to produce a single image and like 100 dollars").

## What worked
- **Feeling first produced an idea, not a template.** The moment and its feeling were written before any picture; the
  concept (the canal's reflection as the city's twin made of old paper, waking when someone asks) is invented for this
  story, and the still is okay by the owner's eye.
- **Imagine first, then the library** gave three different ideas and a pick by feeling; the library only refined it.
- **The fresh evaluator's first line** ("a hushed, cosy dusk wonder ... a secret found") named the feeling honestly and
  found what holds it back (the water doesn't read as water; the paper twin doesn't show; she doesn't read as the one
  being answered), with a paint-over.
- **Fact discipline held:** the only source is the vendor's page, and its "about 2 % digitized" was flagged as a claim
  the film must not overstate.

## What did not
1. **Cost and time (the main finding).** About 2 hours and about $100 for one still. The build was bespoke engine code
   (a new scene, its own paint bake and light solve, a hand-authored pose; six build rounds, two lost to undocumented
   bake traps). Four agents each started cold. There is no kit to compose a frame from.
2. **The docs still contradict each other.** PIPELINE_V2's "THE LOOP NOW" says compose with the library by code; the
   card says imagine first. LAYOUT's figure sizes contradict "people small and simple". The playbook's "the caption band
   stays light" can't hold at dusk. PEOPLE_DESIGN has two "IN USE" blocks. The playbook and CREATIVE_BRIEF disagree on
   exaggeration. `check_banding` fails any painted sky.
3. **Missing pieces.** No short "from a piece of news" start (only the heavy G0-G1 path); no card for the ideas step; no
   way to resume a stopped build; the people kit had no pose for the moment; the concept's material (paper in water) was
   never proven in colour before the build.
4. **Too much writing.** The evaluator's report ran to about 2,000 words for one verdict.

## What to change (cheapest first)
1. **Budgets:** every step's brief carries a time and cost budget; at the limit it stops and shows what exists.
2. **Fix the contradictions above** (one small doc pass).
3. **A one-page start "From a piece of news"** (facts and what not to claim, the core line, the concept, the moment in
   the card's words), used by both modes.
4. **An ideas card:** three ideas as three different picture devices, thumbnails carrying the scene's key colour
   contrast, and the concept's material proven in colour at phone size before anyone builds.
5. **A cold read inside the build:** a fresh agent sees only the frame at phone size and answers in two lines (what is
   happening, what it feels like) before the builder calls it built. That's cheap, and it would have caught the "well,
   not reflection" and "not her" problems in the build.
6. **A short evaluator report:** the feeling line, the verdict, at most five notes.
7. **The kit** (the biggest lever): reusable world pieces, the people with a pose library, light and paint presets, so
   a still is a short scene description and some tuning, not new code. Two agents per still: the idea, then the build.
