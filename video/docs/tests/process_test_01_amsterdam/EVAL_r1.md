The feeling it gives: a hushed, cosy dusk wonder: a quiet Amsterdam canal at blue hour where something warm and magic glows under the bridge (a ring of light, pages flying up out of the water) while a tiny figure on the bridge bends over her phone; calm, dreamy, "a secret found", not surprise and not news.

# EVAL r1: the hero frame (fresh evaluator, THE FRAME LOOP)

## 1. The picture alone (written before reading CORE, VISION or CARD)

Seen at phone width (390 px wide copy) and at full size, with crops of the person, the ring, the quays, the bow and the
words.

- **Where the eye goes:** first the warm ring under the arch (the brightest, warmest, most central thing), then the
  words in the sky ("answer" in glowing gold), then, on a third look, the woman on the bridge. The thread of light from
  her phone down into the ring is what ties them, but at phone width it is a hair-thin line.
- **What I read in a second:** "something magical glows under a bridge in Amsterdam at dusk". With the words: "now
  answer your questions". Alone, that phrase reads as an order to me ("now answer your questions!"), not as news.
- **What I do not read in a second:** who is asking and who is answering. The woman is about 120 px tall at full size,
  about 45 px at phone width; her phone and the light falling from it are specks. The ring's lower half reads as a
  brick well or tunnel seen from above (a fan of book spines like a sunburst), not as a reflection in water, so "the
  city's library answering from the canal" is a second-or-third-look idea, if it is one at all.
- **Pause-worthy?** The top half, yes, mildly: a lovely dusk sky, gabled houses with warm windows, a lamp, bikes on a
  brick bridge, one warm glow at the centre. The bottom 40 % pulls it down: the left quay wall is a flat grey-brown
  block, the right side is a confused stack (a green cabin with a lit window, a shrub, a grey block with two dark
  windows), the water below the ring is dark with procedural streaks, and the boat's bow at the bottom is two flat
  grey-violet wedges round a black hole.
- **Craft I saw at full size:** the ring's lower half and the glowing pool are aliased (stair-stepped edges on the dark
  silhouettes and on the pages; the "writing" on the pages and pool is a repeated stamp of tiny glyph rows); a
  saturated lavender-blue blob behind the railing left of centre (a second person? a bin?) reads as nothing and sits
  outside the dusk palette; the woman herself is stiff but legible at full size (auburn hair, dark coat, white skirt,
  phone held out over the water).
- **Words:** handwritten face, "now" and "your questions" thin white strokes, "answer" bold gold with a glow; they read
  at phone width, the thin strokes are the weakest part; the block sits well in the sky.

## 2. Beside the brief (read after section 1: CORE "THE MOMENT", VISION, CARD)

- **Wanted** (CARD §1): "a hushed 'whoa', the held breath of seeing something you know (a bridge and its reflection)
  turn quietly impossible: the city remembers, and it is answering her ... warm and full of wonder, the feeling of a
  candle in a dark room". Understood in a second: the water under this street holds its past in old paper, and that
  paper is lighting up and answering her.
- **Given:** the register is right: hushed, warm, a candle in a dark room, never ghostly. Two parts of the wanted
  feeling are missing: the **whoa** (the double take "that reflection is paper" needs the eye to know it is looking at
  a reflection, and it does not: no water reads around or across the ring) and **"answering her"** (she is a small dark
  shape outside the light; the warmth is not personal yet). What I felt, "a secret found", is the wanted feeling with
  its idea unread.

## 3. The four questions

1. **The moment's feeling and idea in a second?** The feeling's temperature, yes; the idea, no. In a second I get
   "a magic glow under a bridge"; "old papers", "the reflection" and "her" come on a second or third look, at full size.
2. **Final production quality?** No. The top half is close (sky, gables, windows, lamp, bridge, the words); the
   bottom 40 % is not finished: stair-stepped edges in the twin and the pool (they step in blocks of about 4 px, a layer
   rendered at low resolution), the water's glints as rows of regular dashes, the left quay's twin a flat block, the
   houseboat's twin a grey box, the bow two flat wedges round a black hole. Beside the owner's references
   (`refs_paint/01_beach_van_dusk.jpg`, `refs_human_scale/02_bus_stop_night.jpg`) the gap is plain: their small figures
   sit inside the light, and their foregrounds are painted and alive.
3. **Worth pausing on?** Half of it. A thumb would slow for the warm ring in the blue evening; the pause has little
   to reward below the bridge, where the paper twin (the second look the card promises) is lost in black.
4. **Of this film's world?** Yes for the place, the light and the gold accent (VISION's canal, bridge, bikes, lamp,
   houseboat, gold only on the pages, the stream, the phone and the word). Not yet for the film's device: VISION's
   whole street's paper twin running on down the water hardly shows in the frame meant to show "the most of this
   world"; only the ring carries it.

## 4. The card's questions, on the built frame

- **§1 moment and feeling:** see §2: right register, missing the whoa and "her".
- **§2 idea:** the ring (arch + reflection) reads at once, and it is a strong, simple picture. "The lower half is
  old paper" reads at full size (spines, page-blocks), not at a glance; on a phone the ring's lower half reads as a
  brick well or a sunburst of blocks.
- **§3 life and action:** the action is in the middle, where the eye goes. Her action (phone held out, receiving)
  is too small and unlit to carry it. The lived-in street is there and quiet (windows, lamp, bikes, tower); the
  houseboat on the right is a confused stack (green cabin, shrub, grey block with two dark windows) that fights a little.
- **§4 the eye:** planned glow -> up the stream to her lit face -> the ring and the twin -> the words. Built: glow ->
  the words -> her. The stream (about 2-3 px wide, about 1 px on a phone) does not carry the eye. One place to look:
  yes.
- **§5 space and scale:** the far canal through the arch is the best depth in the frame. The near plane (bow, water)
  gives no depth: it is black. Small her against the big ring works as an idea once she reads.
- **§6 light, value, colour:** one story light, yes, and it lights the vault's underside well. It does not light her
  (her head is a mid-dark shape against the pale sky, no gold on her face). The near water is black where the card
  and VISION say "real darks, painted, alive, never black" (82 % of the frame below y 1280 is under L* 20, the near
  water about L* 4-5). The brick is a warm red-brown even where the gold cannot reach (far left: hue about 35°), so
  the gold does not fall off like a candle's; the card asked for a cool plum brick warmed only near the light.
- **§7 shapes, edges, paint:** soft round shapes for the wonder, yes (ring, hump, gables). Two paint languages: the
  top half painted, the bottom half procedural (dashes, blocks, aliasing). The flying sheets are white with straight
  printed lines, so they read as new paper, not old.
- **§8 words:** in the band, readable at phone size, the lit "answer" in the pages' gold: good. No words in the
  picture. (Alone, "now answer your questions" reads as an order to the viewer; see §7 below.)
- **§9 motion and sound:** not judged on a still; the plan is sound. On a still the stream's direction (down from the
  phone, or up to it) is unclear; motion will settle it.
- **§10 hand-off:** the gold reaching her phone and face is the hand-off; it does not read yet.
- **§11 test:** my three words at phone size: "a glowing hushed portal". Close to the card's "a hushed, glowing
  doorway", without its "(it is answering her)". Mistakes: captions in the band and readable; nothing that must read
  under the app's bars (the houseboat under the right rail is a mass); no paper edge; no ink lines; no banding I can
  see in the sky at either size.

## 5. Keep (so the adjust round does not lose it)

The ring as the frame's one shape; the calm dusk sky holding the words; the far canal through the arch; the quiet
lived-in street of the top half; the one gold accent and the lit word; the low boat viewpoint, frontal and calm.

## 6. Verdict: ALMOST

The feeling's register is there; specific things hold back the whoa, the "her", and the finish. Each note: what, why,
and the fix in words. The paint-over (`EVAL_r1_paintover.jpg`: r1 marked up, a rough paint-over of values and colours
beside it, both at phone width too) shows 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 roughly; it is a direction, not a frame.

1. **The water must read as water** (what: no mirror sheen, no ripple, no waterline around or across the ring; why:
   the double take needs "this is a reflection" before "and it is paper"; fix: the dusk sky and the lit windows faintly
   mirrored in the near water, soft ripple breaks cutting across the paper half and the twin, a thin light waterline
   where the arch meets its twin).
2. **The paper twin of the street must show** (what: the hanging paper houses sit at about L* 5 in the near water, the
   bridge face's twin is a flat grey-brown block, the houseboat's twin a grey box; why: the picture alone must say "old
   papers", and the twin running on is the film's own device and the frame's second look; fix: the twin painted in
   sepia and umber pages, its gables hanging down the water, catching the gold near the ring and sinking into deep
   violet-umber with distance, never black; the bridge face's twin as long written sheets in courses).
3. **She must be the one answered** (what: about 120 px tall, dark on the pale sky, face unlit, a hair-thin stream;
   why: the warmth must be personal, "it is answering her"; fix: the gold lights her from below (chin, face, hands, coat
   front, a rim on her hair) so she is the second warm spot; a stream bold enough to read on a phone, with the loose
   loops of script the card planned, blooming at the phone).
4. **The paper must look old** (what: bright white sheets with straight lines, a stamped glyph texture in the pool;
   why: "old papers" is the line's subject; white rectangles read as notes or printer paper; fix: parchment and sepia
   sheets, curled, with deckled edges and looping brown script marks, the ledgers' spines and gilt bands catching the
   gold so "books" reads on a phone).
5. **Finish the lower half to the top half's standard** (what: aliased edges in the twin and the pool, regular dash
   glints, the flat bow; why: the hero frame is the film's bar for finish; fix: render those layers at full resolution,
   paint the glints by hand along the ripples, paint the bow as wood that catches the gold on its gunwales).
6. **Cool the brick away from the light** (what: warm red-brown everywhere; why: one candle-gold in a cool dusk is the
   hush; fix: plum-violet brick, warmed only where the gold reaches).
7. **Pip** (what: a lavender-blue blob behind the railing, outside the dusk light; why: a "find Pip" should be a
   pleasure on a second look, not a mistake; fix: give it the dusk's light and a readable shape, or another spot).

## 7. For the orchestrator (not frame fixes)

- Judged alone, the caption "now answer your questions" reads as an order. In the film the subject comes in the
  groups before; as a still (the owner's review, a cover) it does not. Notes 2 and 4 carry "old papers" in the
  picture; if this frame is ever the cover, its words need the subject.
- The builder's own §11 read named the weakest part (her size, the thin stream); a fresh eye agrees and adds the
  reflection read and the black lower half.
