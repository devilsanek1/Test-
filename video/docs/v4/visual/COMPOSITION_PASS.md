## The verdict: "one continuous camera, no cuts" against his reels and the library

**His reels** join every idea with a hard cut through a black line card (RJ1, NOT FOR US) because they are lists; his
one continuous take, reel 03's sheet (RJ4, CORE 4·5·5), is the closest model for a story told in one place. **The
library** ranks RJ4 CORE and the plain cut RJ2 RARE (3·3·2), its condition DIRECTION 1's: at most two F a film, each
on a "But"; the through-line card already said a plain cut "would be allowed on this 'But'".

**Keep the continuous camera for 10 of the 11 joins between shots.** The film is one place, one thread and one
travelling look; its joins sit inside sentences' pauses of 0.32-0.72 s (none at all between S9 and S10), which only a
moving camera can bridge; and "one flowing story, never a bunch of shots stitched together" is the owner's own rule.

**Cut once, on "But" (S6 -> S7, 23.46 s): a plain cut serves better than the written dive.** The reasons:
1. *Story:* "But" is the film's turn and its one new idea; his reels mark each new idea with a cut, and the library's
   turn grammar enters the off-balance by RJ2 (SR18).
2. *Craft:* the written dive runs ~49x back through every step of the planet move in 1.16 s, about two push-throughs'
   work in half their time (J2's measured form is 7x in 1.1 s), so it reads as a whip (J9, NOT FOR US) and repeats
   steps the viewer has just seen.
3. *Attention:* one cut at 23.4 s is a single pattern interrupt where the second half begins (the plan), after 23 s of
   unbroken camera (M12: every cut costs the eye something, so cuts are rationed and related).
4. *Sound:* the cut and the bed's step on "But" make one event (SOUND_SYNC S13); the thread is on both sides (the
   plumes, then the computer's steam: heat let go).
5. *Cost:* no reverse nested chain to build.
**Kept as the owner's alternative (no cut):** one 7x push-through into Helsinki's lit spot (J2, f1373-1442, its midpoint
on "But"), landing on the same S7 frame. The owner decides; nothing else changes either way.

**Considered and not recommended:** a down-the-street S11 (his B1, B2 and E7 in one frame, the water racing up a
receding melt line to the door), reached by a carry (J7) from S10: it would cost two angle changes (into S11, and back
