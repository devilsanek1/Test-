Words on screen

TYPE_GUIDE · picked

  • One hand (Gaegu) for every spoken word, 2-4 words at a time, set in the open space by the subject; pops or builds with the voice, never clipped.
  • The lit stress: the same hand, bolder, bigger, lowercase, softly lit. Never red, boxes or capitals.

On video 4: R2 the hook "Did you know" at frame 0 · R3 "70,000" · R4 "wants", "heat" · R6 "That's a lot of tea." · R7 the "2027" tag · R9 the payoff.

Composition and motion

COMPOSITION_SHOTS · video 4's COMPOSITION_PASS

  • 103 entries: 67 frames, 17 moves, 19 joins; your 41 reel ideas first; ranked CORE, situational or rare by three judges.
  • In every shot: the rule of thirds, a soft near layer, atmospheric layers, one colour accent.
  • Rhythm: shots average 3.5 s or more, 6-9 lead framings per film, a change on the subject every 1.5-3 s.

On video 4: each of the 12 shots leads with one device (leading lines along the warm pipe, the vanishing point in the planned hall); 10 of 11 joins one continuous camera, one plain cut on "But".

Sound sync

SOUND_SYNC

  • Spend sync, don't sprinkle it: a quiet floor; three sounded changes and a late fourth in the first ~2-4 s; rest while the voice explains; ONE release at the payoff, in a pause of 0.8 s or more.
  • A hard sync is one short sound on one visible change, never before it; the picture leads its word by about 0.1 s.

The look

DIRECTION · ORGANIC · PAINT_RECIPES · painter v7 · PALETTE

  • Organic, not geometric: organic shapes from the design stage, edges sharp in places and soft in others, light and colour moving inside shapes, brush marks with intent, near crisp and far painterly.
  • Painted pieces: sky, clouds, water, trees, buildings and ground brushed in layers, each a little softer.
  • Light: one colour key and one story light, big quiet masses.

On video 4: a cold blue snowy night in the low key, one warm accent (the heat), the lit window as the story light; painter v7 sets the look clip's look.

Story flow and the animatic

CRAFT · LAYOUT · G3_ANIMATIC · STORYBOARD_STYLE · the visual script METHOD

  • One flowing story: something carries the eye across every join, at most 2 plain cuts; the warm-water thread on screen 80 % of the time, never gone more than 3 s; one subject at a time.
  • The frame: a band for the words, a zone for the action, the app's buttons kept clear.
  • The animatic: an animated sketchbook, monochrome at 60 fps, every move, join and key action played with real timing; one sound-off and one sound-on watch, one fix round, then to you.
