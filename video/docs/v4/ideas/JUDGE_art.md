# Video 4 ideas: the art director's judgment across the four sequences (fresh judge, 2026-10-31)

My eye: THE ART DIRECTOR. One world, one light, one warm accent, organic painted pieces, whole frames worth pausing
on: how rich, specific and beautiful each world can become when painted with our tools, how imaginative and readable
each picture is, whether every frame belongs to one world. Read first from the four sheets at phone size, in beat order,
before any notes. (Honesty: `ORCHESTRATOR_LOOK.md`, on C and D, was opened in the same listing before the sheets; the
readings below are from the frames.)

## 1. From the frames alone (before any notes)

### A, "The frosted city"
**The film it makes:** a snowy Helsinki night in charcoal greys where every warm thing is the heat. A ribbon of warm
air crosses the harbour; a canyon of building-tall machines heats up until the hall glows; one machine stands beside a
whole block of flats boiling kettles in every window; a woman raises one cup at her window; fans breathe warm plumes
over the frozen sea where an ice fisher sits; the city flings its windows open to catch it; a finger draws the plan
(2027, a line from the machine to a home) in window frost; a door opens in the frost onto the machine; a pipe runs cold
in, warm out; warm lines glow up a street to her window, and your phone faces it. A whole city, told kindly; but the
night has no colour of its own (neutral grey), the grey caption slab sits on every frame like a UI bar, and B9-B10 leave
the city for a pale frost-world.
**The arc of feeling:** B1 calm curiosity (a warm ribbon over a cold harbour; the "wait, what?" left to the words) ->
B2 awe (a canyon of machines, one tiny person) -> B3 heat flooding the same canyon (warm, a little alarm) -> B4 a grin
(one machine beside a block of steaming kettles) -> B5 a smile at home (one cup raised to it) -> B6 a pang (warmth
drifting over the empty ice) -> B7 delight (windows thrown open, arms up) -> B8 tender hope (a plan drawn in frost) ->
B9 a door opens (curious, but into another world) -> B10 a quiet demonstration, pale and close to a diagram -> B11 calm
warmth up a street -> B12 a warm wink (your phone, her lit window).

### B, "The open machine"
**The film it makes:** a misty, pale-skied harbour where a molten seam of warmth runs through the snow towards a
building that is only a white outline. Inside the machines the heat is so fierce a woman throws her coat open; one
machine opens on forty kettles; one mug against a wall of mugs; a fan in the ground lets heat and snow fall upwards into
the night; a whole house leans out for it; a 2027 sign stands where the outline building will be; its door opens on
light; a pipe goes cold to warm through a rack; the seam runs up the street into a window and ends at your phone. The
pale skies give the words a lovely open field; the night beneath them is soft grey mist; the warm seam is the one thing
that glows.
**The arc of feeling:** B1 curiosity (a warm crack in the snow leading to a ghost building) -> B2 hushed awe (a tall
corridor, a tiny figure) -> B3 a laugh of heat (the hall blazing, a woman fanning herself) -> B4 astonishment (the
machine opened: kettles) -> B5 a small smile (one mug, a wall of mugs, in a white void outside the world) -> B6 a quiet
pang (warm sparks rising off a fan into the night) -> B7 delight (a house reaching out) -> B8 hope (2027 on a sign by the
outline) -> B9 leaning in (a door of light) -> B10 clarity (cold to warm) -> B11 cozy (the seam climbs to a window) ->
B12 a warm wink (your phone at the end of the seam).

### C, "The warm current"
**The film it makes:** one deep-blue Helsinki night and one honey-gold warmth we follow. It melts a steaming patch in
the snow of a harbour quay while you read your phone; it is born in a street of computers, swells inside one machine,
turns out to be forty kettles, and then the machine is wearing a giant knitted tea cosy; fans throw it over the
rooftops; the quay's manhole covers light one by one; a chalk outline of a building with a 2027 tag catches the stream;
its door opens; blue water in, gold out; the warm line runs up the snow to a lit classical facade and ends at her
window, your phone in the corner. The cleanest world of the four: one palette, one sky, the words floating in open
night; flat and vector-clean as drawn, so its richness is still all to come.
**The arc of feeling:** B1 curiosity (a warm wound in the snow, your phone) -> B2 cool awe (a street of computers) ->
B3 the heat swelling (a glow inside the machine; abstract) -> B4 playful astonishment (forty kettles inside) -> B5 a
real laugh (the tea cosy) -> B6 a pang (warmth thrown over the roofs) -> B7 a quiet turn (manholes lighting a path; calm
more than a spark) -> B8 hope (a chalk plan with its tag) -> B9 leaning in (a door of light; the frame sparse) -> B10
clarity (blue in, gold out, as a clean diagram) -> B11 cozy relief (a lit facade, the line at its door) -> B12 personal
warmth and the loop (her window, your phone).

### D, "The warmth comes home"
**The film it makes:** one woman and her flat. Her frosted window clears onto the city; her armchair sits in the aisle
of an endless data hall; a dark data centre steams with the snow melted round it; her kitchen floods with forty steaming
kettles and she drinks one cup; from above, a box of fans breathes steam over the roofs; the whole block leans out to
grab ribbons of warm air; across the bay she sees the plan as an outline with a warm line to her; its door opens; a pipe
goes white in, warm out; the line comes up her street; she ends with her socks on a warm radiator, phone in her lap.
The most human film and the warmest ending; the night is near-black and grey, murky through the middle, with a grey
caption slab on every frame.
**The arc of feeling:** B1 cozy curiosity (frost clearing onto the city's windows) -> B2 surreal awe (her armchair among
the giants) -> B3 cold and muted (a steaming building; the heat seen, not felt) -> B4 a laugh (her kitchen full of
kettles) -> B5 a sip and a smile (tea for one) -> B6 a muted pang (a fan box, a white plume) -> B7 delight (the whole
block reaching for warm ribbons) -> B8 hope (the plan across the bay, a warm line to her) -> B9 a door of light (leaning
in) -> B10 a pale demonstration -> B11 the warm line climbs her street (calm) -> B12 warm, personal, a wink (socks on
the radiator).

## 3. The ranking, through the art director's eye

1. **A, "The frosted city":** the richest, most specific world, and its one device is a material of the place, so each
   chapter gets its own picture inside one city (frosted panes, the plan in frost, the frost hall, panes clearing up a
   street) and nearly every frame is a place full of homes. Held back by a quiet hook (B1), a cold, ghostly frost hall
   (B10) and a comparison that takes a second look (B4).
2. **C, "The warm current":** the cleanest one film (one blue night, one honey warmth that travels and always holds the
   eye), the round's best joke (the tea cosy) and a real hook event. But most frames are one glowing object or one line
   on an empty dark field (B3, B8, B9, B10 thin or diagram-like) in a generic city: its richness would have to be added
   after the ideas, the one thing we never do.
3. **D, "The warmth comes home":** the most human film, the round's most surprising picture (her armchair among the
   giants) and the warmest payoff (socks on the radiator). But the concept only holds where she is: the four machine and
   plan exteriors (B3, B6, B9, B10) are grey, empty and from another world, the darks are murky, and her back seen from
   behind repeats.
4. **B, "The open machine":** a real quay (the icebreakers, the turret row, the warm seam in the snow), the most vivid
   heat (B3) and a wry fox. But one soft grey mist over every frame, four frames in one generic hall (B2-B5), a mug in a
   white void (B5), the plan drawn as a white pencil wireframe, and a B8 that reads as B7 plus a sign.

## 4. The one I would make: A, "The frosted city"

It has the most to paint, and all of it belongs together: a snowy harbour city of frosted double windows, sea smoke over
the open channel, the frozen bay with its ice fisher, stucco blocks and a tram, one windowless building at the edge
breathing warm air, and the honey of the heat travelling from it into the windows. The frost gives the film one
signature texture and its colour script (frosted panes turning honey: the world bible's own "Frost & Honey"), and each
chapter changes the picture without leaving the world. The city itself reaches for the heat (B7); the plan is a
fingertip in the frost (B8), the round's best picture of "a plan, coming soon"; one woman's toast (B5), returned to you
(B12), gives it a heart. The base: A's twelve as they stand, B4 kept over B4alt (the city as the measure is A's own
picture; B4alt is the kettles-inside-the-machine picture the other sequences share). The hero frame, the world's bar:
the home view (B1, returning in B12).
