# Video 4 ideas, imagination B: the machine and the mechanism

The sheet: `B_sheet.png` (B-B1 to B-B12 in beat order, then the alternates). Source: `B_src/` (`frames.js` draws every
frame, `lib.js` the pieces, `make_sheet.py` the sheet; the real Helsinki photos behind the world: `B_src/refs/`).
Thumbnails are value sketches: the night in cool greys, ONE warm accent (the heat, honey), the caption band kept as a
light calm field (in the film it is the night's calm band; its value is the key frames' call).

## THE FILM CONCEPT: "The open machine" (one piece missing)

**In one line:** Helsinki already has warm-water pipes under every snowy street, and AI's giant computers already make
heat by the hall-full; the film opens that whole warm machine up for us one door at a time, and shows the one piece
still missing between the machines and the homes being drawn in.

**The world:** one snowy harbour neighbourhood of Helsinki on a winter night, the real Katajanokka quay (from the photos
in `B_src/refs/`): Jugendstil stone blocks with corner turrets and steep tin roofs, the green tram under lamps hung on
wires across the street, shovelled snow banks, the state icebreakers moored along the quay (pale decks over black
hulls), Uspenski's onion domes dark on their rock, the frozen harbour with a winter swimmer's ice hole by a ladder, and at
the quay's end an empty snowy lot by the water. Everything cold is night-blue and snow-white; the only warm colour in
the film is the heat.

**The central device: the machine seen through warm openings, and the one opening still missing.** Every frame's place
to look is one warm opening onto the mechanism in a cold, shut winter: a hall's big door, one computer's cabinet door, a
soft thaw in the snow over a pipe, a home's window, your phone's screen. The heat writes its own route: where it flows,
the snow melts into a dark wet line and the manholes puff steam; where the snow lies untouched, the heat isn't getting
through. Between the city's warm lines and the harbour lies one stretch of untouched snow: the missing piece. The plan is
the only thing in the film drawn instead of painted: pale pencil lines over that snow (painted = real, pencil = planned).

**The thread's journey (the heat):** born in the giant computers (their backs go honey-hot) -> measured (one cabinet
opens on forty boiling kettles) -> thrown out the wrong way (roof fans blow it up into the snowing night, where it
carries the snowflakes UP and is gone) -> wanted (the city's own warm lines race through the streets to the water's edge
and stop) -> drawn in (a pencil hall and a pencil pipe over the empty lot, a 2027 tag) -> carried (a drawn door opens;
inside, cool water runs in and honey water runs out) -> home (through the drawn piece, along a soft thaw under the
street, up into one window's radiator) -> in your hand (your chat below that window: frame 0's view, the loop).

**The arc of feeling:** a cozy snowy quay lit by an odd warm machine (curiosity) -> a cathedral of giant computers (awe)
-> the heat pouring off them (warm, a touch of alarm) -> forty kettles inside one (playful astonishment) -> one cup of
tea (a smile, a breath) -> warmth blown into the night, snow falling upward (a pang) -> the city's warm lines racing to
the water (a spark of delight) -> the missing piece drawn in pale pencil (hope) -> a drawn door opening on warm light
(leaning in) -> cool in, honey out (clarity) -> warmth under the snow, arriving home (cozy relief) -> your chat under a
warm window, a toast from inside (a wink; back to frame 0).

**One view is the stage** (the library's N14, the deliberate match): the home view along the quay (B1) returns with ONE
change each time: the plan drawn on its empty lot (B8), and you in it (B12 = the loop). The machine's inside (B2-B5,
B9-B10) is always seen through a door, so the snowy city stays in reach.

**Truth** (`video/docs/v5/research/factlock.md`): the new data center is only ever a plan: B1 and B12 show the wish
(the voice: "wants", "could soon"), B8 shows it is a drawing with a year on it, B9-B11 show how it WOULD work inside
pencil walls; B2-B6 are data centers in general. One cabinet = forty kettles (FL 2.2). The heat goes into a radiator,
never a tap. No company name or logo anywhere; the plan's site is a generic empty lot, never a real one.

**People:** the one person near the camera is the resident, a young woman (walking home in B1, at the rock's railing in
B7, in her warm window in B11-B12); the one technician stays small in the hall (B2-B5); the one "you" is POV only (B12).
Pip (hidden, for G3): on an icebreaker's rail (B1, B8, B12), in a tram window (B11).

## The beats (one idea each, beat order; times from `voice_words.json`)

Each: the idea, what the viewer sees / gets / feels, how it hands on; then its card in short (feeling, light, the eye,
the library devices it already uses, the words).

**B-B1 · The warm line** (0.13-3.92) "Did you know Helsinki wants AI to heat seventy thousand homes?"
Sees: the Katajanokka quay at night from a person's eye: icebreakers moored on the left, Jugendstil blocks on the right,
snow falling, the resident walking home; at the quay's end by the water a hall of giant computers, its big door open on
rows of small lights, steam rolling off its roof; a honey line races out of its door under the snow along the quay
(manholes puffing) and turns into the blocks, whose windows light warm, then the city's across the ice. Gets: AI could
heat Helsinki's homes. Feels: "wait, what?". Frame 0's event: the hall's lights blink on at "AI" (1.5 s), the line fires
on "heat" (2.14 s), the windows pop warm in a wave on "seventy thousand homes". Hands on: the line leads the eye back
into the hall's open door (B2).
Card: cozy and curious; a night of cool greys, the warm points spreading; eye: the one near window -> the line -> the
lit door at the quay's end; devices: B1 leading lines, B2 one point closed by the hall (E7), E1 her small figure, E2 the
lit window; words: "70,000 / homes?" (R3).

**B-B2 · A cathedral of computers** (4.28-8.22) "AI actually runs on giant computers, in buildings called data centers."
Sees: from a data center's open loading door (snow on the sill, flakes blowing in) an aisle runs away between walls of
giant computers twice a person's height, thousands of small lights, cable trays overhead, the floor shining; the
technician walks small down the aisle; far at its end another door stands open on the snowy night and a few warm
windows. Gets: AI is real machines, huge, in a building. Feels: a little awe. Hands on: she stops halfway, where the
aisle behind the machines runs hot (B3).
Card: awe; cold even light, a long tunnel of value; eye: the far open door (the vanishing point) via the rows, then her;
devices: B2 one point, D2 scale, C1 the door's frame, E7 the closed vista, D7 the repetition; words: "giant computers," /
"data centers."

**B-B3 · Too hot for snow** (8.76-10.64) "Those computers get really hot."
Sees: the hot aisle behind the machines: both walls of grilles glow honey, the air shimmers up to the vents; the
technician in the middle pulls off her beanie, her hair lifting in the hot draught; flakes blown in behind her turn to
drops and puffs before they reach the machines. Gets: they make heat, a lot. Feels: warm, a touch of alarm. Hands on: she
turns to one computer's front door (B4).
Card: heat rising; the warmest frame so far, honey on both walls; eye: her between the glowing walls; devices: B4 tunnel,
E1 the figure where the lines meet; words: "really / hot." (R4, "hot" lit).

**B-B4 · Forty kettles in one** (10.96-17.34) "To put it into perspective, just one of these computers makes as much heat
as FORTY kettles, boiling non-stop."
Sees: one giant computer's front door swung wide; inside, where its trays should be, forty electric kettles in eight
rows of five, all boiling, lids rattling, forty spouts of steam rolling up into one cloud; the technician leans back to
take it in; the rest of the row stands shut. On "To put it into perspective," the door swings; on "FORTY" the kettles
are counted on, row by row. Gets: one computer = forty boiling kettles. Feels: playful astonishment. Hands on: the
steam rolls down to one mug (B5).
Card: astonishment; the cabinet a lit, steaming opening in a dark row; eye: the kettle wall, then her; devices: C1 frame
in a frame, D7 "how many", D2 her scale; words: "40" (R3) / "kettles," then "boiling non-stop."

**B-B5 · One cup** (17.76-18.84) "That's a lot of tea."
Sees: closer: the forty kettles soft and steaming behind; in front, the technician raises ONE mug, its tea-bag tag over
the rim, one curl of steam: a toast to the wall of kettles. Gets: forty kettles' worth, one cup. Feels: a smile and a
breath. Hands on: the kettles' steam rises out of the top of the frame, up through the roof (B6).
Card: a smile; calmer, the kettles a soft pattern; eye: the dark mug on the light steam; devices: D7 with its ONE break
(the mug), A2 the golden box; words: "That's a lot / of tea."

**B-B6 · Snow falling up** (19.26-22.88) "Normally, data centers just use big fans to blow that heat outside."
Sees: on the hall's roof at night, a row of huge round fans blows the warm air straight up; above them the falling snow
is swept back UP and melts away; the warm plumes thin into the dark; beyond the roof's edge a snowy coast with other
halls far off, each sending up its own plume, and far across the water Helsinki's small warm lights. Gets: normally
that heat is just thrown away into the sky. Feels: a small pang. Hands on: the plume drifts toward Helsinki's lights,
then the plain cut on "But" (B7).
Card: a pang; dark and wide, the warm only at the fans' mouths, fading to grey; eye: the nearest plume rising, then the
far lights; devices: D1 negative space, Z1 the extreme wide (the far halls), E6 layers; words: "big fans" / "blow that
heat outside."

**B-B7 · The city reaches** (23.46-25.22) "But Helsinki WANTS that heat."
Sees: from the terrace on Uspenski's rock, the resident at the railing looks down over Katajanokka's snowy roofs to the
harbour; under the snow the city's own warm pipes light up as honey lines racing down every street to the quay, and
stop at the water's edge, pointing across the ice at the plume of thrown-away heat. Gets: Helsinki's own pipes want that
heat. Feels: the turn, a spark of delight, the city reaching. Hands on: the lines lead down to the empty lot by the water
(B8).
Card: delight; the night city dark, its streets white, the honey racing; eye: the lines converging on the quay's edge,
then the plume beyond; devices: B3 radiating, N12 the high angle (the film's one), E10 her over the shoulder; words:
"But Helsinki / wants / that heat." (R4, "wants" lit).

**B-B8 · The missing piece, drawn** (25.54-27.96) "The city has a plan to use it, starting next year."
Sees: the home view again: the lot at the quay's end is empty snow, the city's honey line stops short of it; pale pencil
lines draw themselves over the snow (a big hall's outline, a dashed pipe from the line's end to its door), and on a
survey stake a paper tag swings: "2027". Gets: a real plan, not built yet, starting next year. Feels: hope and
anticipation. Hands on: the drawn door is the next thing to move (B9).
Card: hope; the calmest night, the pale drawing the lightest thing below the band; eye: the tag, then the drawn hall at
the line's end; devices: N14 (B1's view, one change), Z4 the paper, E9 the camera holds while the drawing travels; words:
"a plan" / "starting next year." and the tag "2027".

**B-B9 · A drawn door opens** (28.34-29.56) "So how would that work?"
Sees: closer, the drawn hall's big door slides open and warm light spills over the snow from a painted inside; the
resident stands small at the threshold, leaning in. Gets: we are about to see how. Feels: leaning in. Hands on: we step
through (B10).
Card: leaning in; the brightest opening of the film in a pale drawn wall; eye: the opening, her silhouette in it;
devices: E2 the lit opening, B4 the portal; words: "So how would / that work?"

**B-B10 · Cool in, honey out** (29.56-33.98) "A big new data center would take the heat out of its computers with water
in pipes."
Sees: inside the drawn hall (its walls still pencil), a row of giant computers; the nearest stands open: a cold, frosted
pipe brings cool water in at its foot, winds over every shelf of chips, and leaves at its top as a honey pipe with a
breath of steam; every computer's honey pipe joins one big honey pipe running out through the drawn wall. On "take the
heat out" the cool water runs in; on "with water in pipes" it leaves warm. Gets: water in pipes carries the heat out of
the computers. Feels: clarity and satisfaction, "oh, that's how". Hands on: the big honey pipe leads out through the wall
(B11).
Card: clarity; the clearest, least crowded frame, cool below, warm above; eye: the open computer, its pipe rising from
cold to warm; devices: Z3 the medium, C3 level change, E8 the accent travelling up; words: "with water / in pipes." The
one cool note of the film (the cold water) lives here.

**B-B11 · Under the snow, into a home** (34.46-37.76) "Then pipes under the streets carry that heat into people's homes."
Sees: the snowy street before a Jugendstil block: a soft thaw opens along the street like a warm seam in the snow,
showing the honey pipe in the earth; a green tram slides over it, a dog sniffs a steaming manhole; the pipe climbs the
block's wall in a soft thawed seam to the radiator under one window, which glows, the resident inside. Gets: pipes under
the streets carry the heat into homes. Feels: cozy relief. Hands on: the warmth rises to the window, and we step back
into the street below it (B12).
Card: cozy relief; the street dark and soft, two warm things, the seam and the window; eye: the seam leads in and up to
the window; devices: C3 level change (pipe low, window high, the light handing over), B1 leading lines, E2 the lit window;
words: "pipes under the streets" / "into people's homes."

**B-B12 · Your chat, a warm window** (38.48-42.24) "So your chatbot could soon heat an apartment in Helsinki."
Sees: the home view once more, from where you stand: your gloved hand holds your phone at the bottom left, a chat bubble
popping; at your feet the honey line runs in the snow to the block; above, the one window glows a step warmer and the
resident in it raises her mug (the cup from B5, now hers); at the quay's end the hall's lights blink. Gets: your chat ->
the computers -> the water -> the pipes -> this home. Feels: personal warmth and a wink. Hands on: the loop: the world
un-paints into frame 0 (B1).
Card: warm and personal; your screen's cool light low, the window's honey high; eye: the bubble -> the line -> the
window; devices: E11 the POV, N14 (B1's view, one change: you), E2 the lit window; words: "So your chatbot / could
soon" -> "heat" (lit) -> "an apartment / in Helsinki."
