**The job:** for each of the 12 beats, imaginative picture ideas a regular viewer reads in a second, each drawn as ONE

kettles; the tea; the water carrying warmth under the snow), never a metaphor that needs explaining or contradicts the
facts. Each idea: a title, one line (what the viewer sees and gets), the thumbnail.

to PNG; see `video/scripts/type/README.md`) or with Python; one render at a time; starts no agents.
