picture say what its line says, in the voice's order, true to the facts (a plan, starting next year; no company names),

## 1. From the frames alone (the four final sheets at phone size, in beat order, before any notes)

### A, "The Frosted City"

of light sweeps across the city's blocks (B1); a street whose buildings are giant computers (B2) goes hot and steams, a
man with his hand to his brow (B3); one black machine stands beside a whole apartment block whose windows are steaming
kettles (B4); a woman raises her cup to that block through her window (B5); fans breathe warm clouds that drift off over
the frozen bay past a lone figure (B6); people throw their windows open and reach up (B7); a finger draws "2027" and a
line from a plant to a house in the window frost (B8); a frosted door opens on the machine corridor (B9); a pipe runs
through a machine, a frosty woman touches it (B10); warm lines run down both kerbs of a street into the doors (B11);
your phone's chat bubble and her one warm window, in the same city view as B1 (B12). I understood every line in order,
but mostly through the caption: the pictures are calm views of a place more than events, and the first frame does not
show "AI heats homes" or a "wait, what?" without the words.
**The arc of feeling:** B1 calm, a little curious (no jolt) -> B2 awe (good: a tiny man in a canyon of machines) -> B3
heat (good: the same canyon gone warm) -> B4 astonishment, mild (the kettles are small marks in windows) -> B5 a quiet

kerbs, a tram, a dog walker) -> B12 the B1 view comes back with her window and your phone: it loops.

## 5. The ranking (the story editor's eye)
1. **B, "The open machine":** with C it says the most lines in the voice's order, and its gaps are fixes on the frames,
   not in the ideas; its device is the fact itself (the plan is the drawn piece missing between the city's warm line and
   the machines); the clearest "one computer = forty kettles", the clearest "oh, that's how", and the turn and the plan

   the story turns (a path of manhole lights is calm by its idea), the awe and the pang are soft, and one navy key

3. **D, "The warmth comes home":** the truest arc and the warmest payoff (B1's cold chair answered by B12's socks on the

   true loop; but it is calm views more than events: a beam for a hook, a second look for the kettles, the mechanism in
