# Video 4 ideas, imagination D: one home, one person

The sheet: `D_sheet.png` (D-B1 to D-B12 in beat order, then the alternates); its source: `D_src/` (`frames.py` with `kit.py`
writes one SVG page per frame; `bash render_all.sh` renders them one at a time with `video/scripts/type/render.mjs`
and lays out the sheet with `sheet.py`). Thumbnails are value
sketches in the night's cool greys with ONE warm accent (honey: the heat and the home), the caption seat a light band.

## The film concept: "The warmth comes home"

**The world.** One snowy winter night in Katajanokka, Helsinki's harbour neighbourhood, as it really is (from winter
photos of its streets): Jugend apartment blocks shoulder to shoulder, the green-and-cream number 4 tram in the snow,
snowbanks, bikes and cars under snow, Uspenski's onion domes over the roofs, the icebreakers moored at the quay, the
frozen bay. Her home: a top-floor corner flat with a deep old double window over the bay, a cast-iron radiator under the
sill, a small kitchen with its own window. Across the bay, at the city's edge, an empty snowy lot by the water: where the
plan will be drawn.

**The person, the one "you".** A young woman at home for the evening (an oversized knit, wool socks, her phone, a mug of
tea), always seen from behind or in profile at a real size, acting with her pose. She is the measure of every beat: the
big system always stands next to her one small, cozy life.

**The central device: her evening acts it out first.** Every big thing is met in one of her small things: her chat is
the AI (B1-B2), her kettle measures the heat (B4-B5), her open window lets it out like the fans do (B6), her outstretched
hand reaches for it (B7), her window frames the plan (B8), her street carries the pipes (B11), her radiator receives it (B12). The camera
leaves her home only to follow the heat, and every beat is either seen from her window or keeps her home in the frame (the
far warm city through the hall's door, her window at the foot of the big rise, her window at the end of the hot pipe). Her
window is the film's gate: we look out through it, leave through it, and the heat comes home to the radiator under it.

**The thread's journey (the heat).** Born in a giant computer (steam, the snow that can't settle on its building), shown
in her kitchen as forty boiling kettles, let out of her window and blown into the night by data centres' fans everywhere,
reached for by her, planned across the bay in pale chalk, taken out of the computers by water (frosted blue pipe in,
steaming honey pipe out), carried up her street under the snow (the melt line, a manhole's warm puff, windows warming one
by one), arriving in the radiator under her socked feet.

**The arc of feeling.** A frosted window that clears onto a warming city ("wait, what?") -> awe over a city of machines ->
alarm at a building the snow can't touch -> a laugh in a kitchen of forty kettles -> a sip, a breath -> the pang of warmth
floating away past her window -> delight as she reaches out for it -> hope across the bay -> leaning in -> "oh, that's how"
-> relief as the warmth comes up her street -> her socks on a warm radiator, her chat in her hand: the wink. Then the
frost again: the loop.

**Light and colour.** The night is cold (blue-grey values); the one warm accent is the heat and the home, and it travels
with the thread: the radiator and the city's windows (B1), the far city through the hall's door (B2), the steam's root
(B3), forty little kettle lights (B4-B5), the plumes' roots (B6), the plume's tip at her fingers (B7), the plan's pipe (B8-B10),
the melt line (B11), her radiator and socks (B12). Each frame's darkness follows its feeling: dark and curious (B1),
stark (B3), airy and light (B4-B5), vast and quiet (B6), clear (B10), warm (B12).

**The two cities.** B2 looks down on a cold city of machines (walls of blue-white lights, tiny people); B11 looks down
from her window on her warm street. The heat travels from the first to the second.

**The joins, from the frames** (the shot plan writes the camera later): B1 -> B2 her phone's lit screen becomes the hall's
far door (J4, a match on light) · B2 -> B3 a pull-out through that door into the snow (J2) · B3 -> B4 the steam fills
the lens and clears into her kitchen's steam, on "To put it into perspective" (J8) · B4 -> B5 a push in to her (C) ·
B5 -> B6 she pushes the window open and the camera rises with the steam: the film's ONE big move (I6) · B6 -> B7 the
one plain cut, on "But" (F) · B7 -> B8 the same camera, she settles on the sill (RJ4) · B8 -> B9 a push across the bay
to the plan's door (RM4) · B9 -> B10 through the door (J2) · B10 -> B11 the hot pipe's line becomes the melt line in
the same spot (J3) · B11 -> B12 a pull back from her window into her room (I4) · B12 -> B1 the room un-builds into frame
0's drawing state (RJ3). The home is ~23 s of the 42.24 s (B1, B4, B5, B7, B8, B11, B12); two scale moments (B2, B6),
never neighbours; 11 different lead devices in 12 shots, one RARE each (N12 in B2, B6's diagonal in B7).

## The beats (one idea each; times from `video/public/v4/voice_words.json`)

### D-B1 "The frost clears onto seventy thousand windows" (0.13-3.92)
**Sees, gets, feels:** from behind her armchair in her dark flat, a young woman reads her chat; the old radiator under the
window warms fin by fin, the frost melts up the glass, and across the bay the city's windows warm one after another:
a chat, a radiator, seventy thousand homes in one cozy picture; "wait, what?".
- **Card.** Feeling: curiosity inside coziness (a cold window becoming warm). Sound off: her phone (AI), the radiator
  warming (heat), the city's windows (homes). Life: snow falling, her tea steaming on the sill, the icebreakers' deck
  lights, a far crane. Eye: her phone (cool) -> the radiator warming (the honey) -> up through the clearing glass to the
  wave of windows. Space: the camera where a person stands behind her chair; near (her, the chair), middle (the sill,
  Katajanokka's snowy roofs, Uspenski's onion domes as a far silhouette), far (the bay, the city). Light: night, the
  window's cool light rimming her hair and shoulders; dark and curious, the window the lit opening. Words: the band in
  the window's top pane. Devices: C1 frame within a frame + E2 the lit opening (her window), E10 the "you" from
  behind, D7 one among many (her window among the city's). Motion and sound: frame 0 paints on, her phone lights first;
  a soft "ding" on the radiator's first warm fin (~1.9 s, the hook's sound in the first second); the frost melts and the
  wave runs on "seventy thousand homes".
- **Into B2:** the phone's lit screen grows into the hall's far door.

### D-B2 "Her chat's other end: a city of machines" (4.28-8.22)
**Sees, gets, feels:** from a catwalk high in a data hall, rows of giant computers rise like a city at night, walls of
cold lights converging on a far door open to the snow, tiny people in the aisles; through the door, across the bay, warm
windows: AI is a real, huge place; a little awe.
- **Card.** Eye: the far door (the vanishing point and the lit opening) -> a tiny figure in the aisle -> the walls of
  lights. Scale: rows twice a house high against people a finger tall; the world going on past every edge. Light: cold
  and dark, the only warmth the city through the door (home, the measure). Words: the band in the hall's dark upper
  space. Devices: B2 the vanishing point + E7 the closed vista, D2 scale with E1 tiny figures, N12 the high angle (its one
  use; it rhymes with B11). Motion: lights blinking, the walkers.
- **Into B3:** the camera pulls out through the door into the snowy night.

### D-B3 "The building the snow can't touch" (8.76-10.64)
**Sees, gets, feels:** outside, the long windowless building sits in deep white snow, but its roof is bare and wet, steam
pours off its vents warm at the root, icicles drip and the ground round it has melted dark: those computers get really
hot; warm, with a touch of alarm.
- **Card.** Eye: the steam's warm roots -> the open loading door with the giants' lights -> the dark melted ring. Light:
  stark (black building, white world, the hardest contrast of the film). Devices: N7 figure and ground (a hot dark mass
  in a white field), D1 the white field. A snowy fence is the near hint; far left, the city's warm windows.
- **Into B4:** the steam fills the lens and clears into her kitchen's steam.

### D-B4 "Forty kettles in her kitchen" (10.96-17.34)
**Sees, gets, feels:** to put it into perspective, one of those computers' heat comes home as forty electric kettles
boiling at once in her small kitchen (counters, stove, table, chairs, floor, the fridge top), each with its little warm
"on" light, the window fogged white, and her in the middle with one mug; playful astonishment.
- **Card.** Eye: her (the one break in the pattern) -> the rows of kettles -> forty small warm lights. Light: airy and
  light, steam everywhere under the pendant lamp: the funniest frame is the lightest. Words: the band sits in the steam
  under the ceiling (a natural calm, light field). Devices: D7 the pattern with one break, the kitchen's one-point
  perspective, RM8b the build (the kettles pop on by rows on "forty", lids rattling from "boiling"). The truth stays the
  voice's: forty kettles is the comparison, the computer is not in her kitchen.
- **Into B5:** a push in to her.

### D-B5 "Tea for one" (17.76-18.84)
**Sees, gets, feels:** closer: among the steaming crowd she takes one quiet sip from her one small mug, a tea bag's tag
over its rim, eyes closed: that's a lot of tea; a smile, a breath.
- **Card.** Eye: her profile and the mug (dark on the light steam: the counterchange). Devices: N9 the close (one person,
  one mug, one quiet backing), D1. Soft, light, calm: the film breathes before the pang.
- **Into B6:** behind her she pushes the window open; the steam pours out and the camera rises with it.

### D-B6 "Out her window, up into the night" (19.26-22.88)
**Sees, gets, feels:** the film's one big move: the camera rises with her kitchen's steam over Katajanokka's snowy roofs
and lands high over the snowy land, where a data centre's big roof fans throw warm columns up into the cold, merging into
one plume that thins to nothing; more plumes to the horizon; her small open window at the bottom; a small pang.
- **Card.** Eye: the fans (their warm roots) -> the plume -> the far plumes -> her window (the measure). The fans read
  at phone size on "big fans" (~21.2 s; "fans" settled as machines). Light: vast, quiet, low contrast; the warmth only
  where it leaves. Devices: I6 the rise-over (the one big move) landing on Z1 the extreme wide with E6 the atmospheric
  layers. Generic boxes, no country, no name (FL 8.2).
- **Into B7:** the one plain cut, on "But".

### D-B7 "She reaches for it" (23.46-25.22)
**Sees, gets, feels:** back home, from behind: she leans out of her open kitchen window into the snowfall, one hand on the
frame, the other arm stretched far toward the nearest plume, which bends to her hand, warm where it meets her fingers:
Helsinki wants that heat; a spark of delight.
- **Card.** Eye: her outstretched hand and the plume's warm tip. Devices: B6 the diagonal (her arm: the film's turn),
  C1 the window. Light: the kitchen's light behind her, the night beyond; the warm accent at her fingertips.
- **Into B8:** the same camera: she settles onto the sill with her tea.

### D-B8 "Across the bay, a plan" (25.54-27.96)
**Sees, gets, feels:** she leans on the sill; across the bay, on an empty snowy lot at the city's edge, a big new data
centre stands drawn in pale chalk (no fill: the night shows through it), survey stakes in the snow, its pipe drawn in
warm chalk along the shore toward her: real, coming soon, not built; hope and anticipation.
- **Card.** Painted = real, chalk = planned (FL 6.7): no name, no real site, never a landmark spot. Eye: the chalk
  building -> its warm pipe line -> her. Devices: C1 (her window), E10 (her from behind), RM8 the line drawn live (the
  one place after frame 0 where a draw-on is the beat: the chalk draws on, the pipe last, on "next year").
- **Into B9:** a push across the bay to the plan's door.

### D-B9 "The plan's door opens" (28.34-29.56)
**Sees, gets, feels:** up close on the chalk front of the plan, a tall drawn door swings open and a cool light spills over
the snow; inside, chalk giants with their pipes, one already drawn warm: so how would that work? Leaning in.
- **Card.** Devices: B4 the tunnel (a threshold waiting to be pushed through), RM4 a slow push. Through the chalk walls,
  the night and her city's far windows stay visible.
- **Into B10:** through the door.

### D-B10 "Blue in, honey out" (29.56-33.98)
**Sees, gets, feels:** inside the chalk walls stands one real giant computer; a frosted pale pipe brings cold water in at
its foot (icicles on it) and a honey pipe carries it out of its top, steaming, down through the chalk wall and away
across the shore toward the city, where one window glows: water in pipes takes the heat out; "oh, that's how".
- **Card.** The water stays inside its pipes, never touching the machine's insides. Eye: the cold pipe -> the giant ->
  the hot pipe -> its line to her far window. Devices: E9 the camera holds while the water travels the line (RM1): the
  cold runs in on "take the heat out" (~31.1 s), the hot runs out on "with water in pipes" (~32.7-34.0 s); B1 leading
  lines (the hot pipe leads to her window). Clear, mid-key contrast between the two pipes.
- **Into B11:** the hot pipe's line becomes the melt line in the same spot.

### D-B11 "Up her street, under the snow" (34.46-37.76)
**Sees, gets, feels:** from her window high above her street: the snow over the pipe has melted into a long dark wet line
with a warm sheen, running up from the harbour (the chalk plan tiny across the bay) to her door; a manhole breathes warm
steam, the number 4 tram comes up the street, two people walk home, and windows on both sides warm as the line passes
under them; cozy relief.
- **Card.** Eye: the melt line's front at the manhole -> the windows warming -> her (bottom left). Devices: the BALCONY
  dose (the owner's own cozy reference, a street seen from a 4th-5th floor), B1 leading lines, D7 the windows. It answers
  B2's cold city with her warm one. Life passes sideways and slow: the tram, the walkers, the snow.
- **Into B12:** the camera pulls back from her window into her room.

### D-B12 "Socks on a warm radiator" (38.48-42.24)
**Sees, gets, feels:** B1's room again, warm now: the frost gone, the city's windows warm across the bay, the radiator hot,
her wool socks crossed up on it while a warm reply pops in her chat: your chatbot could soon heat an apartment, and this
one is hers; personal warmth and a wink.
- **Card.** Devices: N14 the deliberate match (B1's frame, one change: the warmth), E2 + E8 the film's warmest step at the
  payoff, the chat in her hand. Sound: the bubble's pop on "chatbot", a warm tick of the radiator on "apartment"; the
  release in the 0.9 s hold. The loop: the camera eases back to B1's framing and the room un-builds into frame 0's state
  (frost, a cold radiator, the phone lighting).

## The alternates (the beats I am least sure of)

- **D-B2alt "Her phone, lined up with it":** she holds her phone up to the window like a viewfinder; on its screen the
  data centre across the bay, close and huge; the real building sits small across the water. The lens at its purest
  (seen from her home), but the giants are only seen on a screen, and it puts an existing (generic, unnamed) data centre
  in her view.
- **D-B7alt "The whole block reaches":** outside, a Jugend facade at night, every window thrown open, her and her
  neighbours leaning out into the warm steam drifting past: the city itself reaching for it. Funnier and more "Helsinki",
  but strangers, a frontal grid (the key frame would turn and vary it) and no longer one person.
- **D-B8alt "She draws it on the steamed glass":** the kitchen window fogged by the kettles; with a fingertip she draws a
  building and a pipe on the fog, and through the cleared lines the night shows, lined up with the empty lot across the
  bay. Personal and playful (a plan IS a drawing), but it can read as her daydream more than the city's real plan.
- **D-B11alt "Her building ends the street":** at eye level up the snowy street, the melt line runs from our feet to her
  door, the manhole steams, the tram passes, and her window glows high on the building that closes the street. The
  human eye-level view and a closed vista ending on home, but it loses "seen from her window" and the rhyme with B2.

## People, Pip, truth
- **Her, the one "you":** a 20-30-year-old in an oversized knit and wool socks; a messy high bun is her silhouette from
  behind; always from behind or in profile, open eyes looking away (closed only for the sip, B5). The thumbnails' figure
  is a stand-in: the key frames draw her with the people in use (`PEOPLE_DESIGN.md` top). Strangers stay tiny (B2's
  aisles, B11's walkers).
- **Pip (spots for G3, not drawn):** B1 and B12 on a dormer ledge of the roofs below her window; B8 at an icebreaker's
  rail; B11 in a lit window across the street; none in the hall, the closes, the rise or the plan.
- **Truth:** no names or logos; the plan only ever chalk (never built), its site generic at the city's edge; "next year"
  said by the voice, no 2027 drawn; forty kettles is the voice's comparison; the water stays in its pipes; Uspenski only
  a far silhouette (red brick with gold onions when coloured, never the white cathedral).

## The look (my pass over the sheet at phone size, in order, as one film)
Redrawn before this sheet: B1's figure three times (she now reads as a young woman from behind, her bun the signature)
and its radiator (warming fin by fin instead of one loud orange slab); B2 reworked from the idea twice (a corridor of dots
read as nothing: now a city of machines from above, with the rhyme to B11); B6 rebuilt so the fans read (it looked like
smoke from fires); B7 restaged (the steam sat on her head like a fire: now one clear reach); B8's plan made big enough to
read; B10's cold pipe made as present as the hot one; B11's black void filled with her side of the street; B12 made
closer and unmistakably warm; stress set in the lit hand, never in capitals. Still the weakest, honestly: B7's delight is
mild (the pose reads as reaching more than joy); B12's socks are lumpy; B2's warm door is small at phone size; the hook's
events (the frost melting, the wave of windows) live in motion, and the still only hints at them.
