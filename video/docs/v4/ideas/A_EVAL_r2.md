## 1. The feeling each thumbnail gives (picture alone, written first)

- **A-B1** The feeling it gives: a hushed, cold winter night across a frozen bay, one block of the city glowing warm
  through the snow; calm and a little lonely, a gentle curiosity rather than a jolt (a pretty postcard; the lone figure
  is too small to carry a feeling).
- **A-B2** The feeling it gives: cold awe at scale; a tiny person dwarfed in a long dark canyon of towering black
  cabinets that read at a glance as night skyscrapers with lit windows; clinical, impersonal, a bit eerie.
- **A-B3** The feeling it gives: the same canyon turned stifling: everything glows amber, steam billows overhead, the
  little figure wipes his brow; close, heavy heat, mildly comic ("phew").
- **A-B4** The feeling it gives: whimsical and warm; a whole block where every window holds a steaming kettle, and one
  dark machine-tower in front puffs a cloud that matches theirs; a playful comparison, cozy, a smile (the kettles are
  small at phone size, the read leans on the caption).
- **A-B5** The feeling it gives: cozy, wry intimacy; a woman at home, back to us, lifts her tea toward the window of
  glowing kettles like a toast; warm lamp on one side, cold night on the other; a smile, the human scale after the
  machines (her body is a soft grey mass).
- **A-B6** The feeling it gives: quiet waste and loneliness; three fans on a dark building puff warm steam that turns
  grey and drifts off over cold homes while a lone ice-fisher sits on a huge empty frozen bay; melancholy, a bit bleak
  (at a glance it could be factory smoke).
- **A-B7** The feeling it gives: joy and welcome; a whole block throws its warm windows open, people with arms up
  cheering, one window still frosted, a person in the street raising a cup; communal, celebratory, hopeful.
- **A-B8** The feeling it gives: tender, private hope; a fingertip draws "2027" and a little plan (a box joined by a
  line to a home) in the frost of a window, the lines glowing warm; a promise, a secret, a small smile; quiet and still.
- **A-B9** The feeling it gives: wonder and invitation; a frosted door swings open, its frame glowing warm, onto a
  bright corridor drawn in frost; a magic-portal "come and see" moment; the strongest surprise on the sheet.
- **A-B10** The feeling it gives: hushed, cold and technical; a dark corridor drawn in thin white lines, one warm pipe
  and one pale pipe along the floor; calm, a little empty, more blueprint than place (at phone size: mostly dark, a few
  thin lines and one warm diagonal).
- **A-B11** The feeling it gives: homecoming and everyday warmth; a snowy street of blocks with warm windows, glowing
  lines along both pavements into lit doorways, a tram coming, a dog-walker; comfortable, ordinary life; calm, almost
  static.
- **A-B12** The feeling it gives: a quiet, warm resolution; the opening city now glowing in many windows, one opened
  window with a woman in a warm halo, and in the foreground a hand holding a phone with a warm chat bubble; tender,
  personal ("this is you"), a satisfied close (the frost-drawn gate at the left is a small puzzle).
- **A-B4alt** The feeling it gives: playful logic; a level seesaw in a snowy courtyard, a machine-tower on one end and
  a heap of kettles on the other; a cartoon equation, a grin (at phone size the heap reads as grey rubble, and the
  plank looks doubled).
- **A-B7alt** The feeling it gives: longing, wishing; every frosted window of a block has a warm sun drawn in it, a
  parent and child on the pavement point up; sweet, hopeful, a whole street wishing for warmth; flat and pattern-like.
- **A-B9alt** The feeling it gives: curious, toy-like; a finger lifts the roof of a glowing outline house to show rows
  of machines inside; a dollhouse peek; flatter, more diagram than world, little depth or mood.
- **A-B10alt** The feeling it gives: a clear, calm lesson; a cold pipe enters a tall machine and leaves warm, a stick
  figure beside it pointing; easy to understand, but a whiteboard diagram, not a place.
