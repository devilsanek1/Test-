# Imagination C, THE WARM CURRENT: frame loop evaluation, round 2

Fresh evaluator (built none of it). Sheet judged as it is now: `C_sheet.png` (10:32), at full size (2x crops) and
at phone size (the sheet's own ~300 px thumbnails, then the sheet at half size and in grey for the glance).

## First look: the feeling each thumbnail gives, from the picture alone (written before reading any doc)

- **C-B1** The feeling it gives: a hushed, pretty, cold night street by the frozen water with a small warm curl of
  steam glowing in the snow ahead; calm curiosity ("huh, what's that?"), a mood more than a jolt; the big dark hand
  with the phone is the loudest shape, the warm thing is small and mid-distance.
- **C-B2** The feeling it gives: cool awe at a long canyon of blinking blue-white machines that read like apartment
  facades, one person walking it alone; orderly, chilly, a little lonely; one warm strip on the right hints that
  something inside is hot.
- **C-B3** The feeling it gives: "it's hot in here": a big egg of gold glow behind a dark perforated wall, shimmer
  rising to the ceiling, a rim-lit figure raising an arm (flinching or wiping a brow); but static and abstract, the
  glow reads as a dot pattern before it reads as heat, and the figure's gesture is lost at phone size.
- **C-B4** The feeling it gives: a funny, warm reveal: someone opens a rack and it is packed with steaming kettles;
  instant "ha, that's a lot", the boldest picture of the top row.
- **C-B5** The feeling it gives: gentle but puzzling whimsy: a tilted copper slab with a giant tea-bag tag swinging
  off it and a small figure sitting with a mug; a small smile once decoded, but at a glance I could not say what the
  big object is.
- **C-B6** The feeling it gives: wistful, a little sad: warm plumes from rooftop fans drifting up into the cold air
  in front of a dark snowy city, heat let go; quiet and gentle rather than wasteful.
- **C-B7** The feeling it gives: the same roof, now five glowing hoses curl out of the dark city's doorsteps towards
  the fans; a soft reaching, faintly hopeful, but ambiguous at a glance (five glowing hooks) and calm; not a turn I
  feel.
- **C-B8** The feeling it gives: light anticipation: a chalk outline of a building drawn over the harbour, a "2027"
  sign, a person with a rolled plan, the warm ribbon arriving to meet the chalk line; "something will be built here".
- **C-B9** The feeling it gives: invitation: a warm lit door opened in the chalk outline, a figure stepping in, a
  wedge of gold light spilling towards me; "come and see how"; the most welcoming frame so far and the clearest read.
- **C-B10** The feeling it gives: calm clarity: a tall rack with a pipe zigzagging up from cold blue to warm gold, a
  person touching it; "oh, I see how it works"; tidy, a touch diagrammatic.
- **C-B11** The feeling it gives: homecoming: the gold line runs up the snowy street, through a manhole and into a
  house whose windows fill with warm light from the bottom up, someone at the window; quiet comfort arriving;
  frontal and still.
- **C-B12** The feeling it gives: a tender payoff: my phone in the cold, across the way a warm window with a woman
  and her cup; "my question keeps her warm"; cosy, intimate, the warmest frame of the film.
- **C-B1alt** The feeling it gives: an overview from a balcony: a warm seam runs down the middle of a snowy street
  with branches to the doors; curious, map-like, calm; "there is a warm network under the city" rather than an event.
- **C-B5alt** The feeling it gives: delight: a giant teapot in a knitted cosy standing among the racks, a person
  holding a little cup up to the spout; an instant smile, absurd and cosy, reads in a blink.
- **C-B7alt** The feeling it gives: anticipation with a little magic: manhole lids lifting one after another down
  the street, warm light pouring up; "a road is opening for the heat"; clear and forward-pulling.
- **C-B11alt** The feeling it gives: the same as C-B11; it is the same picture (a pixel diff finds only the figure
  in the middle window changed, a bust become a tiny standing silhouette that seems to float), so it offers no
  different feeling.
