# Video 4 ideas, sequence B, evaluation round 2 (fresh evaluator)

## First look, from the picture alone (written before reading any brief)
Looked at `B_sheet.png` at full size (each thumbnail at 1:1 and 2x) and then at phone size (1:1 strip, and a half-size
glance strip).

- B-B1: The feeling it gives: hushed curiosity on a cold blue night; a molten-gold seam glows through the snow toward a
  lit glass box and one small figure stops to look. Calm and mysterious more than surprising.
- B-B2: The feeling it gives: cold awe; a long hushed hall of blinking machines narrows to a bright door with a tiny
  person at its end. Impressive, sterile, a little lonely.
- B-B3: The feeling it gives: bathed in heat; the same hall now glowing orange with shimmer rising, a bundled-up woman
  standing in the middle of it. Radiant and warm, more "phew, cosy-hot" than alarm; her undressing gesture only reads
  zoomed in, not at phone size.
- B-B4: The feeling it gives: a mild, curious peek; a cabinet door opens on a foggy grid of small steaming grey shapes
  and a woman looks in. Whimsical once the word says "kettles", but grey and soft, the surprise muted.
- B-B5: The feeling it gives: a small cosy smile; a mittened hand lifts one warm mug out of a wall of kettles. Reads at
  once, but plain, nearly half the frame empty fog.
- B-B6: The feeling it gives: lonely, wistful waste; a glowing vent on a dark roof breathes sparks up into a huge empty
  night, a tiny figure beside it. Moody; the pang is there but faint, and the rising snow reads as sparks.
- B-B7: The feeling it gives: a lively, funny turn; a whole house leans out of its glowing windows, arms out toward a
  plume of heat over the harbour, a woman on the street waving at it. A smile, eagerness.
- B-B8: The feeling it gives: a promise pinned up; the same house and people with a "2027" sign over a ghostly drawn door
  that the warm line now starts from. Hopeful but static: it reads as "B7 again, plus a sign".
- B-B9: The feeling it gives: a threshold, being invited in; a figure silhouetted in a door that pours light toward us.
  Curiosity and pull, though what stands inside (grey bars with lines on top) reads like a bar chart.
- B-B10: The feeling it gives: calm clarity; she turns a valve and a pipe goes cold-white to gold out of the machine and
  across to the lit town. "Oh, I see": tidy, quiet, a little diagrammatic.
- B-B11: The feeling it gives: a gentle arrival; a gold seam runs under a snowy street into one lit window where someone
  stands. Homely and quiet, though the grey street and the bright tram keep it cool more than cosy.
- B-B12: The feeling it gives: a personal, warm wink; my phone's typing bubble sends a gold line across the snow to a
  window where someone raises a mug at me. Intimate and playful, the warmest frame of the board.
- B-B5alt: The feeling it gives: a still life, calm and plain; one mug on a shelf before a faded wall of kettles. Tidy,
  almost a product shot, little life.
- B-B6alt: The feeling it gives: tender and wry; a fox curls asleep in the warm patch under two glowing fans on a snowy
  roof. Sweet, with a soft "all that heat, and only the fox gets it".
- B-B7alt: The feeling it gives: an eager, comic turn; a tall house full of people leaning out to grab at the heat rising
  over the harbour. Lively and busier, nobody on the street.
- B-B12alt: The feeling it gives: snug calm at home; a black cat asleep on a sill above a glowing radiator, the snowy city
  and a steaming box far outside. Cosy and peaceful; the chat bubble is small, and the words "heat" and "an" collide.

Whole board at a glance: one consistent world (cool night greys, one gold accent, snow), every frame built the same way
(pale words on top, the scene below); the strongest pictures are B1, B3, B7, B9, B12 and the fox; the greyest and
quietest are B4, B5, B6, B8.
