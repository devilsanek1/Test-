## Each frame: the feeling intended beside the one it gives, and the verdict
Judged in the board, in order, at phone size first. Notes are one change each, with why; "polish" notes go to the key
frames and do not hold a THERE.

**B-B1 · The warm line.** Intended: surprise and curiosity, a cozy snowy city and an odd true idea. Gives: hushed
curiosity; a molten seam through blue snow toward a lit, see-through hall; calm more than surprising. **THERE.** The
seam is the board's best thumb-stopper, the deep row of warm windows gives "70,000 homes" a place to land, and the
pencil hall says "not built yet" from frame 0. The surprise lives in the motion its card plans (the lights blink on, the
seam fires on "heat", the windows pop in a wave): the animatic must prove it. Polish: the hall's thin white outlines are
the crispest lines on the board; in the key frame they are soft graphite in paint (no ink lines in the build).

**B-B2 · A cathedral of computers.** Intended: a little awe, AI as a real, huge thing. Gives: cold awe; impressive,
sterile, a little lonely. **THERE.** The walls climb out of sight toward a bright far door and she is the scale; the cold
is right before the heat. Polish: the drift at the foot reads as a pale strip laid across the frame, not snow blown in
at a door (give it the sill's edge and her tracks, or drop it); the walls' tops meet the pale band in a hard horizontal
edge on both sides.

**B-B3 · Too hot for snow.** Intended: the heat rising; warm, a touch of alarm. Gives: bathed in a golden glow; "phew,
warm", pleasant, no alarm. **ALMOST** (its last adjust round). The cut from B2's cold is the right jolt, but the frame
itself is a warm room you would sit in, which spends the film's coziness on the machines instead of the homes.
1. Make the heat come OUT of the machines: dark rack faces, their grille slits glowing from honey near us to white-hot at
   the core, the glow spilling onto the floor and her (painted on the left wall of the paint-over). Why: flat orange
   panels with dark slats read as lit lockers or wooden doors; glowing slits read as an oven, heat that is too much.
2. Bring her nearer and put the heat in her body at phone size: fanning herself with the beanie, coat flung open. Why:
   the touch of alarm is hers to show, and her gesture now only reads zoomed in (a small grey figure on a warm floor).
3. Let the air bend: the far aisle's straight lines wobbling in the haze. Why: shimmer that warps lines reads as "too
   hot" at a glance; now it is a few faint squiggles inside the band's haze.

**B-B4 · Forty kettles in one.** Intended: playful astonishment at the size of it. Gives: a mild, curious peek; a grey,
soft wall; the surprise muted. **ALMOST** (its last adjust round). The idea is the film's best invention and reads with
the word, but the biggest "wow" of the film is one of its greyest frames, right after its hottest (to explain the dip:
under the band 0.7 % of B4 carries the warm accent, against 29.5 % of B3).
1. Make the kettle wall the warm, blazing opening: the cabinet's inside lit honey by the boiling, the kettles catching

2. Draw the boil as forty jets of steam curling up off the spouts into one cloud rolling over the cabinet's top, the

   blurs them at phone size and keeps the wall still.
3. Her astonishment: a step back, both hands up, face tipped up to the top of the wall. Why: her one raised hand now
   reads as resting on the next cabinet, calm.

**B-B5 · One cup.** Intended: a smile and a breath; the joke lands. Gives: a small cosy smile; reads at once; airy.
