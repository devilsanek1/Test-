# Video 4 (Helsinki): does the G2.5 visual script fit the look we developed on 2026-10-29/30?

**The owner** (2026-10-30, to the city-poster study session, sending `Helsinki_Visual_Script.zip`: the G2.5 visual script,
12 shots on one continuous camera, on the locked 42.24 s take): "What do you think about this script / Would it fit into our
style?"

**Short answer (the study session's read):** the STORY structure fits our process very well; the PICTURE is written for
the older clean-pen, diagram look and needs a translation pass into the new look before G3 (not a rewrite).

## What already fits
- One home place, one continuous camera, no cuts; one thread object (the warm water) with a home spot (the one window);
  one scale move (out to the planet and back); one word in the picture (2027); the payoff loops to frame 0.
- A winter night or dusk in Helsinki, with windows lighting one by one, is the ideal case for the new rules: ONE COLOUR
  KEY (a cold blue snowy night) with ONE warm accent, and PROPER LIGHT (the lit window as the story light; the giant
  computer's small lights as a second, cooler source). It sits very close to the owner's bus-stop reference.
- Snow, steam, the harbour, the sky and the far city are natural PAINTED PIECES (organic, soft, layered by depth).
- Sound-off literal beats (kettles, a mug of tea) and life in every hold (snow, a ferry, a tram).

## What to translate before G3 (playbook rules of 2026-10-29/30)
1. **The pen becomes a brush.** Frame 0 still draws itself, but as paint laid on in a painter's order (sky washes, the
   far city, then the near pieces), not outlines: we draw no outlines now. The un-draw at S2 and the loop is the same
   reveal run backwards (`src/lib/paint/reveal.tsx`).
2. **The cut-away should not read as a diagram.** A straight cut through the ground and a cut wall read "square and
   proper" (ORGANIC, NOT GEOMETRIC). Prefer showing the heat organically: the melt line in the snow above the pipe (the
   script already has it), steam from grates and manholes, a warm glow through the snow; if a cut is kept, make it a
   soft, irregular, painted cut of snow and earth, with the pipe glowing inside.
3. **A human viewpoint and a person.** The rules now ask for depth and scale from a person near the camera at a real
   size against the vast world beyond (DEPTH AND SCALE FROM A HUMAN VIEWPOINT). The script's "you" appears only at S12
   (a gloved hand and a phone). Consider a figure on the street in the foreground for the hold shots (walking home in
   the snow), drawn in the new people design (beautiful, contemporary 20-30 styling, generic face), which is being
   researched now.
4. **Composition: one place to look.** The home view holds a street, a block, the computer, the harbour, the far city,
   a pipe, a box, the window, a ferry, a tram and Pip: group them into a few value masses (the dark street and block,
   the mid harbour, the light sky), with the lit window as the focal point (SCENE COMPOSITION).
5. **The giant computer as a painted, glowing monolith,** not a hard geometric box: soft edges in the steam, rows of
   small lights, the snow melting on it; near pieces sharper, far pieces more painterly.
6. **The planet (S6):** a painted view, beautiful at phone size, in the same colour key.
