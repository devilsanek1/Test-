## A. A virus designed by AI was given to a sick person for the first time (announced ~2026-10-21/22) [DRAFT]
- **What happened:** Radical Numerics (San Francisco AI-for-biology lab) says a phage cocktail containing variants designed by its AI model Omnii-1 was given to a patient with an antibiotic-resistant infection, under compassionate use. Phages are viruses that only kill bacteria.
- **AI did / didn't:** AI designed phage variants; in the lab dish the cocktail wiped out the bacteria taken from this patient. No result in the patient yet (privacy; "one patient doesn't establish safety or efficacy"). Stage: single-patient compassionate use, company blog, no peer review.
- **Sources:** The Register, 2026-10-22, https://www.theregister.com/science/2026/10/22/human-receives-ai-designed-virus-for-the-first-time/5304874 · company blog https://www.radicalnumerics.ai/blog/omnii-phage (to check)

## B. A free AI that hunts for new antibiotics, open to every lab on Earth (Semafor, 2026-10-28) [DRAFT]
- McMaster University (Stokes Lab): ESKAPE model, free; upload compounds, get predicted bacteria-killing activity; >12 million molecules screened, >40,000 a day. Lab-stage tool, no new drug yet. Real people: Jonathan Stokes ("All you need is an internet connection"), PhD student Autumn Arnold built it.
- Source: Semafor (Zoë Schiffer), 2026-10-28, https://www.semafor.com/article/10/26/2026/a-free-ai-tool-speeds-up-the-search-for-new-antibiotics

## C. AI solved hard medical cases twice as often as doctors, now peer-reviewed in Science (2026-10-22) [DRAFT]
- Microsoft AI's diagnostic orchestrator (MAI-DxO / DxO): 83.2 % right on 304 NEJM case records vs 36.1 % for generalist physicians; 80.3 % vs 46.8 % on 325 emergency admissions. Simulation ("idealized information conditions"), not real care. Paper: Nori et al., "Sequential diagnosis with language models", Science, DOI 10.1126/science.aeh7930.
- Sources: Medical Xpress 2026-10-23 https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-10-ai-generalist-doctors-medical.html · TechCrunch 2026-10-22 https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/microsofts-mustafa-suleyman-says-its-ai-outperforms-doctors-on-hard-cases-as-he-chases-medical-superintelligence

## D. AI spots pancreatic cancer on CT scans ~475 days before diagnosis (BMJ release 2026-10-26; paper first out ~Apr 2026) [DRAFT]
- REDMOD (Mayo Clinic), Gut, DOI 10.1136/gutjnl-2025-337266: 73 % sensitivity vs 39 % for radiologists on scans read as normal; retrospective; prospective trial needed. Mayo's own release dated 2026-04-29, so not new.
