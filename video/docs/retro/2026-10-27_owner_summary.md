# Video 3: what went wrong, and what we change (2026-10-27)

- **The story got lost between the shots.** The film jumps 13 times between 6 places, so it feels like separate shots. Your laptop's price, the thing it is all about, is off screen for 18 seconds in the middle.
- **Too much fights for your eye.** About 25 things move at once, all drawn as boldly as the main thing, and Pip usually stands out more than the story.
- **Rough lines, odd light.** The sketch's scratchy lines went straight into the final film. Lamp light on the snow is darker than the snow itself, and almost nothing has a shadow.
- **Did the sketch hurt? The way we used it, yes.** It locked the story, but not how one shot flows into the next, where to look, or the light. Then we piled detail on top, and every chart got a new place of its own.
- **Change 1:** one thing to follow through the whole film (here the price tag) and one home place we travel out from and back to. Each shot flows into the next instead of jumping.
- **Change 2:** one main thing at a time. Birds, people, snow and Pip all stay, just smaller, softer and calmer, so the world feels alive without pulling your eye.
- **Change 3:** smooth, joined lines, and only the main thing gets a full outline; the room, the street and the people become soft flat shapes (like your cozy references), just as full of things. Gentle light from one lamp or sun per place, with soft shadows under and beside things.
- **Change 4:** we keep a quick sketch, but it now plans the flow, where to look and the light. Then you get a short clip in the final style to approve before we build the whole film.
- The v3 rework you asked for is being built this way now.
- **The one decision we need:** OK to make every video this way from now on? The other option is to skip the sketch and build the finished world first: a stronger look early on, but a slower first watchable cut.
