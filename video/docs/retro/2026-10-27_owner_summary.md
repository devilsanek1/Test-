# Video 3: what went wrong, and what we change (2026-10-27)

- **The story got lost in the cuts.** The film jumps between 6 places with 13 hard cuts, so it feels like separate shots. Your laptop's price, the thing it is all about, is off screen for 18 seconds in the middle.
- **Too much fights for your eye.** About 25 things move at once, and the background is drawn as boldly as the main thing. Pip is often louder than the story.
- **Rough lines, odd light.** The sketch's scratchy pen went straight into the final film. Lamp light on the snow is darker than the snow itself, and almost nothing has a shadow.
- **Why:** the sketch locked the story, but not how one shot flows into the next, where to look, or the light. Then we piled detail on top, and every chart got a new place of its own.
- **Change 1:** one thing to follow through the whole film (here the price tag) and one home place we travel out from and back to. Each shot flows into the next instead of a hard cut.
- **Change 2:** one main thing at a time. Birds, people and Pip get smaller, softer and calmer, but stay alive.
- **Change 3:** a clean new pen (smooth, joined lines, far fewer of them), and gentle light with soft shadows from one light per place.
- **Change 4:** we keep a quick sketch, but it now plans the flow, where to look and the light. Then you get a short clip in the final style to approve before we build the whole film.
- The v3 rework you asked for is being built this way now.
- **The one decision we need:** OK to make every video this way from now on? The other option is to skip the sketch and build the finished world first: a stronger look early on, but a slower first watchable cut.
