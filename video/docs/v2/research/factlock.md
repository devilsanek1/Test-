Verdicts:

- **UNCERTAIN** = thin, conflicting, or our own reading. Never on screen as fact.
- **FALSE-as-worded** = the wording overclaims or is contradicted. Use the safe wording in the same row.

House rules: quote exactly, always attributed, never splice two quotes or put a paraphrase in quote marks. While a BBC quote is on screen,
show "Source: BBC News, 21 Oct 2026". The narrator reads the quotes (never a synthetic voice of Liguori or Kirsch).
