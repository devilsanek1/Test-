## 1. One company or the whole industry? 2024-2025: every big lab drops or carves out its "no military" line

  OpenAI called it a readability rewrite and cited national-security uses (a DARPA cyber project). VERIFIED (The Intercept https://theintercept.com/2024/01/12/open-ai-military-ban-chatgpt/ ;
  Army Technology https://www.army-technology.com/newsletters/openai-removes-ban-on-military-use-of-ai-tools-for-national-security-scenarios).

  ... espionage" uses. It calls this an exception. On 2025-05-29 it partners with Anduril on military XR headsets. VERIFIED (Bloomberg https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-11-04/meta-opens-llama-ai-models-to-us-defense-agencies-contractors ;
  The Register https://www.theregister.com/2024/11/06/meta_weaponizing_llama_us/ ; Defense News https://www.defensenews.com/pentagon/2025/05/30/meta-and-anduril-work-on-mixed-reality-devices-for-the-us-military/).

## 2. 2026: "any lawful purpose" becomes the industry standard
- 2026-02-27: Anthropic refuses "all lawful purposes" terms. Its red lines are mass domestic surveillance and fully autonomous weapons. Trump orders a 6-month phase-out of Anthropic, and
  Hegseth labels it a "supply chain risk" and bars military contractors from working with it. VERIFIED (Axios https://axios.com/2026/02/27/altman-openai-anthropic-pentagon ; The Hill ; timeline.md).
- On 2026-08-27, US District Judge Rita Lin (California) rules the measures unlawful: the government wanted "to make a public example" of Anthropic for its "arrogance". On 2026-09-25 a separate
  D.C. Circuit case goes 2-1 for the Pentagon, so the label and the ban stay. VERIFIED (AP via WUNC https://www.wunc.org/2026-08-28/judge-says-pentagons-measures-against-anthropic-were-illegal-and-baseless ;
  AP via CP24 https://www.cp24.com/news/world/2026/09/25/federal-court-says-pentagon-can-label-anthropic-a-supply-chain-risk/ ; Fast Company).
- 2026-02-27/28: OpenAI agrees to "any lawful purpose" terms, relying on technical guardrails and a cloud-only deployment. After a backlash it amends the deal around 03-02/03: models "shall not be
  intentionally used for domestic surveillance of U.S. persons and nationals". Altman says the rollout "looked opportunistic and sloppy". Critics say there is no explicit ban on analysing
  purchased or public data about Americans. VERIFIED (Fortune https://www.fortune.com/2026/03/03/sam-altman-openai-pentagon-renegotiating-deal-anthropic/ ; The Hill
  https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5765480-openai-pentagon-deal-backlash/amp/ ; Axios https://www.axios.com/2026/03/01/openai-pentagon-anthropic-safety).
- At an all-hands in early March 2026, Altman reportedly said OpenAI "can't weigh in on individual use cases". Kirsch says the same about Google. REPORTED (CNN via ABC17
  https://abc17news.com/money/cnn-business-consumer/2026/03/04/when-the-pentagon-gave-anthropic-the-boot-openai-swooped-in-some-staff-are-frustrated-with-how-it-unfolded/).
- Late Feb 2026: xAI reportedly agrees to put Grok into classified systems on the Pentagon's terms "without reservation" (Axios via TechRepublic
  https://www.techrepublic.com/de/article/news-xai-grok-pentagon-classified-ai-deal/). xAI was folded into SpaceX (announced 2026-02-02), which probably explains SpaceX on the BBC list. REPORTED.
- 2026-05-01, the BBC's list: AI from SpaceX, OpenAI, Google, Nvidia, Reflection, Microsoft and AWS (plus Oracle the same day) goes onto IL6/IL7 classified networks, to "augment warfighter
  decision-making". Nvidia, Microsoft, Reflection and AWS signed "for lawful operational use", and Google's deal says "any lawful government purpose". The exact permitted uses are not public. Bloomberg
  says the terms could allow help with targeting. VERIFIED (Bloomberg Law https://news.bloomberglaw.com/tech-and-telecom-law/nvidia-microsoft-aws-expanding-classified-military-ai-use ;
  TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/01/pentagon-inks-deals-with-nvidia-microsoft-and-aws-to-deploy-ai-on-classified-networks ; GeekWire).
  According to WaPo, the Pentagon agrees to follow its existing human-oversight policy for weapons and US surveillance law. REPORTED (WaPo via ADN
  https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/05/01/top-ai-companies-agree-to-work-with-pentagon-on-secret-data/). OpenAI said on 05-01 that it was not yet on classified networks. REPORTED (Bloomberg Law).
- Overall: of the big US labs, only Anthropic refused, and it was punished for it. Every company the BBC names signed a similar deal. VERIFIED (above).

## 4. Palantir's Maven Smart System: what it does and how big it is
- It is the Pentagon's command-and-control platform. It pulls battlefield data together and "identifies targets" (Reuters). Palantir says humans select and approve the targets. On 2026-03-09 a memo
  from Deputy Defense Secretary Feinberg makes it an official "program of record". VERIFIED (Reuters via FMT https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/business/2026/03/21/pentagon-to-adopt-palantir-ai-as-core-us-military-system ;
  GovConWire https://www.govconwire.com/articles/pentagon-palantir-maven-ai-program-of-record).
- 20,000 active users in May 2025 (NGA director), 4x as many as in March 2024. VERIFIED (Breaking Defense https://breakingdefense.com/2025/05/ai-unchained-ngas-maven-tool-significantly-decreasing-time-to-targeting-agency-chief-says/ ;
  MeriTalk https://www.meritalk.com/articles/nga-expands-maven-ai-system-in-year-of-ai-whitworth-says/). "100,000+ users by Sept 2026": UNCERTAIN (one aggregator).
- In May 2025 the contract ceiling rose to nearly $1.3bn through 2029. That is a ceiling, not money spent. VERIFIED (DefenseScoop https://defensescoop.com/2025/05/23/dod-palantir-maven-smart-system-contract-increase/ ; Inside Defense).
- Iran: the US struck about 1,000 targets in the first 24 hours, nearly double the opening of the 2003 Iraq campaign, with Maven as the platform. VERIFIED (NPR 2026-03-26
  https://www.npr.org/2026/03/26/nx-s1-5762019/americas-first-ai-fueled-war-is-unfolding-right-now-in-iran ; Bloomberg Law 2026-03-05 https://news.bloomberglaw.com/ip-law/us-military-relying-on-ai-as-key-tool-to-speed-iran-operations).
  Other figures: Maven went from under 100 to 5,000 targets a day after LLMs were added (WaPo via Rudaw, REPORTED). About 13,000 strikes and 20 billion tokens a day (Breaking Defense snippet, REPORTED).
- Minab, 2026-02-28: US missiles hit a girls' school and killed 150+ people (counts go up to 168). Bloomberg, citing an unreleased review, says Maven put the site forward as a target using stale
  records, and the civilian-harm team had been cut from 10 people to 1. Palantir disputes this. REPORTED (Bloomberg via ThePrint
  https://theprint.in/world/us-strike-on-school-in-irans-minab-was-result-of-over-reliance-on-ai-outdated-sat-images-bloomberg/3047604/). That the strike happened and raised AI questions is VERIFIED
  (Defense News 2026-03-24 https://www.defensenews.com/news/your-military/2026/03/24/deadly-iran-school-strike-casts-shadow-over-pentagons-ai-targeting-push/ ; NPR).

## 7. Afraid to speak: the pattern of contracts and retaliation
- May 2024: Vox reveals OpenAI exit papers that could cancel departing staff's vested equity unless they signed a lifelong non-disparagement agreement. On 2024-05-18 Altman says he was "genuinely
  embarrassed", and OpenAI then releases former staff from the clauses. VERIFIED (Inc https://www.inc.com/kit-eaton/openais-harsh-nda-forces-openais-altman-to-say-sorry.html ; Euronews
  https://www.euronews.com/next/2024/05/20/openai-changes-exit-contracts-so-employees-can-leave-without-having-equity-revoked). Daniel Kokotajlo (quit Apr 2024) refused to sign, risking equity he
  put at 85% of his family's net worth. REPORTED (his own account).

## 8. Whistleblower protection proposals, 2024-2026
- US: the AI Whistleblower Protection Act (S.1792, Grassley, bipartisan, 2025-05-15) would override NDAs that block reports of AI dangers to safety or national security. It has not passed; in late Sept 2026
  Grassley was trying to fast-track it. VERIFIED (Senate Judiciary https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/press/rep/releases/grassley-introduces-ai-whistleblower-protection-act ; Deseret News
  https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/09/25/john-curtis-back-bill-protecting-ai-whistleblower/).
- California SB 53 (in force 2026-01-01) protects lab staff who report "catastrophic risk" (50+ deaths or $1bn of damage). Critics say it is too narrow. VERIFIED (Greenberg Traurig
  https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2025/10/california-expands-whistleblower-retaliation-protections-for-employees-in-the-ai-sector ; Tech Policy Press).
- EU: the AI Office's whistleblower tool went live in Nov 2025, and AI Act breaches are covered by the EU Whistleblower Directive from 2026-08-02. VERIFIED (eucrim https://eucrim.eu/news/commission-launches-ai-act-whistleblower-tool/ ; Law Society Gazette).
- UK, where Liguori and Kirsch work: we found no AI-specific law. Under the Public Interest Disclosure Act, a gag on "protected disclosures" is void, but going to the media faces a strict test and general
  criticism is not covered. REPORTED (Protect https://protect-advice.org.uk/?p=5722 ; Mondaq).
- Our analysis (not a sourced claim): all of these cover illegal acts or catastrophic risk. Lawful military use, which the 2026 deals rest on, does not break any rule, so none of them protects it.

## 10. What this does to the BBC framing
- It supports "bigger than Google": every company the BBC names signed, OpenAI lost a senior leader over its deal, and Microsoft and Amazon fired protesters.
- It also complicates it. (a) Anthropic, the lab that refused, had Claude inside Maven for the Maduro raid and the Iran targeting. (b) The firings were for sit-ins and disruptions, which the companies
  call conduct violations, not for speech alone. (c) Named resignations and on-record interviews keep happening (Kirsch, Kalinowski, Liguori), so "afraid to speak" means speaking is risky, not that
  nobody speaks. (d) The protesters are a small minority (at Google, about 600 of about 190,000 staff). (e) The deals refer to human oversight and US law (WaPo), and Google says it has rules against retaliation.

## Hook-worthy facts (all VERIFIED)
1. In 2024 OpenAI quietly deleted "military and warfare" from its banned uses. By 2026 it let the Pentagon use its AI for "any lawful purpose".
2. In May 2026 the Pentagon put AI from 8 companies on its classified networks: SpaceX, OpenAI, Google, Nvidia, Reflection, Microsoft, Amazon and Oracle.
3. The one lab that said no (Anthropic) was branded a "supply chain risk", and a judge said the government wanted to "make a public example" of it.
4. In the Iran war the US hit about 1,000 targets in the first 24 hours using Palantir's Maven, with Claude inside it suggesting and ranking targets.
5. Maven had 20,000 military users by May 2025, 4x as many as in March 2024. The goal is 1,000 targeting decisions an hour: one every 3.6 seconds.
6. OpenAI's exit papers once threatened ex-staff's vested equity unless they promised never to criticise the company. Altman said he was "genuinely embarrassed".
7. In 2024, 13 OpenAI and DeepMind insiders wrote that "broad confidentiality agreements block us from voicing our concerns". Six of them signed anonymously.
8. Since Oct 2024 Microsoft has fired at least 8 staff over protests about its Israel contracts (2 + 1 + 1 + 4, section 6).
