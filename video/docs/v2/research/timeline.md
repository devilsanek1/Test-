## 1. 2014 background
- 2014 (Jan): Google buys DeepMind; the deal was reported to require an internal AI ethics board. VERIFIED (The Information via
  Mail & Guardian https://mg.co.za/article/2014-01-28-google-scoops-ai-firm-for-r400m/ ; Guardian per Wikipedia).

## 2. 2018: Project Maven revolt
- 2018-04-04/05: NYT publishes a staff letter to Pichai, "We believe that Google should not be in the business of war", with about 3,100
  signatures. It asks Google to cancel Maven (AI to analyse drone footage). VERIFIED (CNBC https://www.cnbc.com/2018/04/05/google-employees-protest-pentagon-partnership-to-ceo-sundar-pichai.html ; YourStory).
- By mid-May 2018 the letter had about 4,000 signatures (outlets also give 4,600 and "3,000+"). VERIFIED for "about 4,000" (Gizmodo AU
  https://www.gizmodo.com.au/2018/05/google-employees-resign-in-protest-against-pentagon-contract/ ; Bloomberg via Engadget).
- 2018-05-14: Gizmodo reports that "about a dozen" employees resigned in protest. VERIFIED (Gizmodo AU above; FedScoop
  https://fedscoop.com/google-employees-resign-project-maven/ ; Fortune https://www.fortune.com/2018/05/14/report-google-employees-resign-pentagon-contract).
- 2018-06-01: Cloud CEO Diane Greene tells staff that Google will not renew Maven, so the contract runs out in March 2019 (Defense News says
  end of 2019). The contract was worth about $15M, with a reported ceiling of up to $250M. VERIFIED (Engadget https://engadget.com/2018/06/01/google-will-not-renew-military-ai-contract-project-maven ;
  Defense News https://www.defensenews.com/news/pentagon-congress/2018/06/03/reports-google-wont-renew-pentagon-contract-to-use-ai/).
- After 2018, Palantir took over as Maven's main contractor. Maven Smart System is now the Pentagon's main targeting/intel platform. No source found
  that puts Google's Gemini inside Maven; Reuters (Mar 2026) said Maven workflows were built on Anthropic's Claude. REPORTED
  (Reuters via AOL https://www.aol.com/articles/palantir-faces-challenge-remove-anthropic-213754143.html).

## 3. The 2018 AI Principles and their deletion
- 2018-06-07: Pichai publishes "AI at Google: our principles" (https://blog.google/technology/ai/ai-principles/). Under "AI applications we will not
  pursue", the exact wording is: (1) "Technologies that cause or are likely to cause overall harm..." (2) "Weapons or other technologies whose principal
  purpose or implementation is to cause or directly facilitate injury to people." (3) "Technologies that gather or use information for surveillance
  violating internationally accepted norms." (4) "Technologies whose purpose contravenes widely accepted principles of international law and human
  rights." Google said it would still do other government and military work (cybersecurity, training, search and rescue). VERIFIED (Fortune
  https://www.fortune.com/2018/06/07/google-ceo-makes-vague-pledge-limit-ai-weapons-surveillance ; MIT Tech Review
  https://www.technologyreview.com/2018/06/07/142429/dont-be-ai-vil-google-says-its-algorithms-will-do-no-harm/).
- 2025-02-04: Google deletes the whole "applications we will not pursue" section. Bloomberg spotted it first, and the page still had the list on 2025-01-30
  (Internet Archive). The new text promises "appropriate human oversight, due diligence, and feedback mechanisms" aligned with "international law and human
  rights". VERIFIED (WaPo via JWR https://jewishworldreview.com/0225/google_surveillance.php ; CNN via KVIA
  https://kvia.com/news/business-technology/cnn-business-consumer/2025/02/04/google-erases-promise-not-to-use-ai-technology-for-weapons-or-surveillance/ ;
  Fortune https://fortune.com/2025/02/05/google-drops-pledge-not-use-ai-weapons-surveillance ; The Register https://www.theregister.com/2025/02/05/google_ai_principles_update/).
- Google's explanation was a blog post by Demis Hassabis and James Manyika. It cites "a global competition taking place for AI leadership within an
  increasingly complex geopolitical landscape", says "democracies should lead in AI development", and says AI should "support national security". The post did not mention
  the deletion. VERIFIED (same sources).
- 2025-02-19: Sen. Markey and colleagues write to Pichai asking why. Their letter dates the removal to 2025-02-04. VERIFIED (primary:
  https://www.markey.senate.gov/download/letter-to-google-on-ai-principles-revisions?download=1).

## 4. Israel / Project Nimbus
- 2021: Google and Amazon sign Nimbus, a $1.2bn cloud + AI contract with the Israeli government. In April 2024 Google told TIME that Nimbus is not aimed at
  "highly sensitive or classified military workloads relevant to weapons or intelligence services". The BBC uses the Al Jazeera wording, "not directed at
  highly sensitive, classified, or military workloads...". VERIFIED (TIME https://time.com/6966102/ ; Al Jazeera
  https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/4/23/what-is-project-nimbus-and-why-are-google-workers-protesting-israel-deal).
- 2024-04-16: No Tech for Apartheid stages 10-hour sit-ins at Google NYC and Sunnyvale; 9 staff are arrested (5 CA, 4 NY). 2024-04-17: Google fires 28.
  VERIFIED (KTVU/CNBC https://www.ktvu.com/news/google-fires-28-workers-anti-israel-protests-april-2024 ; Anadolu
  https://aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/google-fires-28-employees-over-protests-against-companys-contract-with-israel/3194947).
- 2024-04-22/23: About 20 more are fired, taking the total past 50. This count comes from the workers' group, not Google. REPORTED (AP via Manufacturing.net
  https://www.manufacturing.net/software/news/22893871/google-fires-more-workers-who-protested-israel-deal ; Business Today).
- 2025-01-21: WaPo, citing internal documents, reports that Google Cloud worked directly with the IDF after 7 Oct 2023 (Vertex AI access, a race against Amazon).
  Google denied working with the military. REPORTED (WaPo, relayed by DCD https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/google-provided-ai-technology-to-idf-during-israel-gaza-war/).
- Sep-Oct 2026: The film NAZA (Venice special jury prize) has 24 anonymous Israeli soldiers alleging AI-assisted targeting in Gaza. Netanyahu calls it
  "false, misleading", and the IDF "categorically" rejects it. The film does not tie the allegations to Google. VERIFIED (Al Jazeera 2026-10-12
  https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/12/netanyahu-demands-probe-into-sources-for-award-winning-gaza-film-naza ; Arab News).

## 5. DeepMind staff letters and union drive, 2024-2026
- 2024-05-16: Nearly 200 DeepMind staff (about 5% of DeepMind) sign a letter asking Google to drop military contracts, citing Nimbus. TIME reported it on 2024-08-22.
  Google said it complies with its AI Principles. VERIFIED (TechCrunch https://techcrunch.com/2024/08/22/deepmind-workers-sign-letter-in-protest-of-googles-defense-contracts ;
  TIME/Perrigo https://billyperrigo.substack.com/p/trouble-inside-deepmind ; TechRadar).
- 2025-04-26: The FT reports that about 300 London DeepMind staff are seeking to join the CWU over the Israel links and the deleted pledge. "At least five" staff had quit over it
  in the two months before. REPORTED (FT via Reuters/Yahoo https://www.yahoo.com/news/deepmind-uk-staff-plan-unionise-125916277.html ; TechCrunch
  https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/26/googles-deepmind-uk-team-reportedly-seeks-to-unionize/).
- 2026-05-05: London DeepMind staff ask for recognition of CWU + Unite, for a bargaining unit of at least 1,000 people. The CWU says 98% of its DeepMind members backed the bid.
  Their demands: restore the weapons/surveillance pledge, create an independent ethics body, and a personal right to refuse projects. VERIFIED (Fortune
  https://fortune.com/2026/05/05/google-deepmind-unionize-vote-military-ai-contracts-internal-backlash-pentagon-deal-israeli-defense-forces/ ;
  Novara https://novaramedia.com/2026/05/06/google-ai-workers-in-uk-vote-to-unionise-amid-deal-with-us-military/ ; Computer Weekly).
  The 98% is a vote of union members only: Google says "there has been no vote to unionise".
- 2026-05-19/20: Google refuses voluntary recognition and offers talks through ACAS. No outcome or CAC filing found after June 2026. VERIFIED (Reuters via WHBL
  https://whbl.com/2026/05/20/google-rejects-uk-union-recognition-but-offers-talks-via-conciliation-body/ ; CWU
  https://yw.cwu.org/press_release/google-ai-workers-to-enter-union-recognition-talks/ ; Express & Star).
- 2026-05-20: A former DeepMind employee files a UK employment-tribunal claim, saying he was dismissed after protesting the Israel ties and the removed ban.
  Google DeepMind says his account "does not accurately reflect the facts". REPORTED (Guardian via L'Espresso
  https://lespresso.it/c/mondo/2026/05/20/ingegnere-google-licenziamento-israele-intelligenza-artificiale/62204).

## 6. Pentagon work, 2025-2026
- 2025-07-14: The CDAO awards Google, OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI "frontier AI" deals, each with a ceiling of up to $200M (a ceiling, not money committed). VERIFIED (GovConWire
  https://www.govconwire.com/articles/cdao-frontier-ai-contract-awards-national-security ; The Decoder).
- 2025-12-09: The Pentagon launches GenAI.mil with Google's Gemini for Government as its first model (IL5, unclassified), for about 3 million military and civilian staff.
  VERIFIED (Fox Business https://www.foxbusiness.com/technology/pentagon-launches-military-ai-platform-powered-google-gemini-defense-operations ;
  Decrypt https://decrypt.co/351691/war-department-launches-googles-gemini-military-ai-push).
- 2026-01-09 (published 01-12): Hegseth's AI Acceleration Strategy says the department will become an "'AI-first' warfighting force". It also orders standard "any lawful use"
  language into DoD AI contracts within 180 days. VERIFIED (DefenseScoop https://defensescoop.com/2026/01/13/hegseth-ai-tech-hubs-reorganization-dod-dow/ ; MeriTalk
  https://meritalk.com/articles/pentagon-unveils-ai-acceleration-strategy/ ; Lawfare https://www.lawfaremedia.org/article/military-ai-policy-by-contract--the-limits-of-procurement-as-governance).
- 2026-02-11: Google pulls out of a $100M Pentagon prize challenge for voice-controlled autonomous drone swarms after its entry had advanced. Google cited "resourcing";
  Bloomberg says the exit followed an internal ethics review. VERIFIED (Bloomberg Law https://news.bloomberglaw.com/legal-ops-and-tech/google-drops-out-of-pentagon-drone-swarm-contest-after-advancing ;
  Tom's Hardware https://tomshardware.com/software/security-software/google-signs-classified-pentagon-ai-deal-but-exits-100-million-drone-swarm-program).
- 2026-02-27 onwards: Anthropic refuses "all lawful purposes" terms (it wants no mass domestic surveillance and no fully autonomous weapons). Hegseth labels it a "supply chain
  risk" and Anthropic sues. The day after, OpenAI signs on "all lawful purposes" terms. Pentagon CTO Emil Michael: "Speed defines victory in the AI era". VERIFIED
  (The Hill https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5759630-pentagon-designates-anthropic-risk/amp ; TechInformed ; Fortune
  https://fortune.com/2026/02/27/emil-michael-the-silicon-valley-exec-turned-trump-official-leading-the-war-against-anthropic-has-deep-ties-to-the-tech-world).
- 2026-04-27 (Mon): More than 600 Google staff, mostly from DeepMind and Cloud and including 20+ directors, senior directors and VPs, write to Pichai asking him to reject classified work:
  "Classified workloads are by definition opaque." VERIFIED (AFP via The Peninsula https://www.thepeninsulaqatar.com/article/27/04/2026/600-google-staff-urge-ceo-to-reject-classified-military-ai-contract ;
  Quartz https://qz.com/google-employees-letter-pentagon-ai-classified-042726). That is about 600 of roughly 190,000 Alphabet staff (about 0.3%).
- 2026-04-27/28: Hours later, Google signs an amendment to its existing Pentagon deal that brings Gemini onto classified networks. Use is allowed for "any lawful government purpose";
  it "should not be used for domestic mass surveillance or autonomous weapons (including target selection) without appropriate human oversight and control". Google must
  "assist in adjusting its AI safety settings and filters at the government's request", and the deal does "not confer any right to control or veto lawful Government operational
  decision-making". VERIFIED (The Information via Reuters/Japan Times https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/04/28/tech/google-classified-ai-deal-pentagon/ ; 9to5Google
  https://9to5google.com/2026/04/28/googles-updated-pentagon-deal-uses-gemini-for-any-lawful-government-purpose-with-classified-data/ ; Android Authority
  https://www.androidauthority.com/google-pentagon-deal-for-gemini-3661815/ ; BBC source file). Google said it was "proud" to be part of a consortium "in support of national
  security". Legal experts say the guardrail clause is not legally binding. REPORTED (Fortune 2026-05-05, The Decoder).
- 2026-05-01: The Pentagon names 8 firms for classified (IL6/IL7) networks, accessed via GenAI.mil: SpaceX, OpenAI, Google, NVIDIA, Reflection, Microsoft, AWS and Oracle.
  Anthropic is excluded. VERIFIED (Nextgov https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/05/pentagon-makes-agreements-7-companies-add-ai-classified-networks/413264/ ;
  Breaking Defense https://breakingdefense.com/2026/05/pentagon-clears-7-tech-firms-to-deploy-their-ai-on-its-classified-networks/).
- 2026-07-02: Sen. Warren writes to DoD; she says the classified deals allow "lawful operational use" and that few details have been disclosed. VERIFIED (primary:
  https://www.warren.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/2026.07.02-Letter-to-DoD-re-AI-contracts.pdf).

## Hook-worthy facts (all VERIFIED)
1. In 2018 about 4,000 Googlers signed "Google should not be in the business of war", about a dozen quit, and Google dropped Pentagon Project Maven.
2. Google then promised in writing not to build AI "weapons ... to cause or directly facilitate injury to people", and on 4 Feb 2025 it quietly deleted that promise.
3. On 27 Apr 2026, 600+ Google staff, including 20+ directors and VPs, begged Pichai to say no to classified military AI. Within about a day Google signed anyway.
4. The deal lets the Pentagon use Gemini for "any lawful government purpose", and it gives Google no "right to control or veto" the Pentagon's lawful operational decisions.
5. Google must help "adjust its AI safety settings and filters at the government's request".
6. Anthropic refused the same "all lawful purposes" terms and the Pentagon branded it a "supply chain risk". Google signed.
7. Hegseth's January 2026 strategy: the US will become an "'AI-first' warfighting force". Google's Gemini was already in the hands of about 3 million Pentagon staff via GenAI.mil.
8. The quitting did not start with the BBC: a Google director (Mayrhofer, May) and a DeepMind safety researcher (Turner, June) had already resigned over the same deal.
