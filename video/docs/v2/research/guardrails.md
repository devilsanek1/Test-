## 2. Legal
- **Quoting named people:** use their exact BBC words, always with "told the BBC" or "says"; no splicing of two quotes into one; no paraphrase in quote marks. Never synthesise Liguori's or Kirsch's voice: the
  narrator reads the quotes, marked as quotes on screen. GUIDANCE.
- **Defamation (UK, where Liguori and DeepMind London are):** the defences are truth (s2), honest opinion (s3, opinion that states its basis) and public interest (s4). The repetition rule means repeating someone
  else's allegation counts as making it. VERIFIED (Defamation Act 2013 notes https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/26/notes ; Inforrm 2024-12-30
  https://inforrm.org/2024/12/30/defamation-act-2013-a-summary-and-overview-10-years-on-iain-wilson-max-campbell-tom-double-and-percy-preston/). So: attribute opinions ("she says she feels ashamed"), state facts
  only from documents, and never voice an allegation (targeting, Gaza) as our own.
- **Lifetime non-disparagement, how common:** NDAs are widespread (about 57 % of US workers covered, Starr & Yamaguchi via FT; 45 % of 947 job seekers signed one to get a job, Penn State 2025). REPORTED
  (https://scholars.org/sites/scholars/files/2021-08/The%20NDA%20boom%20is%20bad%20for%20both%20employers%20and%20workers%20_%20Financial%20Times.pdf ;
  https://www.businessinsider.nl/nondisclosure-agreements-are-now-rampant-in-the-workplace-covering-nearly-half-of-employees). No reliable figure exists for *lifetime non-disparagement* clauses: UNCERTAIN. The
  AI-lab precedent: in May 2024 Vox showed OpenAI's exit papers bound leavers to non-disparagement with no end date, on pain of losing vested equity; Altman said he was "embarrassed" and OpenAI released leavers.
  VERIFIED (https://businessinsider.nl/despite-recent-apologies-and-reassurances-documents-leaked-to-vox-show-openai-pressured-departing-employees-over-equity/ ;
  https://au.news.yahoo.com/openai-employees-forced-sign-nda-180729233.html).
- **"Right to Warn" (2024-06-04):** 13 current and former OpenAI and Google DeepMind staff: "broad confidentiality agreements block us from voicing our concerns, except to the very companies that may be failing to
  address these issues." VERIFIED (https://righttowarn.ai/ ; TechRepublic https://www.techrepublic.com/article/openai-google-deepmind-right-to-warn/).
- **Liguori's clause** ("in principle I'm not allowed to criticise Google for the duration of my life"): her words to the BBC only, and no outlet has seen the contract. REPORTED.
- **UK law:** ERA 1996 s43J voids any term that stops a *protected disclosure*. VERIFIED (OUP, Whistleblowing: Law and Practice https://academic.oup.com/book/44670/chapter/378761341 ; EAT, Ajaz 2023
  https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/eat/2023/142). A disclosure is protected only if it shows one of six "relevant failures" (crime, a breached legal duty, health and safety risk...) in the public interest,
  and going to the media is protected only in narrow cases (s43G/H). VERIFIED (CMS https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/whistleblower-protection-and-reporting-channels/united-kingdom ; Thompsons
  https://thompsonstradeunion.law/news/employment-law-review/weekly-issue-452/blowing-the-whistle). So criticism of a *lawful* contract ("ashamed of the direction") is probably not protected, and a non-disparagement
  term may still bind it. UNCERTAIN (our reading). Victims and Prisoners Act 2024 s17 (in force 2025-10-01) voids NDAs only for crime victims reporting to police, lawyers or regulators, not to the media. VERIFIED
  (Farrer https://www.farrer.co.uk/news-and-insights/new-restriction-on-enforceability-of-ndas-against-victims-of-crime/ ; DAC Beachcroft). Employment Rights Act 2025 (Royal Assent 2025-12-18) voids NDAs on
  harassment and discrimination only; expected to start in 2027, not retrospective. VERIFIED (Addleshaw Goddard
  https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/insights/insights-briefings/2025/employment/ban-confidentiality-clauses-ndas-covering-harassment-discrimination-work-other-developments/ ; MFMac 2026-05-20
  https://www.mfmac.com/insights/employment/what-does-the-future-hold-for-non-disclosure-agreements/). **Never say "illegal" or "unenforceable".**
- **US law:** NLRB *McLaren Macomb* (2023-02-21): offering severance deals with broad non-disparagement or confidentiality terms breaks NLRA s8(a)(1), for non-supervisory staff only. It still stands, but GC Carey
  lists it as a reversal target. VERIFIED (BakerHostetler https://www.bakerlaw.com/alerts/nlrb-restricts-employers-ability-include-standard-confidentiality-non-disparagement-provisions-severance-agreements ; Venable
  2026-06 https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2026/06/will-the-nlrb-revisit-mclaren-macomb ; Foley 2026-08
  https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/08/a-new-nlrb-roadmap-for-reversing-mclaren-macomb-and-other-biden-era-labor-precedent/). It covers US staff and speech about working conditions; whether
  military-ethics criticism counts is UNCERTAIN. California's SB 53 (signed 2025-09-29, in force 2026-01-01) protects frontier-AI whistleblowers on *catastrophic risk* only. VERIFIED (FPF
  https://fpf.org/blog/californias-sb-53-the-first-frontier-ai-law-explained/ ; Greenberg Traurig). The federal AI Whistleblower Protection Act was introduced in May 2025 and had not passed as of 2026-09-25.
  VERIFIED (Coons https://www.coons.senate.gov/news/press-releases/senators-coons-grassley-introduce-ai-whistleblower-protection-act ; Deseret
  https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/09/25/john-curtis-back-bill-protecting-ai-whistleblower/).
- **Google's own record:** in a 2019 NLRB settlement Google posted a notice of staff's right to discuss workplace issues. VERIFIED
  (https://www.engadget.com/2019-09-12-google-employees-political-discussions-workplace-issues-nlrb.html ; The Register).
- **Real people in cartoon form:** in the US, a caricature of a public figure that can't reasonably be read as stating facts is protected (*Hustler v. Falwell*, 1988). VERIFIED
  (https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-485/pdf/USREPORTS-485-46.pdf). English law has no general image right; the risk is passing off only where a false endorsement is implied (*Fenty v Arcadia* [2015]
  EWCA Civ 3). VERIFIED (https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewca/civ/2015/3 ; Law Gazette). TikTok bans synthetic likenesses of private people and political endorsements by public figures. VERIFIED (PetaPixel
  https://petapixel.com/2023/03/21/tiktoks-new-guidelines-say-ai-generated-media-must-be-disclosed ; BGR). So Pichai and Hegseth may appear as respectful caricatures. Liguori and Kirsch get generic figures plus a
  name caption (never traced from BBC photos), and the anonymous staff get faceless silhouettes. GUIDANCE.
- **Logos:** Google lets articles and videos *reference* Google without permission if nothing implies Google wrote or endorsed them; the logo itself needs approval. VERIFIED (primary:
  https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/media ; https://about.google/brand-resource-center/guidance/entertainment). Hand-letter "Google" in our ink; never deface a logo (no blood, crosshairs or skull
  on the "G"). GUIDANCE.
- **Crediting the BBC:** quotation (CDPA s30(1ZA)) and current-events reporting (s30(2)) are fair dealing *with sufficient acknowledgement*, and s30(2) excludes photographs. VERIFIED
  (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1988/48/section/30 ; https://www.copyrightuser.org/understand/exceptions/news-reporting/). Put "Source: BBC News, 21 Oct 2026" on screen while BBC quotes run and in the
  caption; no BBC logo, footage or photos.
- **AI label:** YouTube exempts clearly unrealistic animation from its synthetic-content disclosure, but a realistic synthetic voice of a real person needs it. VERIFIED
  (https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content ; CNN 2024-03-18). Keep our "AI label ON" rule for the narration voice.

## 4. Platform reach
- **Instagram/Threads, 2024:** no proactive recommendation of "political content" from accounts you don't follow (Explore, Reels, feed recommendations, Suggested Users). Political content is defined as "potentially
  related to things like laws, elections, or social topics" (settings text: "social topics that affect a group of people and/or society at large"). VERIFIED (Axios 2024-02-09
  https://www.axios.com/2024/02/09/meta-political-content-moderation-threads ; The Markup 2024-03-25 https://themarkup.org/automated-censorship/2024/03/25/instagram-creators-check-if-your-posts-are-political).
- **2025 change:** Meta is phasing political content back in with a "more personalized approach" and a less/standard/more control; Mosseri said it has "proven impractical to draw a red line around what is and is not
  political content". VERIFIED (TechCrunch 2025-01-07 https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/07/meta-to-phase-back-in-political-content-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads ; Business Today 2025-01-09 ; Washington Times
  2025-01-09). The current default per country (still "Limit"?) is UNCERTAIN (TechTarget, undated: https://www.techtarget.com/whatis/feature/How-to-control-Instagrams-political-content-limits). Professional accounts
  can see recommendation eligibility in Account Status and appeal. REPORTED. Content that breaks the Recommendations Guidelines (incl. violent material) is shown less in Explore and Reels. VERIFIED
  (https://help.instagram.com/313829416281232 ; https://help.instagram.com/613868662393739). Teen accounts have been PG-13 by default since 2025-10-14 (global in 2026). VERIFIED (CNN via KRDO
  https://krdo.com/news/2025/10/14/instagram-will-now-be-pg-13-for-teens-with-metas-latest-update/ ; CBC).
- **Is this political?** Pentagon + Hegseth + war + Israel is very likely a "social topic/government" item. UNCERTAIN (Meta doesn't publish its classifier); plan as if yes.
- **TikTok:** violent or graphic content is ineligible for the For You feed (FYF); public-interest graphic footage may get a warning screen, an 18+ limit or FYF exclusion; unverified claims about unfolding events
  are excluded. VERIFIED (Engadget https://www.engadget.com/tiktok-is-trying-to-clean-up-its-for-you-recommendations-210057825.html ; TechCrunch 2025-08-15
  https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/15/tiktoks-new-guidelines-add-subtle-changes-for-live-creators-ai-content-and-more/ ; TikTok to UK Parliament https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/141369/default/). No
  general demotion of political content found (only political ads and state media are limited): UNCERTAIN. The US joint venture closed around 2026-01-22; users then alleged suppression of political posts (ICE,
  anti-Zionism) and California opened a review, while TikTok blamed an Oracle outage. VERIFIED (MacTrast https://www.mactrast.com/2026/01/tiktok-finalize-joint-venture-deal-to-avoid-us-bans/amp/ ; AAP via Katherine
  Times https://www.katherinetimes.com.au/story/9161969/tiktok-accused-of-suppressing-content-critical-of-trump/ ; Reuters via Arab News https://www.arabnews.com/node/2630875/offbeat).
- **Trigger words:** TikTok publishes no banned-word list, and "algospeak" evidence is anecdotal. UNCERTAIN (PBS https://www.pbs.org/video/is-social-media-censorship-unaliving-language-tab75t ; ICWSM 2025). Don't
  use algospeak ("unalive" reads as unserious). Keep "kill", "bomb", "genocide", "Gaza" out of on-screen text, captions and hashtags. GUIDANCE.
- **YouTube:** context must be *in the video* (not the title or description); war-zone footage may be age-restricted. VERIFIED (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802008 ; EDSA
  https://blog.youtube/inside-youtube/look-how-we-treat-educational-documentary-scientific-and-artistic-content-youtube/). Since Jan 2026, non-graphic videos on controversial issues can earn full ads. VERIFIED
  (TechCrunch 2026-01-16 https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/16/youtube-relaxes-monetization-guidelines-for-some-controversial-topics/ ; AP via ABC).
- **Election windows:** Israel's Knesset election is on 2026-10-27 (REPORTED, JPost https://jpost.com/israel-news/article-904060) and the US midterms on 2026-11-03 (VERIFIED, 2 U.S.C. §7
  https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/2/7). Election-integrity modes are on, so keep partisan framing (Trump, Hegseth) out of the hook and caption.
- **Reach DO:** frame it as a work/tech story (a person quits her job over AI); no gore; on-screen context; Google's reply on screen; check Account Status 24 h after posting. **DON'T:** use #politics #Gaza #Israel
  #war; put weapons or explosions on frame 0 or the cover; use real war footage or photos; put Hegseth or Trump in the hook. GUIDANCE.

## 5. Tone (war-related topic, cartoon style). All GUIDANCE.
- DO: keep the camera on people and paper (a badge, a resignation letter, a contract with clauses highlighted, a laptop closing); show "classified networks" as locked doors or a server room; use a muted palette and
  silence for the war lines; give the narration a sincere, quiet voice direction; give Google's reply equal visual weight; keep the mascot concerned, never joking.
- DON'T: draw explosions, bodies, blood, drones striking, skulls, crosshairs on people, Gaza imagery or "killer robots"; put comic sound effects on war beats; draw an evil Pichai or a devil Hegseth; defame by image
  (Google's logo on a bomb); use sarcasm about victims; use alarm sirens or glitch "EXPOSED" graphics. The jolt should come from the human stakes (quit, afraid, "for life"), not from violence.

## 6. Safe phrasings for the strongest claims
- Hook (attributed, plain): "Google's own AI engineers are quitting, and they say they're scared to speak." Defensible: BBC "several" + "staff are scared to speak out".
- Personal: "She quit Google's AI lab. She says her contract bans her from criticising Google for life." REPORTED: keep "she says".
- "Google's AI is now cleared for classified military work, for 'any lawful' purpose." VERIFIED.
- "Google once promised: no AI weapons. In February 2025 it quietly deleted that promise." VERIFIED.
- "Over 600 staff asked the CEO to say no. Google signed within a day." VERIFIED (timeline §6).
- "The deal gives Google no right to veto how the Pentagon uses it." VERIFIED (contract wording).
- Google's side: "Google says it follows a consistent process, checks its AI principles, and has strict policies against retaliation." VERIFIED.

## Hook-worthy facts (all VERIFIED)
1. Google's deal lets the Pentagon use Gemini for "any lawful government purpose" on classified networks, and gives Google no "right to control or veto" the Pentagon's lawful operational decisions.
2. In 2018 Google promised in writing not to build AI weapons; on 4 Feb 2025 it deleted that promise.
3. On 27 Apr 2026, 600+ Google staff, including 20+ directors and VPs, asked Pichai to refuse classified work; Google signed within about a day.
4. Anthropic refused the same "all lawful purposes" terms and was branded a "supply chain risk"; Google signed.
5. Since May 2026 a Google director (Mayrhofer) and two DeepMind researchers (Turner, Liguori) have publicly quit over the deal.
6. In 2024, current and former OpenAI and DeepMind staff warned that "broad confidentiality agreements block us from voicing our concerns".
7. In 2024 OpenAI's exit papers barred leavers from ever criticising it, on pain of losing vested equity; it dropped this once Vox exposed it.
8. Staff told the BBC they're scared to speak out; Google answers that it has "strict workplace policies in place to prevent retaliation of any kind".
