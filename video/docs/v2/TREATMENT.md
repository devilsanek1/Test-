## 1. Concept, the understanding shift, why it stops, holds and gets shared
**One sentence:** a small woman walks out of Google's canal-side building into a golden October evening, and on each clause of her claim an
outline copy of her, a little greyer each time, pops up ahead of her on the towpath; on "for life" the whole row takes one step and more outlines
pop in down the towpath; the film then measures her one small window against a long wall of windows, a deal the size of the Pentagon and
Google's own words, and walks her home, where her words go up on a board in her street.
- **Shift.** BEFORE: "once you quit, you can say what you like about your old job." AFTER: *a Google AI engineer who quit over its military work
  says her contract bans her from criticizing Google for life; staff told the BBC they're scared too; the April deal lets the Pentagon use
  Google's AI for "any lawful government purpose" (with its oversight clause) and Google can't veto how it's used; Google says it follows its AI
  principles and bans retaliation; she talks anyway because, in her words, "talking about facts is not criticising".*
- **Stops:** a canal world drawing itself under SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.; her big window goes dark, our exit ding, the glass
  doors slide open: she's out, free, in the gold... and by 2.5 s copies of her, already greying, walk ahead of her.
- **Holds:** five blinds drop on "scared" in a long wall of lit windows; the open loop as a picture (her home window, blind UP, 13.0 s); the
  one crane (21.3 s); a new fact in the world every 3-5 s.
- **Shared:** the cover (5.30 s): her greying selves walking ahead of her under the claim ("she'll be grey and still can't say anything");
  the loop, "criticising" landing on "NO CRITICIZING".

Retention checkpoints = the pilot's sound-off cold read at 216 px [T6]; the question: "what will happen to her?"

## 2. The world, its sets, the light, the palette, the line
**One world:** one London canal (King's-Cross-like, generic, never traced) on one late-October evening, plus ONE trip through the sun to an April
morning in Arlington, Virginia. **The map** (K2 `map.ts`, §9): the canal runs east-west. Along its NORTH bank, from east to west: Google's canal
wing → a towpath walk → ONE dated fingerpost (both plates pointing west) → her brick terrace, the café boat moored below it → gasholders and a
viaduct far west. Her way home is WEST, so S4 and S8 are one walk home (the post is behind her in S8). Lenses: S1-S4 and S7 look north from the
south bank or the water (west = screen LEFT); S8 looks west down her towpath; S6 looks south (its morning sun in the east = screen left).
- **A, Google's bank:** Google's long building meets the canal as a CANAL WING: light brick, stone fins, tall glass, TWO storeys under a planted
  roof terrace (parapet <= y 600 in every shot); the rest of the building steps up in tiers far behind it, hidden from the canal-level lenses of
  S1 and S7, seen only in S2 as small stepped tiers, all under y 600. Glass double doors under a canopy hand-lettered "Google" in ink (no logo or
  logo colours); the far towpath; moored boats; a road bridge in the west gap. Our bank: broad canal-side steps with people sitting (S1), the
