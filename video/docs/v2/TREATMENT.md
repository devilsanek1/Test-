## 1. Concept, the understanding shift, why it stops, holds and gets shared
**One sentence:** a small woman walks out of Google's long canal-side building into a golden October evening, and on each clause of her claim an
outline copy of her, a little older each time, pops up ahead of her on the towpath until the row runs off the frame on "for life"; the film then
measures her one small window against a wall of hundreds, a deal the size of the Pentagon and Google's own words, and walks her home, where her
words go up on a board in her street.
- **Shift.** BEFORE: "once you quit, you can say what you like about your old job." AFTER: *a Google AI engineer who quit over its military work
  says her contract bans her from criticizing Google for life; staff told the BBC they're scared too; the April deal lets the Pentagon use
  Google's AI for "any lawful government purpose" (with its oversight clause) and Google can't veto how it's used; Google says it follows its AI
  principles and bans retaliation; she talks anyway because, in her words, "talking about facts is not criticising".*
- **Stops:** a canal world drawing itself under SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.; a window goes dark, our exit ding, glass doors open:
  she's out, free, in the gold... and by 2.5 s copies of her, already greying, walk ahead of her.
- **Holds:** five blinds drop in a wall of hundreds on "scared"; the open loop as a picture (her window, blind UP, 13.0 s); the one crane (20.9
  s); a new fact in the world every 3-5 s.
- **Shared:** the cover (5.30 s), her older selves running off the frame under the claim ("she'll be 80 and still can't say anything"); the loop,
  "criticising" landing on "NO CRITICIZING".

Retention checkpoints = the pilot's sound-off cold read at 216 px [T6]; the question: "what will happen to her?"

## 2. The world, its sets, the light, the palette, the line
**One world:** one London canal (King's-Cross-like, generic, never traced) on one late-October evening, plus ONE trip through the sun to an April
morning in Arlington, Virginia. **The map** (a contract file, §9 K2): the canal runs east-west; the NORTH bank holds Google's long building
(east) and, a walk west along the same towpath, her brick terrace (never opposite Google); two dated fingerposts stand on that towpath west of
her terrace; the camera looks north from the south bank or the water, so west (the sun, her way home) is screen LEFT in S1-S4.
- **A, Google's bank:** an 8-storey building of light brick, stone fins and glass; a glass door with a speed gate, the canopy hand-lettered
  "Google" in ink (no logo or logo colours); far towpath, moored boats, a road bridge far west; our near bank (railing, bollards, a bench, a
