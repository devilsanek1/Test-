# T1 "Into the Sunset": camera-journey treatment for the finished v2 film (39.08 s)

Treatment writer T1 (lens: one continuous camera journey through one living world), 2026-10-27. Read with `../house_digest.md`
(HD), `../beat_map.md` (BM), `../teardown.md` (TD), `../toolkit.md` (TK). Clock: take 2, `public/v2/voice.wav`; f = round(s x 60).
Nothing here comes from the dropped production (no desk, contract page, scroll of years, cat, room or its palettes).

## 1. Title, concept, why it works
**Into the Sunset.** One travelling camera spends one October evening with her on a London canal: out of Google's door into
the sunset, along the water past the staff's windows, west through the setting sun to Washington in April for the deal, and
back at dusk to a bench across from the door, where the film loops to her walking out again.
- **Stop:** frame 0 is a golden canal being drawn under SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE., a bright chime, and a
  small woman walking out into the sunset (the oldest happy ending). By 2 s it turns: on each clause the sun steps down and her
  shadow stretches back across the plaza, until on "life" it touches Google's door. She is out; the reach (she says) is not.
- **Understand:** BEFORE "once you quit, you can say what you like". AFTER: she walked out, but says her contract still reaches
  her; staff told the BBC the same fear (three blinds come down); she talks anyway because what she talks about is on the record
  (dated posts on her towpath, Dec 2025 then Apr 2026; the deal's own words on a billboard; the gate that closes on Google), and
  Google's answer stands on the same kind of board, same size, same light. The places of the walk carry the facts, so the payoff
  lands without narration, and the loop sends you back to watch the walk again "as facts".
- **Share:** two frames worth sending: the shadow touching the door under the frame-0 words (the cover), and the crane landing
  on the Pentagon under the same sun.

## 2. The world, the light, the palette
**One world under one sun.** A canal quarter in London (generic, no real address). North bank: a long brick-and-glass office,
"Google DeepMind" hand-lettered in ink on its entrance canopy (never the logo or its four colours), its plaza, the far towpath;
a footbridge west of the door. South bank (ours), west to east: two dated posts, the footbridge foot, the newsagent kiosk with
two A-boards and a bench (straight across from the door: "View B"), the café terrace (across from the east wing). Behind: a
London roofline (chimney pots, a spire, cranes, a far gasholder frame). Through the sun: **Washington in late April**: a rooftop
terrace in the office towers south of the Pentagon, the Pentagon, the Potomac, the far city and the Washington Monument; a
roadside billboard by the Pentagon's delivery gate. Sets: canal north bank + plaza, footbridge, south towpath (kiosk, bench,
posts, café), the floating-bookshop narrowboat, Washington rooftop, Pentagon vista, billboard + gate.
**Time = light (story time).** Golden hour (W01) -> sunset (W02-W04) -> Washington noon, late April (W05-W06) -> afterglow
dusk with lamps on (W07-W09) -> un-build -> golden again. London has blowing October leaves; Washington has blossom and tulips:
the season says, with no words, that the deal came months before she quit (BM V13). Never night-black: dusk is a light rose sky.
**Palette: new house sister "10 Towpath" + one cool sister "10b Towpath Spring" for Washington only** (the scale beat; the
owner's taste signal: a clean, calm page for a serious story, a cold sister only for the scale beat). Values measured (CIE L*/C*):

## 6. The scale moment and the cozy home base
**Scale (W05, 3.57 s, 9 % of the runtime, the one big move):** from two people on their lunch break to the US Department of
Defense, under the same sun she was looking at. It must feel GREAT: the world runs past every edge, the Monument huge yet far
in the cloud gap, birds and a jogger as tiny anchors, the drop under the parapet; daylight, blossom, people at work: vast, never
monstrous (BM V3). Agency within 5 s: W06's people reading and handing over. It is also the one break of the coziness (the turn
from her canal to the system), and the heaviest line (W06 "can't veto") has no cute props.
**Cozy home base: the south towpath** (RIVERSIDE dose: level across water, the far facade as the backdrop, one lane of life,
the water as the moat): the café (W04 to 17.9), the bridge breath (W02), the kiosk and her bench under one lamp (W07-W09):
enclosure from the facade belt and the kiosk awning, a lamp pool, steam, her plant and Pip as the signs of care. Cozy = 18.2 s of
the 35.0 s outside the W06 insert (52 %). Cozy holds are locked or float (8-15 px/s), never a zoom.
