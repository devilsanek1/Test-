# T3 "pip-witness": THE LONG SHADOW (treatment for the finished v2 film, 39.08 s, voice take 2)

Lens: Pip, the channel's mascot, is the viewer's stand-in, a small curious witness walking through her story in one living city: warm and dry, never
mocking. Built to `house_digest.md`, `beat_map.md` (clock, facts F, rules V/A), `teardown.md` and `toolkit.md`. Nothing comes from the dropped
production (no contract paper, scroll of years, desk, cat, badge-into-box, highlighter, speech bubbles, Cream & Cobalt or Dusk).

## 1. Title, concept, why it travels
- ***The Long Shadow.*** Pip follows one woman's story through an autumn canal city at sunset. She walks out of Google, and her own shadow still reaches
  back to its door. Pip watches staff talk to a reporter under a bridge, crosses the sun to the April spring of the Pentagon deal, and comes home to two
  equal noticeboards. Every fact is something Pip sees happen in a real place.
- **Stops:** the claim is on frame 0, a ding plays as she leaves Google into a huge low sun, and her shadow won't leave the door; on "for life" every
  shadow shoots to the horizon. **Understands:** each line is an event: the bench, a door match into the deal, a handle changing hands for "can't
  veto", boards matched at equal size. **Shares:** the shadow frame (the screenshot), ONE named joke (Pip's false-relief shrug), and a payoff board that
  fills with the facts we saw. **Shift:** BEFORE "quit, and you can say what you like"; AFTER the beat map §1 sentence.

## 2. The world: one canal city, one sky, one October sunset (plus one April across the sea)
- **An unnamed brick city with a London flavour**, never traced. **North bank:** Google's AI lab, a 5-storey warehouse behind a one-storey glass
  pavilion with "Google" hand-lettered on its fascia (no logo). **South bank:** her two-storey terrace (shop below, flat above). The canal runs WEST to
  the low sun, and every London shot keeps it.
- **Sets (all new):** L1 Lab Street (north bank, looking west). L2 the bench by the footbridge stair (south towpath, facing the lab). L3 Her Corner
  (newsagent, teal door, lit window). L4 Bridge Ends (two identical noticeboards). Across the sea: D1 the Potomac bank (cherry blossom, a rail, the
  Pentagon across the river, a skyline strip with the obelisk) and D2 the Pentagon gate (limestone, a gatehouse wall, a plain service door).
- **Time = story:** London is late October; the sun touches the horizon on "for life", then holds while the lamps come on. DC is April, late
  afternoon. With the sound off, leaves mean now and blossom means April. She is never in April (V13).
- **Palette: new house sister "10 Canal Sunset"** (+ "10b April", the one deliberate shift), CIELAB-checked:

## 4. The opening, frame by frame (S1, one continuous shot, BUSY), and the loop
**Layout:** L1 at eye level, looking west. Right third: the glass pavilion (door x ~640-740, y ~980-1190; "Google" fascia y ~640-700), with the
warehouse set back behind it. Left: the canal, a moored boat with smoke, her terrace across. A footbridge with a busker; the sun at the VP (centre

(x ~590, y ~1150) facing the door. One slow ~5 % pull-out. **The device (physics-true):** she walks straight from the door toward the sun. As the sun
sinks, her shadow lies along her path, so its tip stays on Google's doorstep however far she walks. When the sun touches the horizon, shadows run to
infinity.

## 6. The scale moment and the cozy home base
- **Scale (S5, 9 % of the runtime, exactly one):** from Pip's tiny world at a rail to a building that won't end at the frame edge. It hits every cue:
  6 planes, a packed far band, flat atmospheric steps (0 / 12 / 22 / 40 / 65 % toward the sky), the drop to the river, a huge-yet-far obelisk with the
  hero cloud behind, huge things moving slow, and 3 tiers of life. It stays daylight-calm, and agency returns within 3 s (the handover).
- **Cozy home base (her corner S3/S4/S8; with S2's bench and S7's forecourt, ~65 % of the runtime):** a LANE/NOOK with brick on 2-3 sides and one
  opening, the street west to the sun (the threat far and outside). The lamp pool is warm on one side, the duck-egg sky cool on the other; full edges
  (racks, buckets, bikes, window boxes) frame a calm centre, with signs of care (swept leaves, her window box, a neighbour watering). **It breaks
  once:** April and the gate are open, institutional and comfortless, the heaviest stretch.
