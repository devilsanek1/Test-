# T5 "small-vs-vast": ONE SMALL WINDOW (treatment for the finished v2 film)

T5, 2026-10-27. Take 2 (39.08 s, 2345 f at 60 fps), seconds per `beat_map.md`. **W** = "her window" spot (300, 700),
**W2** = (640, 820). All art and sound new; nothing from the dropped pass (desk, contract, years, cat, Cream & Cobalt).

## 1. Title, concept, why it works
**One Small Window.** A small woman walks out of a vast building by a London canal, and the film keeps measuring her
claim against bigger things (her whole life laid out along the towpath, a wall of ~400 windows where a few blinds come
down, the vastest office building on earth across the ocean), then brings her home to one lit window, where she says
what she says: facts.
- **Stops:** frame 0 is a big, busy canal side being drawn, a tiny figure, SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.;
  a happy door chime, she steps out free... then outline copies of her, each one older, pop up along her path until the
  row runs off the frame edge on "for life". That row is the screenshot.
- **Understands:** every shot sets ONE small thing against ONE vast thing (her / her life ahead; her dark window / a
  wall of ~400; one person on a bench / the Pentagon; her lit window / the dusk city), so the stakes are seen, not
  narrated (Google's own beat is human-sized: fair). The shift is the beat map's, from "once you quit, you're free".
- **Shares:** the row of her older selves; the ocean's far side behind one person on a bench; a loop that bites.

## 2. World, light, palette
**One canal neighbourhood in London** (King's-Cross-like, generic, never traced), plus ONE trip across the ocean under
the same sun (when the sun is low over her canal it is early afternoon over the Pentagon: a true 5-h gap).
- **A. Campus bank:** a long 8-storey brick-and-terracotta building running off both edges, fins, 40-70 % lit windows,
  a glass door with "Google" hand-lettered small (no logo or colours); towpath, narrowboats, ducks, a footbridge with a
  brick-red bus; the forecourt (steps to the water, a fountain grid, plane trees).
- **B. Her bank (down the canal; the campus small in her views):** brick terrace + warehouse, balconies, pots, string
  lights, chimney pots, aerials, gasholder frames; her top-floor window; her roof terrace; a café boat.
- **C. Washington (20.4-28.1 only):** a hillside viewpoint terrace (balustrade + panorama plaque, bench, US newspaper
  box, lamppost) over the vista; the plaza at the Pentagon's Metro entrance (escalators, bus stop, food cart).
**Light = story time** (`tod` keys): golden -> late golden -> sun low west -> Washington afternoon -> London sunset -> dusk.
**Palette "Towpath"** (new sister of Mint & Clay: v1's mint moves from the sky into the water; `palettes.json` #10):
page/sky `#F6E6D6` (L* 92), page2 `#F1D9C6`, stone `#EBCDB6`, brick `#D98F6F`/`#B9705A`, water `#A9CFC6`/`#7FAFA8` (the
>= 10 % cool), **hero `#0B8577`** canal teal (L* 50, C* 33: her coat, door, window frame, boats, canal people; never on
Google's or the Pentagon's buildings), **anchor `#2E2236`** plum (L* 15), **pop `#EE6A2E`** = the sun disc only (1-3 %),
highlight `#F7BC97`, lamp `#F7CF86`, lines `#9A563C`, ink `#2B1B0A` (13.6:1), limestone `#ECE3D6` / lilac `#B9B3C9`,
dusk sky `#F0D3C8` (L* 87). **Why:** a calm, warm page for serious news; saturation only in the sun and the people;
institutions in quiet stone, no hero colour, glow or rim; shadows plum and teal, never grey or beige; no violet world.

## 3. Cast
- **Her:** SMALL and generic (120-170 px tall, never larger), back or rear 3/4, round head, dark plum low bun, long teal
  coat, cream tote (+ her desk plant in the hook); never a face, never traced, no photo reference; never in Washington.
- **The ghosts:** outline copies of her (ink + paper-white fill: reference 1's "not yet real" grammar), each older: G1
  now -> G2 longer coat -> G3 grey bun -> G4 cardigan -> G5 slight stoop -> G6 cane -> G7 cut by the frame edge.
- **"Google's staff":** five faceless window silhouettes in a wall of ~400; at Google's beat, small backs in its
  forecourt. **People** (new generator: round heads, tombstone torsos, one accessory, no two alike): walkers, joggers,
  cyclists, a pram, boat dwellers, window cleaners, neighbours, the Washington stranger, commuters, kids at fountains.
- **Pip, redrawn ("canal Pip"):** a pebble-shaped small elephant, leaf-shaped ears that fold (`ears-cover`) and flick, a
  short curled trunk, a three-hair tuft, teddy-bear soles, a short knitted scarf; body `#9398D8` (dE76 44-63 from every
  ground), belly/ears `#EDC0CB`, scarf `#5E6FB8`; ink ~3.4 px tapered, fill offset 3/2 px; 86-130 px; never in the
  caption band, the right rail or on the subject. Every shot, hops across every cut, leads the eye, ONE reaction per
  shot, idles always; neutral on Google's beat, never a gag near harm.
