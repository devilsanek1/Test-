# T4 "documentary": THE LONG WAY HOME

The finished v2 film (take 2, 39.08 s, 60 fps) from `house_digest`, `beat_map`, `teardown`, `toolkit` only, nothing from
the dropped cut. Times are voice seconds, hits on audible onsets ("life" 4.98, "veto" 26.50); scene ids D1-D9.

## 1. Title, concept, why it works
- **Title:** The Long Way Home. **Concept:** a hand-drawn documentary that follows one small figure walking home along a
  London canal on the evening she leaves Google's AI lab, as the light falls from gold to blue; the canal's bridges open
  onto every year her contract covers, the same setting sun carries us once to April on the Potomac where the Pentagon
  deal was made, and back to the towpath, where her words go up on the evening placard.
- **Stops:** frame 0 is a golden canal being drawn under SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.; a cheerful exit-gate
  chime, doors open, she walks out free; at 2 s the stretch beyond the bridge turns to winter, then spring, and on "for
  life" every bridge to the horizon runs through the seasons: a tiny teal figure walking into a tunnel of years.
- **Understands:** BEFORE "once you quit, you can say what you like"; AFTER: she says her contract follows her for life,
  staff told the BBC they're scared too, and she talks anyway, about facts: the deal's own words, the missing veto and
  Google's own answer, each on its own board with its source. **Shares:** the tunnel-of-years frame, her quotable
  distinction, and the exact deal clause worth forwarding.

## 2. The world, light and palette
- **One world, one sky, one sun: the waterside.** 82 % of the runtime is ONE London canal quarter (generic, never traced):
  a long lab (glass bays, brick piers, roof garden, hand-lettered "Google" on its canopy, no logo, no CCTV), the towpath
  (plane trees in October gold, lamps, benches, bollards), narrowboats (chimneys, pot plants, bikes), far-bank warehouses
  and gasholder rings, a road bridge with a newsstand kiosk at its foot, a lock with black-and-white beams and a keeper's
  cottage, a skyline of a station roof, chimneys, cranes. Once (D5-D6, 7.6 s) the same sun over the Potomac in April.
- **Story time = light; the palette never changes:** D1 golden hour, D2 sun on the roofs, D3-D7 sunset (April and London
  share it: the deal, Google and her get the same light), D8-D9 blue hour, lamps on; the loop un-builds to golden frame 0.
- **Palette "10 Canal Gold"** (new sister of 1 Mint & Clay and 9 Autumn Ember; add to `palettes.json`, run the DNA check).
  Page: sky `#F8E6D0` (L* 92), stucco `#F2DEC9`, towpath `#F1D2B6`, water `#9CC0B8` (cool, L* 75). Mids: brick `#C9705A` /
  `#DD9273`, red-brown line `#9A4A2E`. **Hero** canal teal `#008C8C` (L* 53, C 32) on HER only (coat, tote): the one teal
  figure in every London frame, strongest contrast, a 30-60 px calm ring (share 0.3-2 % by design: people small, owner
  2026-10-27). **Anchor** plum `#3B2433` (L* 18): silhouettes, hair, lock beams. **Pop** vermilion `#EE6A35` (C 71): the
  sun disc, FOR LIFE on frame 0, "facts" in D9, nothing else. Caption box `#F6B892`. Support: dusk blue `#5B6A94` /
  `#46527C` (shade sides; blue-hour top sky step `#8E9CC4`), Potomac slate `#A9B6CF`, lamp gold `#F7C98B` (every lit window
  and lamp in BOTH cities). Hook-only season confetti: snow `#EEF2F5`, blossom `#F4BFC6`, spring `#B9D29A`, summer

- **Why it fits:** gravity from a day ending, not darkness or a villain colour; warmth belongs to her city and its people;
