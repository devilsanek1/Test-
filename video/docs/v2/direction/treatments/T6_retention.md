# T6 "The Years Ahead": the retention editor's cut (v2 restart treatment)

T6, lens: retention (2026-10-27). Fixed: voice take 2 (39.08 s), `beat_map.md`'s clock and fairness rules, `house_digest.md`. Nothing from the dropped production;
every set, figure, Pip, prop and note made new. Times in voice seconds; f = frame at 60 fps.

## 1. Title, concept, why it holds

**THE YEARS AHEAD.** On an October evening she walks out of Google's door, and the rest of her life lines up ahead of her down the canal, one season after another,
all the way to the sun ("for life"). The film then walks that city through one night (staff windows lowering their blinds, her window staying open, the view from her
roof to the far Pentagon, the deal's words on a billboard, Google's words on its own board) and ends at sunrise on her answer, looping to the evening she left.

**Built on v1's leak** (76 % at 2 s, 58 % at 5 s, 43 % at 10 s, 33 % at 15 s, then flat): four jolts and an open loop by 15 s, then one clear picture per fact.

| Mark | What a cold scroller has by then, sound off | Device |
|---|---|---|
| 1 s | "SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE." over a scene drawing itself; a woman walks out of a door lettered "Google" with a box; the gate lights up with a chime | claim + person + sound + motion |
| 3 s | the expected (she walks off free into the sunset) is overtaken: two more of her have popped up down the canal, one in snow, one under blossom | a visual paradox: "wait, what?" |
| 5 s | the line of her reaches the sun, twelve seasons long, the last with a walking stick; the music stops on "life" | the claim as a picture: a lifetime |
| 10 s | a new place: just "one odd contract"... then three windows in Google's building light, and on "scared" their blinds come down | not just her: the second jolt |
| 15 s | her window, blind UP, she's on the phone: "So why is she talking?" | open loop, paid at 36.5 s |
| end | the sun rises behind her roof on "talking about facts is not criticising"; the seam lands on "NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE." | last line against first line = the share |

**Why they send it:** the cover (5.2 s) is the sentence in one picture; the loop seam sets her answer against the claim for the rewatch. **Understanding shift:**
from "once you quit you can say what you like" to the beat map's AFTER (her claim, the staff's fear, the April deal with its clause, Google's reply).

## 2. The world

**One canal quarter of a big northern city** (London-like, generic, nothing traced), one evening into the next dawn, on one map: the long brick-and-glass Google
building on a towpath (its door under a slim sign hand-lettered "Google" in red-brown ink, never logo colours), the canal with moored narrowboats and a footbridge
with a newsstand, a terraced lane up the hill, her top-floor flat and roof terrace, the billboard roof in front of it, and from up there the city to the sea and, on
the far shore, huge yet far, the Pentagon at storybook distance. Sets: GATE, FACADE, LANE, TERRACE, VIEW, BILLBOARD ROOF, PLAZA (§5).

**Time = light, never palette:** golden 0-13 s, sunset 13-20.5, dusk 20.5-28, blue hour 28-33, night 33-36.2, dawn 36.5-39, golden at the seam; October leaves fall.
