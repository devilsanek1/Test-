## 1. Pick one

**A (recommended). "She quit Google, and says her contract bans criticizing it, for life."** Lever: injustice + curiosity (one real person). 12 words.
- Frame 0 / cover, ALL CAPS, 7 words, no quote marks: **SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.** over a drawn contract page (her signature, one highlighted line, no readable clause text), plus a small "Source: BBC News, 21 Oct 2026".
- Hook seed, 0-2 s: "She quit Google. And she says her contract bans her from criticizing it... for life." [quiet disbelief, like reading a friend's text aloud; lean on CONTRACT; small beat, then FOR LIFE low and flat]. By ~4 s: "And she's not alone: Google AI staff told the BBC they're scared to speak out."
- Angle and emotion: "they can DO that?" (quitting should set you free), then "so why is she talking?" The video: the military work she quit over, the fear inside, and her answer, "talking about facts is not criticising".
- Why: the idea is the judges' #1 (L37, 73.3/90, #1 for 3 of 4 judges) and these words are the viewers' #1 (L15: #1 for Maria, Dev and Sam, #3 for Jayden; the only line all 4 would stop on AND share, 0 bait). Sam: "The word 'contract' is what makes me believe it."
- Sceptics: minor x8 across the 4 versions of this idea; none could refute it. Fixes applied: "her contract" named (all of them asked for it), and "for life" (short for her "for the duration of my life", so never in quote marks) instead of "ever" (all 4 viewers found EVER dramatic, 2 called it bait).
- Risk: one person's account of a contract we haven't seen, and Google's statement doesn't mention the clause. "SHE SAYS" stays full size on frame 0, the cover and the caption. Viewers will ask "is that legal?" and we never answer (N10).
- Truth status: PASS, attributed. The lifetime ban is her account (FL 5.2, REPORTED: "In principle I'm not allowed to criticise Google for the duration of my life"), backed by the BBC's own "despite her contract restricting her from doing so" (5.3) and staff's claim that their contracts bar public criticism (5.1). No outlet has published the contract and Google's reply doesn't address it, so "she says" stays on frame 0, the cover, the hook and the caption; "for life" is never in quote marks; no legal claim (N10). "Quit" = resigned in October 2026 (1.4); the hook's "staff told the BBC they're scared to speak out" is FL 5.1's safe wording.

"Engineers" is loose (one research engineer and one AI researcher are named) but not false. **Recommendation: A.** It's the judges' idea in the viewers' words, the only line that made all 4 viewers stop and share, and it carries both halves of "resigning and afraid" through one real person. B is the plain fallback.
