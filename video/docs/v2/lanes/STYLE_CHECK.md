# v2r style check: 12 questions every lane answers before it builds further

Answer in `video/docs/v2/lanes/stylecheck_<your id>.md` (COMMON.md "Style check"), numbered 1-12, about 60-90 lines in all. Answer from the sources,
not from memory: exact hex, px, % and times; after each answer the section you took it from (DIRECTION rule, CRAFT §, THROUGHLINE §, your
conform brief, STYLE.md §, TREATMENT §, CG / CS / LD / PAL, map.ts, rigs.ts, a kit README). Where a question says "your shots", answer for the
shots you own. Questions only here; the orchestrator grades against a private key. (Conformed to DIRECTION on 2026-10-27: questions 1-4, 6 and
9-12 changed; answers written before that don't count.)

1. **Colour and light.** Give the exact hex of the sky, the hero, the anchor and the pop; where each may go and two places each must never go. Which
   locked `CM` tokens are at least as saturated as the hero, and how may they be used? Which light state and which rig (`rigs.ts`) is each of your
   shots in, where is its key and which way do its shadows fall? What gets contact, cast and form shadows, where may rim light go, how is a lit
   window drawn, and which command with which flags checks your light?
2. **People and sizes.** Who may be in your shots' foreground (the bottom third), what goes there instead of the old head-backs, and where do strangers
   go? Name three older people or size rules that are now void. How tall is she, the biggest stranger in the film, the biggest seated figure? What is
   the legibility floor, and what makes an extra a forbidden look-alike?
3. **A full frame, alive but calm.** What is this film's density bar (it has no G3 board), and what is v3's G5b pilot good for? Which profile is each
   of your shots, and is any of its numbers a quota? How many noticeable supporting movers may move at once, and what may happen in the 0.4 s
   around a join? Name six checks a paused frame must pass at 216 px, what fills your shots' y 1250-1920, and the commands (with flags) that prove it.
4. **The opening and the clock.** What is on frame 0 (what is pale, what draws on, which colour shows)? In what order does colour arrive, what lands
   last and at what time? When do the voice and the first sound start? What is the expected thing, when does reality overtake it, and what happens at
   4.54-4.79 and at 4.98? How does the film loop (what un-builds onto what, and where is Pip)? Which file gives the beat times, and is a K2 / kit
   default time film or shot time (V2S6 and V2S7's new starts)?
5. **The Arcane crane and scale.** Which Arcane shot is the owner's favourite, and what is its SHAPE (the parts of the one move, the pivot)? Give V2S5's ease
   and times and its suspense beats by progress. Give six scale cues with their numbers. What breaks the feet-to-horizon cue in S5, and what happens now?
6. **Cozy enclosure, the held camera and your joins.** What does "held, not shut" require (mass, perimeter, opening, top gutter, care, lamp pool)? Why
   is our sky the only void, and where must the enclosure come from? What is the value sandwich? Give the float law (all numbers), why there is never
   a zoom in a hold, which camera moves were retired in your shots, and the camera-speed limits with the command you run. For each of your joins: its
   carrier letter, its engine type and numbers, its midpoint against the next first heard word, and what may move in its window.
7. **The caption band and screen zones.** Where is nothing ever drawn? What may sit in y 290-600 and why must y 299-317 stay clear? Where may flyers and puffs
   go, and which kit default must you override? Give the key-read zone, the right rail and the bottom-third rule; the caption font, size, baselines and
   the only way captions may appear.
8. **Fairness on the boards.** Which two boards are the identical pair, and what must match between them (at least six things)? What is the deal board's
   timing rule? Which exact words may carry quote marks? What may Google's beat never have, and until when is it uninterrupted? Who owns every board,
   tag, post, news box and plaque, and what is the type scale?
9. **Line, texture and edges.** Which pen draws the film, and what sets a line's width and colour? Give tier A's outline and detail widths and colour,
   tier B's line (and who keeps one), tier C's. What may you never do by hand to `INK_PX` or sketch options, which marks must you delete, and how is an
   A outline authored and promoted? How do the ghosts keep their outline? Is texture on? How does a big field avoid being bare? Are paper edges on,
   and if the owner asks for them, where may they never go and what is wrong with the stock preset?
10. **The NEVER list.** Name eight things DIRECTION retired that this film used to do (each with its v2 instance), six from the owner's rejected first
    cut, ten fairness breaks with their V numbers, four rendering or camera NEVERs, and the flash rule (with the two lamp runs it bites).
11. **Pip.** Its tier, size range (and the exceptions), share of the frame, four colours with their C* cap, its line, where it may never be, its ONE
    reaction in each of your shots (word and time), how it crosses each of your joins (the carrier, its seats on both sides), and how many Pips may be
    on screen (the seam; S7 into S8).
12. **The references.** For reference 1, v1, reference 2, the v3 G5b pilot, the enclosure photo, the palette refs, the texture ref and Arcane: what
    you study in each and what you never take; why the animated sketchbook is not a reference here. At what size do you compare, how do
    you get the frames, and what may never be committed?
