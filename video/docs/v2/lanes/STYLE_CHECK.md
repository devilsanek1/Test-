# v2r style check: 12 questions every lane answers before it builds further

Answer in `video/docs/v2/lanes/stylecheck_<your id>.md` (COMMON.md "Style check"), numbered 1-12, about 60-90 lines in all. Answer from the sources,
not from memory: exact hex, px, % and times; after each answer the section you took it from (STYLE.md §, TREATMENT §, CG / CS / LD / PAL, map.ts,
a kit README). Where a question says "your shots", answer for the shots you own. Questions only here; the orchestrator grades against a private key.

1. **Colour jobs.** Give the exact hex of the sky, the hero, the anchor and the pop; where each may go and two places each must never go. Which locked
   `CM` tokens are at least as saturated as the hero, and how may they be used? Which light state is each of your shots in, and what may a light state
   change (and never change)? Where does the light come from in your shots?
2. **People and sizes.** How wide may a near head-back be, what exactly does that number measure, and what hv does it allow for K4 `NearBack`? Name three
   older size numbers that are now void. How tall is she, the tallest standing extra, the biggest seated figure? What makes an extra a forbidden look-alike?
3. **A full frame.** Which profile is each of your shots, and what are its paused-frame numbers and its life numbers? Name six checks a paused frame must
   pass at 216 px, the order in which you fix an empty frame, and what fills your shots' y 1250-1920. Which commands (with which flags) prove it?
4. **The opening and the clock.** What is on frame 0? In what order does colour arrive, what lands last and at what time? When do the voice and the first
   sound start? What is the expected thing, when does reality overtake it, and what happens at 4.54-4.79 and at 4.98? How does the film loop? Which file
   gives the beat times, and is a K2 / kit default time film or shot time?
5. **The Arcane crane and scale.** Which Arcane shot is the owner's favourite, and what is its SHAPE (the parts of the one move, the pivot)? Give V2S5's ease
   and times and its suspense beats by progress. Give six scale cues with their numbers. What breaks the feet-to-horizon cue in S5, and what happens now?
6. **Cozy enclosure and the held camera.** What does "held, not shut" require (mass, perimeter, opening, top gutter, care, lamp pool)? Why is our sky the only
   void, and where must the enclosure come from? What is the value sandwich? Describe the cozy lane, the float law (all numbers), why there is never a zoom
   in a hold, and the camera-speed limits with the command you run.
7. **The caption band and screen zones.** Where is nothing ever drawn? What may sit in y 290-600 and why must y 299-317 stay clear? Where may flyers and puffs
   go, and which kit default must you override? Give the key-read zone, the right rail and the bottom-third rule; the caption font, size, baselines and
   the only way captions may appear.
8. **Fairness on the boards.** Which two boards are the identical pair, and what must match between them (at least six things)? What is the deal board's
   timing rule? Which exact words may carry quote marks? What may Google's beat never have, and until when is it uninterrupted? Who owns every board,
   tag, post, news box and plaque, and what is the type scale?
9. **Line, texture and edges.** Give the ink colour and weights by depth (and for near backs, ghosts, Pip), the open-contour share, the overshoot and
   long-line rules, the misregistration and how shade is drawn. Is texture on? How does a big field avoid being bare? Are paper edges on, and if the owner
   asks for them, where may they never go and what is wrong with the stock preset?
10. **The NEVER list.** Name eight things from the owner's rejected first cut and his other rejections, ten fairness breaks with their V numbers, four
    rendering or camera NEVERs, and the flash rule (with the two lamp runs it bites).
11. **Pip.** Its size range (and the exception), its four colours, its line, where it may never be, its jobs in every shot, its ONE reaction in each of your
    shots (word and time), how it crosses cuts (arc rule; S3's exit edge), and how many Pips may be on screen (S1 at t < 0; S7 into S8).
12. **The references.** For reference 1, v1, reference 2, the v3 G5b pilot, the enclosure photo, the palette refs, the texture ref, the sketchbook and
    Arcane: what you study in each and what you never take. At what size do you compare, how do you get the frames, and what may never be committed?
