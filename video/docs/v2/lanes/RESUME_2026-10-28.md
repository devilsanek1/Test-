## What every shot gets (with the shared tools; never hand-roll them)
- **Depth** (`lib/depth.ts`): 4-6 declared planes, each receding toward the air behind it (`makeAtmosphere({air: horizonColor(...)})`,
  `recede` / `planePalette`); the subject separated from what is behind it (`backing`: a real thing of the set, never a blank slab);
  ONE camera per shot (`groundCam`): the ground, the quays and the buildings share its horizon and vanishing point, no level facade on a
  perspective floor; sizes and texture by distance (no nearer thing smaller per metre than a farther one).
- **Sky** (`lib/sky.tsx`): add your shot's preset to `src/v2r/world/skies.ts` next to S1's (`S<n>_SKY` from `eveningSky(...)` or the
  April morning's keys, `S<n>_AIR = horizonColor(...)`), the caption band nearly flat, warm at the sun and the horizon; `skyProblems`
  empty. Arlington (S5-S6) is an April morning: its own keys, same method.
- **Light** (`lib/light.tsx`, `src/v2r/world/rigs.ts`): your set's rig; contact shadows under everything that touches a surface, form and
  cast shadows; water shades take `tint="water"` (TINT_CANAL.water).
- **Particles** (`lib/particles.tsx`): a few, and SEEN at phone size (owner, on v4: "I don't see any changes in the particles"): leaves
  26-40 px crossing the mid ground (2-3 per hold), glints 20-36 px (4-6 at once in the sun's path), motes 6-10 px against shade (CRAFT §f.6),
  inside the mover budget, none entering in the 0.4 s around a join; `particleProblems` clean.
- **Parallax float** (`lib/depth.ts` `parallaxOffset` / `floatAt` / `floatProblems`, as S1 uses it in `V2S1_float.tsx`): where your shot
  holds, a slow float with planes moving by depth (sky and captions 0), at rest at every join and map-critical frame.
