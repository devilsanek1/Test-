# v2r G5 assembly v1: fresh review 1

Film: `video/deliverables/v2/v2r_G5_assembly_v1.mp4` (39.08 s, 2345 frames, 60 fps, 1080x1920, with the mix).
Reviewer: one fresh agent, two stages kept apart. Stage 1 was written before any doc was opened.
Method: frames every 0.25 s at 360 px wide, the first 3 s every 0.1 s, every frame (60 fps) around the joins and
the loop seam, a few 1:1 stills; the captions stand in for the voice.

## Cold read

Written before reading any doc, as a stranger scrolling on a phone.

**What it is about.** A woman who quit Google's AI lab says her exit contract bans her from criticising Google for
life, and she is talking anyway, about Google's AI deal with the Pentagon.

**The story in 3 sentences.**
1. She quit Google, and she says her contract means no criticising Google, for life; Google staff told the BBC
   they are all too scared to speak because of their contracts.
2. She talks anyway: she worked on Google's AI (DeepMind) and objected to where its military work was heading,
   which in April became a deal letting the Pentagon use its AI for "any lawful government purpose", and Google
   can't veto how it's used.
3. Google says it follows its AI principles and bans retaliation "of any kind"; she says she can't criticise
   Google, but "talking about facts is not criticising".

**Where I got confused.**
- 0-2 s: three text tiers at once (the big headline "SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.", the smaller
  "She quit Google." under it, a tiny source line). My eye read the big line first, but the voice (the highlight)
  starts on the small line. "SHE SAYS:" with a colon first read to me as if *she* is the one banning criticism;
  "You'd think that's one odd contract" (6.3 s) cleared it up.
- 2-6 s: a line of identical beige-trench-coat people builds out of her, fading to white down the quay. I liked
  it, but I did not know who they were (colleagues? a queue for Google?) until "Google's staff" at 8.5 s.
- 6.05 s and 14.82 s: a crash zoom into a dark window, then about 0.1 s of a flat dark plum frame, then out of
  the next shot. On the phone it reads as a blink; the first time I thought the video glitched.
- 19-20 s: the signpost ("DEC 2025 unclassified work", "APR 2026 classified work") is a nice idea, but only the
  dates are readable on a phone and the voice never says "December", so the left arm means little.
- 20.5 s: we jump from a British canal to a cherry-blossom terrace; I only knew it was Washington when the
  Pentagon and its plaque came in at 22.3 s.
- 24-28 s: the "APRIL 2026 DEAL" board carries a long small-print quote ("should not be used for domestic mass
  surveillance or autonomous weapons ... without appropriate human oversight and control") that the voice does
  not read. I stopped to read it and lost the caption; it also half-contradicts the voice (so there *are* limits?).
- 34-39 s: two white, colourless figures stand by the green door. They look unfinished, not meaningful.
- "CRITICIZING" / "criticize" (z) in the hook and captions, "criticising" (s) in the last quote. I noticed it.
- The little lilac elephant is in every shot (covers its ears at 5 s, sits on posts, benches, a boat). Cute and
  it ties the places together, but I never learned what it is for.

**Where I got bored.** From about 24 s to the end the film turns into a row of text boards: the deal board (4 s,
static), the "GOOGLE SAYS" board (5 s, its text types on), the "SHE SAYS" board (6 s, mostly blank until 36.5 s,
then types on). Each board repeats what the caption already says, so I read the same words twice, three times in
a row. 15-19 s on the narrowboat shot is also slow (she stands, a name card types on), but it is short.

**Would the first 3 s stop my scroll?** Probably, on the words: "NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE." is big,
legible on frame 0 and provocative. The picture alone would not: frame 0 is a pale cream wash, the subject is a
tiny figure (about a tenth of the frame height) in a busy wide shot, and the first two seconds only bring small
moves (the colour fills in, a window goes dark, the "Google" sign writes on). The strong visual (the line of
copies of her) only starts at 2 s and only means something at 8.5 s. Words 8/10, picture 5/10.

**What I remember 10 s later.** "Ex-Google AI engineer: no criticising Google, for life." The Pentagon deal for
"any lawful government purpose". The last line, "talking about facts is not criticising". Pictures: the pastel
canal town, the line of identical trench coats, the lilac elephant covering its ears, the Pentagon behind cherry
trees, and an orange sun disc that sits on top of every board. Not her name.
