# v2r G5 assembly v1: fresh review 1

Film: `video/deliverables/v2/v2r_G5_assembly_v1.mp4` (39.08 s, 2345 frames, 60 fps, 1080x1920, with the mix).
Reviewer: one fresh agent, two stages kept apart. Stage 1 was written before any doc was opened.
Method: frames every 0.25 s at 360 px wide, the first 3 s every 0.1 s, every frame (60 fps) around the joins and
the loop seam, a few 1:1 stills; the captions stand in for the voice.

## Cold read

Written before reading any doc, as a stranger scrolling on a phone.

**What it is about.** A woman who quit Google's AI lab says her exit contract bans her from criticising Google for
life, and she is talking anyway, about Google's AI deal with the Pentagon.

**The story in 3 sentences.**
1. She quit Google, and she says her contract means no criticising Google, for life; Google staff told the BBC
   they are all too scared to speak because of their contracts.
2. She talks anyway: she worked on Google's AI (DeepMind) and objected to where its military work was heading,
   which in April became a deal letting the Pentagon use its AI for "any lawful government purpose", and Google
   can't veto how it's used.
3. Google says it follows its AI principles and bans retaliation "of any kind"; she says she can't criticise
   Google, but "talking about facts is not criticising".

**Where I got confused.**
- 0-2 s: three text tiers at once (the big headline "SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE.", the smaller
  "She quit Google." under it, a tiny source line). My eye read the big line first, but the voice (the highlight)
  starts on the small line. "SHE SAYS:" with a colon first read to me as if *she* is the one banning criticism;
  "You'd think that's one odd contract" (6.3 s) cleared it up.
- 2-6 s: a line of identical beige-trench-coat people builds out of her, fading to white down the quay. I liked
  it, but I did not know who they were (colleagues? a queue for Google?) until "Google's staff" at 8.5 s.
- 6.05 s and 14.82 s: a crash zoom into a dark window, then about 0.1 s of a flat dark plum frame, then out of
  the next shot. On the phone it reads as a blink; the first time I thought the video glitched.
- 19-20 s: the signpost ("DEC 2025 unclassified work", "APR 2026 classified work") is a nice idea, but only the
  dates are readable on a phone and the voice never says "December", so the left arm means little.
- 20.5 s: we jump from a British canal to a cherry-blossom terrace; I only knew it was Washington when the
  Pentagon and its plaque came in at 22.3 s.
- 24-28 s: the "APRIL 2026 DEAL" board carries a long small-print quote ("should not be used for domestic mass
  surveillance or autonomous weapons ... without appropriate human oversight and control") that the voice does
  not read. I stopped to read it and lost the caption; it also half-contradicts the voice (so there *are* limits?).
- 34-39 s: two white, colourless figures stand by the green door. They look unfinished, not meaningful.
- "CRITICIZING" / "criticize" (z) in the hook and captions, "criticising" (s) in the last quote. I noticed it.
- The little lilac elephant is in every shot (covers its ears at 5 s, sits on posts, benches, a boat). Cute and
  it ties the places together, but I never learned what it is for.

**Where I got bored.** From about 24 s to the end the film turns into a row of text boards: the deal board (4 s,
static), the "GOOGLE SAYS" board (5 s, its text types on), the "SHE SAYS" board (6 s, mostly blank until 36.5 s,
then types on). Each board repeats what the caption already says, so I read the same words twice, three times in
a row. 15-19 s on the narrowboat shot is also slow (she stands, a name card types on), but it is short.

**Would the first 3 s stop my scroll?** Probably, on the words: "NO CRITICIZING GOOGLE. FOR LIFE." is big,
legible on frame 0 and provocative. The picture alone would not: frame 0 is a pale cream wash, the subject is a
tiny figure (about a tenth of the frame height) in a busy wide shot, and the first two seconds only bring small
moves (the colour fills in, a window goes dark, the "Google" sign writes on). The strong visual (the line of
copies of her) only starts at 2 s and only means something at 8.5 s. Words 8/10, picture 5/10.

**What I remember 10 s later.** "Ex-Google AI engineer: no criticising Google, for life." The Pentagon deal for
"any lawful government purpose". The last line, "talking about facts is not criticising". Pictures: the pastel
canal town, the line of identical trench coats, the lilac elephant covering its ears, the Pentagon behind cherry
trees, and an orange sun disc that sits on top of every board. Not her name.

## Art + flow (stage 2, after the docs)

**Verdict: not ready to post. Fix the 6 MUSTs below, then re-render the master and re-check.** MUSTs 1, 5 and 6 are small;
2, 3 and 4 are medium. The flow, the look, the water, the sky and the sound hold, and nothing is structural. Three of the
MUSTs (1-3) are the owner's own written rules from 2026-10-28.

Read: `OWNER_PLAYBOOK.md`, `look/DIRECTION.md`, `look/CRAFT.md` (with "Failures the eye catches first"),
`v2/THROUGHLINE.md`, `v2/ASSEMBLY.md`, `v2/ASSEMBLY_TODO.md`, then `v2/OWNER_NOTES.md` and `v3/OWNER_NOTES.md` for when
the opening rules were written, and `src/v2r/film/Film2R.tsx`, `captions.json` and `public/v2r/cue_sheet.json` to check
what the eye saw. Watched at 360 px (every 0.25 s), at 1:1 (35 stills), every frame at the joins and the seam, and every
on-screen word at 0.25 scale (the playbook's check).

**What the docs explained that the cold read missed.** The row is her own copies, aging down the quay (plum hair to
grey to white: "for life"), not her colleagues. The window is the thread (it goes dark when she quits and lights when
she talks). The two SAYS boards are the fairness pair. The cold read still got the story right; the misreadings are
small (SHOULD 6).

**What holds (keep it through the fix pass).**
- **Flow:** 8 shots, 7 joins, 0 plain cuts, and every carrier plays: the dive into W (6.10), the window match at M
  (12.98, same spot and size), the dive into her (14.86), the sun push (20.55), the blossom-tree wipe (23.80), the sun

- **The clean pen holds at 1:1:** closed shapes, no jitter, no doubled strokes, contact shadows under people and props,
  gentle cast shade (S5's paving shade is -9 to -13 L*). The two exceptions are S8's hairline ghosts (MUST 6) and the
  smudge on its board (SHOULD 2).

- **Water** is premium in S1-S4 (the mirrored wing, the gold light column, calm ripples). S5's far Potomac is flat and

- **Captions, edges and sound:** the captions pop in place (checked frame by frame at 6.25, 6.90, 8.43 and 24.02: no

## MUST-FIX (6, most severe first)

**1. The frame-0 title shows words the voice does not say, out of order.**
- **Where:** 0.00-5.95 s (f0-f357), S1, the caption band.
- **What:** `FILM2R_FRAME0` draws "SHE SAYS: NO CRITICIZING / GOOGLE. FOR LIFE." (72 px) above the first spoken line
  "She quit Google." (52 px, lowered) and its source line, and it hides caption pages 2-6.
  - The voice says "And she says her contract bans her from criticizing it... for life." (2.00-4.98), while the box
    walks "SAYS:", "NO", "NO CRITICIZING", "FOR LIFE.".
  - So the words on screen are not the words heard, the bigger line is the later one, and "her contract" (the
    mechanism) is never on screen.
  - This is v3's `spoken: 'first'` state, which the owner rejected ("Why are we having this out of order weirdly?
    Should we just show buying ..?"). It is also what confused my cold read.
- **Fix:**
  - Drop `frame0` from Film2R's `useCommon`, as Film3 did. Page 1, "She quit Google.", then shows from f0 at the normal
    caption size and baseline.
  - Pages 2-6 play as pop captions, re-paged into two (`captions_pages.txt` + `film_captions.py`): "And she says her
    contract / bans her from criticizing it..." (2.00-4.79) and "for life." (4.79-6.28).
  - The S1 source line goes; sources belong in the post caption and the pinned comment. The core line goes on the cover.
  - Re-check the loop's caption state at `captionAt` 39.04 (it must land on page 1) and gate_check's caption pages.
- **Cost:** small (one prop, the paging and a re-render).

**2. The opening draw-on is a 0.3 s pale wash.**
- **Where:** 0.00-0.30 s (f0-f18), S1.
- **What:**
  - Frame 0 is the whole scene washed toward paper: mean L* is 90.7 under the sky, against 76.9 at f24.
  - Her, the door and W are already about 80 % drawn in the 3 px A line. `pen_drawon` starts at -0.26 s, under the
    loop's un-build, so a first-time viewer never sees a stroke begin.
  - The colour flood is over by ~0.3 s.
  - This is the effect the owner called "barely visible", and the frame the owner asked "What's this tho?" about on v3.
    At 360 px it reads as a fade-in.
- **Fix** (DIRECTION rule 9; playbook, Hook and pacing):
  - Frame 0 is paper, with C as flat pale shapes, and the strokes start at f0: her outline, the door, then W.
  - Draw them in the A line at full contrast and bolder for this beat (>= 5 px at 1080, so the line reads at 360 px),
    with a visible pen tip leading each stroke, over ~0.45 s.
  - Then the colour floods from SUN0 over ~0.3 s, her coat last, done by ~0.8 s.
  - Keep W's lamp-off, the doors and the ding on their words (0.40-0.52) as the flood lands.
  - Stretch `pen_drawon.wav` to the new length.
  - The un-build lands on the new f0, so the loop still closes.
- **Cost:** medium (S1's first second and the seam). If the week can't take it, ask the owner in one line rather than
  drop it silently: v6's approval of the water came after this rule.

**3. Words in the world: fine print everywhere, and the same sentence twice.**
- **Where:** 0-39 s; worst in S6 (24.0-28.1) and S8 (36.5-39.1).
- **What:**
  - **Fine print.** At 0.25 scale (the owner's check) none of these read:
    - both caption-band source lines (S1 0-5.95, S8 33.16-36.54);
    - S2's board sub-lines and source (8.44-12.98);
    - the name tag's four small lines and source (15.07-18.80);
    - the plates' source lines (18.92-20.55);
    - the news box's source (20.7-21.8);
    - S6's five-line clause and source;
    - S7's "Google's AI Principles" line and statement line;
    - S8's credit line.
  - **The same words twice.** The boards type the caption's own words while the caption shows them:
    - "told the BBC" (8.44-10.10);
    - "any lawful government purpose" (24.03-26.08);
    - "AI principles", "bans retaliation" and "of any kind" (29.6-33.16);
    - the payoff quote (36.54-39.08).
  - My cold read stopped on S6's clause and lost the caption, and read the whole back half twice.
- **Fix:**
  - **(a) Cut every in-world line under ~40 px, and both source lines.** The sources, and the S6 clause's point (the
    deal's own limits), go to the post caption and the pinned comment (`POSTING_KIT`). Keep only these, every kept line
    >= 44 px:

    - the tag: "Sofia Liguori" + "Google DeepMind";
    - the plates: "DEC 2025 · unclassified" / "APR 2026 · classified";
    - the news box: its headline;
    - S6: "APRIL 2026 DEAL", the purpose line and "Lawful use: decided by the Pentagon";
    - S7 and S8: the heading and the quoted lines.
  - **(b) One copy of each sentence.** The boards carry the quoted words in step with the voice, as built (they are the
    beats), so the caption steps aside while a board shows them:
    - hide page 19 (24.03-26.08);
    - hide pages 22-24 (29.48-33.16), and enlarge "follows its" and "bans retaliation" on the board to >= 44 px;
    - hide page 27 (36.54-39.08), and enlarge her quote to the board's width (it is the payoff).
  - Or the reverse: the captions keep everything, and the boards keep their heading and one token the caption isn't
    saying. Either way, one copy. The two-word "SAYS" headings are the boards' labels and can stay.
- **Cost:** medium (props in six shots and the caption paging; no new drawing).

**4. The sun sits on the boards.**
