Draft, 2026-10-26. Not committed and not owner-reviewed. One writer's draft of the winning spine (`one_room`) with the

## The story

- **Throughline question:** she says she can't criticize Google, for life. So why is she talking?
- **Situation:** you've had a job where you bit your tongue and thought "once I leave, I'll say what I think". Quitting is
  supposed to set you free, so "for life" stops you cold: they can DO that?
- **The chain:** she says the ban is for life → so maybe it's just her contract → except staff told the BBC their contracts
  ban public criticism, so they're scared → but Google says staff can raise concerns safely → either way, she told the BBC
  where she stood → like the deal Google signed in April → so why is she talking, if she says she can't criticize Google?
  → because, in her words, "talking about facts is not criticising".
- **The turn (line 8):** her answer changes how everything before it reads. The deal's wording, the missing veto and
  Google's own reply were all facts, and the pages stack up beside her contract while she says it. The turn is shown on
  screen, and the narrator never explains it. There are no opinion quotes anywhere ("ashamed", "watched"), so nobody can
  answer "but that IS criticism".
- **Payoff:** the question is asked once, out loud and plainly. It holds the core line ("she says she can't criticize
  Google"), and her own words answer it. The ending has no tag and no mic-drop. Then the frame melts back to frame 0.
- **Retell test:** "A Google AI engineer quit, and she says her contract bans her from criticizing Google for life. Staff
  there told the BBC their contracts ban public criticism, so they're scared to speak out, though Google says they can
  raise concerns safely. She talked
  anyway, about Google's military deal, because in her words, talking about facts isn't criticizing."

## Where the quiet tension comes from (staging, not words)

- **One lamp-lit desk at dusk** (a generic illustration, never her real home; the narration never claims a real
  evening). Outside the window it gets a little darker through the film, and the lamp pool is the only warm light.
- **The orange highlighted line is a clock.** It starts on frame 0 and keeps running, off the desk and across the floor,
  in the background of every shot. It never stops, because the ban she describes isn't over.
- **A soft paper-unrolling rustle** sits under the voice the whole time, barely there: the one sound that never stops.
- **Names.** The staff badge has a blank name and says NAME WITHHELD. Hers has her name. The difference carries "she
  went on the record" without a word.

## What changed from the spine, and why (the judges' notes)

- **Google's beat moved up, to right after the fear (line 4).** The spine's "but Google" only landed because the
  "watched" quote came just before it. All three judges cut that quote (it's an opinion that hints at surveillance, and
  a second fear beat). Without it, "no veto, but Google says staff can raise concerns" is an "and also". Here, 6.2
  answers the fear it's actually about, which is the N9 pairing the standards judge called the fairest. Google's
  side of the deal (6.3) sits on screen at the deal. The deal now comes just before the payoff, so "facts" lands on the
  freshest facts.
- **"Either way"** is the fair hinge from the two accounts (staff scared, Google says it's safe) to what she did. It
  picks no side, and it replaces the spine's "whoever you believe".
- **One question, asked once (line 7).** It replaces the spine's re-ask followed by the callback's repeat. The
  restatement of the core line sits inside the question, so it's 11 words and nothing is said twice.
- **Grafted from `mystery`:** the throughline question said out loud, and the pages stacking as the turn.
- **Grafted from `two_contracts`:** the heavy stamp on Google's deal (the chime stays hers), Google's page drawn the same
  size as her contract, and her contract and the deal side by side for the question.
- **Kept from `one_room`:** the one lamp-lit room, no opinion quotes, the scale moment through the laptop, "maybe it's
  just her contract, but…" (here "Except…").
- **Cut:** "watched"; "so her talking is a risk" (a claim in our voice); the lit windows (they don't read with the sound
  off; replaced by a badge with a blank name); "this month"; "objected because in April" (it ran backwards; now "where
  Google's military work was heading", then "like the deal"); the anonymous staffer's quote spoken (on screen only).
