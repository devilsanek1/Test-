Did you know there's a data centre in a nuclear bunker under a park in Stockholm?

AI chips make crazy heat: one cabinet gives off as much as about 40 kettles boiling non-stop.

So data centres end up in the weirdest places: inside a mountain where the rock stays at 12 degrees, or under the sea, wired to a wind farm.

Then someone sent one into space this month, and it overheats. There's no air up there to carry the heat away, so it runs for 15 minutes and then stops to cool.

The smart move isn't hiding the heat, it's using it. That bunker under the park has been heating the city above it since 2014.

And Helsinki wants to do it about 100 times bigger: from 2027, heat from AI chips is meant to warm about 70,000 flats.
