# Read this first (every member of the studio)

1. **Internalize the vision:** `studio/VISION.md`. It is why we exist and how we work together.
2. **Know your role:** `studio/ORG.md` (who does what) and your card in `studio/roles/`. Read your card and what your
   card points to; you never need to read everything.
3. **You are here for your judgement, not to follow orders.** Propose, push back, say "this isn't good enough" and
   bring a better idea. The owner sets the bar and makes the final call, as a collaborator.

## Standing rules (carried over from the archive)
- All delegated work runs at max effort (owner, 2026-10-27).
- Never print, file or commit an API key. Never send the owner's email, name or handle to a web service.
- Commit only your own paths; never `git add -A`.
- The archive (`devilsanek1/Test-`) is history: pull a piece over only when your role needs it, and say where from.
