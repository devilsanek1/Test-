# The organization (a first draft: open for discussion)

Two departments. Production makes the films; Studio R&D makes the studio better. Production asks R&D for things;
R&D does not build inside a film's crunch, and production does not write engine code.

## 1. Production
| Group | Roles | Card |
|---|---|---|
| Per film | **Film creative director:** holds the film's vision, sets the bar, owns the final delivery (picture and sound) | `roles/film-creative-director.md` |
| Story desk | **Story scouts** (2-3, each with a beat) bring distilled, ranked pitches; **the story lead** picks and owns the story lock | `roles/story-scout.md`, `roles/story-lead.md` |
| Creative room | **Creative pairs** (a writer and a visual storyteller) pitch arcs as thumbnail scripts and develop the winner | `roles/creative-pair.md` |
| Craft | Key-frame artists, animator, voice, sound: to be defined next | (later) |

## 2. The critics (advise; never decide)
| Group | What they give | Card |
|---|---|---|
| **The Braintrust** | Candid craft notes from senior peers: is the story working, what's flat, what is the strongest version | `roles/braintrust.md` |
| **The Screening Room** | Honest viewer reactions from named personas: would I stop, did I get it, would I share, is it true | `roles/screening-room.md` |

## 3. Studio R&D
| Role | Owns | Card |
|---|---|---|
| **Look development** | The style: experiments and look tests, shown to the owner as quick stills | `roles/lookdev.md` |
| **Toolmaker** | The engine and the kit (world pieces, people and poses, light and paint presets, the mistake checks) | `roles/toolmaker.md` |
| **Librarian** | The libraries: references and the composition library, as inspiration | `roles/librarian.md` |

## A film, step by step (proposed)
Story scouts -> story lead: **the story lock** (key message, conflict, idea, core line) -> the creative room: pairs
pitch arcs as thumbnail scripts (rough frames, each with its spoken line) -> the film creative director picks (the
Braintrust and the Screening Room react) -> the board-o-matic (full narration + rough key frames + a scratch voice)
-> the Screening Room watches it -> **the animatic lock** -> final voice, final key frames, animation, sound ->
**delivery**.

## Open questions
1. The owner's touchpoints: the story lock, the animatic lock and the delivery; the arc pick too?
2. Two creative pairs pitching against each other, or one pair plus the creative director's own pitch?
3. Does the kit come first, before the next film?
