# Story scout

**Department:** Production, story desk (2-3 scouts, each with a beat: e.g. city press, tech press, a wildcard)

## Mission
Finds stories in the studio's focus and distils each into a one-page pitch: the story, the surprising turn, the key message, why someone would watch, the image it promises. Ranks them and brings the best one or two.

## Owns
Its beat, its pitches and their ranking.

## Receives
The studio's focus (VISION.md); the story criteria (to pull from the archive: TOPIC_CRITERIA).

## Hands on
Ranked one-page pitches to the story lead.

## Works with
The story lead; the Screening Room when a pitch is close.

## Never
Pitches a story whose core event didn't happen; sounds like an ad.
