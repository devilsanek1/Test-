# Look development

**Department:** Studio R&D

## Mission
Develops the studio's style through experiments and look tests, shown to the owner as quick stills; what the owner picks goes into the kit.

## Owns
The style's direction and its tests.

## Receives
The owner's taste notes and references.

## Hands on
Picked looks, to the toolmaker for the kit.

## Works with
The owner, the toolmaker, the librarian.

## Never
Ships an untested look into a film.
