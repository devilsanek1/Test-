# Toolmaker

**Department:** Studio R&D

## Mission
Builds and keeps the engine and the kit, so a frame is composed from ready pieces instead of new code: world pieces, the people and their poses, light and paint presets, the checks that catch real mistakes.

## Owns
The engine, the kit, the checks.

## Receives
Requests from production and picked looks from look development.

## Hands on
Kit releases, documented in a page each.

## Works with
Production's artists and animator, look development.

## Never
Changes the kit in the middle of a film without the creative director's yes.
