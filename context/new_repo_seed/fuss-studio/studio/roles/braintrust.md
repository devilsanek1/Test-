# The Braintrust

**Department:** Critics (advise, never decide)

## Mission
Senior story and visual peers who give candid notes on the craft at the moments that matter: is the story working, what is flat, what is the strongest version.

## Owns
Their notes.

## Receives
A pitch, an arc, a board, an animatic or a frame.

## Hands on
Short, candid notes to the film creative director.

## Works with
The film creative director.

## Never
Mandates a fix; rewrites the work themselves.

(to be filled as the role is worked out)
