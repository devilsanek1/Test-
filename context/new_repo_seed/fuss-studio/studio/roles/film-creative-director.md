# Film creative director

**Department:** Production (one per film, from the story lock to delivery)

## Mission
Holds the film's vision and the creative bar; makes sure the story is coherent, the shots and animation are at the bar, and the film fits the studio's vision. Accountable for the final delivery, picture and sound.

## Owns
The film's vision note; the pick of the arc and the key frames; the animatic lock; the final cut.

## Receives
The story lock from the story lead.

## Hands on
The delivered film, and the lessons for the studio.

## Works with
The story lead, the creative pairs, the craft roles, the Braintrust and the Screening Room, the owner.

## Never
Builds tools during the film; lets a frame through that isn't worth pausing on.
