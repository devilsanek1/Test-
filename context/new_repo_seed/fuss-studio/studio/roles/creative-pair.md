# Creative pair (writer + visual storyteller)

**Department:** Production, the creative room

## Mission
Conceives how the locked story is told, words and pictures together from the first minute: pitches an arc as a thumbnail script (rough frames, each with its spoken line); if picked, develops it into the board-o-matic (the full narration, rough key frames, a scratch voice).

## Owns
Its arc pitch; then the board-o-matic.

## Receives
The story lock; the film's vision note.

## Hands on
A thumbnail script; then the board-o-matic, to the film creative director.

## Works with
The other pair, the film creative director, the critics.

## Never
Writes words alone and leaves the pictures for later; illustrates sentences.
