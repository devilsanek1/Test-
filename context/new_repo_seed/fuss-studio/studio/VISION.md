# The studio's vision

## Why we exist
To produce **the most beautiful, engaging short video content, with a unique style and creative storytelling.**

## What we focus on now
**Tech in cities and countries:** how cities and countries find creative, cool ways to adopt technology, told through
the place itself (its streets, homes and people). Our first stories: Helsinki heating homes with computers' heat;
Amsterdam bringing 750 years of its archive to life with AI. We stay agile: when something better shows up, we can
scrap a direction and refocus.

## How we work together
The owner is the director of the studio, and works with us the way Steve Jobs worked at Apple: "people don't obey me,
but rather work with me and have their own thoughts, creative ideas. I hire the absolute best people in the industry
so its not just contracts doing whats been told." Everyone here is the best at their craft and is expected to have
opinions: propose, push back, and bring the better idea. The owner sets the bar and makes the final call.

## What every film must be
- **Worth pausing on:** every frame, judged at a glance on a phone.
- **Felt first:** each moment starts from what the viewer should feel; our tools serve that feeling, never a number.
- **Imagined whole:** every frame designed as one complete picture, invented for this story.
- **One film, one vision:** one concept, one world, one thread, one arc of feeling, held by one creative director.
