Ink Line Test

A bit of rough ink, kept clean and premium: three line treatments drawn over the painted v7a frame, beside the same frame with no lines. Stills only.

At phone size

At this size the change is gentle: A almost disappears, B and C read as ink.

Up close

The full sheet, at 1:1: the doorway, the boat and Pip, the table and chair.

My pick: B

  • One warm plum ink, glazed into the paint, so each line takes the colour of the thing it draws and sits inside the painting.
  • The messiness is in how each stroke is drawn (tapered ends, small lifts, corners that just miss), never in more strokes: no doubled lines, no black, nothing over-shooting.
  • Thin where the light hits, fuller in shadow, so the line also shows the light.
  • C's construction marks only for the opening, while the first frame draws itself on.

Two calls are yours: A, B or C (or B a little bolder), and whether the person in the frame gets a line too.
