## props:build

The props lane is built, along with the 10 s SharedKitSheet. Every prop from the build list (P1–P17) is in `src/scenes/shared/props/`. `npx tsc --noEmit` is clean. I compared the sheet and a film-size check frame against the ref and ref2 stills, and checked the line-art, fills-only and un-build states and frame-by-frame strips of the lock opening and the shutter slam.

**Conventions every prop follows**
- Each takes `x, y`, a `size` (or `scale`), `depth` (ink 4.8 / 3.4 / 2.2 / 1.6 / none) or `ink` in world px, `at`, `dur`, `outAt`, `outDur`, `seed` and `state`. Every beat is a named event time passed in as a prop, in seconds; nothing is hard-coded.
- Ink is in world units, so it grows with the camera. Pass `ink={w / zoom}` to hold a screen weight.
- The fill offset is 2.4–4 px in screen space, one per object, divided by camera zoom × prop scale. It stays the same on screen at the S10 zoom-6 cut.
- Detail drops by on-screen size. Nothing animates opacity.
- In the line-art look (`fills="paper"`) everything goes white. Bit, the sun and its overlays keep their colour, and glows and the scan beam disappear.

**How every set works**
- **Coordinates:** world coordinates are canvas pixels at that scene's camera rest (1080x1920 storyboard numbers). `x / y / scale` only move the whole set.
- **Timing:** all times are in scene seconds and every event is a prop. A missing build time means already built; a missing event time means it never happens. No beat times are hard-coded; the demo times live only in `catalog.tsx`.
- **Look:** no fades (pop-ups, 1-frame pops, draw-ons, swings), ink by depth, misregistration, people from behind, palette tokens only. Light only shows in the coloured state.
- **Layers:** `wrap` splits a set into named depth layers (nearness from the storyboard).
- **Build-out:** added last, on every set. `outAt` / `outDur` (default `MOTION.unbuild.total`) un-build just that set (lines loosen, fills shrink, the slab collapses). The shared base type is `SetBase` in `common.tsx`. I checked it with a render of OldQuarter.
