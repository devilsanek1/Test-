## build

I built the paint edge with three presets and rendered everything to `/home/user/Test-/video/review/edges/loose/`. Trim, panel and clean are unchanged, and the film default is still `clean` until you pick.

**How it works.** The frame is white paper with the scene drawn on it as line art. The colour is laid on top as a few flat blocks with crisp straight edges, like the colour blocks in reference 1 (the ground block running past the panel, a facade fill stopping dead on a vertical). Where blocks meet, the side steps in or out. Some corners stay bare, some blocks bite in, and some run off the frame. The line art carries on past the colour into the white for 14 to 66 px, ending in the white in some runs and running off the screen in others. No texture, blur, gradients or opacity.

The edge stays fixed while the camera moves, and each scene seed gives a different painting. The colour always covers the caption block. Beside the captions the paper stays under 38 px on the left and 77 px on the right, and under the right-hand button rail it stays at 18 px or less. The messiest parts sit in the top 180 px and bottom 400 px, which the phone app covers.

**The presets:**
- **paint-subtle:** basically trim, but painted. The margin wanders 8 to 40 px with small steps, one small bare corner, and the ground runs off the bottom once. About 7% of the frame is paper. Ink reaches 14 to 34 px past the colour.
- **paint-medium:** clearly painted. Three blocks stepping in and out, two bites, one bare corner of 150 to 250 px, one corner where the colour fills to the frame, and two stretches where colour reaches the edge. Typical margin is 36 px left, 64 px bottom, about 11% paper. Ink reaches 24 to 58 px.
- **paint-loose:** as messy as reference 1 dares. Four blocks, bites up to 130 px deep, and two diagonally opposite corners left as bare line art (210 to 340 px). The bottom is broken up under the app UI, with figures standing as line art on white paper. About 17% paper, with the bottom margin typically 136 px and up to about 280 px. Ink reaches 30 to 66 px.

Use `variant: 'paint'` with `amount` as the looseness (0.5, 1 and 1.6 are the three presets). A preset with `amount` scales its own looseness.

**Checks:**
- The geometry check passed 600 edges (3 presets, looseness 0.6 to 1.4, 40 seeds) with 0 failures. It covers the caption block, the paper limits, specks of white trapped between blocks, and hairlines along the frame edge.
- In the clips, 85 to 98% of edge samples stay within 2 px of their position. The small drift comes from scene content crossing the edge, not the edge moving; a debug probe at three times confirms the contour itself doesn't move.
- The edges code typechecks clean, and the existing shape checks still pass. A file in `src/scenes/scene11` from another lane currently fails typecheck; I didn't touch it.

**Render cost:** paint costs the same as trim, because both render the scene a second time as line art. Other lanes were rendering at the same time (load around 10 to 13 on 4 cores), so I took the best of four interleaved runs:

| ms/frame | clean | trim | subtle | medium | loose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| street | 135 | 184 | 195 | 166 | 202 |
| monument | 146 | 203 | 211 | 191 | 227 |

That's +23 to 55% over clean. The differences between presets are within the noise. Building the shape takes about 3 ms once per scene.

**Limitations:**
- The blocks don't follow objects the way reference 1's fills do, so a block edge can cut a key prop awkwardly. If it does, pick another seed for that scene.
- Lines past the colour end with a square cut rather than tapering.
- The loose bottom puts white paper under the app's white description text.

**One painting per scene:** I added an optional `scene` prop to `FilmEdges` that appends the scene id to the seed. Film still needs to pass `<FilmEdges scene={id}>`, which is in the core lane.

## refine

All three paint presets are refined from both judges' notes, and the final material is in `/home/user/Test-/video/review/edges/loose/final/`. While I worked, the brief recorded that you picked **`paint-subtle`** at 22:30, and the core lane made it the film default and wired it into Film. My changes work with that and don't conflict.

The geometry check passes on 2,400 edges (3 presets × amounts 0.6–1.4 × 4 scene setups × 40 seeds) with 0 failures: no holes or slivers, the captions always sit on colour, and the phone limits hold. Compared with the version the judges scored (same scenes, same settings), small glitch-like jogs dropped from about 1.5 to 0.1 per edge, and dashed line art from 1.5–5.2 to about 0 per edge.

The main changes:
- **Fewer, bigger moves:** each side is a few long, slightly tilted blocks with one clear step. The small shelves, tabs and notches are gone.
- **Bare white only where there is drawing:** each scene can now say where its line art is and which areas must stay in colour. A corner goes bare only where it holds drawing (branches, a crowd); a flat sky corner gets colour to the edge instead.
- **Figures stay whole:** heroes, the mascot, lamps and the monument's banner and pillar are never half colour, half line art.
- **No dashed lines:** a scene line running along the edge now shows as one stretch and ends once.

**The three presets, in plain words:**
- **`paint-subtle`:** trim, but painted. Each side is one long tilted edge with one clear step, plus a single bare corner where the scene has drawing (on the street: the branches at top right). If no corner qualifies, the bare white goes into the drawing at the bottom instead (the monument's line-art crowd). On a phone it looks as calm as trim.
- **`paint-medium`:** clearly painted, but calm in the part of the screen a phone shows. It adds a fully bare corner and a partly bare top corner where there's drawing, colour run to the edge in the emptiest corner, and one bite out of the left side with the scene's line art inside (the vista's city). The messiest parts sit under the app's top and bottom bars.
- **`paint-loose`:** as messy as reference 1 dares. Two bare corners, a deep left bite and a right bite in the visible area (the street's car and roofline continue as line art on white), and the bottom broken up under the app bar.

**Recommendation:** `paint-medium` as the film default, once each scene has its own content map. Medium is the first preset that reads as "white paper, colour laid on top, unfinished" at a glance. It keeps that look out of the way of the captions and the app's buttons, and it costs the same as trim. Keep `paint-subtle` as the default until those maps exist, because it's the safest on a scene the code knows nothing about. Use `paint-loose` for the hook and loop moments, not the whole film.

**What's still needed:** the film's own scenes have no content maps yet; only the three lab shots do. Each scene needs a short entry in `src/lib/edges/hints.ts`, made with a scan tool from a clean render plus hand-added rectangles for heroes and props. This is already on the core lane's to-do list. Until then a scene stays calm in the visible area, but a figure standing right at the frame edge can get trimmed by the white margin, as trim's sliver would.

**Cost:** a paint edge costs what trim costs, about +40% render time over no edge. The measurement was noisy because other lanes were rendering on the same machine.
