# arcane-cinematic-guide

Editor:

1. Length follows the job: action 0.5-1 s, transitions ~1.5 s, scale and establishing 2.5-3.5 s, intimate 3 s, the cozy refuge 3-6 s (held longest), so ~15 shots in a 40 s film.
2. Half the shots barely move: 48 % are locked or barely moving (< 3.5 %); a still shot keeps a 1-1.5 px float, and the movement comes from inside the frame (steam, light, a walk).
3. On a wide, pull back slightly: default is a 1.5-3 % pull-out or a hold (Arcane pulls out more than it pushes in, 46:34). A push-in needs a reason, and the closer the shot, the bigger the move: median 4.8 % wide, 6.5 % mid, 7.5 % close.
4. No single house easing: moves that arrive or reveal start fast and slow down; pushes that matter speed up and get cut at full speed. A third of Arcane's moves start at full speed on the cut, and a third never come to rest.
5. ONE big move per video: the camera rises 0.2-0.3 of the frame height while pulling out 1.3-2x (or pushing in 4 %) over 3.5-5 s, then holds 1-1.5 s. It is the only shot with 5 depth layers (default 3, 4 for establishing shots), and no layer moves faster than 450 px/s.
6. Scale comes from a cut, not a long wide: cut from a close shot (often under 1 s) to a tiny figure at 0.04-0.08 of the frame height, about 10x smaller (6-20x). Light and colour temperature stay the same, and a face or hands come back within 5 s.
7. Eye level is home (48 %), a low angle means power (33 %); high or overhead shots (13 %) are for institutions, never looking down on one person under critique; at most 2° of roll (6° in action), never a held tilted frame, no spins.
8. One light, behind the head, in the middle third of the frame (72 % of shots): its bright area runs up into the captions at lightness L* >= 70, and a silhouette is at least 25 L* darker. Copy Arcane's light-and-dark layout, never its darkness (our frame average >= 55; Arcane's median is 19).
9. One hue per place (78 % of shots); the opposite temperature appears only as one accent at 1-3 % of the frame; a warm/cold switch happens across a cut, only during motion (a wipe or a whip), once per video.
10. One change per cut (size, brightness or temperature); the brightest spot stays within 0.2 of the frame height of where it was; a brightness jump of >= 25 L* means a new world or an impact; never more than 3 flashes per second.
