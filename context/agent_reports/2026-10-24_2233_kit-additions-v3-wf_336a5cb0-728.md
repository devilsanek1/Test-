## props:build

All the props requests in `storyboard_v3.json` are in the shared kit and exported from the barrel (`src/scenes/shared/props/index.ts`). Every existing export still works. `npx tsc --noEmit` is clean. I checked each piece in stills and frame strips (the line-art look and the un-build too). They show on a new sheet page: `--props='{"mode":"v3"}'`. The default kit sheet renders exactly as before.

**What landed (names / props)**
- **`LabelTag`** (`props/Labels.tsx`): every label kind.
  - Kinds: `tag` (string), `card` (pin), `slip` (paper clip), `notice` (4 tape strips), `sign` (A-frame), `stamp`.
  - Placement: either `box` [x0,y0,x1,y1], which is the paper, or `x,y` plus optional `w,h`. With x,y the anchor is the paper centre, except a tag hangs from x,y and a sign stands on it.
  - Text: `text` or `lines`. `fontSize` is in world px (divide the on-screen target by the camera zoom); the font only shrinks to fit a box, never grows.
  - Timing: `at`, `outAt` (a one-frame shrink, or `outMode="unbuild"`), `flipTo` (`{at, text}` or `flipAt`), `swing` (true, a time, or a list of times).
  - Extras: `leader`, `rot`/`tilt`, `paper`, `ink` / `inkPx` (screen px), `tapeStagger`.
  - It also accepts the names the S11 stand-in uses: `style`/`variant`/`type` for the kind, `size` for the font size, `color` for the paper. S02 already imports it.
- **`BackSoonTag`**: new `lettered`, `text`, `font`, `w`, `inkPx`, `twirlAt`, `liftAt` (swings up stepped, then pops off) and `outAt` (pops off). The old clock tag is unchanged. **`ShutterPadlock`** has a new optional `outMode="pop"`.
- **`RewindGlyph`** (`r`, `at`, `outAt`, `nudgeAt`): two cream triangles in the PowerGlyph's size and weight.
- **`SunPress`** has two new modes:
  - `mode="release"`: the frame-0 state, sun at the bottom of the squash with the ripple ring at its emission radius.
  - `mode="reverse"`: the S12 ending, stepped at 12 Hz, then held at the bottom.
  - Both use the same curve, so S12's last frame matches S01's frame 0.
- **`RollerShutter`**: `stopAt` is a fraction (govC 0.5) and stops with a one-frame jolt; `liftMode="reverse"` plays the slam backwards, stepped. Helpers: `stepTime`, `slamCurve`, `slamLength`.
- **`CounterBoard`** (`props/CounterBoard.tsx`):
  - Placement: `box`, `riseAt`.
  - Text slots: `header`/`headerAt`, `flagAt` + `note`, `footer`/`footerAt`.
  - Count: `gatesAt[]`, hand-drawn tallies of 5 bars, no digits. `stamps: [{at, text}]`.
  - Emblem: the shield-dome sits top-left.
  - Helper: `counterBoardZones(box)` gives the zones from the storyboard layout, plus Bit's perch on the top edge.
- **`DiagramHand`, `SiteIcon`, `KeyCross`** (`props/Diagram.tsx`): these are the S02 stand-ins moved into the kit with the same props, plus `dashPath` and `pointAlong`. The hand also gets `drawAt`/`undrawAt` for the S05 "redraws" beat, and `dash`, `sleeve`, `cuff`.
- **`StickyNote`**: takes the S11 stand-in's props, plus `strike`, which strikes only the measured width of that phrase.
- **`Sticker`**: "I ♥ PHP" with a drawn peach heart and a scribbled "?", 124 px wide, static.
- **`CheckBoxTag`** (`ticked` / `tickAt`), **`CheckBoxTags`** (hundreds of tags drawn in 4 paths), **`SupportTag`** (`checkAt`, `stampAt`).
- **`SunGlowSteps` at r260** and **`SunPie` n=2 cream=1** needed no code changes; both are checked on the sheet.

**Differences from the scene agents' stand-ins**
- `DiagramHand` has no hand fill by default, as the request asks. The S02 stand-in used white; pass `handFill` to get it back.
- `BackSoonTag` defaults to the old clock tag, so S11 must pass `lettered`.
- `StickyNote` now flutters in by default when `slapAt` is set; `flutter={false}` turns that off.

**Render cost** (24 frames, one at a time, with the machine heavily loaded): FontTest baseline 208 ms/frame; the v3 sheet with about 45 new pieces 216 ms/frame. A label, tag or note costs well under 1 ms and the board about 2 ms.

**Deferred / known gaps** (also in `docs/SHARED_PROPS.md`, new section 9b)
