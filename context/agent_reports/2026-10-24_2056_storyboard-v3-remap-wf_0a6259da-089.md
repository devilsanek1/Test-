**Usage** (run from `/home/user/Test-/video`):
```
python3 -I scripts/anchor/estimate_timing.py        # rebuild assets/timing_est_v3.json
python3 -I scripts/anchor/words.py [--find purpose] [--at 4.2] [--anchor 14 -0.1] [--pages]

python3 -I scripts/anchor/reanchor.py SB --timing T -o OUT     # exit 3 = some anchors landed on changed words
python3 -I scripts/anchor/selftest.py
```
- **Anchor format:** `{"w", "dt"}`, plus optional `"edge": "e"` (word end), `"gap": 0.5` (a fraction of the pause after the word, so a cut stays inside the pause) and `"word"` (a text check). `w` can also be `START`, `END`, `VO_START` or `VO_END`. The storyboard needs a top-level `wordsRef` (the plain script).
- **Captions can be text only:** `resolve.py` fills in every line, page and highlight time from the words, and checks the lines cover the script word for word.
- **Timing choice:** `resolve.py` uses `assets/timing_v3.json` if it exists, otherwise the estimate. It also accepts a raw ASR word list (e.g. `assets/vo_v3_draft_take_words_raw.json`) and splits glued digits like "about7".
- **Mismatches:** if `wordsRef` doesn't match the timing's words, it prints a word-level diff and exits 2 without writing anything.
- **`reanchor.py`:** it re-maps anchors through a difflib alignment. It flags anchors whose word was changed or deleted, rebuilds caption lines, and drops emptied lines, pages and highlights. It handled the draft take, which differs from the final text in 22 places.

**What the tests cover:**
- Anchor arithmetic, auto-filled caption times, scene-local caption tones and the loop un-build.
- Error handling: wrong-word checks, un-anchored seconds, the end-of-voice guard and `--force`.
- The mismatch diff, and re-anchoring both from a file and in memory.
- The estimate's structure, and that it matches the current script.
- My emitter's `scenes.json` and `captions.json` are byte-identical to `gen-scenes.mjs` and `gen-captions.mjs --storyboard` on both `docs/storyboard_v2voice.json` and `docs/storyboard.json`.
